# Walla Walla Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small, ambitious valley near the Oregon border, known for powerful, polished reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[Columbia Valley]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** loess, gravel, silt
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Syrah]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling foothills of the Blue Mountains, with a diverse set of vineyard sites.

## Soil and geology
Windblown silt and loess, with cobbles in the Rocks District of Milton-Freewater.

## Climate
Slightly cooler and wetter than most of the Columbia Valley.

## History
Leonetti Cellar was founded in 1977 and started the area's rise to fame.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Syrah.

## Key wines
- Walla Walla Cabernet Sauvignon
- Walla Walla Syrah

## What to look for
- Rich fruit and fine tannins.
- The Rocks District of Milton-Freewater is known for unusual savoury Syrah.
