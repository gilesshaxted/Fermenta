# Avignonesi

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Montepulciano estate famous for one of Italy's rarest Vin Santos as well as Vino Nobile.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Vino Nobile di Montepulciano]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sangiovese]], [[Merlot]], [[Malvasia]]
> - **Styles:** [[Vin Santo]]

## About
A large estate in Montepulciano, farmed biodynamically in recent years. The Vino Nobile is classic and savoury, and the sweet wines are deeply concentrated.

## Known for
- Vin Santo di Montepulciano Occhio di Pernice
- Vino Nobile di Montepulciano
- Desiderio
