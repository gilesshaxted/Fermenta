# Terrantez do Pico

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A rare, high-acid white from Pico, revived after near extinction.

## Where it's from
Native to Pico and nearly lost to phylloxera and mildew. It is not the same as Terrantez from Madeira.

## Where it grows
- **Pico, Azores:** small plantings

## What it tastes like
- Citrus, apple, herbs and salt

## Classic wines
- Pico Terrantez

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Fresh cheese

## Good to know
- Rare and mineral.
- Different from Madeira's Terrantez.
