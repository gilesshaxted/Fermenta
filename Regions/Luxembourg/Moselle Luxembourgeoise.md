# Moselle Luxembourgeoise

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Luxembourg's only wine region, a narrow strip of south-facing vineyards along the Moselle river, making crisp whites and sparkling Crémant.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Luxembourg]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Cool continental
> - **Soils:** limestone, marl, clay, sandstone, gypsum
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Pinot Blanc]], [[Auxerrois]], [[Müller-Thurgau]], [[Elbling]]

## Landscape and topography
Vineyards run along the left bank of the Moselle for about 40 kilometres, on slopes facing east and south-east.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, marl and clay, with sandstone and gypsum in places.

## Climate
Cool continental, with the river moderating temperatures and a long, slow ripening season.

## History
The Romans planted vines along the Moselle, and the tradition continued. The Elbling grape is thought to be one of the oldest of the area.

## Production and wine styles
Light, dry whites and Crémant de Luxembourg, a traditional method sparkling wine. Rivaner (the local name for Müller-Thurgau) and Elbling are widely grown, with Pinot Gris, Auxerrois and Riesling the quality leaders.

## Key wines
- Crémant de Luxembourg
- Moselle Luxembourgeoise Riesling
- Auxerrois
- Pinot Gris
- Elbling

## What to look for
- Crisp, refreshing whites.
- Try the Crémant.
