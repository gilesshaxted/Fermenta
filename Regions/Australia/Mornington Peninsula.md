# Mornington Peninsula

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cool peninsula surrounded by water, known for Pinot Noir and Chardonnay.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Australia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Oceania]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** clay, loam, volcanic, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Gris]]

## Landscape and topography
A hilly peninsula with sea on three sides, south-east of Melbourne.

## Soil and geology
Red volcanic soils on hills, with sandy and clay soils elsewhere.

## Climate
Strongly maritime, with wind and a long, cool season.

## History
Vines were planted in the 1950s and the modern industry grew from the 1970s.

## Production and wine styles
Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Pinot Gris.

## Key wines
- Mornington Peninsula Pinot Noir
- Mornington Peninsula Chardonnay

## What to look for
- Silky, perfumed Pinot.
- Fresh, mineral Chardonnay.
