# Passito

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Italian sweet wine made from grapes dried before pressing.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Category:** Sweet
> - **Colour:** Varies
> - **Sweetness:** Sweet
> - **Country:** Italy
> - **Main grapes:** [[Muscat of Alexandria]], [[Corvina]], [[Garganega]], [[Malvasia]]
> - **Regions:** [[Sicily]], [[Valpolicella]], [[Veneto]], [[Puglia]], [[Sardinia]]

## How it's made
Ripe grapes are dried on mats or racks to concentrate sugar. Fermentation then leaves residual sweetness. Recioto della Valpolicella is the red example and Pantelleria makes famous Muscat Passito.

## Where it's made
Found across Italy, especially Veneto, Sicily, Sardinia and Puglia.

## What it tastes like
- Raisin, dried apricot, honey
- Orange peel
- Sweet and concentrated

## Pairs with
- Dessert
- Aged cheese
- Dried fruit

## Good to know
- Zibibbo is the Sicilian name for Muscat of Alexandria.
- Amarone is the dry sibling of Recioto.
