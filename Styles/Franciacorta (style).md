# Franciacorta (style)

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Italy's top traditional method sparkling wine, from Lombardy.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Category:** Sparkling
> - **Colour:** White
> - **Sweetness:** Dry
> - **Country:** Italy
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Pinot Blanc]]
> - **Regions:** [[Franciacorta]], [[Lombardy]]

## How it's made
Made by the bottle fermentation method, mainly from Chardonnay and Pinot Noir. Franciacorta must age at least 18 months on lees, longer than Champagne's minimum for non vintage.

## Where it's made
The hills south of Lake Iseo in Lombardy.

## What it tastes like
- Ripe apple, citrus, brioche
- Creamy, rounded bubbles
- Hazelnut with age

## Pairs with
- Seafood risotto
- Fish
- Antipasti

## Good to know
- Satèn is an all white wine made with slightly less pressure and a silky texture.
- Franciacorta was one of the first Italian wines to earn DOCG status.
