# Koshu

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Japan's best known wine grape, making delicate dry whites.

## Where it's from
Koshu has been grown in Yamanashi for a very long time and is thought to be a hybrid of Vitis vinifera with a wild Asian vine. It is Japan's flagship wine grape.

## Where it grows
- **Yamanashi, Japan:** the heartland
- **Katsunuma, Japan:** many wineries

## What it tastes like
- Citrus, pear, white flowers and light mineral

## Classic wines
- Koshu
- Koshu sur lie

## Pairs with
- Sushi
- Tempura
- Light seafood

## Good to know
- Pale pink skins but usually made into white wine.
- Subtle and low in aroma, so lees ageing is common.
