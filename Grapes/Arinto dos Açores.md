# Arinto dos Açores

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A salty, volcanic white grown on Pico island in the Azores.

## Where it's from
A local variety of Pico, grown in lava-walled plots. It is considered distinct from the mainland Arinto.

## Where it grows
- **Pico, Azores:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Lemon, salt, mineral and citrus

## Classic wines
- Pico Arinto dos Açores

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Grilled fish
- Shellfish

## Good to know
- A salty, mineral white.
- Not the same as mainland Arinto.
