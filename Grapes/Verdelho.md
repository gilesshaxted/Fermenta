# Verdelho

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A zesty grape that makes fortified Madeira and also fresh, fruity whites in Australia.

## Where it's from
Madeira in Portugal, from where it travelled to Australia in the 19th century.

## Where it grows
- **Madeira, Portugal:** off-dry fortified wine
- **Hunter Valley and Western Australia:** dry whites

## What it tastes like
- Lime, tropical fruit and a touch of honey

## Classic wines
- Verdelho Madeira
- Australian Verdelho

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Thai food
- Grilled fish

## Good to know
- Keeps high acidity in hot places.
- A good value white.
