# Château Margaux

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A First Growth estate that defines the silky, floral and perfumed style of Margaux.

## About
Château Margaux is the benchmark estate of its appellation, making a refined, elegant red alongside a rare dry white from Sauvignon Blanc. The wines are known for finesse and aromatic lift.
