# Hunter Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Australia's oldest wine region, known for unique, low-alcohol Sémillon that ages beautifully.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Australia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Oceania]]
> - **Climate:** Humid subtropical
> - **Soils:** volcanic, alluvial, sandy loam, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sémillon]], [[Syrah]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Verdelho]]

## Landscape and topography
Flat to gently rolling land about 150 km north of Sydney.

## Soil and geology
Volcanic soils, alluvial loams and sandy soils.

## Climate
Warm and humid, with summer rain and harvest threatened by rain.

## History
Vines were planted in the 1820s, and Sémillon has been the regional specialty.

## Production and wine styles
Sémillon, Shiraz, Chardonnay and Verdelho.

## Key wines
- Hunter Valley Semillon
- Hunter Valley Shiraz

## What to look for
- Lemon and toast as Sémillon ages.
- Hunter Shiraz is medium bodied and earthy.
