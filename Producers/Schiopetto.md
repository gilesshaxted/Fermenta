# Schiopetto

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A pioneering Collio estate that modernised white winemaking in Friuli.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Collio]], [[Friuli-Venezia Giulia]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Friulano]], [[Pinot Blanc]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Pinot Gris]]

## About
Mario Schiopetto brought cool fermentation and cleaner winemaking to the region from the 1960s. The wines are precise, aromatic and bright.

## Known for
- Collio Friulano
- Collio Pinot Bianco
- Collio Sauvignon
