# San Antonio Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> An even cooler coastal region, with Leyda Valley making crisp whites and cool-climate Syrah.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Chile]]
> - **Part of:** [[South America]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** granite, clay, loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Syrah]]

## Landscape and topography
Low hills close to the ocean, in a series of valleys.

## Soil and geology
Granite and clay soils.

## Climate
Cool, foggy and windy.

## History
Developed in the late 1990s, with Leyda as its best-known subzone.

## Production and wine styles
Sauvignon Blanc, Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and cool-climate Syrah.

## Key wines
- Leyda Sauvignon Blanc
- Leyda Pinot Noir

## What to look for
- Mineral, steely and salty whites.
- Fresh, peppery Syrah.
