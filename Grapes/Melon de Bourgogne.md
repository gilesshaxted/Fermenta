# Melon de Bourgogne

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The grape of Muscadet. Crisp, saline and made for oysters.

## Where it's from
From Burgundy, but long since the grape of the Atlantic Loire after a hard frost in 1709 forced a replanting with a hardy variety.

## Where it grows
- **Muscadet, Loire Valley, France:** Sèvre-et-Maine is the top area

## What it tastes like
- **Basic:** neutral, lemony and light
- **Sur lie:** creamier texture and a salty finish

## Classic wines
- Muscadet Sèvre-et-Maine sur lie
- Cru communaux such as Clisson

## Pairs with
- Oysters
- Moules marinières
- Grilled sardines

## Good to know
- Sur lie means the wine rests on its fine lees.
- Better Muscadets can age well.
