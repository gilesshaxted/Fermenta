# Donkey & Goat

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A pioneering Berkeley natural wine producer using Rhône varieties from northern California.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Region:** [[Mendocino County]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Roussanne]], [[Pinot Noir]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]]

## About
Donkey & Goat uses fruit from vineyards in Mendocino and El Dorado and works with native yeasts and minimal sulphur. The wines are fresh and savoury.

## Known for
- Stone Crusher Roussanne
- Five Thirteen
- Filigreen Farm Pinot Noir
