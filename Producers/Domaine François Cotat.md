# Domaine François Cotat

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A tiny Chavignol domaine producing some of the most sought-after Sauvignon Blanc in the Loire.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Sancerre]], [[Loire Valley]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sauvignon Blanc]]

## About
The Cotat family farm steep limestone slopes at Chavignol with a very traditional approach. The wines are intense, mineral and slow to evolve, quite unlike a fruity modern Sauvignon.

## Known for
- Les Culs de Beaujeu
- La Grande Côte
- Sancerre Cuvée Les Caillottes
