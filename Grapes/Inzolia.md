# Inzolia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Sicilian white used in Marsala and in everyday dry wines.

## Where it's from
Grown in Sicily for centuries and known as Ansonica in Tuscany.

## Where it grows
- **Sicily, Italy:** across the west of the island

## What it tastes like
- Almond, citrus, herbs and melon

## Classic wines
- Marsala
- Inzolia

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Pasta
- Salads

## Good to know
- Known as Ansonica in Tuscany.
- Often blended with Catarratto.
