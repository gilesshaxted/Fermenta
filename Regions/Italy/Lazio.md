# Lazio

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The region around Rome, with volcanic soils and easy whites such as Frascati.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** volcanic, tuff, clay, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Malvasia]], [[Trebbiano]], [[Cesanese]], [[Merlot]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills around Rome, including the Alban Hills (Castelli Romani) and the lake-filled volcanic country.

## Soil and geology
Volcanic ash, tuff and lava.

## Climate
Mediterranean, with hot summers.

## History
Frascati was the Roman wine of choice for centuries. Quality is improving thanks to producers who prefer lower yields.

## Production and wine styles
White Frascati from Malvasia and Trebbiano, Est! Est!! Est!!! di Montefiascone, and Cesanese red.

## Key wines
- Frascati Superiore
- Cesanese del Piglio
- Est! Est!! Est!!!

## What to look for
- Light and fresh with pear and almond.
- Cesanese is a local red worth trying.
