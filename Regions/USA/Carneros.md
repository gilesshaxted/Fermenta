# Carneros

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cool bayside district shared by Napa and Sonoma, ideal for Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and sparkling wine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[Napa Valley]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** clay, loam, shallow clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]]

## Landscape and topography
Low rolling hills on the northern shore of San Pablo Bay.

## Soil and geology
Shallow clay loams over compact subsoil, which restrict vigour.

## Climate
Windy and foggy, cooler than any other part of Napa.

## History
Planted heavily with Pinot and Chardonnay since the 1970s, partly for sparkling wine.

## Production and wine styles
Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and sparkling wines.

## Key wines
- Carneros Chardonnay
- Carneros Pinot Noir
- Carneros sparkling wine

## What to look for
- Elegant, lively wines with higher acidity than most of Napa.
- Boundaries overlap with Sonoma.
