# Bagrationi 1882

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Georgia's best known maker of traditional method sparkling wine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Georgia]]
> - **Region:** [[Kartli]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chinuri]], [[Mtsvane]]
> - **Styles:** [[Traditional method]]

## About
Based near Tbilisi, with a cellar dating back to the late nineteenth century. It makes bottle fermented sparkling wines, some using local grapes.

## Known for
- Bagrationi Brut
- Bagrationi Classic
