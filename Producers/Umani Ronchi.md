> [!abstract] At a glance

## About
A family owned house working across the Marche and into Abruzzo. The Verdicchio is fresh and mineral, and the reds are ripe and robust.

## Known for
- Casal di Serra Verdicchio dei Castelli di Jesi
- Plenio Verdicchio Riserva
- Pelago
- Cùmaro Rosso Conero
