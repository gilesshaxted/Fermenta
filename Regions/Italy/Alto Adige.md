# Alto Adige

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Italy's northernmost wine zone, with high-altitude whites and local reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Trentino-Alto Adige]]
> - **Climate:** Alpine
> - **Soils:** porphyry, limestone, moraine, granite
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Gris|Pinot Grigio]], [[Gewürztraminer]], [[Lagrein]], [[Pinot Blanc]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]]

## Landscape and topography
Steep terraces and slopes in the Adige and Isarco valleys, at roughly 200 to 1,000 metres.

## Soil and geology
Porphyry, limestone, moraine and granite.

## Climate
Alpine, with strong day-night temperature swings.

## History
Home to many cooperatives, and a long tradition of high-quality Pinot Bianco and Gewürztraminer.

## Production and wine styles
Mostly white wines. The area also makes Lagrein, a deep, plummy local red.

## Key wines
- Alto Adige Pinot Grigio
- Gewürztraminer (Traminer Aromatico)
- Lagrein
- Alto Adige Sauvignon

## What to look for
- Peach, citrus and fresh acidity.
- Lagrein is dark, plum and violet.
