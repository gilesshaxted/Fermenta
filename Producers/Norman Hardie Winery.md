# Norman Hardie Winery

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Prince Edward County producer that helped define the region's cool climate style.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Canada]]
> - **Region:** [[Prince Edward County]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]]

## About
Norman Hardie makes minimal intervention Chardonnay and Pinot Noir on limestone soils. The wines are mineral and restrained.

## Known for
- County Chardonnay
- Cuvée L Chardonnay
