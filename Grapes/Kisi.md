# Kisi

> [!abstract] At a glance
> An aromatic Kakheti white, now often made as an amber qvevri wine.

## Where it's from
Almost lost in the Soviet period, Kisi was revived by modern producers in Kakheti.

## Where it grows
- **Kakheti, Georgia:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Apricot, peach, herbs and spice

## Classic wines
- Kisi qvevri

## Pairs with
- Roast pork
- Spiced dishes
- Cheese

## Good to know
- Best known as an amber wine.
- Floral and fruity.
