# Slovakia

Part of [[Europe]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small wine country in the Carpathians, with fresh whites, a share of historic Tokaj and growing quality.

## How wine is organised here
- Slovakia has six wine regions: Small Carpathians, Južnoslovenská, Nitra, Central Slovakia, Eastern Slovakia and Tokaj.
- Quality wines are labelled by ripeness, with terms such as Neskorý zber and Výber z hrozna.
- The Slovak name for Grüner Veltliner is Veltlínske zelené and Blaufränkisch is Frankovka modrá.
