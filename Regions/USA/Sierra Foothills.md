# Sierra Foothills

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Gold Rush country, with high-elevation old vines on granite and volcanic soils.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[North America]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** granite, volcanic, red clay, loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Zinfandel]], [[Syrah]], [[Barbera]], [[Grenache]]

## Landscape and topography
Steep foothills of the Sierra Nevada, with vineyards at 1,000 to 3,000 feet.

## Soil and geology
Decomposed granite, volcanic soils and red clay.

## Climate
Hot days and cool nights, tempered by altitude.

## History
Gold Rush settlers planted vines in the 1850s, and Amador County's Zinfandel is among the state's oldest.

## Production and wine styles
Zinfandel, Syrah, Barbera and Rhône and Italian varieties.

## Key wines
- Amador Zinfandel
- Sierra Foothills Barbera

## What to look for
- Rustic, ripe and spicy.
- Old vine Zinfandel is a speciality.
