# Parellada

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A light, floral grape and one of the three main Cava grapes.

## Where it's from
Native to Catalonia, grown at higher altitudes in Penedès. It gives Cava its freshness and aroma.

## Where it grows
- **Penedès, Spain:** at high altitude

## What it tastes like
- Green apple, lemon, white flowers and herbs

## Classic wines
- Cava

## Pairs with
- Tapas
- Seafood
- Aperitifs

## Good to know
- Adds elegance to Cava.
- Needs cool sites.
