# Luján de Cuyo

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The classic Malbec area with some of the oldest vines in Mendoza.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Argentina]]
> - **Part of:** [[Mendoza]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** alluvial, clay, gravel
> - **Main grapes:** [[Malbec]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## Landscape and topography
A flat to gently sloping area just south of Mendoza city, at about 900 to 1,100 metres.

## Soil and geology
Alluvial clay and gravel soils.

## Climate
Warm and dry, with cool nights.

## History
Argentina's first DOC (Denominación de Origen Controlada), created in 1989 for Malbec.

## Production and wine styles
Malbec above all, with Cabernet and others.

## Key wines
- Luján de Cuyo Malbec

## What to look for
- Rich plum, violet and soft tannin.
- Wineries such as Catena and Achaval-Ferrer are well known.
