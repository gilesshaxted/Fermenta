# Sula Vineyards

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The Nashik winery that did most to put Indian wine on the map, with a big visitor operation.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[India]]
> - **Region:** [[Nashik]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Chenin Blanc]], [[Syrah]]

## About
Based in the Nashik area of Maharashtra, Sula is a large, commercially minded producer that also runs a popular tasting and hospitality side. Wines are fresh and fruit-led, with the whites generally the easiest way in.

## Known for
- Rasa
- Dindori Reserve
- Sula Chenin Blanc
