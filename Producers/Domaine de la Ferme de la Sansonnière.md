# Domaine de la Ferme de la Sansonnière

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Mark Angeli's biodynamic Anjou domaine, an early influence on the Loire natural wine movement.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Anjou]], [[Loire Valley]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chenin Blanc]], [[Cabernet Franc]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]]

## About
Ferme de la Sansonnière works organically and biodynamically in Anjou, making Chenin Blanc with little intervention. The wines are energetic and individual and have inspired many younger growers.

- La Lune
- Les Bonnes Blanches
