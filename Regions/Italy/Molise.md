# Molise

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Italy's second smallest region, with the rediscovered red grape Tintilia.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Montepulciano]], [[Tintilia]], [[Falanghina]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills between the Apennines and the Adriatic, south of Abruzzo.

## Soil and geology
Clay and limestone.

## Climate
Mediterranean, tempered by altitude.

## History
Molise was part of Abruzzo until 1963. Tintilia, nearly lost, was revived in the 21st century.

## Production and wine styles
Montepulciano, Tintilia, Falanghina and Biferno. Small production.

## Key wines
- Biferno
- Tintilia del Molise
- Molise Falanghina

## What to look for
- Quiet and rarely exported.
- Tintilia is a distinctive local red.
