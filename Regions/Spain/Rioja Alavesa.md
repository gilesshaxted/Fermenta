# Rioja Alavesa

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A north-bank strip within the Basque Country, sheltered by mountains, with chalky soils and lighter wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Part of:** [[Rioja]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tempranillo]]
> - **Styles:** [[Carbonic maceration]]

## Landscape and topography
Small vineyards on terraces facing south beneath the Sierra de Cantabria, with villages such as Laguardia.

## Soil and geology
Chalky clay and limestone, giving fine-grained, aromatic wines.

## Climate
Cooler, with the sierra protecting from the north.

## History
Alavesa is in the province of Álava, not La Rioja. Many small family growers sell to local cellars.

## Production and wine styles
Tempranillo, often carbonic maceration for young wines (vino joven), plus complex oak-aged wines.

## Key wines
- Rioja Alavesa Crianza
- Rioja Alavesa Joven

## What to look for
- Fresh, perfumed and fruit-forward reds.
- Good balance of acidity.
