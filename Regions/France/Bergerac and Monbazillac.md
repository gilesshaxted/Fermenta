# Bergerac and Monbazillac

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Bordeaux's eastern neighbour, with similar grapes at modest prices, plus the sweet wine Monbazillac.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[South-West France]]
> - **Climate:** Maritime
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, gravel
> - **Main grapes:** [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Sémillon]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]]
> - **Styles:** [[Sauternes (style)|Sauternes style]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling hills on the Dordogne river, not far from Saint-Émilion.

## Soil and geology
Clay, limestone and gravel.

## Climate
Maritime, very similar to Bordeaux.

## History
Monbazillac was once exported to Holland and is still made in the style of Sauternes.

## Production and wine styles
Red and white blends like Bordeaux. Monbazillac is sweet from botrytis-affected Sémillon.

## Key wines
- Bergerac rouge
- Bergerac sec
- Monbazillac
- Pécharmant

## What to look for
- A taste of Bordeaux at lower prices.
- Monbazillac is a worthy alternative to Sauternes.
