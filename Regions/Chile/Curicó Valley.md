# Curicó Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A central valley region with Chile's biggest names, such as Miguel Torres, and reliable value wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Chile]]
> - **Part of:** [[South America]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** alluvial, clay, volcanic
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Merlot]]

## Landscape and topography
A flat valley between the Andes and the coastal range.

## Soil and geology
Alluvial, clay and some volcanic soils.

## Climate
Warm and dry, with cooling breezes in the Pacific-facing areas.

## History
Miguel Torres brought stainless steel and modern technology to Chile in the 1970s.

## Production and wine styles
Sauvignon Blanc, Cabernet Sauvignon, Chardonnay and Merlot.

## Key wines
- Curicó Sauvignon Blanc
- Curicó Cabernet Sauvignon

## What to look for
- Fruity, reliable wines.
- Good value.
