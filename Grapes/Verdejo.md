# Verdejo

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Fresh, herbal white from the high plateau of Rueda.

## Where it's from
Possibly brought to central Spain by Mozarabs in the 11th century. It nearly died out in the 20th century.

## Where it grows
- **Rueda, Spain:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Lime, fennel, fresh herbs and a slightly bitter finish

## Classic wines
- Rueda Verdejo
- Rueda Superior

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Salads
- Fresh goat's cheese

## Good to know
- Often a great value white.
- Best young.
