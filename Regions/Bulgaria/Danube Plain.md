# Danube Plain

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cooler northern region along the Danube, home of the light red Gamza (Kadarka) and many international varieties.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Bulgaria]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** loam, chernozem, limestone, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Kadarka]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Aligoté]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]]

## Landscape and topography
Gently rolling land on the south bank of the Danube, which marks the border with Romania.

## Soil and geology
Fertile loam and chernozem, with limestone and clay in places.

## Climate
Continental, with colder winters and hotter, drier summers than the south, tempered a little by the river.

## History
The region has a long winegrowing tradition and was one of the main areas for state wine production before 1990. Gamza, known elsewhere as Kadarka, is the traditional local red.

## Production and wine styles
Fresh whites and light to medium reds, with Gamza (Kadarka), Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Chardonnay and Aligoté all grown.

## Key wines
- Danube Plain Gamza
- Danube Plain Chardonnay
- Danube Plain Cabernet Sauvignon

## What to look for
- Light, juicy reds from Gamza.
- Easy-drinking whites.
