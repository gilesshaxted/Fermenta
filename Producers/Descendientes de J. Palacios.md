# Descendientes de J. Palacios

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The estate that put Bierzo on the world map with fragrant, mineral Mencía.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Region:** [[Bierzo]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Mencía]]

## About
Founded by Álvaro Palacios and his nephew Ricardo Pérez Palacios, it farms steep, old vineyards around Corullón. The wines are perfumed, fine and lightly oaked.

## Known for
- Pétalos
- Corullón
- La Faraona
- Las Lamas
