# Chianti

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The wider Chianti zone around Classico, from simple everyday reds to excellent subzones such as Rufina.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Tuscany]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sangiovese]]
> - **Styles:** [[Vin Santo]]

## Landscape and topography
A large area of hills spread across central Tuscany, with seven subzones including Rufina, Colli Senesi and Colli Fiorentini.

## Soil and geology
Clay, limestone and sand, with local variations.

## Climate
Mediterranean.

## History
The old straw-covered fiasco bottles made Chianti famous. Quality has moved up since the 1990s.

## Production and wine styles
Minimum 70% Sangiovese. Chianti Rufina is the best-regarded subzone.

## Key wines
- Chianti
- Chianti Rufina
- Chianti Colli Senesi

## What to look for
- Cherry, plum and herbal notes, bright and food-friendly.
- Good value, and Rufina can age well.
