# Moschofilero

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A pink-skinned, aromatic grape making fragrant, crisp whites.

## Where it's from
The Peloponnese plateau of Mantinia.

## Where it grows
- **Mantinia, Greece:** the main home

## What it tastes like
- Rose petal, citrus and spice

## Classic wines
- Mantinia Moschofilero

## Pairs with
- Salads
- Seafood
- Spicy dishes

## Good to know
- Also made as a rosé and sparkling wine.
- Often compared with Gewürztraminer or Muscat.
