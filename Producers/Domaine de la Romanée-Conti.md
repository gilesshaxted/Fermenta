# Domaine de la Romanée-Conti

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Burgundy's most celebrated estate, based in Vosne-Romanée.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Côte de Nuits]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]]

## About
A single estate of grand cru monopoles and parcels, farmed biodynamically. The wines are exceptionally rare and defined by complexity and longevity.
