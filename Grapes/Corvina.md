# Corvina

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The main grape of Valpolicella and Amarone, with bright cherry and floral lift.

## Where it's from
The hills near Verona and Lake Garda.

## Where it grows
- **Valpolicella, Italy:** blended with Rondinella and Molinara
- **Bardolino, Italy:** for light reds

## What it tastes like
- Sour cherry and almond when fresh
- Dried fruit and chocolate in Amarone

## Classic wines
- Valpolicella
- Ripasso
- Amarone della Valpolicella
- Bardolino

## Pairs with
- Pasta
- Risotto
- Aged cheese (Amarone)

## Good to know
- Thick skins suit drying for Amarone.
- Corvinone is a related, larger-berried variety.
