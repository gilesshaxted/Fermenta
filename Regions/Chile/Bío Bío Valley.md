# Bío Bío Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A southern, cool and rainy zone making fresh Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Riesling.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Chile]]
> - **Part of:** [[South America]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** clay, granite, volcanic
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Riesling]], [[País]]

## Landscape and topography
Low hills and valleys, close to the Pacific.

## Soil and geology
Clay, granite and volcanic soils.

## Climate
Cool, with lots of rain and a late harvest.

## History
Expanding rapidly as producers look for cooler sites.

## Production and wine styles
Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, Riesling and old País.

## Key wines
- Bío Bío Riesling
- Bío Bío Pinot Noir

## What to look for
- High acidity and delicate fruit.
- A region to watch.
