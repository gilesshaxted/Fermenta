# Morocco

Part of [[Africa]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small North African wine country with a French influence, making fruity reds, rosés and the pale pink vin gris.

## How wine is organised here
- Quality wines are labelled as AOG, Appellation d'Origine Garantie, or as IGP.
- Most vineyards are in the north and northwest, around Meknès and Casablanca.
- Wine is made mostly for tourism and local drinkers, with a small export trade.
