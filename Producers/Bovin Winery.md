# Bovin Winery

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A respected family winery near Negotino, known for modern, polished Tikveš reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[North Macedonia]]
> - **Region:** [[Tikveš]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Vranac]], [[Syrah]], [[Chardonnay]]

## About
Located in the Tikveš basin, this is a privately owned estate with its own vineyards. It makes clean varietal wines and blends, with Vranec as a regional specialty.
