# Puglia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The heel of Italy, producing ripe, sunny reds and a lot of the country's wine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, terra rossa, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Zinfandel|Primitivo]], [[Negroamaro]], [[Nero di Troia]]
> - **Styles:** [[Passito]]

## Landscape and topography
Flat to gently rolling land, with long coastlines on the Adriatic and Ionian seas.

## Soil and geology
Limestone with red terra rossa clay.

## Climate
Hot Mediterranean, with sea breezes and low rainfall.

## History
Historically a source of blending wine for the north. Quality has improved from the 1990s with old bush vines.

## Production and wine styles
Primitivo (related to Zinfandel), Negroamaro, Nero di Troia and Susumaniello. Rosato is a speciality in Salento.

## Key wines
- Primitivo di Manduria
- Salice Salentino
- Castel del Monte
- Salento Rosato

## What to look for
- Ripe, plummy and generous.
- Look for old-vine bottles for depth.
