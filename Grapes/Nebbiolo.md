# Nebbiolo

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Pale in colour but huge in tannin and acidity. Roses and tar on the nose, built to age.

## Where it's from
Piedmont in north-west Italy. The name is usually linked to nebbia, the autumn fog that settles over the Langhe hills at harvest.

## Where it grows
- **Barolo and Barbaresco, Piedmont:** the great wines, 100% Nebbiolo
- **Langhe and Roero, Piedmont:** lighter, earlier-drinking styles
- **Gattinara and Ghemme, Alto Piemonte:** called Spanna locally
- **Valtellina, Lombardy:** called Chiavennasca, steep Alpine terraces

## What it tastes like
- **Young:** rose petal, red cherry, liquorice, tar
- **With age:** truffle, dried orange peel, leather, tobacco, dried herbs

## Classic wines
- Barolo
- Barbaresco
- Langhe Nebbiolo
- Gattinara
- Valtellina Superiore

## Pairs with
- Truffle risotto
- Braised beef
- Game
- Mushroom dishes
- Aged hard cheese

## Good to know
- Garnet-pale colour but very high tannin and acidity. Give it time, or decant it.
- Barolo and Barbaresco are both 100% Nebbiolo.
