# Grevenmacher

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The northern part of the Luxembourg Moselle, around the town of Grevenmacher, with slightly cooler conditions.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Luxembourg]]
> - **Part of:** [[Moselle Luxembourgeoise]]
> - **Climate:** Cool continental
> - **Soils:** limestone, marl, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Pinot Blanc]], [[Elbling]]

## Landscape and topography
Vineyards on slopes around Grevenmacher and Wormeldange.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, marl and clay.

## Climate
A little cooler than the south, which suits Riesling.

## History
Grevenmacher has been a wine town for centuries, with several large wine houses and a wine school nearby.

## Production and wine styles
Crisp dry whites and Crémant, with Riesling especially good here.

## Key wines
- Grevenmacher Riesling
- Wormeldange Riesling

## What to look for
- Mineral, crisp Riesling.
- A cooler feel.

- [[Bernard-Massard]]
