# Chile

Part of [[South America]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A long, thin country between the Andes and the Pacific, protected from phylloxera, with a focus on Cabernet Sauvignon, Carmenère and cool coastal whites.

## How Chilean wine is organised
- Regions run from the **Aconcagua** to **Bío Bío**, with a newer east-west system of **Costa** (coast), **Entre Cordilleras** (middle) and **Andes**.
- **Reserva**, **Gran Reserva** and **Reserva Especial** are common terms, with minimum ageing requirements.
- A label naming a region must have at least 75% of the wine from there.
- **VIGNO** is a quality label for old vine Carignan.
