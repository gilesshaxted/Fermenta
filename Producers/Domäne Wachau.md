# Domäne Wachau

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The Wachau's major cooperative, with a wide range of reliable and sometimes outstanding Grüner Veltliner and Riesling.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Austria]]
> - **Region:** [[Wachau]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grüner Veltliner]], [[Riesling]]

## About
A grower cooperative based in Dürnstein that farms a large part of the Wachau terraces. The wines follow the local Steinfeder, Federspiel and Smaragd ripeness scale.

## Known for
- Terrassen Federspiel
- Achleiten Riesling Smaragd
- Kellerberg Smaragd
