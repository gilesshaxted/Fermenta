# Canada

Part of [[North America]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Canada makes wine in a handful of cool climate pockets, led by Ontario's Niagara Peninsula and British Columbia's Okanagan Valley, and is one of the world's top icewine producers.

## How Canadian wine is organised
- **VQA** (Vintners Quality Alliance) guarantees origin and quality in Ontario and British Columbia.
- **Icewine** must be made from grapes naturally frozen on the vine, picked at minus 8 degrees Celsius or colder.
- Wines labelled **Product of Canada** use only Canadian grapes, while the other labels may blend imported juice.
- Hybrid grapes such as Vidal and Baco Noir are widely used.
