# Krasato

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of the three grapes of Rapsani, grown on the slopes of Mount Olympus.

## Where it's from
Native to the foothills of Mount Olympus. It is blended with Xinomavro and Stavroto in Rapsani.

## Where it grows
- **Rapsani, Greece:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Red cherry, spice, herbs and earth

## Classic wines
- Rapsani

## Pairs with
- Lamb
- Roast meat

## Good to know
- Rarely bottled on its own.
- Adds colour and warmth.
