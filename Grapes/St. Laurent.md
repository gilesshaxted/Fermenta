# St. Laurent

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A silky, cherry-scented red related to Pinot Noir, and a parent of Zweigelt.

## Where it's from
Probably from Alsace or the Rhine, and a close relative of Pinot Noir. It is grown in Austria and the Pfalz.

## Where it grows
- **Austria:** in Burgenland and Thermenregion
- **Pfalz, Germany:** also planted

## What it tastes like
- Black cherry, spice, earth and violet

## Classic wines
- St. Laurent

## Pairs with
- Duck
- Roast poultry
- Mushroom dishes

## Good to know
- Hard to grow and low yielding.
- Often compared with Pinot Noir.
