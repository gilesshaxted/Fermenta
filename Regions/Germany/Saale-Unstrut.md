# Saale-Unstrut

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of Germany's northernmost and eastern wine regions, with terraces on limestone slopes.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Germany]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** limestone, muschelkalk, sandstone, loess
> - **Main grapes:** [[Müller-Thurgau]], [[Pinot Blanc]], [[Sylvaner|Silvaner]], [[Riesling]]

## Landscape and topography
Terraced slopes along the Saale and Unstrut rivers, in former East Germany.

## Soil and geology
Muschelkalk (shell limestone), sandstone and loess.

## Climate
Cool and dry continental, with frost risks.

## History
Vines have grown here for over a thousand years, with Cistercian monks building terraces. Production shrank under East German rule and has revived since 1990.

## Production and wine styles
Müller-Thurgau, Weissburgunder, Silvaner and Riesling.

## Key wines
- Saale-Unstrut Weissburgunder
- Saale-Unstrut Silvaner

## What to look for
- Crisp, mineral whites.
- A tiny, curious region.
