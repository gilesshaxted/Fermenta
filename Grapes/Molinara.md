# Molinara

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A pale, high-acid red grape that gives lift to Valpolicella and Bardolino.

## Where it's from
A minor grape of the Verona area. Its popularity is falling because it lacks colour and flavour.

## Where it grows
- **Bardolino and Valpolicella, Italy:** in small amounts

## What it tastes like
- Cranberry, herbs, pepper and spice

## Classic wines
- Bardolino
- Valpolicella

## Pairs with
- Salads
- Light pasta

## Good to know
- Has largely fallen out of favour.
- Adds freshness more than flavour.
