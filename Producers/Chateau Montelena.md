# Chateau Montelena

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The Calistoga estate whose Chardonnay won the 1976 Judgment of Paris.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Region:** [[Napa Valley]]
> - **Type:** Major producer

## About
A historic Napa winery with a stone château, making both Chardonnay and Cabernet. The wines are restrained and classically built.

## Known for
- Estate Cabernet Sauvignon
- Napa Valley Chardonnay
