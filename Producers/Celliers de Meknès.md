# Celliers de Meknès

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The largest wine producer in Morocco, based in Meknès, with brands such as Aït Souala and Domaine de la Zouina.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Morocco]]
> - **Region:** [[Meknès]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Grenache]], [[Cinsault]]

## About
Celliers de Meknès makes a broad range, from simple everyday reds, whites and rosés to its more ambitious cuvées. Domaine de la Zouina is among its better known labels.

## Known for
- Aït Souala
- Médaillon
- Domaine de la Zouina
