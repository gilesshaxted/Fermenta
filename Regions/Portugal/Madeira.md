# Madeira

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A volcanic Atlantic island producing fortified wine that is baked, oxidised and almost indestructible.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Portugal]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Subtropical
> - **Soils:** volcanic, basalt
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sercial]], [[Verdelho]], [[Boal]], [[Malvasia]], [[Tinta Negra]]
> - **Styles:** [[Madeira (style)|Madeira style]]

## Landscape and topography
Steep volcanic island terraces, called poios, rising from the sea with small plots of vines.

## Soil and geology
Volcanic basalt and ash.

## Climate
Subtropical with cool Atlantic influence, humidity and high acidity.

## History
Ships stopping on the way to the Americas and Indies carried barrels, and heat and movement transformed the wine, a process mimicked today by estufagem (heating). Madeira was reputedly used to toast the American Declaration of Independence.

## Production and wine styles
Heated (estufagem) or aged in warm lofts (canteiro). Styles go from Sercial (dry) to Verdelho, Boal and Malvasia (sweet).

## Key wines
- Sercial
- Verdelho
- Boal
- Malvasia (Malmsey)
- Vintage Madeira

## What to look for
- Burnt caramel, nuts, citrus peel and bright acidity.
- Opened bottles last for months.
