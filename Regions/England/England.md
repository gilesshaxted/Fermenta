# England

Part of [[Europe]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cool-climate country whose chalk and warm recent summers have helped it make sparkling wine to rival Champagne.

## How English wine is organised
- **PDO** wines come from Sussex, Kent and other protected areas, and **PGI** covers broader regions.
- Traditional-method sparkling is the flagship, from Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and Pinot Meunier.
- Aromatic whites such as Bacchus are the main still style.
- The industry is young, and grows fast.
