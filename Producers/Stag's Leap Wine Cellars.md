# Stag's Leap Wine Cellars

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Napa estate famous for its role in the 1976 Judgment of Paris.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Region:** [[Stags Leap District]], [[Napa Valley]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]]
> - **Styles:** [[Bordeaux blend]]

## About
Based in the Stags Leap District, it makes silky, elegant Cabernet Sauvignon. The style is finer and less heavy than many Napa neighbours.

## Known for
- Cask 23
- S.L.V. Cabernet Sauvignon
- Fay Cabernet Sauvignon
