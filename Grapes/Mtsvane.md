# Mtsvane

> [!abstract] At a glance
> An aromatic Georgian white grape, often used in Tsinandali blends with Rkatsiteli.

## Where it's from
An old Kakheti variety whose name means green. It has several types, and Mtsvane Kakhuri is the most common.

## Where it grows
- **Kakheti, Georgia:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Peach, apple, floral and citrus

## Classic wines
- Tsinandali
- Mtsvane

## Pairs with
- Fish
- Light cheese
- Salads

## Good to know
- Often blended with Rkatsiteli.
- Also made in qvevri.
