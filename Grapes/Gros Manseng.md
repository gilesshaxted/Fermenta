# Gros Manseng

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The larger-berried cousin of Petit Manseng, behind many dry Pyrenean whites.

## Where it's from
South-West France, around Jurançon and Gascony.

## Where it grows
- **Jurançon Sec, France:** dry white
- **Gascony, France:** Côtes de Gascogne whites
- **Irouléguy, France:** Basque whites

## What it tastes like
- Grapefruit, peach and passion fruit

## Classic wines
- Jurançon Sec
- Côtes de Gascogne blanc
- Irouléguy blanc

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Salads
- Goat's cheese

## Good to know
- A good-value, fresh white.
- Often blended with Colombard or Ugni Blanc.
