# Codru

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The central, forested heartland of Moldovan wine, with rolling hills and some of the country's best whites and reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Republic of Moldova]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Temperate continental
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, loam, black earth
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Fetească Albă]], [[Aligoté]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]]

## Landscape and topography
Hilly, wooded country in the centre of the Republic of Moldova, with vineyards on slopes at roughly 100 to 300 metres.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, clay and loam over limestone, with fertile dark soils in the valleys.

## Climate
Temperate continental, with warm summers, cold winters and good rainfall compared with the south.

## History
The name means forest. The area has long been the heart of Moldovan wine, and its vast underground cellars at Cricova and Milestii Mici are famous.

## Production and wine styles
Whites such as Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc, Fetească Albă and Aligoté, plus reds from Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Rară Neagră. Sparkling wine is a speciality.

## Key wines
- Codru Chardonnay
- Codru Fetească Albă
- Codru Cabernet Sauvignon
- Codru sparkling

## What to look for
- Fresh whites and fine sparkling wine.
- Huge underground cellars worth a visit.
