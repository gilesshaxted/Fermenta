# Spain

Part of [[Europe]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Over a million hectares of vines, more than any other country, from cool Atlantic Galicia to sun-baked La Mancha and the volcanic Canaries. Rioja, Ribera del Duero, Sherry and Cava lead the story.

## How Spanish wine is organised
- **DO** (Denominación de Origen) is the main quality tier, and **DOCa** is the higher tier, held by Rioja and Priorat.
- **DO Pago** recognises single estates of exceptional quality.
- Ageing terms matter: **Joven**, **Crianza**, **Reserva** and **Gran Reserva** describe time in oak and bottle.
- Cava, Sherry and Rioja each have their own rules and styles.
