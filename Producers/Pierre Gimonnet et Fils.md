# Pierre Gimonnet et Fils

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Cuis family estate specialising in blanc de blancs from Côte des Blancs vineyards.

> - **Country:** [[France]]

## About
Gimonnet grows only Chardonnay and blends across villages, including grand cru parcels. The wines are crisp, fresh and clean with a chalky edge.
