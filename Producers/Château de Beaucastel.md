# Château de Beaucastel

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A leading Châteauneuf-du-Pape estate, known for Mourvèdre-rich reds and long-lived Roussanne.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Châteauneuf-du-Pape]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Mourvèdre]], [[Grenache]], [[Syrah]], [[Roussanne]]
> - **Styles:** [[GSM]]

## About
A family estate run organically, using all thirteen permitted grape varieties. The reds are savoury, complex and age very well, while the white Roussanne is rich and slow to develop.

## Known for
- Châteauneuf-du-Pape Rouge
- Hommage à Jacques Perrin
- Roussanne Vieilles Vignes
