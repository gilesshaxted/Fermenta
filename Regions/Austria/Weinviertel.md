# Weinviertel

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Austria's largest wine area, northern hills of Grüner Veltliner with a bright, peppery style.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Austria]]
> - **Part of:** [[Lower Austria]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** loess, clay, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grüner Veltliner]]

## Landscape and topography
Gentle hills and plains north of Vienna, along the Czech border.

## Soil and geology
Loess, clay and limestone, with deep cellars dug in loess.

## Climate
Warm and dry continental, with cool nights.

## History
In 2002 Weinviertel became Austria's first DAC, for Grüner Veltliner. Many wine cellar villages (Kellergassen) line the hills.

## Production and wine styles
Weinviertel DAC wines are Grüner Veltliner, light and peppery. Reserve is richer.

## Key wines
- Weinviertel DAC Klassik
- Weinviertel DAC Reserve

## What to look for
- Pepper, citrus and green apple.
- Great value, a classic with schnitzel.
