# Carrau

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family house with vineyards in Rivera, close to the Brazilian border, known for Tannat.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Uruguay]]
> - **Region:** [[Rivera]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tannat]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]]

## About
Carrau has a long history in Uruguayan winemaking and was one of the first to plant on the northern Rivera hills. Its wines show tension and freshness.
