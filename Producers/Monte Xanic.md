# Monte Xanic

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Valle de Guadalupe pioneer for quality, known for polished Bordeaux-style reds and fresh whites.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Mexico]]
> - **Region:** [[Valle de Guadalupe]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]]
> - **Styles:** [[Bordeaux blend]]

## About
Monte Xanic was one of the first Baja wineries to focus on premium bottles. The style is clean and modern, with careful oak and good ageing potential in the best reds.

- Gran Ricardo
- Viña Kristel Sauvignon Blanc
