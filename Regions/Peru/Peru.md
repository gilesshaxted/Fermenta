# Peru

Part of [[South America]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Peru has a long wine history on its desert coast, though it is better known today for pisco.

## How wine is organised here
- Wine is mostly sold by producer and grape, with little formal appellation structure.
- Pisco is a grape spirit with its own protected designation and is separate from wine.
