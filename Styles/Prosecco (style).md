# Prosecco (style)

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Light, fruity, easy sparkling wine from Glera, made by the tank method.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Category:** Sparkling
> - **Colour:** White
> - **Sweetness:** Dry
> - **Country:** Italy
> - **Main grapes:** [[Glera]]
> - **Regions:** [[Prosecco]], [[Veneto]]

## How it's made
Glera is fermented to a dry base wine, then given a second fermentation in a sealed tank, the Charmat method. This keeps the fresh, fruity aromas and makes the wine good value.

## Where it's made
Mainly the hills of Veneto and Friuli. The best wines come from the steep hills around Conegliano and Valdobbiadene.

## What it tastes like
- Pear, green apple, white peach
- Honeysuckle
- Light, frothy bubbles

## Pairs with
- Aperitivo
- Cured meats
- Light seafood
- Spritz cocktails

## Good to know
- Most Prosecco is Brut or Extra Dry, and Extra Dry is actually a little sweeter than Brut.
- Drink young, usually within a couple of years.
