# Grechetto

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A thick-skinned white grape of Umbria that adds body and structure to blends.

## Where it's from
Grown in central Italy for a long time, now mainly in Umbria. It gives structure to Orvieto blends.

## Where it grows
- **Umbria, Italy:** the heartland
- **Orvieto, Italy:** in blends with Trebbiano

## What it tastes like
- Pear, almond, herbs and citrus

## Classic wines
- Orvieto
- Torgiano Bianco

## Pairs with
- Roast chicken
- Fish
- Soft cheese

## Good to know
- Not the same as Greco from Campania.
- Also used in Vin Santo.
