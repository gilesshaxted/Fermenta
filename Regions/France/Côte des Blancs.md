# Côte des Blancs

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A line of east-facing chalk slopes south of Épernay, planted almost entirely to Chardonnay, the source of the finest blanc de blancs.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Champagne]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** chalk
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]]
> - **Styles:** [[Champagne (style)|Champagne style]]

## Landscape and topography
A single east-facing escarpment about 20 km long, running south from Épernay.

## Soil and geology
Pure, deep chalk that retains water and reflects light, giving tight, mineral Chardonnay.

## Climate
Cool and continental, with the east-facing slope catching the morning sun.

## History
Grand Cru villages include Cramant, Avize, Oger and Le Mesnil-sur-Oger, where tiny walled vineyards are among the most prized in Champagne.

## Production and wine styles
Chardonnay gives freshness, finesse and long ageing potential. Wines can seem austere in youth.

## Key wines
- Blanc de Blancs
- Salon
- Krug Clos du Mesnil
- Grower blanc de blancs

## What to look for
- Lemon, chalk, green apple and brioche with age.
- Needs time in bottle to soften.
