# Oltenia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A western region of hills and vines along the Danube and the Carpathian foothills, with warm conditions.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Romania]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** clay, sand, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Fetească Neagră]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Pinot Noir]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills and plateaus in southwest Romania, near the Danube.

## Soil and geology
Clay, sand and limestone.

## Climate
Warm continental.

## History
Oltenia has long wine traditions and is known for its Drăgășani, where Tămâioasă and Crâmpoșie are grown.

## Production and wine styles
Reds and whites, with local grapes such as Crâmpoșie in Drăgășani.

## Key wines
- Drăgășani Negru de Drăgășani
- Drăgășani Crâmpoșie

## What to look for
- Fresh, local white wines.
- A small region with character.
