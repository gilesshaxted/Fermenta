# Garganega

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The grape of Soave, delicate and almondy, and better than its reputation.

## Where it's from
The hills east of Verona, in Soave.

## Where it grows
- **Soave, Italy:** the main home
- **Gambellara, Italy:** a similar neighbour

## What it tastes like
- Almond, lemon, pear and a gentle mineral edge

## Classic wines
- Soave Classico
- Recioto di Soave
- Gambellara

## Pairs with
- Seafood risotto
- Fried fish
- White asparagus

## Good to know
- Volcanic soils give it salinity.
- Good Soave can age for a decade.
