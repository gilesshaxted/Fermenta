# Airén

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Spain's most planted white grape and once the most planted in the world, grown on the high plains of La Mancha.

## Where it's from
Native to central Spain, where it covers huge areas. It is drought-tolerant, and much was used for brandy.

## Where it grows
- **La Mancha, Spain:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Neutral, apple, citrus and herbs

## Classic wines
- La Mancha white
- Spanish brandy

## Pairs with
- Tapas
- Fried fish

## Good to know
- Low acidity and neutral flavours.
- Modern winemaking has improved quality.
