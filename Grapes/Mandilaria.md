# Mandilaria

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A deep, tannic red grape grown on Aegean islands such as Paros, Rhodes and Santorini.

## Where it's from
Widely grown across Aegean islands. Paros Mandilaria is the best known version, blended with Monemvasia.

## Where it grows
- **Paros, Greece:** Mandilaria and Monemvasia
- **Rhodes, Greece:** in the local PDO

## What it tastes like
- Black cherry, spice, earth and herbs

## Classic wines
- Paros red
- Rhodes red

## Pairs with
- Grilled meat
- Stews
- Hard cheese

## Good to know
- Deeply coloured and tannic.
- Also called Mandilari.
