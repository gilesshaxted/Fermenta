# Vega Sicilia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Spain's most famous red wine estate, producing long aged, majestic wines in Ribera del Duero.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Region:** [[Ribera del Duero]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tempranillo]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]]

## About
Vega Sicilia is the benchmark for age worthy Spanish red wine, with its top wines released after many years of ageing. It also makes the more approachable Alión and Pintia as sister wines.

## Known for
- Único
- Valbuena 5º
- Único Reserva Especial
