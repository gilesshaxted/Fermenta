# Zenata

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A coastal area near Casablanca, cooled by the Atlantic, producing fresher wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Morocco]]
> - **Part of:** [[Africa]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** clay, sand, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Syrah]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]]

## Landscape and topography
Low coastal plains and hills north of Casablanca.

## Soil and geology
Clay, sand and limestone.

## Climate
A maritime Mediterranean climate with Atlantic breezes, so cooler than the interior.

## History
The area is one of Morocco's established appellations, near the main cities and markets.

## Production and wine styles
Reds and whites with fresher fruit than the interior.

## Key wines
- Zenata red
- Zenata white

## What to look for
- Fresher, lighter styles.
- Near the Atlantic.
