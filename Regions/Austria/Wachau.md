# Wachau

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Steep, terraced Danube vineyards, producing some of Europe's best Grüner Veltliner and Riesling.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Austria]]
> - **Part of:** [[Lower Austria]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** gneiss, granite, loess
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grüner Veltliner]], [[Riesling]]

## Landscape and topography
A narrow stretch of the Danube between Melk and Krems, with stone-walled terraces and castle ruins.

## Soil and geology
Primary rock, gneiss and granite on terraces, with loess on lower sites.

## Climate
Hot days from the Pannonian plain and cool nights from the Waldviertel, which keeps acidity.

## History
A UNESCO World Heritage cultural landscape. The Vinea Wachau association created the Steinfeder, Federspiel and Smaragd categories by must weight.

## Production and wine styles
Steinfeder (light), Federspiel (medium) and Smaragd (fullest and riper), all dry. Grüner Veltliner and Riesling.

## Key wines
- Wachau Steinfeder
- Wachau Federspiel
- Wachau Smaragd

## What to look for
- Rich, mineral and ageworthy.
- Smaragd is the most powerful but often fully dry.
