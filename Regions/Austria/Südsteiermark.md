# Südsteiermark

> [!abstract] At a glance
> South Styria's steep, forest-edged hills make Austria's finest Sauvignon Blanc.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Austria]]
> - **Part of:** [[Styria]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, marl, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Welschriesling]]
> - **Styles:** [[Orange wine]]

## Landscape and topography
Steep, narrow ridges and terraced slopes, near Gamlitz and Ehrenhausen, at the Slovenian border.

## Soil and geology
Fossil-rich marl and limestone, with sandy soils.

## Climate
Cool nights and warm days, with abundant rain.

## History
The South Styrian Wine Road is a touristic route, with traditional Buschenschank wine taverns.

## Production and wine styles
Sauvignon Blanc, Morillon and Welschriesling, with Klassik, Ortswein and Riedenwein classification.

## Key wines
- Südsteiermark DAC Klassik
- Südsteiermark Ortswein
- Südsteiermark Riedenwein

## What to look for
- Gooseberry, elderflower and mineral spice.
- Superb with local pumpkin seed oil dishes.
