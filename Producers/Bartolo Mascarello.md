# Bartolo Mascarello

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A tiny, much loved traditional producer in Barolo, famous for blending vineyards into a single Barolo.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Barolo]], [[Piedmont]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Nebbiolo]], [[Barbera]], [[Dolcetto]]

## About
A family cellar in the heart of Barolo village, committed to old methods and large casks. The Barolo is traditionally assembled from several vineyards rather than bottled by cru.

## Known for
- Barolo
- Barbera d'Alba
- Dolcetto d'Alba
