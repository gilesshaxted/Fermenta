# Giuseppe Rinaldi

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small, ultra traditional Barolo grower in Barolo village, loved by purists.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Barolo]], [[Piedmont]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Nebbiolo]], [[Barbera]], [[Dolcetto]]

## About
Rinaldi uses long macerations and large old botti, with minimal intervention in the cellar. The wines are savoury, perfumed and built for the long haul.

## Known for
- Barolo Brunate Le Coste
- Barolo Tre Tine
- Barolo Ravera
