# Château Pontet-Canet

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Pauillac Fifth Growth known for its committed biodynamic farming and pure, vibrant wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]

> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Petit Verdot]]
> - **Styles:** [[Bordeaux blend]]

## About
Pontet-Canet is one of the best known biodynamic estates in the Médoc, working with minimal intervention in the vineyard. The wines are pure, fresh and detailed, with firm Pauillac structure.
