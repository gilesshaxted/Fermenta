# Dolcetto

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Soft, fruity and slightly bitter. The Piedmontese everyday red.

## Where it's from
Piedmont, where its name means little sweet one, referring to the grape and not the wine.

## Where it grows
- **Alba and Dogliani, Italy:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Black cherry, liquorice and a bitter almond finish

## Classic wines
- Dolcetto d'Alba
- Dogliani

## Pairs with
- Antipasti
- Pasta
- Grilled sausages

## Good to know
- Low acidity and soft tannin make it easy.
- Drink young.
