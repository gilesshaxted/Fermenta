# GSM

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A red blend of Grenache, Syrah and Mourvèdre, from the southern Rhône and now worldwide.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Category:** Still
> - **Colour:** Red
> - **Sweetness:** Dry
> - **Country:** Various
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grenache]], [[Syrah]], [[Mourvèdre]]
> - **Regions:** [[Châteauneuf-du-Pape]], [[Gigondas]], [[Vacqueyras]], [[Rhône Valley]], [[Barossa Valley]], [[McLaren Vale]], [[Paso Robles]], [[Languedoc]]

## How it's made
Grenache gives fruit and warmth, Syrah gives colour and spice, and Mourvèdre brings structure and meaty depth. Blends are made across many styles.

## Where it's made
Châteauneuf-du-Pape is the benchmark, and Australia, California and Languedoc have followed.

## What it tastes like
- Red fruit, black pepper, herbs
- Warm and spicy
- Garrigue (wild herbs)

## Pairs with
- Roast lamb
- Stews
- Sausages

## Good to know
- Châteauneuf-du-Pape allows up to 13 grape varieties.
- Cinsault and Carignan often join the blend.
