# Turkey

Part of [[Asia]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of the oldest wine lands in the world, with hundreds of native grapes, though only a few are widely used for wine.

## How wine is organised here
- Most vineyards are used for table grapes and raisins, so wine uses only a small share.
- Wine is made under national rules, and most production is now private.
- Labels often name the grape and the region, such as Öküzgözü from Elazığ.
