# Rubin

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Bulgarian cross of Nebbiolo and Syrah, bred in the 1940s, making dark, spicy reds.

## Where it's from
Created in Bulgaria by crossing Nebbiolo and Syrah. It became widely planted in the 20th century and is mostly blended or made as a varietal red.

## Where it grows
- **Thracian Valley, Bulgaria:** the main area
- **Danube Plain, Bulgaria:** also grown

## What it tastes like
- Black cherry, plum, pepper and spice

## Classic wines
- Rubin

## Pairs with
- Grilled meats
- Game
- Hard cheese

## Good to know
- A modern cross, not an ancient native.
- Often blended with Mavrud or Cabernet.
