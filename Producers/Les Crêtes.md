# Les Crêtes

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The leading estate in Italy's smallest wine region, high in the Alps.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Valle d'Aosta]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]]

## About
Based in Aymavilles, Les Crêtes grows grapes at high altitude. The wines are bright and mountain fresh and include rare local varieties.

## Known for
- Petite Arvine
- Cuvée Bois Chardonnay
- Torrette
