# Agiorgitiko

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A soft, plummy red from Nemea, also called St George.

## Where it's from
Nemea in the Peloponnese. The name means St George's grape.

## Where it grows
- **Nemea, Greece:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Plum, cherry and soft spice

## Classic wines
- Nemea Agiorgitiko
- Agiorgitiko rosé

## Pairs with
- Grilled meat
- Moussaka
- Pasta

## Good to know
- Versatile: rosé, red, and sweet styles.
- Soft tannins.
