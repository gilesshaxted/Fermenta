# Merwah

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A native Lebanese white, grown at altitude, which has more body and weight than its partner Obaideh.

## Where it's from
A local Lebanese white used in blends, and best known as the other half of the white Chateau Musar. ==It has been linked to Semillon, though this is not firmly settled.==

## Where it grows
- **Bekaa Valley, Lebanon:** the heartland
- **Batroun, Lebanon:** also planted

## What it tastes like
- Lemon, herbs, stone fruit and honey

## Classic wines
- Chateau Musar Blanc
- Merwah blends

## Pairs with
- Mezze
- Roast chicken
- Grilled fish

## Good to know
- Old vines are valued.
- Often blended with Obaideh.
