# Barbeito

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A quality-focused family Madeira house that has built a strong reputation for single-grape bottlings.

## About
Barbeito works with small parcels and stocks old wines, making precise, characterful Madeira. The wines are admired by sommeliers.
