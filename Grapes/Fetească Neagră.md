# Fetească Neagră

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Romania's signature red grape, dark and spicy with soft tannin.

## Where it's from
Romania and Moldova, where it has been grown for centuries. Its name means black maiden.

## Where it grows
- **Dealu Mare, Romania:** the best-known region
- **Murfatlar and Moldova, Romania:** also grown

## What it tastes like
- Black cherry, plum, spice and a hint of smoke

## Classic wines
- Dealu Mare Fetească Neagră

## Pairs with
- Grilled meats
- Stews
- Hard cheese

## Good to know
- Nearly vanished under communism, and now being revived.
- Good ones can age.
