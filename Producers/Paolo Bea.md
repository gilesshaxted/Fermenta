# Paolo Bea

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small, traditional Montefalco family estate and an icon of natural, low intervention wine in Italy.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Montefalco]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sagrantino]], [[Trebbiano]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]], [[Orange wine]]

## About
A farm run organically, with wild yeast ferments and long skin contact. The reds are dense and earthy, and the white is amber and tannic.

## Known for
- Sagrantino di Montefalco Pagliaro
- Rosso de Véo
- Santa Chiara
