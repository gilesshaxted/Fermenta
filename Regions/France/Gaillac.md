# Gaillac

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of France's oldest wine areas, with local grapes such as Mauzac and Duras and a surprising range of styles.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[South-West France]]
> - **Climate:** Maritime
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, gravel
> - **Main grapes:** [[Duras]], [[Braucol]], [[Syrah]], [[Mauzac]], [[Len de l'El]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling hills around the Tarn river, east of Toulouse.

## Soil and geology
Limestone and clay slopes on the left bank, gravel terraces on the right.

## Climate
Maritime with a Mediterranean touch.

## History
Wines were made here under Roman rule, and the area has a tradition of sparkling wine from Mauzac made by the ancestral method.

## Production and wine styles
Red, white, rosé, sparkling and sweet, from local grapes.

## Key wines
- Gaillac rouge
- Gaillac Méthode Ancestrale
- Gaillac doux

## What to look for
- Mauzac gives apple and spice, Duras is peppery and light.
- A treasure for adventurous drinkers.
