# Nosiola

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A hazelnut-scented white grape of Trentino, and a base for Vino Santo.

## Where it's from
A very old variety of the Valle dei Laghi, in Trentino. The name probably comes from nocciola, meaning hazelnut.

## Where it grows
- **Valle dei Laghi, Italy:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Hazelnut, apple, herbs and citrus

## Classic wines
- Trentino Nosiola
- Vino Santo Trentino

## Pairs with
- Fish
- Soft cheese
- Desserts

## Good to know
- Dried for the sweet Vino Santo.
- Light and delicate.
