# Grace Wine

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A leading Katsunuma family winery, known for refined Koshu and Bordeaux-style reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Japan]]
> - **Region:** [[Katsunuma]], [[Yamanashi]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Koshu]], [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## About
Grace Wine, from the Misawa family, works with its own vineyards in Yamanashi and has been a key voice for quality Koshu. The whites are delicate and dry, while the reds are polished and moderate in alcohol.

## Known for
- Hishiyama Koshu
- Cuvée Misawa Akeno
