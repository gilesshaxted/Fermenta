# Montsant

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A ring around Priorat with similar grapes at gentler prices and a fruitier style.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, granite
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grenache|Garnacha]], [[Carignan|Cariñena]], [[Syrah]]

## Landscape and topography
A horseshoe-shaped region around Priorat, with hills and a rugged landscape.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, clay and granite, without Priorat's slate.

## Climate
Mediterranean, with altitude and sea breezes.

## History
Montsant became a DO in 2001, separating from Tarragona. Local cooperatives are important.

## Production and wine styles
Garnacha and Cariñena, with other varieties. Many are old-vine.

## Key wines
- Montsant Garnacha
- Montsant Cariñena

## What to look for
- Black cherry, spice and juicy fruit.
- A good-value alternative to Priorat.
