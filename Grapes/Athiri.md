# Athiri

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A soft, fresh white of the Aegean islands that helps Santorini blends.

## Where it's from
One of the oldest grapes of the Aegean, grown on Santorini and Rhodes, and on mainland Greece.

## Where it grows
- **Santorini, Greece:** in blends with Assyrtiko
- **Rhodes, Greece:** as a main white

## What it tastes like
- Citrus, pear, herbs and white flowers

## Classic wines
- Santorini white
- Rhodes white

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Salads

## Good to know
- Lower in acidity than Assyrtiko.
- Good in blends.
