# Sultaniye

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A very widely planted Turkish grape, mostly eaten fresh or dried, but also made into light white wine.

## Where it's from
Grown in the Aegean region. It is the same variety as Thompson Seedless and Sultana, and is mainly used for table grapes and raisins.

## Where it grows
- **Aegean, Turkey:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Lemon, green apple, melon and herbs

## Classic wines
- Sultaniye white

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Salads
- Mezze

## Good to know
- Same grape as Sultana.
- Mostly a raisin and table grape.
