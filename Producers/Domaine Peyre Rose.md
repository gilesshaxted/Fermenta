# Domaine Peyre Rose

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cult Languedoc estate making rich, slow-evolving reds from schist soils.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Languedoc]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Grenache]], [[Mourvèdre]]

## About
A small domaine whose wines are aged for a long time before release and have a distinctive, spicy style. They are highly regarded and in limited supply.
