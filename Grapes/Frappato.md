# Frappato

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A light, perfumed Sicilian red, best chilled.

## Where it's from
Native to south-east Sicily. It is one half of Cerasuolo di Vittoria, blended with Nero d'Avola.

## Where it grows
- **Vittoria, Italy:** the home

## What it tastes like
- Strawberry, red cherry, rose and pepper

## Classic wines
- Cerasuolo di Vittoria
- Frappato

## Pairs with
- Grilled fish
- Pasta
- Charcuterie

## Good to know
- Serve slightly cool.
- A fresh partner to Nero d'Avola.
