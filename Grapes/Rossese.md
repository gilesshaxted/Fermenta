# Rossese

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A light, scented red from the Ligurian coast near the French border.

## Where it's from
Grown on steep terraces in western Liguria, especially around Dolceacqua.

## Where it grows
- **Dolceacqua, Italy:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Red cherry, herbs, rose and spice

## Classic wines
- Rossese di Dolceacqua

## Pairs with
- Grilled fish
- Pasta with pesto
- Rabbit

## Good to know
- Often compared with light Pinot Noir.
- Very small production.
