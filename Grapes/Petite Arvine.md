# Petite Arvine

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Valais white with a distinctive salty, grapefruity tang, made dry and sweet.

## Where it's from
Native to the Valais, and one of its most prized white grapes. A related vine is also found in the Aosta Valley.

## Where it grows
- **Valais, Switzerland:** the heartland
- **Aosta Valley, Italy:** also grown

## What it tastes like
- Grapefruit, rhubarb, salty minerality and pear

## Classic wines
- Petite Arvine of Valais

## Pairs with
- Fish
- Shellfish
- Soft cheese

## Good to know
- Also called Arvine.
- Late-harvest versions are sweet and rich.
