# Finger Lakes

> [!abstract] At a glance
> New York's cool lakeside wine country, best known for crisp Riesling and Cabernet Franc.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[North America]]
> - **Climate:** Cool continental
> - **Soils:** shale, limestone, glacial till
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Pinot Noir]]

## Landscape and topography
Long, deep glacial lakes with steep vineyard slopes, especially on Seneca, Cayuga and Keuka.

## Soil and geology
Shale, limestone and glacial till.

## Climate
Cold winters, but the deep lakes moderate temperatures and extend the season.

## History
Dr Konstantin Frank planted vinifera vines here in the 1950s, against advice that they would not survive.

## Production and wine styles
Riesling, Chardonnay, Cabernet Franc, Pinot Noir and sparkling wine.

## Key wines
- Finger Lakes Riesling
- Finger Lakes Cabernet Franc

## What to look for
- Lime, stone fruit and high acidity.
- Often compared to Germany or Alsace.

## Producers
- [[Dr. Konstantin Frank]]
- [[Hermann J. Wiemer]]
