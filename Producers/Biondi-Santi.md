# Biondi-Santi

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The historic estate often credited with creating Brunello di Montalcino.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Montalcino]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sangiovese]]

## About
A traditional estate at Greppo near Montalcino. The wines are old school, with high acidity and a long ageing life, and are made in a very classic style.

## Known for
- Brunello di Montalcino Tenuta Greppo
- Brunello di Montalcino Riserva
- Rosso di Montalcino
