# Soave

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Garganega on volcanic hills below a medieval castle, in styles from simple to excellent.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Veneto]]
> - **Climate:** Temperate
> - **Soils:** volcanic, limestone, basalt
> - **Main grapes:** [[Garganega]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills east of Verona around the walled town of Soave, with the Classico zone on the steep slopes.

## Soil and geology
Volcanic basalt in the east and limestone in the west.

## Climate
Temperate.

## History
Soave was hugely popular in the 1970s, then fell from favour due to overproduction. The Classico hills have driven a quality revival.

## Production and wine styles
At least 70% Garganega, with Trebbiano di Soave and Chardonnay. Soave Classico and Soave Superiore are the quality tiers.

## Key wines
- Soave Classico
- Soave Superiore
- Recioto di Soave

## What to look for
- Almond, lemon, white flowers and a mineral edge.
- Good with fish and risotto.
