# Slovak Tokaj

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A tiny region on the Slovak side of the Tokaj wine area, with sweet and dry wines from Furmint and Hárslevelű.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Slovakia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** volcanic, loess, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Furmint]], [[Hárslevelű]], [[Muscat]]

## Landscape and topography
Low volcanic hills on the Slovak side of the border with Hungary, around the villages of Malá Tŕňa and Viničky.

## Soil and geology
Volcanic soils, with loess and clay.

## Climate
Continental, with hot summers, cold winters and misty autumns which encourage noble rot in good years.

## History
The Tokaj area was historically one wine region that stretched across what are now two countries. The Slovak part is small, with only a few villages.

## Production and wine styles
Dry and sweet white wines from Furmint, Hárslevelű and Muscat, including botrytised sweet wines.

## Key wines
- Tokaj Slovakia dry whites
- Tokaj Slovakia sweet wines

## What to look for
- Mineral whites with high acidity.
- Sweet wines in the Tokaj style.
