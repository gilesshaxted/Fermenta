# Tetramythos

> [!abstract] At a glance
> An organic, low intervention Peloponnese winery from Achaia, working with local grapes and Retsina.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Greece]]
> - **Region:** [[Peloponnese]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Roditis]]
> - **Styles:** [[Retsina]], [[Natural wine]]

## About
A family winery in the hills above the Gulf of Corinth. The wines are fresh and mostly made without additions, including a modern take on Retsina.
