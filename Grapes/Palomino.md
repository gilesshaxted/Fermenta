# Palomino

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A neutral grape that becomes Sherry through ageing and flor yeast.

## Where it's from
Andalusia in southern Spain, grown on the white albariza soils around Jerez.

## Where it grows
- **Jerez, Spain:** the heartland, on chalky albariza

## What it tastes like
- Neutral as a young wine, with nutty, saline notes after ageing

## Classic wines
- Fino
- Manzanilla
- Amontillado
- Oloroso

## Pairs with
- Almonds and olives
- Jamón
- Fried fish

## Good to know
- Low acid, so the winemaking creates the flavour.
- Albariza soil holds moisture in hot summers.
