# Maule Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Chile's largest wine area, with old bush vines of País and Carignan, and a growing quality scene.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Chile]]
> - **Part of:** [[South America]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** granite, clay, alluvial, volcanic
> - **Main grapes:** [[País]], [[Carignan]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Carmenère]]

## Landscape and topography
A wide valley and coastal hills, with vineyards on granite slopes.

## Soil and geology
Granite, clay and alluvial soils, with volcanic soils in places.

## Climate
Warm and dry, with more rain than the north.

## History
Dry-farmed old vines of País and Carignan, many a century old, have been rediscovered by young producers.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Carmenère, País and Carignan.

## Key wines
- Maule Carignan
- Maule País
- VIGNO

## What to look for
- Rustic and fresh from old vines.
- VIGNO is a quality label for old vine Carignan.
