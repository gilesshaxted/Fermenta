# Château Simone

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small historic estate in the tiny Palette appellation, making distinctive reds, whites and rosés.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Provence]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grenache]], [[Mourvèdre]], [[Clairette]]

## About
A family estate near Aix-en-Provence with old vines and traditional winemaking. The wines are unusual, savoury and long-lived, and quite unlike most Provence wine.

## Known for
- Palette Rouge
- Palette Blanc
- Palette Rosé
