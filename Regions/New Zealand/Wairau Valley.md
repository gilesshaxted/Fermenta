# Wairau Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The broad, flat heart of Marlborough, with stony soils and classic Sauvignon style.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[New Zealand]]
> - **Part of:** [[Marlborough]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** gravel, silt, loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
A wide valley floor with the Wairau River.

## Soil and geology
Free-draining stony soils on the north bank, with heavier soils on the south.

## Climate
Warm, bright days and cool nights.

## History
Where the first vines were planted in the 1970s.

## Production and wine styles
Sauvignon Blanc, Pinot Noir and Chardonnay.

## Key wines
- Wairau Valley Sauvignon Blanc

## What to look for
- Tropical, ripe and pungent.
- A benchmark for the style.
