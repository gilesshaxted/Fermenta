# Alfrocheiro

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A perfumed red of Dão that brings colour and fresh fruit to blends.

## Where it's from
Grown in Dão and Alentejo, often as part of a blend.

## Where it grows
- **Dão, Portugal:** in blends

## What it tastes like
- Strawberry, cherry, herbs and spice

## Classic wines
- Dão red

## Pairs with
- Roast meat
- Pasta

## Good to know
- Aromatic and fresh.
- Often blended with Touriga Nacional.
