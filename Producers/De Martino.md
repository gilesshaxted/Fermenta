# De Martino

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A thoughtful Chilean producer with a strong record for site driven wines and a leading role in reviving old vines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Chile]]
> - **Region:** [[Maipo Valley]], [[Itata Valley]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Carmenère]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Cinsault]], [[País]]

## About
A family estate in Maipo that also works with old vines in Itata. Large old vessels and tinajas feature, and the wines tend towards freshness over power.

## Known for
- Viejas Tinajas
- Legado
- Single Vineyard Alto de Piedras
- Quebrada Seca Chardonnay
