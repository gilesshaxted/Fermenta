# Domaine Cauhapé

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A leading Jurançon estate making both dry and sweet wines from the Manseng grapes.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Jurançon]], [[South-West France]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Petit Manseng]], [[Gros Manseng]]

## About
Cauhapé works the steep hillsides of Jurançon in the foothills of the Pyrenees. The sweet wines are rich yet kept lively by high acidity, and the dry whites are bright and aromatic.

## Known for
- Quintessence du Petit Manseng
- Chant des Vignes
- Symphonie de Novembre
