# Ararat Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A warm lowland valley at the foot of Mount Ararat, historically the centre of Armenian brandy and wine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Armenia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Asia]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** volcanic, alluvial, sandy loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Voskehat]], [[Areni]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## Landscape and topography
A flat river plain along the Araks river, with Mount Ararat visible across the border.

## Soil and geology
Volcanic and alluvial soils, with sandy loam.

## Climate
Hot and dry in summer and cold in winter, with irrigation widely used.

## History
The valley has long been the heart of Armenian agriculture and the brandy industry, and grapes grown here were often used for brandy.

## Production and wine styles
Fortified wines, brandy, and table wines from Voskehat and Areni.

## Key wines
- Ararat Valley Voskehat
- Armenian brandy
- Armenian fortified wine

## What to look for
- Warm, ripe wines.
- Brandy is as important as wine.
