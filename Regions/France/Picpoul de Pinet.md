# Picpoul de Pinet

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Zesty, saline white from the Bassin de Thau, made for shellfish and sunny lunches.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Languedoc]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Picpoul]]

## Landscape and topography
Flat to rolling land beside the Étang de Thau lagoon, close to the Mediterranean.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, clay and sand.

## Climate
Mediterranean with sea breezes tempering the heat.

## History
Picpoul de Pinet gained appellation status in 2013. Its name means lip-stinger, referring to the acidity.

## Production and wine styles
100% Picpoul, in unoaked stainless steel to keep the freshness.

## Key wines
- Picpoul de Pinet

## What to look for
- Lemon, green apple and a salty finish.
- Perfect with oysters from the nearby Thau lagoon.
