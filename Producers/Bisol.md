# Bisol

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A long established Valdobbiadene producer with a strong Cartizze reputation.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Prosecco]], [[Veneto]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Glera]]
> - **Styles:** [[Prosecco (style)]]

## About
Bisol works with growers across the hills of Conegliano Valdobbiadene, and holds fruit from the prized Cartizze hill. The wines are clean, floral and well balanced.

## Known for
- Cartizze
- Crede
- Jeio
