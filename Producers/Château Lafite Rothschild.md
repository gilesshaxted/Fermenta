# Château Lafite Rothschild

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A First Growth Pauillac estate known for the most elegant, perfumed style of the Médoc.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Pauillac]], [[Bordeaux]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Petit Verdot]]
> - **Styles:** [[Bordeaux blend]]

## About
Lafite is a long-established Pauillac estate whose wines are built on Cabernet Sauvignon, with Merlot and small amounts of other varieties. The style is restrained and fine-boned rather than powerful, ageing for decades.
