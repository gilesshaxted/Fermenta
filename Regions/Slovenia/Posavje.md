# Posavje

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The south east region along the Sava river, known for Cviček, a pale, light red, and Bizeljsko sparkling wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Slovenia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** clay, marl, limestone, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Blaufränkisch]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Riesling]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills and valleys along the Sava river, with vineyards on south-facing slopes near the Croatian border.

## Soil and geology
Clay, marl and limestone, with some sandy soils.

## Climate
Continental, with warm summers, cold winters and cooling hill air.

## History
Posavje is best known for Cviček, a blend of red and white grapes made in Dolenjska, a style protected by Slovenian rules. Bizeljsko-Sremič produces notable sparkling wines.

## Production and wine styles
Cviček, a light, tart red blend of red and white grapes, plus Blaufränkisch, Pinot Noir, Riesling and sparkling wine.

## Key wines
- Cviček
- Bizeljsko Sremič
- Dolenjska

## What to look for
- Cviček is a light, tart red for summer drinking.
- Sparkling wines from Bizeljsko are worth trying.
