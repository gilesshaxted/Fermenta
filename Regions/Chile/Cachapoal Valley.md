# Cachapoal Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The northern half of Rapel, ranging from warm valley to cool coastal sites.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Chile]]
> - **Part of:** [[Rapel Valley]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** alluvial, clay, granite
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Carmenère]], [[Merlot]], [[Pinot Noir]]

## Landscape and topography
A flat to rolling valley beneath the Andean foothills.

## Soil and geology
Alluvial and clay soils with granite.

## Climate
Warm Mediterranean.

## History
The Peumo area is renowned for Carmenère.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon, Carmenère and Merlot.

## Key wines
- Peumo Carmenère
- Cachapoal Cabernet

## What to look for
- Warm, ripe reds.
- Peumo is the Carmenère heartland.
