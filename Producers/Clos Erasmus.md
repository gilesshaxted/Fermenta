# Clos Erasmus

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A tiny, much admired Priorat estate making rich but balanced Grenache led wines.

> - **Country:** [[Spain]]

## About
Created by Daphne Glorian, the estate farms organically and has a cult following. The wines are concentrated, but with freshness and finesse.
