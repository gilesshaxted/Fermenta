# Dundee Hills

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The valley's best-known AVA, with red volcanic soil and rich, elegant Pinot Noir.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[Willamette Valley]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** volcanic, Jory soil, basalt
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Gris]]

## Landscape and topography
A cluster of hills in the northern valley, rising up to about 1,000 feet.

## Soil and geology
Deep red Jory soils derived from ancient basalt.

## Climate
Warmer and drier than the valley floor, with a long ripening season.

## History
The first Pinot Noir vines in the area were planted in the 1960s, and Eyrie, Domaine Drouhin and others followed.

## Production and wine styles
Pinot Noir mostly, with Chardonnay and Pinot Gris.

## Key wines
- Dundee Hills Pinot Noir

## What to look for
- Silky red fruit and spice.
- Often a good place to begin with Oregon Pinot.
