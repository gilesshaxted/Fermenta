# Texas Hill Country

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A warm Texas plateau region that suits heat-loving grapes like Tempranillo, Mourvèdre and Viognier.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[North America]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, granite, sandy loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tempranillo]], [[Viognier]], [[Mourvèdre]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling limestone hills west of Austin, near Fredericksburg and Johnson City.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, clay and sandy loams.

## Climate
Hot summers, mild winters and risk of storms.

## History
Texas has grown grapes since Spanish missionaries, and the modern industry grew from the 1970s.

## Production and wine styles
Tempranillo, Viognier, Mourvèdre, Cabernet Sauvignon and others.

## Key wines
- Texas Tempranillo
- Texas Viognier

## What to look for
- Ripe, spicy reds and floral whites.
- A fast-growing region.
