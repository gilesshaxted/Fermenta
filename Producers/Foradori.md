# Foradori

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The estate that turned Teroldego into a serious, terroir led wine, farmed biodynamically.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Trentino]], [[Trentino-Alto Adige]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Teroldego]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]]

## About
Elisabetta Foradori farms in the Campo Rotaliano and ferments some wines in clay amphorae. The wines are fresh, pure and fine textured, and have inspired many younger growers.

## Known for
- Granato
- Sgarzon
- Morei
