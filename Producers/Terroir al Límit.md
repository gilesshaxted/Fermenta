# Terroir al Límit

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Priorat project led by Dominik Huber, known for fresh, lifted, low intervention wines from old vines.

## About
He works hard in the vineyard and minimally in the cellar, aiming for finesse rather than power. The wines feel quite different from the heavier Priorat of old.
