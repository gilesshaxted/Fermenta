# Crete

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Greece's largest island, with some of its oldest wine traditions and a flourishing modern scene.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Greece]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, sand, schist
> - **Main grapes:** [[Vidiano]], [[Vilana]], [[Kotsifali]], [[Mandilaria|Mandilari]], [[Liatiko]]

## Landscape and topography
Mountainous island with vineyards from sea level up to 700 metres or more, with the sea on all sides.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, clay, sand and schist.

## Climate
Mediterranean, with sea breezes and mountain cooling.

## History
The Minoans made wine, and wine presses have been found at Knossos. Crete exported wine across the Mediterranean in antiquity. Venetian rule brought Malvasia.

## Production and wine styles
Vidiano and Vilana for whites, and Kotsifali, Mandilari and Liatiko for reds. Sweet Liatiko wines are a speciality.

## Key wines
- Peza
- Archanes
- Sitia
- Vidiano
- Liatiko

## What to look for
- A rediscovery of native grapes.
- Fresh, savoury whites.
