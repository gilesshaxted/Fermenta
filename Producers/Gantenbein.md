# Gantenbein

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small, highly regarded estate in Fläsch, Graubünden, famed for Pinot Noir.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Switzerland]]
> - **Region:** [[German-speaking Switzerland]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Riesling]], [[Chardonnay]]

## About
Run by the Gantenbein family, the estate makes Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Riesling in tiny quantities. The wines are precise and elegant, and sought after by collectors.
