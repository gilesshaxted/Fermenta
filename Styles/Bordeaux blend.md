# Bordeaux blend

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A red made by blending Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and others, now made worldwide.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Category:** Still
> - **Colour:** Red
> - **Sweetness:** Dry
> - **Country:** Various
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Petit Verdot]], [[Malbec]]
> - **Regions:** [[Bordeaux]], [[Médoc]], [[Haut-Médoc]], [[Pauillac]], [[Margaux]], [[Saint-Émilion]], [[Pomerol]], [[Pessac-Léognan]], [[Napa Valley]], [[Stellenbosch]], [[Hawke's Bay]], [[Margaret River]], [[Coonawarra]]

## How it's made
Left Bank wines lean on Cabernet Sauvignon and Right Bank wines on Merlot. Cabernet Franc, Petit Verdot and Malbec are minor partners. Blending hedges the risks of a vintage.

## Where it's made
Bordeaux first, and also Napa, Stellenbosch, Hawke's Bay, Margaret River and Coonawarra.

## What it tastes like
- Blackcurrant, cedar, graphite
- Firm tannin
- Tobacco and dark fruit with age

## Pairs with
- Roast lamb
- Steak
- Hard cheese

## Good to know
- Claret is the traditional British name for red Bordeaux.
- American producers sometimes use Meritage for these blends.
