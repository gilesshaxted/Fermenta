# Jim Barry Wines

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family estate in the Clare Valley, known for dry Riesling and the intense Armagh Shiraz.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Australia]]
> - **Region:** [[Clare Valley]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]], [[Syrah]]

## About
Based in the Clare Valley with its own long established vineyards. Rieslings are dry and limey, and the top Shiraz is concentrated and powerful.

## Known for
- The Armagh
- McRae Wood
- Florita Riesling
- The Lodge Hill Riesling
