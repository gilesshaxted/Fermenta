# Cassis

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A tiny coastal appellation known mainly for white wine, ideal with bouillabaisse and seafood.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Provence]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Marsanne]], [[Clairette]], [[Ugni Blanc]]
> - **Styles:** [[Rosé]]

## Landscape and topography
Terraces sheltered by the Calanques cliffs beside the fishing port of Cassis.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, with chalky outcrops.

## Climate
Mediterranean, with sea air.

## History
Not related to the blackcurrant liqueur of the same name. One of the first appellations in France, recognised in 1936.

## Production and wine styles
Primarily white from Marsanne, Clairette and others, with a little rosé and red.

## Key wines
- Cassis blanc

## What to look for
- Citrus, herbs and a saline finish.
- Very good with Mediterranean fish.
