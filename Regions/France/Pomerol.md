# Pomerol

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A tiny, clay-rich appellation making velvety, opulent Merlot wines, including the legendary Pétrus.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Bordeaux]]
> - **Climate:** Maritime
> - **Soils:** clay, gravel
> - **Main grapes:** [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Franc]]
> - **Styles:** [[Bordeaux blend]]

## Landscape and topography
A low, gently raised plateau on the right bank of the Dordogne, only a few square kilometres in size.

## Soil and geology
Clay over gravel, with a famous knoll of blue clay beneath Pétrus and its neighbours.

## Climate
Maritime, with Merlot ripening reliably on the cold, water-holding clay.

## History
Pomerol has no official classification. The reputation of its top estates grew through the 19th and 20th centuries, and Pétrus became world famous in the 20th century.

## Production and wine styles
Mostly Merlot with Cabernet Franc, from small estates. Very little wine is made, so prices are high.

## Key wines
- Pétrus
- Le Pin
- Château Lafleur
- Vieux Château Certan

## What to look for
- Silky, plush and truffle-scented, with a softer feel than the Left Bank.
- No classification, so the producer's name matters.
