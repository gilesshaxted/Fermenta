# Cava

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Spain's traditional-method sparkling wine, mostly from Penedès, with a wide range of quality.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Part of:** [[Penedès]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Xarel·lo]], [[Macabeo]], [[Parellada]]
> - **Styles:** [[Cava (style)|Cava style]]

## Landscape and topography
Spread across the Penedès hills, with some areas in Rioja and elsewhere in Spain.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, clay and sand.

## Climate
Mediterranean.

## History
Cava was officially named in the 1970s to replace the term champán. Many top producers have left to form their own groups, such as Corpinnat and Clàssic Penedès, to emphasise quality.

## Production and wine styles
Traditional method with at least nine months on lees, and more for Reserva (15 months), Gran Reserva (30 months) and Paraje Calificado (single site).

## Key wines
- Cava Brut Nature
- Cava Reserva
- Cava Gran Reserva
- Cava Rosado

## What to look for
- Apple, citrus and biscuit.
- A superb value alternative to Champagne.
