# Delaware

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A pink skinned American variety grown in Japan for light, fruity wines.

## Where it's from
Delaware is an American grape grown in Japan for many decades. It is often eaten as a table grape or used for sweeter wines.

## Where it grows
- **Yamagata, Japan:** a major planting
- **Yamanashi, Japan:** also grown

## What it tastes like
- Grape, strawberry, floral and sweet fruit
