> [!abstract] At a glance
> A landmark Colli Orientali estate recognised by its map label and its elegant whites.

## About
The estate helped define the hill vineyards of Rosazzo and the surrounding area. The wines are fresh, mineral and focused, with the top whites ageing gracefully.
