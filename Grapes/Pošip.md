# Pošip

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A full white from the island of Korčula, with ripe fruit and good body.

## Where it's from
Native to Korčula and the Dalmatian coast, and one of Croatia's best known whites.

## Where it grows
- **Korčula, Croatia:** the heartland
- **Pelješac, Croatia:** also grown

## What it tastes like
- Peach, apricot, citrus and herbs

## Classic wines
- Korčula Pošip

## Pairs with
- Grilled fish
- Shellfish
- Risotto

## Good to know
- Often rich, with a salty finish.
- Can age in bottle.
