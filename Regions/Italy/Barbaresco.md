# Barbaresco

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Barolo's more graceful neighbour, with Nebbiolo from a smaller zone nearer the Tanaro river.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Piedmont]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** marl, limestone, sandstone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Nebbiolo]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills near the Tanaro river, with cru sites such as Asili, Rabajà and Pajé.

## Soil and geology
Calcareous marl, similar to the western Barolo hills, giving finer, more elegant Nebbiolo.

## Climate
Slightly warmer and earlier ripening than Barolo, with the river moderating conditions.

## History
Domizio Cavazza founded a cooperative in 1894. Angelo Gaja later brought international fame to the zone.

## Production and wine styles
100% Nebbiolo. Minimum ageing is 26 months, with at least nine in wood (Riserva is 50 months).

## Key wines
- Barbaresco
- Barbaresco Riserva
- Single-vineyard (MGA) Barbaresco

## What to look for
- Often softer and more approachable earlier than Barolo.
- Violet, red cherry, liquorice and spice.
