# Styria

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Green, hilly Austria in the south, with some of Europe's best Sauvignon Blanc on steep slopes.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Austria]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, sand, slate, volcanic
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Welschriesling]], [[Pinot Blanc]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Blauer Wildbacher]]
> - **Styles:** [[Orange wine]]

## Landscape and topography
Forested hills and steep slopes close to the Slovenian border, with vines up to 600 metres.

## Soil and geology
Clay, limestone, sand, and slate in places, with volcanic soils in Vulkanland.

## Climate
Warm summers, cool nights and rain, with Mediterranean influence.

## History
The area is nicknamed the Tuscany of Austria. Wine growers such as Tement, Polz and Sattlerhof produce world-class Sauvignon Blanc.

## Production and wine styles
Sauvignon Blanc, Morillon (Chardonnay), Welschriesling and Gelber Muskateller. Schilcher, a tart rosé, is a local speciality from Blauer Wildbacher.

## Key wines
- Südsteiermark DAC
- Vulkanland Steiermark DAC
- Schilcher

## What to look for
- Elegant Sauvignon Blanc with elderflower and pepper.
- Schilcher is very tart.
