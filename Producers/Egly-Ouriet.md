## About
Egly-Ouriet works low yields in grand cru vineyards, ferments in barrel and bottles with little dosage. The wines are powerful and structured and often need time.

## Known for
- Grand Cru Brut Tradition
- Les Vignes de Vrigny
- V.P. Vieillissement Prolongé
