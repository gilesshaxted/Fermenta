# Gibbston

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The coolest sub-region, in a gorge, making elegant, structured Pinot Noir.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[New Zealand]]
> - **Part of:** [[Central Otago]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** schist, gravel, silt
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]]

## Landscape and topography
A river gorge by the Kawarau River near Queenstown.

## Soil and geology
Schist and glacial gravels.

## Climate
Cooler and higher, with an extra frost risk.

## History
Early plantings were controversial but successful.

## Production and wine styles
Pinot Noir and sparkling wine.

## Key wines
- Gibbston Pinot Noir

## What to look for
- Fragrant, red-fruited Pinot Noir.
- Often more delicate.
