# Rkatsiteli

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Georgia's most planted white grape, behind both fresh whites and amber qvevri wines.

## Where it's from
An ancient Georgian variety that is the backbone of Kakheti winemaking, and is also grown widely in the former Soviet Union.

## Where it grows
- **Kakheti, Georgia:** the heartland
- **Armenia:** also grown

## What it tastes like
- Apricot, quince, citrus and tea

## Classic wines
- Tsinandali
- Kakheti Rkatsiteli

## Pairs with
- Roast chicken
- Cheese
- Walnut dishes

## Good to know
- Makes both amber and white styles.
- Keeps its acidity in hot years.
