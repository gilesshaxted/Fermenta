# Pinot Blanc

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Soft, clean and food-friendly. A white that rarely shouts.

## Where it's from
A mutation of Pinot Gris, which itself mutated from Pinot Noir, in Burgundy.

## Where it grows
- **Alsace, France:** an easy everyday white
- **Alto Adige, Italy:** known as Pinot Bianco
- **Germany and Austria:** known as Weissburgunder

## What it tastes like
- Apple, pear and a nutty touch
- Medium weight

## Classic wines
- Alsace Pinot Blanc
- Alto Adige Pinot Bianco
- German Weissburgunder

## Pairs with
- Quiche
- Salmon
- Light pasta

## Good to know
- Often mixed up with Chardonnay in blind tastings.
- Often sold as a value alternative to Chardonnay.
