# Goriška Brda

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Rolling terraced hills on the Italian border, the continuation of Collio and Slovenia's best known area for Rebula.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Slovenia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Primorska]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** flysch, marl, sandstone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Ribolla Gialla]], [[Friulano]], [[Merlot]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Chardonnay]]
> - **Styles:** [[Orange wine]]

## Landscape and topography
Steep, terraced hills of up to about 300 metres on the border with Friuli, where Brda is the Slovenian half of the Collio range.

## Soil and geology
Flysch of marl and sandstone, known as opoka.

## Climate
Warm and sheltered, with cooling air from the Alps and Adriatic and good day to night differences.

## History
Brda has been cultivated for centuries, and the border here was drawn through a single wine landscape after the Second World War. Its Rebula wines, especially skin-contact ones, are well known.

## Production and wine styles
Rebula (Ribolla Gialla), Sauvignonasse (Friulano), Chardonnay, Pinot Gris and Merlot, in styles from fresh whites to long skin-contact orange wines.

## Key wines
- Brda Rebula
- Brda Merlot
- Brda orange wines
- Brda white blends

## What to look for
- Rebula is the grape to look for.
- Skin-contact whites have a loyal following.
