# Areni

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Armenia's best known native red grape, giving fresh, peppery wines from high-altitude vineyards.

## Where it's from
An old Armenian variety grown around the village of Areni, which is also home to a cave with evidence of ancient winemaking.

## Where it grows
- **Vayots Dzor, Armenia:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Red cherry, wild berries, pepper and spice

## Classic wines
- Areni red

## Pairs with
- Lamb
- Grilled meat
- Hard cheese

## Good to know
- Often grown on its own roots.
- Fresh and spicy.
