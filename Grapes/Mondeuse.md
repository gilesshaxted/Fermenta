# Mondeuse

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The peppery, dark red of the French Alps.

## Where it's from
Savoie, where it is the star red. DNA shows a link to Syrah.

## Where it grows
- **Savoie, France:** especially Arbin and Chautagne

## What it tastes like
- Blackberry, pepper, violets and bright acidity

## Classic wines
- Mondeuse d'Arbin
- Vin de Savoie rouge

## Pairs with
- Cured meats
- Raclette
- Beef stew

## Good to know
- Related to Syrah, so a similar peppery edge.
- Unrelated to the Argentinian Bonarda.
