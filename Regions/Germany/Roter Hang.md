# Roter Hang

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The Red Slope above the Rhine at Nierstein, with iron-rich red soils and top-class Riesling.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Germany]]
> - **Part of:** [[Rheinhessen]]
> - **Climate:** Temperate
> - **Soils:** red slate, rotliegend
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]]

## Landscape and topography
A steep slope facing east over the Rhine, between Nackenheim and Nierstein.

## Soil and geology
Red Permian sandstone and slate (Rotliegend) rich in iron.

## Climate
Sheltered and warm, with sun on the slope.

## History
Villages such as Nierstein were famous for wine in the 19th century. Recent years have seen a revival of vineyards such as Pettenthal and Hipping.

## Production and wine styles
Riesling with the powerful structure of the red soils.

## Key wines
- Nierstein Pettenthal
- Nackenheim Rothenberg
- Nierstein Hipping

## What to look for
- Peach, spice and a sweet-smoky minerality.
- Deeper and fuller than slate Rieslings.
