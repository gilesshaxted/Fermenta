# Aconcagua Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A warm northern valley with powerful reds, plus the cool, coastal Aconcagua Costa area.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Chile]]
> - **Part of:** [[South America]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** alluvial, granite, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Syrah]], [[Carmenère]]

## Landscape and topography
The Aconcagua River runs from the Andes to the coast, with vineyards from valley floor to coastal slopes.

## Soil and geology
Alluvial soils on the valley floor, with granite and clay on hills.

## Climate
Warm and dry inland, much cooler near the coast.

## History
The Errázuriz family planted here in the 1870s.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon, Syrah and Carmenère inland, with Pinot Noir and Chardonnay near the coast.

## Key wines
- Aconcagua Cabernet Sauvignon
- Aconcagua Costa Syrah

## What to look for
- Ripe, concentrated reds.
- The coastal zone is much fresher.

- [[Errázuriz]]
