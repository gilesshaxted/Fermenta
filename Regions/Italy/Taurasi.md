# Taurasi

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Italy's answer to Barolo in the south: structured, long-lived Aglianico from high-altitude hills.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Campania]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** volcanic, clay, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Aglianico]]

## Landscape and topography
High hills in Irpinia, east of Avellino, with vineyards at up to 600 metres.

## Soil and geology
Volcanic ash and clay over limestone.

## Climate
Continental-influenced, with cold winters and late harvests, often into November.

## History
Mastroberardino rescued the local grapes and made Taurasi famous, and the zone was one of the first southern DOCGs.

## Production and wine styles
100% Aglianico, minimum three years of ageing (four for Riserva).

## Key wines
- Taurasi
- Taurasi Riserva

## What to look for
- Black cherry, tar, leather and firm tannin.
- A superb cellar wine at a fair price.

## Producers
- [[Feudi di San Gregorio]]
- [[Mastroberardino]]
- [[Terredora di Paolo]]
