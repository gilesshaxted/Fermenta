# Bellavista

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of the original Franciacorta estates and a key reason the region has its reputation.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Franciacorta]], [[Lombardy]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]]
> - **Styles:** [[Franciacorta (style)]], [[Traditional method]]

## About
Bellavista makes a wide range of traditional method wines, with careful blending from many small plots. The style is elegant and well balanced.

## Known for
- Gran Cuvée Alma
- Gran Cuvée Brut
- Teatro alla Scala
