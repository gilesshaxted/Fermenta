# Marsala

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Fortified wine from western Sicily, from dry aperitif to rich dessert wine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Sicily]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grillo]], [[Catarratto]], [[Inzolia]]
> - **Styles:** [[Marsala (style)|Marsala style]], [[Solera system]]

## Landscape and topography
Flat coastal land in the far west, around Marsala and Trapani.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, clay and sandy soils.

## Climate
Hot and dry Mediterranean.

## History
John Woodhouse, an English merchant, began shipping fortified Marsala in the 1770s as a cheaper alternative to Port and Madeira. The Florio family later built the region's name.

## Production and wine styles
Fortified with grape spirit and aged in cask. Styles range from Fine to Vergine and Riserva, and from dry (secco) to sweet (dolce).

## Key wines
- Marsala Vergine
- Marsala Superiore
- Marsala Fine

## What to look for
- Choose Vergine or Superiore, not the cooking grade.
- Walnut, dried apricot and caramel.
