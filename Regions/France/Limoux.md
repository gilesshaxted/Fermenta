# Limoux

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A high, cool corner with a legitimate claim to the world's first sparkling wine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Languedoc]]
> - **Climate:** Maritime
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, sandstone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Chenin Blanc]], [[Mauzac]], [[Pinot Noir]]
> - **Styles:** [[Crémant]], [[Traditional method]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling hills in the foothills of the Pyrenees, with altitude and Atlantic influence.

## Soil and geology
Limestone and clay, with sandstone in places.

## Climate
A mix of Atlantic, Mediterranean and mountain influences, cooler than the surrounding Languedoc.

## History
Benedictine monks at Saint-Hilaire are said to have made sparkling wine here in the 1500s, before Champagne. The Blanquette de Limoux tradition continues.

## Production and wine styles
Blanquette de Limoux (Mauzac-led, by the ancestral method), Crémant de Limoux (Chardonnay, Chenin Blanc, Pinot Noir), and still Chardonnay.

## Key wines
- Blanquette de Limoux
- Crémant de Limoux
- Limoux Chardonnay

## What to look for
- Apple, pear and a floral edge in Blanquette.
- A great alternative to Champagne at a friendlier price.
