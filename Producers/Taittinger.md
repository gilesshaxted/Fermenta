# Taittinger

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A well known Reims house with a lighter, Chardonnay-driven style and a famous blanc de blancs prestige cuvée.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Champagne]], [[Côte des Blancs]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Pinot Meunier]]
> - **Styles:** [[Champagne (style)]]

## About
Taittinger blends a high proportion of Chardonnay into its non-vintage wines, giving a fresh and elegant house style. Its cellars sit in old chalk pits and crypts beneath Reims.

## Known for
- Brut Réserve
- Comtes de Champagne
- Prélude Grands Crus
