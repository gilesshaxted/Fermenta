# Burgenland

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Austria's red wine heartland and the home of world-class sweet wines near Lake Neusiedl.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Austria]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** loess, limestone, slate, sand, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Blaufränkisch]], [[Zweigelt]], [[St. Laurent]], [[Welschriesling]], [[Pinot Blanc]]
> - **Styles:** [[Icewine and Eiswein]], [[Noble rot wines]]

## Landscape and topography
Flat plains and low hills around the shallow Lake Neusiedl on the border with Hungary.

## Soil and geology
Varied, including schist, limestone, loess, sand and gravel.

## Climate
Warm, dry Pannonian climate with long autumns. The shallow lake gives morning fog that helps noble rot.

## History
Burgenland was part of Hungary until 1921, and the area has a rich tradition of dessert wines, including Ruster Ausbruch. Blaufränkisch has become the star red.

## Production and wine styles
Blaufränkisch, Zweigelt, St. Laurent, and white Welschriesling and Chardonnay. Sweet wines (Beerenauslese, Trockenbeerenauslese, Ausbruch) from botrytis.

## Key wines
- Leithaberg DAC
- Blaufränkisch Reserve
- Eisenberg DAC
- Ruster Ausbruch
- Neusiedlersee wines

## What to look for
- Spicy, peppery Blaufränkisch with fine tannins.
- Sweet wines of world class.
