# Marqués de Riscal

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of Rioja's best known historic houses, based in Elciego, with a famous Frank Gehry designed hotel.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Region:** [[Rioja Alavesa]], [[Rueda]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tempranillo]], [[Graciano]], [[Verdejo]]

## About
A large and historic Rioja producer, also an early quality force in Rueda with Verdejo. The Rioja reds are classic, oak aged and savoury, with the Barón de Chirel as the top wine.

## Known for
- Barón de Chirel
- Gran Reserva
- Reserva
- Rueda Verdejo
