# Judean Hills

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Rocky hills around Jerusalem, now one of Israel's most prized areas for boutique reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Israel]]
> - **Part of:** [[Asia]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, terra rossa, chalk
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Syrah]], [[Carignan]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling, rocky hills at about 600 to 900 metres in the centre of the country.

## Soil and geology
Limestone and terra rossa, thin and stony.

## Climate
Mediterranean, with hot days and cool nights at altitude, and a long ripening season.

## History
Winemaking is ancient here, with biblical associations. The modern revival started in the 1990s with boutique wineries such as Domaine du Castel.

## Production and wine styles
Bordeaux style blends, Syrah, Carignan and some Chardonnay.

## Key wines
- Judean Hills red blends
- Domaine du Castel
- Judean Hills Chardonnay

## What to look for
- Elegant, structured reds with a savoury edge.
- Mostly boutique wineries.
