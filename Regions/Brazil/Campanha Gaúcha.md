# Campanha Gaúcha

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A drier, flatter southern region near the Uruguayan border, with a sunnier climate.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Brazil]]
> - **Part of:** [[South America]]
> - **Climate:** Warm temperate
> - **Soils:** sandy loam, granite, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tannat]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Touriga Nacional]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
Open plains and rolling pampas in the south of Rio Grande do Sul, with vineyards at modest altitude.

## Soil and geology
Sandy loam over granite, with clay.

## Climate
Warmer and drier than Serra Gaúcha, with less rain during ripening.

## History
A newer region for fine wine, planted from the late twentieth century to take advantage of the sunnier conditions.

## Production and wine styles
Riper reds such as Tannat and Cabernet Sauvignon, plus Touriga Nacional and fresh whites.

## Key wines
- Campanha Tannat
- Campanha Cabernet Sauvignon

## What to look for
- A drier climate helps ripening.
- Fast growing quality region.
