# Château du Cèdre

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family estate in Cahors making polished, modern-leaning Malbec.

> [!info] Quick facts

## About
Château du Cèdre is a respected Cahors producer working with the region's Malbec, known locally as Côt. The wines range from fresh and fruity to deep and oaked.
