# Schioppettino

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A peppery, aromatic red that was nearly lost in Friuli.

## Where it's from
A very old Friulian variety near extinction in the 1970s, since revived. It is grown mostly in the Colli Orientali.

## Where it grows
- **Friuli, Italy:** around Prepotto

## What it tastes like
- Black pepper, blackberry, spice and herbs

## Classic wines
- Schioppettino di Prepotto

## Pairs with
- Game
- Roast meat
- Sausages

## Good to know
- Smells peppery, similar to Syrah.
- The name may mean crackling in the mouth.
