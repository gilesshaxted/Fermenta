# Domaine Ganevat

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Rotalier domaine, led by Jean-François Ganevat, widely loved for its low-intervention wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Jura]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Savagnin]], [[Poulsard]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]]

## About
Ganevat farms organically and makes a large number of small-lot cuvées, often from old and rare varieties. The wines are fresh, vivid and distinctive.

## Known for
- Cuvée Florine
- Les Grands Teppes Vieilles Vignes
