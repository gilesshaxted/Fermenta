# Clos des Papes

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A highly regarded family domaine in Châteauneuf-du-Pape, known for balanced, ageworthy reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Châteauneuf-du-Pape]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grenache]], [[Mourvèdre]], [[Syrah]]
> - **Styles:** [[GSM]]

## About
An estate with vines spread across the appellation, blending several varieties into a single red. The style is rich but fresh, and the white also has a following.

## Known for
- Châteauneuf-du-Pape Rouge
- Châteauneuf-du-Pape Blanc
