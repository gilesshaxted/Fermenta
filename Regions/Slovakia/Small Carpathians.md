# Small Carpathians

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The best known Slovak wine region, in the south-west on the slopes of the Small Carpathians near Bratislava.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Slovakia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Warm continental
> - **Soils:** granite, gneiss, limestone, loess, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grüner Veltliner]], [[Welschriesling]], [[Riesling]], [[Blaufränkisch]], [[St. Laurent]], [[Pinot Noir]]

## Landscape and topography
Foothills of the Small Carpathians, running north-east from Bratislava, with the wine towns of Pezinok and Modra.

## Soil and geology
Granite and gneiss, with limestone, loess and clay in places.

## Climate
Warm and fairly dry, protected from the east by the mountains.

## History
The area has a long winegrowing tradition under Habsburg influence, with German-speaking growers having shaped the area. Since 1989 many private wineries have been set up.

## Production and wine styles
Mainly fresh, dry whites such as Grüner Veltliner, Riesling and Welschriesling, and reds including Blaufränkisch, St. Laurent and Pinot Noir.

## Key wines
- Malokarpatská Grüner Veltliner
- Malokarpatská Riesling
- Malokarpatská Blaufränkisch

## What to look for
- Crisp, fresh whites.
- Light to medium reds.
