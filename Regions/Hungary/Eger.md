# Eger

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A baroque town and wine region in northern Hungary, home of Bull's Blood.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Hungary]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** volcanic, clay, limestone, loess
> - **Main grapes:** [[Blaufränkisch]], [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Kadarka]], [[Merlot]], [[Welschriesling]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
Foothills of the Bükk mountains in north Hungary, with vineyards in the Valley of the Beautiful Woman and on surrounding slopes.

## Soil and geology
Volcanic tuff, clay and limestone, with cellars cut into the soft rock.

## Climate
Warm continental, cooler than the south of the country thanks to the hills.

## History
Egri Bikavér, or Bull's Blood, is a historic red blend with a legend linked to the Ottoman siege of 1552. The name now applies to a controlled blend.

## Production and wine styles
Red blends dominate, including Egri Bikavér and the finer Bikavér Superior. Whites include Olaszrizling and Chardonnay.

## Key wines
- Egri Bikavér
- Bikavér Superior
- Egri Csillag

## What to look for
- Bull's Blood has improved hugely in the last two decades.
- Kékfrankos and Kadarka are the traditional reds.
