# Helan Mountain East

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The foothills on the eastern side of the Helan Mountains, the heart of Ningxia's quality wine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[China]]
> - **Part of:** [[Ningxia]]
> - **Climate:** Semi-arid
> - **Soils:** gravel, sand, alluvial
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Marselan]], [[Cabernet Gernischt]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
Alluvial fans and gentle slopes below the mountain range, with a good deal of sunshine.

## Soil and geology
Gravel and sandy soils, often poor and free-draining.

## Climate
Dry, with hot summers, big day and night contrasts and harsh winters needing vine burial.

## History
The area was identified in the 1980s as suitable for wine, and many of the best known wineries are located here today.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Marselan and Cabernet Gernischt, with Chardonnay as the main white.

## Key wines
- Helan Mountain East Cabernet Sauvignon
- Helan Mountain East red blends

## What to look for
- Ripe fruit with firm tannins.
- Wineries often welcome visitors.
