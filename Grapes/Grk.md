# Grk

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A rare, savoury white from the sandy soils around Lumbarda on Korčula.

## Where it's from
Grown on Korčula's Lumbarda sands, where the vine only has female flowers and needs another variety nearby for pollination.

## Where it grows
- **Lumbarda, Croatia:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Citrus, herbs, almond and salt

## Classic wines
- Lumbarda Grk

## Pairs with
- Shellfish
- Grilled fish
- Salty cheese

## Good to know
- The vine needs a pollinating variety, often Plavac Mali.
- The name means bitter.
