> Trentino's most famous sparkling wine house, making Trento DOC in the traditional method.

## About
Based near Trento, the family business works with high-altitude Chardonnay and Pinot Noir. The wines are crisp, fine and consistent and are often served at big events across Italy.

## Known for
- Giulio Ferrari Riserva del Fondatore
