# Belondrade

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A pioneering Rueda house making barrel fermented Verdejo, aiming for a fine, textured style.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Region:** [[Rueda]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Verdejo]]

## About
Belondrade shows that Verdejo can do more than quick, zesty whites, fermenting and ageing in oak on the lees. The wines are among the most serious in Rueda.

## Known for
- Belondrade y Lurton
- Quinta Apolonia
