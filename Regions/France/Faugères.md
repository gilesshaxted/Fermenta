# Faugères

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Schist hills that produce lifted, fragrant reds, often from small growers.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Languedoc]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** schist
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Grenache]], [[Carignan]], [[Mourvèdre]], [[Cinsault]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling hills at the foot of the Cévennes, with vines on shale-based soils.

## Soil and geology
Almost entirely schist, which retains heat and gives a mineral, spicy character.

## Climate
Mediterranean, tempered by altitude.

## History
A small appellation founded in 1982. Tiny estates and cooperatives now craft refined wines.

## Production and wine styles
Red blends and rosés, with some white.

## Key wines
- Faugères rouge
- Faugères blanc

## What to look for
- Pepper, red berries, garrigue and graphite.
- One of the most elegant reds of the Languedoc.

- [[Domaine Léon Barral]]
