# Jura

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small, quirky mountain region famed for oxidative whites, vin jaune and pale, delicate reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** marl, limestone, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Savagnin]], [[Poulsard]], [[Trousseau]], [[Pinot Noir]]
> - **Styles:** [[Crémant]], [[Pétillant naturel]], [[Natural wine]], [[Vin Jaune]]

## Landscape and topography
A narrow band of vineyards at the foot of the Jura mountains, between Burgundy and Switzerland, at 250 to 450 metres.

## Soil and geology
Blue, grey and red marl, with limestone and clay, giving each village a different personality.

## Climate
Continental, with cold winters, warm summers and rain, plus a risk of frost.

## History
Pasteur worked on fermentation in Arbois. The Jura has a long tradition of unusual styles, including vin jaune made under a veil of yeast, which are now enjoying popularity again.

## Production and wine styles
Whites from Chardonnay and Savagnin, in fresh (ouillé) or oxidative (sous voile) styles. Reds from Poulsard, Trousseau and Pinot Noir. Also Crémant du Jura, vin jaune, vin de paille and Macvin.

## Key wines
- Arbois
- Château-Chalon
- L'Étoile
- Côtes du Jura
- Vin Jaune
- Crémant du Jura

## What to look for
- Vin jaune comes in a 62 cl bottle called a clavelin.
- Nutty, savoury and a perfect match for Comté cheese.
