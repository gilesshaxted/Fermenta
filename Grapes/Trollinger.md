# Trollinger

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A light, fresh red that is Württemberg's everyday wine, drunk by the jug.

## Where it's from
Grown in Württemberg for centuries, and the same grape as Schiava or Vernatsch in South Tyrol.

## Where it grows
- **Württemberg, Germany:** the heartland
- **Alto Adige, Italy:** as Schiava

## What it tastes like
- Red cherry, strawberry, herbs and spice

## Classic wines
- Trollinger mit Lemberger
- Stuttgart house wine

## Pairs with
- Maultaschen
- Sausages
- Cold cuts

## Good to know
- Often drunk young and cool.
- Known as Schiava in Italy.
