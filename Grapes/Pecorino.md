# Pecorino

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A fresh, savoury white that was nearly lost before a revival in the late 20th century.

## Where it's from
Native to the Marche and Abruzzo. The name probably relates to sheep, as it was said to be eaten by grazing flocks. It was nearly extinct until the 1980s.

## Where it grows
- **Marche, Italy:** Offida Pecorino
- **Abruzzo, Italy:** Terre di Chieti Pecorino

## What it tastes like
- Lemon, herbs, white peach and mineral

## Classic wines
- Offida Pecorino
- Abruzzo Pecorino

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Roast chicken
- Pecorino cheese

## Good to know
- Not related to the sheep's milk cheese.
- Gains richness with a little ageing.
