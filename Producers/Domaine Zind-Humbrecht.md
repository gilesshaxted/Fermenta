# Domaine Zind-Humbrecht

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A biodynamic Turckheim estate known for powerful single-vineyard Alsace wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Alsace]], [[Alsace Grand Cru]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Gewürztraminer]]

## About
Zind-Humbrecht farms parcels across Alsace, including steep grand cru sites such as Rangen. The wines are rich and concentrated and often carry some residual sugar.

- Clos Windsbuhl
