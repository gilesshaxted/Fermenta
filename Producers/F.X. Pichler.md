# F.X. Pichler

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A great Wachau estate known for powerful, mineral Smaragd wines from Loiben.

## About
A family estate working steep terraced sites in Loiben and Dürnstein. The wines are intense and concentrated, and they age very well.
