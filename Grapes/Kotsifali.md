# Kotsifali

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A soft, warm Cretan red, often blended with Mandilari.

## Where it's from
A native Cretan variety, known in Peza and Archanes.

## Where it grows
- **Crete, Greece:** Peza and Archanes

## What it tastes like
- Red cherry, spice, herbs and earth

## Classic wines
- Peza red
- Archanes

## Pairs with
- Lamb
- Grilled meat

## Good to know
- Fruit-driven and soft.
- Often blended with Mandilari.
