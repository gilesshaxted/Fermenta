# Domaine de la Rectorie

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A well known Collioure and Banyuls family estate, making fine dry reds and fortified Banyuls.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Collioure and Banyuls]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grenache]], [[Syrah]], [[Mourvèdre]], [[Carignan]]
> - **Styles:** [[Vin doux naturel]]

## About
A family domaine on steep terraced vineyards by the sea. The dry wines are rich and savoury, and the Banyuls is classic, sweet and nutty.

## Known for
- Cuvée Léon Parcé
- Coume Pascole
- Banyuls
