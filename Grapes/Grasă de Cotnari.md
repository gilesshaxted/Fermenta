# Grasă de Cotnari

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A noble-rot white grape, the basis for the famous sweet wines of Cotnari.

## Where it's from
Native to Moldova's Cotnari vineyards, historically prized for sweet wines. The name means fat.

## Where it grows
- **Cotnari, Romania:** the home

## What it tastes like
- Honey, apricot, spice and citrus

## Classic wines
- Cotnari Grasă

## Pairs with
- Blue cheese
- Fruit desserts

## Good to know
- Prone to noble rot.
- Rich and age-worthy.
