# Vale dos Vinhedos

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Brazil's first geographical indication and its best known wine valley.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Brazil]]
> - **Part of:** [[Serra Gaúcha]]
> - **Climate:** Humid subtropical
> - **Soils:** basalt, clay, sandstone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Merlot]], [[Tannat]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Muscat]]

## Landscape and topography
A compact valley of hills and vineyards in the heart of Serra Gaúcha, near Bento Gonçalves.

## Soil and geology
Basalt and sandstone with clay.

## Climate
Humid subtropical, with rain in summer and cooler nights.

## History
It was recognised as Brazil's first geographical indication for wine, and has become the centre of Brazilian wine tourism.

## Production and wine styles
Merlot reds and sparkling wines, with a growing focus on quality.

## Key wines
- Vale dos Vinhedos Merlot
- Vale dos Vinhedos Espumante

## What to look for
- A strong wine tourism hub.
- Merlot is a signature red.
