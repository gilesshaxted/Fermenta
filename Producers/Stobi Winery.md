# Stobi Winery

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Povardarie winery near Gradsko, known for Vranec and the local white Žilavka.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[North Macedonia]]
> - **Region:** [[Povardarie]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Vranac]], [[Chardonnay]]

## About
A family-owned producer working mainly with local grapes in the Vardar valley. The style is ripe and sunny, with a focus on Vranec and the local white grapes.

## Known for
- Stobi Vranec
- Stobi Žilavka
