# Tenuta San Guido

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The home of Sassicaia, the wine that put Bolgheri and Super Tuscans on the world map.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Bolgheri]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Cabernet Franc]]
> - **Styles:** [[Super Tuscan]], [[Bordeaux blend]]

## About
A family estate on the Tuscan coast, inspired by Bordeaux when it began. Sassicaia is elegant and restrained for a Cabernet led wine, and has its own appellation.

## Known for
- Sassicaia
- Guidalberto
- Le Difese
