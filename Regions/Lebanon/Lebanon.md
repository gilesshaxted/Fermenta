# Lebanon

Part of [[Asia]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of the oldest winemaking countries, with high-altitude vineyards and French-influenced reds and whites.

## How wine is organised here
- Lebanon has no formal appellation system for wine, so labels usually name the estate, grape or blend, and a general area.
- The Bekaa Valley produces most of the country's wine.
- French grapes and styles dominate, with native whites such as Obaideh and Merwah used in some wines.
