# USA

Part of [[North America]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> From California's mountain and coastal valleys to Oregon, Washington and the East Coast, the US is the fourth largest wine producer in the world, led by California.

## How American wine is organised
- **AVA** (American Viticultural Area) is a defined grape-growing region. It is about place, with no rules on grapes or winemaking.
- If a label names an AVA, at least 85% of the grapes must come from it.
- A **varietal** label needs at least 75% of the named grape (90% in Oregon for most grapes).
- **Meritage** and proprietary names are used for Bordeaux-style blends.
