# Imereti

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A western region with a gentler, more European style of winemaking and local white grapes.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Georgia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Asia]]
> - **Climate:** Humid subtropical
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, red soils
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tsolikouri]], [[Tsitska]], [[Krakhuna]]
> - **Styles:** [[Qvevri wine]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills and valleys in western Georgia, around Kutaisi, between the Black Sea and the central highlands.

## Soil and geology
Clay, limestone and red soils.

## Climate
Humid and subtropical, with mild winters and a damp, rainy growing season.

## History
Imereti has its own winemaking tradition, with much shorter skin contact than in Kakheti, giving lighter wines. Longer skin-contact versions are also made today.

## Production and wine styles
Lighter, less tannic whites from Tsolikouri, Tsitska and Krakhuna, made with little or some skin contact, plus some reds.

## Key wines
- Imereti Tsolikouri
- Imereti Tsitska
- Imereti Krakhuna

## What to look for
- Lighter, fresher whites.
- A softer take on qvevri.
