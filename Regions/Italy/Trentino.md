# Trentino

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cooler, southern Alpine zone that excels at sparkling wine and Teroldego.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Trentino-Alto Adige]]
> - **Climate:** Alpine
> - **Soils:** limestone, alluvium, moraine
> - **Main grapes:** [[Teroldego]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir|Pinot Nero]], [[Nosiola]]
> - **Styles:** [[Vin Santo]]

## Landscape and topography
Valleys around Trento and the Rotaliano plain, with high vineyards on the sides.

## Soil and geology
Alluvial gravel on the valley floor and limestone slopes above.

## Climate
Alpine, with warm days and cold nights.

## History
Trento DOC, made by the traditional method, has been produced since the early 20th century, and Giulio Ferrari pioneered it.

## Production and wine styles
Sparkling Trento DOC (Chardonnay and Pinot Nero), Teroldego Rotaliano, Nosiola and Vino Santo.

## Key wines
- Trento DOC
- Teroldego Rotaliano
- Vino Santo Trentino

## What to look for
- Trento DOC is Italy's answer to Champagne.
- Teroldego is deep, fruity and spicy.
