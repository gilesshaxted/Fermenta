# Maldonado

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cooler Atlantic facing region near Punta del Este, known for Albariño and fresh wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Uruguay]]
> - **Part of:** [[South America]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** granite, clay, sand, schist
> - **Main grapes:** [[Albariño]], [[Tannat]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Viognier]], [[Syrah]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills and valleys near the coast, with granite outcrops and ocean views.

## Soil and geology
Granite and schist with clay and sand.

## Climate
Cool and breezy, influenced by the Atlantic, with a longer ripening season.

## History
A newer area for quality wine, developed from the late twentieth century with a focus on cooler climate styles and wine tourism.

## Production and wine styles
Fresh Albariño, Sauvignon Blanc and elegant reds, as well as sparkling wine.

## Key wines
- Maldonado Albariño
- Maldonado Tannat

## What to look for
- A cooler alternative to Canelones.
- Albariño is a signature white.

## Producers
- [[Alto de la Ballena]]
- [[Bodega Garzón]]
