# Damiano Ciolli

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small grower who put the local red grape Cesanese on the map.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Lazio]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cesanese]]

## About
A family estate in the hills of Olevano Romano, east of Rome. The wines show the spice and freshness of Cesanese, with a rustic charm.

## Known for
- Cirsium
- Cesanese di Olevano Romano Silene
- Cesanese di Olevano Romano
