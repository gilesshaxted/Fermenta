# Narince

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A fine, floral white from the Tokat area, with a delicate character that can age.

## Where it's from
Grown in Tokat and the Black Sea hinterland. The name suggests something delicate or fine.

## Where it grows
- **Tokat, Turkey:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Pear, citrus, white flowers and almond

## Classic wines
- Narince

## Pairs with

- Chicken
- Mezze

- Also used for sparkling wine.
