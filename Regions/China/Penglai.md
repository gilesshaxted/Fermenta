# Penglai

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A coastal district on the northern edge of the Shandong peninsula, with a cluster of wineries.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[China]]
> - **Part of:** [[Shandong]]
> - **Climate:** Warm continental
> - **Soils:** sandy loam, gravel, brown earth
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Cabernet Gernischt]], [[Merlot]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
Coastal slopes and plains facing the Bohai Sea, with a sea-cooled setting.

## Soil and geology
Sandy loam and gravel, with brown earth soils.

## Climate
Warm, with sea breezes and a monsoon summer, and milder winters than in the inland north-west.

## History
Penglai has developed as a wine centre in recent decades, with many new estates alongside older producers in the surrounding Yantai area.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon, Cabernet Gernischt and Merlot, with Chardonnay.

## Key wines
- Penglai Cabernet Sauvignon
- Penglai Chardonnay

## What to look for
- Sea air tempers the heat.
- A fast-growing area.
