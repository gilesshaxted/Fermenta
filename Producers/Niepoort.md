# Niepoort

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family house, led by Dirk Niepoort, known for Port and for fresh, elegant Douro table wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Portugal]]
> - **Region:** [[Douro]], [[Port]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Touriga Nacional]], [[Touriga Franca]], [[Tinta Barroca]], [[Tinto Cão]]
> - **Styles:** [[Port (style)]]

## About
Niepoort helped show that the Douro could make fine table wines as well as Port. The wines are typically restrained and energetic, with a focus on old vines and field blends.

## Known for
- Batuta
- Charme
- Redoma
- Vintage Port
