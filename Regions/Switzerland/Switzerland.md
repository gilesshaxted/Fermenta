# Switzerland

Part of [[Europe]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small, mountainous producer whose wines are mostly drunk at home, with unusual native grapes and a strong focus on Chasselas and Pinot Noir.

## How wine is organised here
- Wines are labelled by cantonal AOC, so the rules differ from canton to canton.
- Switzerland imports far more wine than it exports, so few bottles travel.
- Label names often give the grape and the place, for example Fendant from Valais.
