# Argentina

Part of [[South America]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The world's fifth largest producer, with high-altitude desert vineyards in the shadow of the Andes and Malbec as its flagship.

## How Argentine wine is organised
- **DOC** (Denominación de Origen Controlada) is the top tier, with Luján de Cuyo the first, but most wine is sold by region and grape.
- Altitude is the key to style: the higher the vineyard, the cooler and fresher the wine.
- **Reserva** and **Gran Reserva** indicate longer ageing, with rules varying by producer.
- Vineyards are irrigated with Andean meltwater.
