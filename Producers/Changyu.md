# Changyu

> [!abstract] At a glance
> China's oldest and largest wine company, from the coastal Yantai area of Shandong.

> - **Country:** [[China]]
> - **Region:** [[Shandong]], [[Penglai]]

## About
Changyu is a major commercial producer with vineyards and wineries across several regions, including Shandong, Ningxia and Xinjiang. It runs a range from everyday wines to showpiece chateaux bottlings.
