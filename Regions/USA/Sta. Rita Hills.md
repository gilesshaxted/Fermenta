# Sta. Rita Hills

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cool, windy corridor making intense, structured Pinot Noir and Chardonnay.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[Santa Barbara County]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** diatomaceous earth, limestone, sand, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Syrah]]

## Landscape and topography
A transverse valley between two ranges, open to the ocean on the west.

## Soil and geology
Sandy loam, diatomaceous earth and clay over marine sediments.

## Climate
Cool and windy, with fog and a long growing season.

## History
Became an AVA in 2001 and has attracted many producers since the 1970s.

## Production and wine styles
Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Syrah.

## Key wines
- Sta. Rita Hills Pinot Noir
- Sta. Rita Hills Chardonnay

## What to look for
- Cool-climate fruit, spice and acidity.
- The name is shortened, as Santa Rita is a Chilean trademark.
