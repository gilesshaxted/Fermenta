# Muscat

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Grapey and floral, in styles from dry to sweet and sparkling.

## Where it's from
One of the oldest known grape families, probably from the Eastern Mediterranean, with many relatives.

## Where it grows
- **Alsace, France:** dry and aromatic
- **Piedmont, Italy:** Moscato d'Asti, light and gently sparkling
- **Roussillon, France:** fortified Muscat de Rivesaltes
- **Rutherglen, Australia:** luscious fortified Muscat

## What it tastes like
- Orange blossom, grape, lychee and peach
- The only wine that really tastes of grapes

## Classic wines
- Moscato d'Asti
- Alsace Muscat
- Muscat de Beaumes-de-Venise
- Rutherglen Muscat

## Pairs with
- Fruit desserts
- Blue cheese (sweet styles)
- Spicy Asian food (dry styles)

## Good to know
- Moscato d'Asti is low in alcohol and lightly fizzy.
- Many different Muscat varieties exist.
