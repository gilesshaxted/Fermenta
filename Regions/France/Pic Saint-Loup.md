# Pic Saint-Loup

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cooler northern corner of the Languedoc with striking limestone peaks and fresh, peppery Syrah-led reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Languedoc]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, marl
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Grenache]], [[Mourvèdre]]

## Landscape and topography
Hillside vineyards beneath the Pic Saint-Loup and the Hortus ridge, north of Montpellier.

## Soil and geology
Limestone and clay slopes, with stony soils on higher ground.

## Climate
Mediterranean with cooler nights and a more continental feel.

## History
Awarded its own appellation in 2017, after years as a sub-zone of Coteaux du Languedoc.

## Production and wine styles
Red must contain Syrah plus Grenache and Mourvèdre. Rosé and white are also made.

## Key wines
- Pic Saint-Loup rouge
- Pic Saint-Loup rosé

## What to look for
- Peppery red fruit, violets and fresh acidity.
- Often a good alternative to northern Rhône Syrah.
