# Domaine Léon Barral

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A biodynamic Faugères domaine and one of the most respected names in Languedoc natural wine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Faugères]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Carignan]], [[Syrah]], [[Grenache]], [[Mourvèdre]], [[Cinsault]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]]

## About
A family estate making rich but wild reds from schist soils, with a focus on old vines and low intervention. The wines are deep, rustic and age well.

## Known for
- Faugères Jadis
- Faugères Valinière
- Faugères Blanc
