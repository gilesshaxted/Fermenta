# Château d'Esclans

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A pioneering producer of premium Provence rosé, famous for Whispering Angel.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Côtes de Provence]], [[Provence]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grenache]], [[Cinsault]], [[Syrah]]
> - **Styles:** [[Rosé]]

## About
An estate that helped to change the image of Provence rosé, with a range that runs from the hugely popular Whispering Angel to serious barrel fermented cuvées. The style is pale, dry and polished.

## Known for
- Whispering Angel
- Garrus
- Rock Angel
