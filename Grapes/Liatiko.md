# Liatiko

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A pale, early-ripening Cretan red that makes both dry wines and sweet wines.

## Where it's from
A very old grape of Crete, whose name means of July, after its ripening time.

## Where it grows
- **Crete, Greece:** Sitia and Heraklion

## What it tastes like
- Red cherry, herbs, spice and dried fruit

## Classic wines
- Sitia Liatiko
- Liatiko sweet wine

## Pairs with
- Roast lamb
- Cheese
- Desserts

## Good to know
- Ripens in July.
- Light and perfumed.
