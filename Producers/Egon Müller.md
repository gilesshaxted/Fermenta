# Egon Müller

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The Saar's most celebrated estate, owner of the Scharzhofberg and legendary for sweet Riesling.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Germany]]
> - **Region:** [[Saar and Ruwer]]

> - **Styles:** [[Noble rot wines]]

## About
A family estate at Wiltingen on the Saar, working the Scharzhofberg vineyard. The wines are pure and high in acidity, and the top sweet bottlings are among the most sought after in the world.
