# Grillo

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Sicilian white, once mainly used for Marsala and now bottled as a fresh dry wine.

## Where it's from
A cross of Catarratto and Zibibbo made in Sicily in the 19th century. It was long used in fortified Marsala.

## Where it grows
- **Western Sicily, Italy:** the heartland
- **Marsala, Italy:** for Marsala and dry wine

## What it tastes like
- Citrus, peach, herbs and salt

## Classic wines
- Grillo
- Marsala

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Tuna
- Couscous

## Good to know
- Resists heat well.
- A pleasant, easy white.
