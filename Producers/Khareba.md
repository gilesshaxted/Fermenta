# Khareba

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Kakheti winery famous for ageing wine in a former tunnel cut into the hillside.

## About
Based near Kvareli, it is a popular visitor stop as well as a sizeable wine producer. The wines span clean modern whites to Saperavi.
