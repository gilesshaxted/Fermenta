# Jean Foillard

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A leading Morgon grower, one of the four Beaujolais friends who shaped the modern natural wine movement.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Beaujolais Crus]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Gamay]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]], [[Carbonic maceration]]

## About
A small domaine in Morgon working old vines on the slopes of the Côte du Py, with minimal sulphur. The wines are vivid, mineral and age beautifully.

## Known for
- Morgon Côte du Py
- Morgon Corcelette
- Morgon Cuvée 3.14
