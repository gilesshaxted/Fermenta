# Catherine et Pierre Breton

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A well-known Bourgueil domaine that has been a driving force for natural Loire reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Loire Valley]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Chenin Blanc]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]]

## About
The Bretons farm organically and have long been admired for light, fragrant Cabernet Franc made with minimal intervention. They also make sparkling and Vouvray from Chenin Blanc.

## Known for
- Trinch!
- Nuits d'Ivresse
- Les Perrières
