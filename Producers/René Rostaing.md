# René Rostaing

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A respected Côte-Rôtie estate known for elegant, perfumed reds from the steep slopes above Ampuis.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Côte-Rôtie]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Viognier]]

## About
A family domaine working Côte-Rôtie vineyards, with a more restrained and floral approach than the powerful old school style. The wines are fragrant and fine-grained, and age very well.

## Known for
- Côte Blonde
- Côte Brune
- La Landonne
