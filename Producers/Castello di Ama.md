# Castello di Ama

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Chianti Classico estate known for single vineyard wines and a famous art collection in the grounds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Chianti Classico]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sangiovese]], [[Merlot]]

## About
Based in the hills of Gaiole in Chianti. The estate focuses on site specific Sangiovese and a celebrated Merlot led red. The style is polished, elegant and aromatic.

## Known for
- Chianti Classico Gran Selezione San Lorenzo
- L'Apparita
- Vigneto Bellavista
- Vigneto La Casuccia
