# Croatia

Part of [[Europe]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A varied wine country of Adriatic islands, Istrian hills and Slavonian plains, with a large collection of native grapes.

## How wine is organised here
- Wines are grouped into continental and coastal regions, each divided into sub-regions.
- Quality wines carry the region and grape on the label, with local names often used.
- Graševina is the local name for Welschriesling, and Malvazija Istarska and Plavac Mali are the most important native grapes.
