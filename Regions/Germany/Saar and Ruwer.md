# Saar and Ruwer

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Two tributaries of the Mosel with a cooler climate, producing steely, high-acid Riesling.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Germany]]
> - **Part of:** [[Mosel]]
> - **Climate:** Cool continental
> - **Soils:** slate, devonian slate
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]]

## Landscape and topography
Narrow, steep valleys feeding the Mosel, with exposed sites and cold air.

## Soil and geology
Grey and blue slate, with a thin layer of weathered soil.

## Climate
The coolest part of the Mosel, with years of poor ripeness, but spectacular in warm vintages.

## History
Saar wines were among the world's most expensive in the 19th century, and top names such as Scharzhofberg are still sought after.

## Production and wine styles
Riesling, often with a little residual sugar and a lot of acidity.

## Key wines
- Saar Riesling
- Scharzhofberg
- Ruwer Riesling

## What to look for
- Lime, green apple and a steely edge.
- Great with spicy Asian food.

- [[Egon Müller]]
