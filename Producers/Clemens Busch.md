# Clemens Busch

> [!abstract] At a glance
> An organic and biodynamic Mosel estate known for slate-driven, mostly dry Riesling from Pünderich.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Germany]]
> - **Region:** [[Mosel]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]]

## About
Working very steep slate terraces on the Marienburg slope with minimal intervention in the cellar. The wines are dry, mineral and textural, with a following among natural wine drinkers and classic Riesling lovers alike.

## Known for
- Marienburg
- Fahrlay
