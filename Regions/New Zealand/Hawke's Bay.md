# Hawke's Bay

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A warm east-coast region, New Zealand's top for Bordeaux-style reds and Syrah.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[New Zealand]]
> - **Part of:** [[Oceania]]
> - **Climate:** Maritime
> - **Soils:** gravel, alluvial, limestone, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Syrah]], [[Chardonnay]]
> - **Styles:** [[Bordeaux blend]]

## Landscape and topography
A coastal plain and river valleys, with vineyards on shingle terraces.

## Soil and geology
Stony river shingle, silt and limestone.

## Climate
Warm, sunny and dry, with a long growing season.

## History
One of the oldest regions, with Mission Vineyard founded in 1851.

## Production and wine styles
Merlot, Cabernet blends, Syrah, Chardonnay and others.

## Key wines
- Hawke's Bay Merlot blends
- Hawke's Bay Syrah
- Hawke's Bay Chardonnay

## What to look for
- Rich, structured reds.
- Fine Chardonnay.
