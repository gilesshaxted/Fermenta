# Muga

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family owned Haro bodega known for traditional methods, including its own cooperage and egg white fining.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Region:** [[Rioja Alta]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tempranillo]], [[Grenache]], [[Graciano]], [[Carignan]]

## About
Muga makes polished, structured reds in a traditional register, mixing American and French oak made in its own cooperage. The range runs from the everyday Reserva to ambitious single bottlings.

## Known for
- Prado Enea
- Torre Muga
- Aro
- Reserva
