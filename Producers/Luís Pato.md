# Luís Pato

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The great champion of Baga in Bairrada, making age worthy reds from old vines on sandy and clay soils.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Portugal]]
> - **Region:** [[Bairrada]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Baga]], [[Arinto]]

## About
A family estate that has kept faith with Baga when many others moved away. The wines are firm and savoury when young, and age very well.

## Known for
- Vinha Barrio
- Vinha Formal
- Vinha Pan
- Quinta do Ribeirinho Pé Franco
