# Essex

> [!abstract] At a glance
> England's driest corner, with a long growing season and a new generation of ambitious estates.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[England]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** clay, gravel, sand, chalk
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Bacchus]], [[Pinot Meunier]]
> - **Styles:** [[English sparkling wine]]

## Landscape and topography
Flat to gently rolling land in the east, near the coast and estuaries.

## Soil and geology
Clay, gravel and sand, with chalk in the north-west.

## Climate
Dry and sunny for England, though winters can be cold.

## History
Essex has become a fast-growing area, with several estates planting in the last twenty years.

## Production and wine styles
Traditional-method sparkling wine and a wide range of still styles.

## Key wines
- Essex sparkling
- Essex Bacchus

## What to look for
- Fresh, fruity whites.
- A rising region to watch.
