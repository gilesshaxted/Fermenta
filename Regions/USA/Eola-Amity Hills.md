# Eola-Amity Hills

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A windy range of hills that gives structured, energetic Pinot Noir.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[Willamette Valley]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** volcanic, basalt, marine sediment
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills that rise above the valley floor, cooled by wind through the Van Duzer Corridor.

## Soil and geology
Volcanic basalt and marine sedimentary soils.

## Climate
Cooler than much of the valley, with Pacific winds in the afternoons.

## History
Established as an AVA in 2006, with a growing set of boutique wineries.

## Production and wine styles
Pinot Noir and Chardonnay.

## Key wines
- Eola-Amity Hills Pinot Noir

## What to look for
- Firm structure with bright acidity.
- Wines tend to be more tense than Dundee.
