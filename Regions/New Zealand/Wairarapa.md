# Wairarapa

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small, cool, windy region at the southern tip of the North Island, best known for Martinborough Pinot Noir.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[New Zealand]]
> - **Part of:** [[Oceania]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** gravel, silt, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Syrah]]

## Landscape and topography
An open, flat plain sheltered by the Rimutaka ranges.

## Soil and geology
Free-draining gravels and silt.

## Climate
Cool, dry and windy, with a large temperature range.

## History
Martinborough vines were planted from 1978, and small estates built the reputation.

## Production and wine styles
Pinot Noir, Sauvignon Blanc, Chardonnay and Syrah.

## Key wines
- Martinborough Pinot Noir

## What to look for
- Savoury, structured Pinot.
- Small production.
