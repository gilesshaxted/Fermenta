# Južnoslovenská

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A southern wine region along the southern border with Hungary and the Danube plain.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Slovakia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Warm continental
> - **Soils:** loess, loam, sand, volcanic
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grüner Veltliner]], [[Welschriesling]], [[Müller-Thurgau]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Blaufränkisch]]

## Landscape and topography
Low hills and plains along the Danube and the border with Hungary, running from the south-west to the east.

## Soil and geology
Loess, loam and sand, with volcanic soils in the hills.

## Climate
Warm and dry, among the sunniest parts of the country.

## History
Winegrowing here is old, with a mixed Slovak and Hungarian tradition. The region is now home to both large and small producers.

## Production and wine styles
Mainly white wines, led by Grüner Veltliner, Welschriesling and Müller-Thurgau, plus Pinot Gris, Chardonnay and some reds.

## Key wines
- Južnoslovenská Grüner Veltliner
- Južnoslovenská Welschriesling

## What to look for
- Round, ripe whites.
- Close to the Hungarian border.
