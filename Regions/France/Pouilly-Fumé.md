# Pouilly-Fumé

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Smoky, flinty Sauvignon Blanc on the east bank of the Loire, facing Sancerre.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Loire Valley]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** flint, limestone, marl
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sauvignon Blanc]]

## Landscape and topography
Gentle slopes along the right bank around the town of Pouilly-sur-Loire.

## Soil and geology
Flint (silex) and limestone-marl, with the flint thought to give a gunflint smokiness.

## Climate
Continental, like neighbouring Sancerre.

## History
Not to be confused with Pouilly-Fuissé in the Mâconnais. The word Fumé is linked to the wine's smoky character, perhaps from the flint or the bloom on the grapes.

## Production and wine styles
100% Sauvignon Blanc, called Blanc Fumé locally. The simple Pouilly-sur-Loire wine is made from Chasselas.

## Key wines
- Pouilly-Fumé
- Pouilly-sur-Loire

## What to look for
- Often a little rounder and smokier than Sancerre.
- Flinty, gooseberry and citrus, with a long finish.
