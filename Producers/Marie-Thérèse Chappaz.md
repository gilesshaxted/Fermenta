# Marie-Thérèse Chappaz

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small Valais domaine, led by Marie-Thérèse Chappaz, famed for biodynamic whites and sweet wines from steep Fully terraces.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Switzerland]]
> - **Region:** [[Valais]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Petite Arvine]], [[Chasselas]]
> - **Styles:** [[Noble rot wines]]

## About
Based around Fully in the Valais, the domaine farms biodynamically on steep slopes. It is best known for Petite Arvine and for exceptionally fine sweet wines, with a precise, pure style.

- Petite Arvine Les Bans
- Grain Cinq
