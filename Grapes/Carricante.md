# Carricante

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Etna's great white grape, high in acidity and capable of ageing for years.

## Where it's from
Grown on the slopes of Mount Etna for centuries, at altitude on volcanic soil.

## Where it grows
- **Etna, Italy:** the heartland, up to 1,000 metres

## What it tastes like
- Lemon, green apple, salt and mineral

## Classic wines
- Etna Bianco
- Etna Bianco Superiore

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Sicilian cuisine
- Shellfish

## Good to know
- Often compared with Riesling.
- Etna Bianco Superiore must come from Milo.
