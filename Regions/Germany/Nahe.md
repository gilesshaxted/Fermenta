# Nahe

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small, geologically diverse region on a tributary of the Rhine, with elegant Riesling.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Germany]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Temperate
> - **Soils:** volcanic rock, slate, quartzite, loam, sandstone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]], [[Pinot Blanc]], [[Müller-Thurgau]]

## Landscape and topography
A winding valley with a mix of hills, and some of Germany's most diverse soils.

## Soil and geology
Volcanic rock, slate, quartzite and loam, sometimes within metres of each other.

## Climate
Mild and relatively dry, sheltered by the Hunsrück hills.

## History
Often seen as a link between the Mosel's elegance and the Rheingau's richness. Dönnhoff and Emrich-Schönleber are important names.

## Production and wine styles
Mostly Riesling, from dry to sweet.

## Key wines
- Nahe Riesling Grosses Gewächs
- Niederhäuser Hermannshöhle
- Schlossböckelheimer Kupfergrube

## What to look for
- Citrus, stone fruit and a mineral spine.
- Excellent value.

## Producers
- [[Dönnhoff]]
- [[Schäfer-Fröhlich]]
