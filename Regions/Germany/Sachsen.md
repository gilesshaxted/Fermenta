# Sachsen

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Germany's most easterly wine region, with steep terraces on the Elbe near Dresden.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Germany]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** granite, loess, gneiss, porphyry
> - **Main grapes:** [[Müller-Thurgau]], [[Pinot Blanc]], [[Riesling]], [[Gewürztraminer|Traminer]]

## Landscape and topography
Terraced vineyards along the Elbe, between Dresden and Meissen.

## Soil and geology
Granite, loess and gneiss, with porphyry soils.

## Climate
Cool, continental, with frosts and extreme conditions, though sheltered on the slopes.

## History
Saxon wine culture dates to the 12th century, and the vineyards were revived after reunification.

## Production and wine styles
Müller-Thurgau, Weissburgunder, Riesling and Traminer.

## Key wines
- Sachsen Weissburgunder
- Sachsen Traminer

## What to look for
- Crisp whites with a fine acid line.
- Small production.
