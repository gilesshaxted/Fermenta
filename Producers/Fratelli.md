# Fratelli

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Maharashtra producer with an Italian-Indian partnership, known for Sangiovese-led reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[India]]
> - **Region:** [[Maharashtra]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sangiovese]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## About
Fratelli grows its vineyards in the Akluj area of Maharashtra, away from the more famous Nashik belt. The style leans towards Italian varieties alongside international grapes.

- Sette
- Fratelli Sangiovese
