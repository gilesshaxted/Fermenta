# Domaine des Ardoisières

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Cevins estate working steep, terraced slate vineyards in the Alps.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Savoie]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer

## About
The estate farms with a focus on healthy soils and makes wines with a clear sense of place. The whites are mineral and fresh, the reds light and spicy.
