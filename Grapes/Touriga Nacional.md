# Touriga Nacional

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Portugal's finest red grape: dark, perfumed and structured.

## Where it's from
Portugal, probably from the Dão region. Low yields and tiny berries give concentration.

## Where it grows
- **Douro, Portugal:** for Port and dry reds
- **Dão, Portugal:** elegant, floral versions
- **Alentejo, Portugal:** riper, richer wines

## What it tastes like
- Blackberry, violet and wild herbs
- Firm tannin and ageing potential

## Classic wines
- Vintage Port
- Douro reds
- Dão Reserva

## Pairs with
- Roast lamb
- Grilled meats
- Aged cheese

## Good to know
- Often blended with Touriga Franca and Tinta Roriz.
- Gaining fans in Australia and South Africa.
