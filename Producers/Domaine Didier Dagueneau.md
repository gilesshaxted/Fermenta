# Domaine Didier Dagueneau

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The most celebrated name in Pouilly-Fumé, known for intense and age-worthy Sauvignon Blanc.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Pouilly-Fumé]], [[Loire Valley]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer

## About
Didier Dagueneau transformed expectations for Sauvignon Blanc with low yields, biodynamic farming and meticulous winemaking. The wines are concentrated, mineral and complex, and the domaine continues his work.

- Silex
