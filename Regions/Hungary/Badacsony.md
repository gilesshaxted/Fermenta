# Badacsony

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A volcanic hill beside Lake Balaton, producing full, mineral whites.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Hungary]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** basalt, volcanic, sandstone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Welschriesling]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Furmint]]

## Landscape and topography
A basalt mesa on the north shore of Lake Balaton, with terraced vineyards facing the lake.

## Soil and geology
Basalt, volcanic tuff and sandstone.

## Climate
Warm continental, moderated by Lake Balaton.

## History
Winemaking around Lake Balaton dates to Roman times. Badacsony is the best known of the Balaton wine districts.

## Production and wine styles
White wines dominate. Olaszrizling, Szürkebarát (Pinot Gris) and native Kéknyelű are the key grapes.

## Key wines
- Badacsony Olaszrizling
- Badacsony Szürkebarát
- Badacsony Kéknyelű

## What to look for
- Rich and mineral whites.
- Look for Kéknyelű, a rare local grape.
