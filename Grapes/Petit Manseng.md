# Petit Manseng

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Small berries, thick skins and piercing acidity, ideal for sweet wine.

## Where it's from
The Pyrenean foothills of South-West France.

## Where it grows
- **Jurançon, France:** dried on the vine for sweet wine
- **Virginia, USA:** a rising star

## What it tastes like
- Tropical fruit, ginger and honey with high acidity

## Classic wines
- Jurançon moelleux
- Pacherenc du Vic-Bilh

## Pairs with
- Foie gras
- Blue cheese
- Spicy dishes

## Good to know
- Passerillage, drying on the vine, concentrates the sugars.
- Acidity keeps sweet wines fresh.
