> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small, low intervention estate in the Valle de Guadalupe, known for fresh, mineral wines.

## About
Mogor Badan is a family estate, run with a gentle, organic-leaning approach. Its wines are lighter and more savoury than many Baja neighbours.
