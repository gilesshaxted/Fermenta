# Vinho Verde

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Fresh, slightly spritzy whites from green, rainy northwest Portugal.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Portugal]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Maritime
> - **Soils:** granite, schist, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Albariño|Alvarinho]], [[Loureiro]], [[Arinto]], [[Trajadura]]

## Landscape and topography
A lush, hilly northwest region near the Atlantic, with vines grown high on pergolas or trained on trees.

## Soil and geology
Granite-based soils, thin and acidic.

## Climate
Cool and wet, with Atlantic influence and the highest rainfall in Portugal.

## History
Verde means green, referring to the young, fresh style, not colour. Alvarinho in Monção e Melgaço is a high-quality sub-region.

## Production and wine styles
Light, low-alcohol whites from Loureiro, Arinto and others, sometimes with a slight fizz. Alvarinho is richer. Some red and rosé exist.

## Key wines
- Vinho Verde
- Monção e Melgaço Alvarinho
- Loureiro
- Vinho Verde rosé

## What to look for
- Citrus, green apple and a spritz.
- Alvarinho is richer and more complex.
