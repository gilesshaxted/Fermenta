# Bohemia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small, cooler region in the north, around Mělník and Litoměřice, known for light whites and Pinot Noir.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Czechia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Cool continental
> - **Soils:** loam, clay, sandstone, marl
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Müller-Thurgau]], [[Riesling]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]]

## Landscape and topography
Gentle hills on the Elbe and Vltava rivers, north of Prague, with the main vineyards around Mělník and Litoměřice.

## Soil and geology
Loam, clay, sandstone and marl.

## Climate
Cooler than Moravia, with cold winters and moderate summers, giving fresh, lighter wines.

## History
Wine was grown here in the Middle Ages and the region is linked with Czech royalty. Today it has a small output, with a focus on fresh whites and Pinot Noir.

## Production and wine styles
Light, fresh whites such as Müller-Thurgau, Riesling and Pinot Gris, and some elegant Pinot Noir.

## Key wines
- Mělník wines
- Litoměřice wines
- Bohemian Pinot Noir

## What to look for
- Fresh whites and light reds.
- Small, local production.
