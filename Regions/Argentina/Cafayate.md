# Cafayate

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A high-altitude valley, heart of Argentina's Torrontés.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Argentina]]
> - **Part of:** [[Salta]]
> - **Climate:** Arid continental
> - **Soils:** sand, alluvial
> - **Main grapes:** [[Torrontés]], [[Malbec]], [[Tannat]]

## Landscape and topography
A valley at about 1,700 to 2,000 metres, ringed by dramatic red rock formations.

## Soil and geology
Sandy alluvial soils.

## Climate
Very dry and sunny.

## History
A long wine town, and still home to some old family estates.

## Production and wine styles
Torrontés, Malbec and Tannat.

## Key wines
- Cafayate Torrontés

## What to look for
- Rose, peach and citrus.
- Great with spicy food.
