# Dimiat

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A widely planted Bulgarian white, making light, fresh wines and often used for brandy.

## Where it's from
One of Bulgaria's best known native white grapes, grown along the Black Sea and in the Thracian Valley. It gives light, easy wines with mild aromas.

## Where it grows
- **Black Sea coast, Bulgaria:** the main area
- **Thracian Valley, Bulgaria:** also grown

## What it tastes like
- Apple, pear, citrus and floral

## Classic wines
- Black Sea Dimiat

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Salads
- Light starters

## Good to know
- Also called Dimyat.
- Often used in brandy.
