# Louis Bovard

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A historic Lavaux family domaine in Cully, known for Chasselas from Dézaley.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Switzerland]]
> - **Region:** [[Lavaux]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chasselas]]

## About
Working in the terraced Lavaux vineyards above Lake Geneva, the domaine specialises in Chasselas. The wines show the minerality and ageing potential of the best Lavaux sites.

## Known for
- La Médinette Dézaley
- Louis Bovard Dézaley Chasselas
