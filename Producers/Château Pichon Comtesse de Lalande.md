# Château Pichon Comtesse de Lalande

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Pauillac Second Growth known for a softer, more perfumed and Merlot-influenced style.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]

> - **Type:** Notable producer

> - **Styles:** [[Bordeaux blend]]

## About
Pichon Comtesse stands on the border with Saint-Julien, and its wines often show more silky fruit and charm than many neighbours. They still age very well.
