# Domaine François Raveneau

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Chablis family estate regarded as one of the greats of the appellation.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Chablis]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]]

## About
Raveneau ferments and ages its wines in mostly old oak, which gives depth without losing precision. The premiers and grands crus are steely and age for decades.

## Known for
- Chablis Montée de Tonnerre
- Chablis Butteaux
- Chablis Valmur
