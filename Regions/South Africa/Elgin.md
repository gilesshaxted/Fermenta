# Elgin

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cool, high plateau with orchards and vineyards, well suited to crisp whites and cool Syrah.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[South Africa]]
> - **Part of:** [[Africa]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** shale, sandstone, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Syrah]]

## Landscape and topography
A high plateau at about 200 to 300 metres, ringed by mountains.

## Soil and geology
Shale and sandstone with clay.

## Climate
The coolest region in the Cape winelands, with more rain and fog.

## History
Known for apples before vines, Elgin's modern wines began in the 1990s.

## Production and wine styles
Sauvignon Blanc, Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and Syrah.

## Key wines
- Elgin Sauvignon Blanc
- Elgin Chardonnay

## What to look for
- Crisp, high-acid wines.
- Good for sparkling.
