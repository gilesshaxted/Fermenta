# Gamaret

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A modern Swiss cross, giving deep coloured, peppery red wines.

## Where it's from
Bred in Switzerland in 1970 as a cross of Gamay and Reichensteiner, and now planted in Geneva and Vaud.

## Where it grows
- **Geneva, Switzerland:** a leading grape
- **Vaud, Switzerland:** also grown

## What it tastes like
- Blackcurrant, pepper, violet and spice

## Classic wines
- Gamaret

## Pairs with
- Roast meat
- Charcuterie
- Hard cheese

## Good to know
- Resists rot well.
- Often blended with Garanoir.
