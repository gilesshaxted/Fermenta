# Nikolaihof

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of Europe's oldest biodynamic estates, making long-aged Wachau wines at Mautern.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Austria]]
> - **Region:** [[Wachau]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grüner Veltliner]], [[Riesling]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]]

## About
A historic family estate with a long commitment to organic and biodynamic farming. The wines are often released late after long ageing on the lees in large old casks.
