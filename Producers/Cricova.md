# Cricova

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A famous Moldovan sparkling wine house, with miles of underground limestone cellars near Chișinău.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Moldova]]
> - **Region:** [[Codru]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]
> - **Styles:** [[Traditional method]]

## About
The cellars are old limestone mines that give steady cool temperatures, and the house is best known for traditional method sparkling wine. It also makes still wines from international varieties and local grapes.

## Known for
- Cricova Brut
- Cricova Cuvée Prestige
