# Prekmurje

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A flat, sunny corner of the country by the Hungarian border, with whites and some softer reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Slovenia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Podravje]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** sand, clay, loess
> - **Main grapes:** [[Welschriesling]], [[Furmint]], [[Blaufränkisch]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Gris]]

## Landscape and topography
Gently rolling hills and plains on the Pannonian side, east of the Mura river.

## Soil and geology
Sand, clay and loess.

## Climate
Warmer and drier than the west, with a Pannonian climate.

## History
A traditional region with a long history of white wine, with a number of modern producers improving quality. It shares grapes with neighbouring Hungary and Austria.

## Production and wine styles
Aromatic whites from Welschriesling, Furmint and Chardonnay, with some reds including Blaufränkisch.

## Key wines
- Prekmurje white
- Prekmurje Blaufränkisch

## What to look for
- Soft, fruity whites.
- Good value.
