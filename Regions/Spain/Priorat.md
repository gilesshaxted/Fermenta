# Priorat

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Steep terraces of dark slate producing intense, mineral red from old vines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** llicorella, slate, quartz
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grenache|Garnacha]], [[Carignan|Cariñena]], [[Syrah]]

## Landscape and topography
Very steep hillsides and terraces in the mountains of Tarragona, at 100 to 700 metres.

## Soil and geology
Llicorella, a reddish-black slate with quartz, which forces vine roots deep.

## Climate
Hot, dry Mediterranean with big daily swings and very low rainfall.

## History
Carthusian monks founded the priory in the 12th century. In the late 1980s René Barbier and others revived it. It is one of only two DOQs in Spain.

## Production and wine styles
Old-vine Garnacha and Cariñena, blended with Syrah and Cabernet. Yields are tiny.

## Key wines
- Priorat
- Priorat Vi de Vila
- Priorat Gran Reserva
- L'Ermita (Álvaro Palacios)

## What to look for
- Black cherry, liquorice, slate and spice.
- Powerful, but the best are balanced and mineral.
