# Xinomavro

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Greece's noble red: high in acid and tannin, often compared with Nebbiolo.

## Where it's from
Northern Greece. Its name means sour black.

## Where it grows
- **Naoussa, Greece:** the best-known appellation
- **Amyndeon, Greece:** cooler zone, also sparkling

## What it tastes like
- Red cherry, dried tomato, olive and spice

## Classic wines
- Naoussa
- Amyndeon sparkling
- Goumenissa

## Pairs with
- Lamb
- Roast meats
- Aged cheese

## Good to know
- Needs time to soften.
- Pale in colour but firm in structure.
