# Saumur

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Tufa caves and limestone producing sparkling Chenin and Cabernet Franc reds of lift and perfume.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Loire Valley]]
> - **Climate:** Maritime
> - **Soils:** tuffeau, limestone, clay, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chenin Blanc]], [[Cabernet Franc]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling hills on the south bank of the Loire around the town of Saumur, with troglodyte cellars carved into the cliffs.

## Soil and geology
Soft white tuffeau limestone with clay and sand, easy to excavate for cellars.

## Climate
Maritime, with warmer, drier conditions than further west.

## History
Saumur is a long-established centre for sparkling wine made by the traditional method, with cellars cut into the rock.

## Production and wine styles
Sparkling wine (Crémant de Loire and Saumur Brut) from Chenin Blanc, still Chenin whites, and red Saumur-Champigny from Cabernet Franc.

## Key wines
- Saumur-Champigny
- Saumur Brut
- Crémant de Loire
- Saumur Blanc

## What to look for
- Saumur-Champigny is raspberry-fresh and gravelly.
- Sparkling Saumur is a good alternative to Champagne, often at friendlier prices.
