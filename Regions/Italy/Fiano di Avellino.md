# Fiano di Avellino

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of southern Italy's great whites: nutty, waxy and honeyed, and able to age.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Campania]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** volcanic, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Fiano]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills of Irpinia, at 400 to 600 metres, near Avellino.

## Soil and geology
Volcanic ash and clay.

## Climate
Cooler than the coast, with Apennine influence.

## History
Fiano, said to be the 'Apiana' praised by the Romans, was revived by Mastroberardino.

## Production and wine styles
100% Fiano, in stainless steel or with some oak.

## Key wines
- Fiano di Avellino

## What to look for
- Hazelnut, pear, honey and herbs.
- A lovely white for white meats and shellfish.

- [[Feudi di San Gregorio]]
- [[Pietracupa]]
