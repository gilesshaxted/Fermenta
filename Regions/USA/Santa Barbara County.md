# Santa Barbara County

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Unusual east-west valleys that funnel Pacific air inland, producing refined Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Syrah.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[North America]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** limestone, diatomaceous earth, clay, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Syrah]], [[Grenache]]

## Landscape and topography
Valleys that run east to west, unlike most of California's coast ranges, so ocean air flows in directly.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, sand and clay, with diatomaceous earth in places.

## Climate
Cool near the coast and warmer inland.

## History
Plantings in the 1970s proved the area's cool-climate potential, and the film Sideways boosted Pinot Noir here in 2004.

## Production and wine styles
Pinot Noir and Chardonnay in the west, Syrah and Rhône varieties inland.

## Key wines
- Sta. Rita Hills Pinot Noir
- Santa Maria Valley Chardonnay
- Santa Ynez Syrah

## What to look for
- Bright, structured cool-climate wines.
- Several AVAs, including Santa Maria Valley and Santa Ynez Valley.
