# Uco Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A high-altitude valley south of Mendoza that makes fresh, mineral wines and leads the region's newest quality drive.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Argentina]]
> - **Part of:** [[Mendoza]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** alluvial, limestone, stony, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Malbec]], [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]]

## Landscape and topography
A broad valley at up to 1,500 metres or more, in the shadow of the Andes.

## Soil and geology
Limestone-rich and stony alluvial soils with varied textures.

## Climate
Cool nights, intense sunshine and a long growing season because of altitude.

## History
Developed in the last 30 years, with sub-zones such as Gualtallary, Altamira and Los Chacayes.

## Production and wine styles
Malbec, Cabernet Franc, Chardonnay and Sauvignon Blanc.

## Key wines
- Gualtallary Malbec
- Altamira Malbec
- Uco Valley Chardonnay

## What to look for
- Violet, fresh fruit and mineral edge.
- More restrained than Luján de Cuyo.
