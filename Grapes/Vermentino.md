# Vermentino

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Zesty and herbal with a salty edge. Made for coastal food.

## Where it's from
Probably Iberian in origin, now grown mainly around the Mediterranean coast.

## Where it grows
- **Sardinia and Liguria, Italy:** classic coastal whites
- **Corsica and Provence, France:** known as Rolle
- **Languedoc, France:** growing plantings

## What it tastes like
- Lime, green apple, herbs and sea breeze

## Classic wines
- Vermentino di Sardegna
- Vermentino di Gallura
- Provence blanc

## Pairs with
- Grilled fish
- Pesto
- Seafood pasta

## Good to know
- Called Rolle in Provence.
- Best drunk young and cool.
