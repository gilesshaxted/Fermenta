# Domaine de la Pépière

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A leading Muscadet estate that showed how mineral and long-lived the grape can be.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Muscadet]], [[Loire Valley]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer

## About
La Pépière is a family domaine in Muscadet Sèvre et Maine making wine on lees with a focus on single sites. The wines are saline, lively and far more serious than most people expect from Muscadet.
