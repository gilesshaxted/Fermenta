# Domaine Alain Graillot

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of the benchmark producers of Crozes-Hermitage, known for fresh, pure Syrah.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Crozes-Hermitage]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Marsanne]], [[Roussanne]]

## About
A family domaine that showed how fine Crozes-Hermitage could be, with fruity, peppery and elegantly structured reds. The wines are made with a light touch and are very drinkable young.

## Known for
- Crozes-Hermitage
- La Guiraude
- Crozes-Hermitage Blanc
