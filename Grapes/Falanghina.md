# Falanghina

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Fresh, floral and citrusy, with a salty, mineral edge. A southern Italian white for seafood.

## Where it's from
Campania in southern Italy, an ancient local grape.

## Where it grows
- **Campi Flegrei, Campania:** volcanic soils, mineral and floral
- **Sannio, Campania:** the bigger area, Falanghina Beneventana
- **Molise, Italy:** fresh, everyday styles

## What it tastes like
- **Fruit:** lemon, white peach, apple, pear
- **Floral:** white flowers, herbs
- **Savoury:** almond, a salty mineral edge

## Classic wines
- Falanghina del Sannio
- Campi Flegrei Falanghina
- Falerno del Massico Bianco

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Grilled fish
- Mozzarella di bufala
- Pasta with clams
- Pizza

## Good to know
- Two distinct types are grown: Flegrea (Campi Flegrei) and Beneventana (Sannio).
- Best drunk young and fresh.
