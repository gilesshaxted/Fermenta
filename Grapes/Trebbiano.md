# Trebbiano

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Italy's most widely planted white grape, neutral and fresh, with many local forms.

## Where it's from
Grown across central and northern Italy for centuries. The best known form is Trebbiano Toscano, which is the same as France's Ugni Blanc. Other types such as Trebbiano d'Abruzzo and Trebbiano di Lugana are distinct.

## Where it grows
- **Tuscany and Umbria, Italy:** a blending grape in Orvieto and Vin Santo
- **Abruzzo, Italy:** Trebbiano d'Abruzzo is the finest expression
- **Emilia-Romagna, Italy:** large plantings for everyday wine

## What it tastes like
- Lemon, green apple, almond and herbs

## Classic wines
- Trebbiano d'Abruzzo
- Orvieto blends
- Vin Santo

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Light pasta
- Salads

## Good to know
- Often neutral and high yielding, so quality varies widely.
- Trebbiano d'Abruzzo can age surprisingly well.
- Ugni Blanc is a close relative of the Toscano type.
