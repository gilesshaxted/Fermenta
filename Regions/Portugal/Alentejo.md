# Alentejo

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A vast, sunny southern plain with rich, soft reds and a growing number of seriously good estates.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Portugal]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** schist, granite, limestone, clay, marble
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tempranillo|Aragonez]], [[Trincadeira]], [[Alicante Bouschet]], [[Touriga Nacional]], [[Antão Vaz]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling plains with cork oak trees, stretching across the south of Portugal, with sub-regions such as Borba, Évora and Vidigueira.

## Soil and geology
Granite, schist, limestone and clay, with marble in the Vila Viçosa area.

## Climate
Hot and dry Mediterranean, with strong sun and little rain. Higher areas such as Portalegre are cooler.

## History
Romans made wine in clay pots (talhas), and some producers still do. Modern investment since the 1990s has brought quality.

## Production and wine styles
Aragonez (Tempranillo), Trincadeira, Alicante Bouschet and Touriga Nacional for reds, Antão Vaz and Arinto for whites.

## Key wines
- Alentejo tinto
- Alentejo branco
- Vinho de Talha

## What to look for
- Ripe plum and spice, soft and generous.
- Strong values.
