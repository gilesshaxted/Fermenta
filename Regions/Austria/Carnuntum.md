# Carnuntum

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A warm region east of Vienna, known for red wines from Zweigelt and Blaufränkisch.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Austria]]
> - **Part of:** [[Lower Austria]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** loess, gravel, sand, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Zweigelt]], [[Blaufränkisch]], [[Grüner Veltliner]]

## Landscape and topography
Gently rolling land and low hills along the Danube near the Slovak border.

## Soil and geology
Loess, gravel and limestone.

## Climate
Warm, with Pannonian influence.

## History
Named after the Roman city of Carnuntum, once the capital of Pannonia.

## Production and wine styles
Zweigelt and Blaufränkisch for reds, with Grüner Veltliner for whites.

## Key wines
- Carnuntum Zweigelt
- Carnuntum Rubin Carnuntum

## What to look for
- Cherry, spice and soft fruit.
- A solid red wine region.
