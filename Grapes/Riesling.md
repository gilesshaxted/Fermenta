# Riesling

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Racy acidity with lime, apple and blossom. Anything from bone dry to lusciously sweet, and wonderful with age.

## Where it's from
Germany, in the Rhine region. Widely regarded as one of the finest white grapes.

## Where it grows
- **Mosel, Germany:** light, delicate, often off-dry
- **Rheingau and Pfalz, Germany:** richer and more structured
- **Alsace, France:** usually dry and weighty
- **Clare Valley and Eden Valley, Australia:** bone dry, limey
- **Wachau, Austria:** dry and powerful, Smaragd level

## What it tastes like
- **Young:** lime, green apple, white flowers
- **Ripe styles:** peach, apricot, honey
- **With age:** petrol, honey, toast

## Classic wines
- Mosel Kabinett and Spätlese
- Alsace Grand Cru
- Clare Valley Riesling
- Wachau Smaragd

## Pairs with
- Spicy Asian food
- Pork
- Smoked fish
- Sushi
- Sausages

## Good to know
- Sweetness varies and the label may not say. On German bottles, Trocken means dry.
- A petrol note in older Riesling is normal and often prized.
