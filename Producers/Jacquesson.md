# Jacquesson

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Dizy house admired for its numbered Cuvée blends and its single-vineyard Champagnes.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Champagne]], [[Vallée de la Marne]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Pinot Meunier]]
> - **Styles:** [[Champagne (style)]]

## About
Jacquesson works with low dosage and treats its non-vintage as a 'cuvée' that changes in number each year to reflect the base vintage. The wines are precise, savoury and age well.

- Avize Champ Caïn
- Dizy Corne Bautray
- Aÿ Vauzelle Terme
