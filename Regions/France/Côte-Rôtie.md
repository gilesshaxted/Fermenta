# Côte-Rôtie

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Dramatic terraced slopes above the river producing perfumed, silky Syrah, sometimes co-fermented with a little Viognier.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Rhône Valley]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** schist, granite, gneiss
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Viognier]]

## Landscape and topography
Extremely steep south-east facing terraces just south of Vienne, with the vines held by dry-stone walls.

## Soil and geology
Schist (the Côte Brune) and lighter granite and gneiss (the Côte Blonde).

## Climate
Continental, with sun on the slopes and cool nights that help the wine keep its perfume.

## History
A Roman vineyard whose name means roasted slope. It slipped in the 20th century and was revived by Guigal's single-vineyard wines.

## Production and wine styles
Syrah with up to 20% Viognier, which can be co-fermented to brighten the colour and lift the aromatics.

## Key wines
- Côte-Rôtie
- Guigal La Mouline, La Landonne and La Turque

## What to look for
- Violets, bacon fat, black pepper and raspberry.
- Elegant rather than heavy, and ages for decades.
