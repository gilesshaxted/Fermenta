# Tikveš

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The country's best known wine district around Kavadarci, a hot, sunny basin sheltered by mountains.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[North Macedonia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Povardarie]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** clay, sand, gravel, granite
> - **Main grapes:** [[Vranac]], [[Žilavka]], [[Kratošija]], [[Merlot]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Smederevka]]

## Landscape and topography
A basin at the confluence of the Crna and Vardar rivers, sheltered by mountains, around the town of Kavadarci.

## Soil and geology
Clay, sand and gravel, with some granite.

## Climate
Hot, dry summers with Mediterranean influence from the south and cool nights in autumn, with long sun hours.

## History
Tikveš has produced wine since ancient times. After the end of Yugoslavia, Tikveš and the surrounding area became the centre of the country's wine export business.

## Production and wine styles
Dark reds from Vranec, Kratošija and Merlot, and whites from Žilavka, Smederevka and Chardonnay.

## Key wines
- Tikveš Vranec
- Tikveš Žilavka
- Tikveš Kratošija

## What to look for
- Vranec is the signature red.
- Ripe fruit and spice.
