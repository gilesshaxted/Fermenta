# Joseph Drouhin

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Beaune family house with holdings stretching from Chablis to the Côte de Nuits.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Côte de Beaune]], [[Burgundy]]

## About
Drouhin farms its vineyards organically and biodynamically and ages the wines in historic cellars. The style is elegant and balanced.
