# Tyrrell's

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family-owned Hunter Valley winery with a long record for age-worthy Sémillon.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Australia]]
> - **Region:** [[Hunter Valley]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sémillon]], [[Syrah]], [[Chardonnay]]

## About
One of the oldest family estates in the region, with old vines and a traditional feel. The Sémillons are low in alcohol, lean when young and complex with age.
