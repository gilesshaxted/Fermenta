# Vidiano

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Cretan white revived from near extinction, with body and aromatic lift.

## Where it's from
Rescued in the 1990s, it has become Crete's flagship white.

## Where it grows
- **Crete, Greece:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Peach, apricot, herbs and citrus

## Classic wines
- Crete Vidiano

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Poultry
- Cretan cheese

## Good to know
- Compared with Viognier.
- Ages well.
