# Israel

Part of [[Asia]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> An ancient wine land with a modern, quality-driven industry, from cool Golan vineyards to desert plantings in the Negev.

## How wine is organised here
- Israel has five official wine regions: Galilee, Shomron, Shimshon, Judean Hills and Negev. Golan Heights and Upper Galilee are sub-regions of the Galilee.
- Kosher is a production standard rather than a place: kosher wines are made under rabbinical supervision, and many Israeli wines are kosher.
- Labels usually name the winery, the grape or blend, and the region.
