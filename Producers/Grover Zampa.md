# Grover Zampa

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of India's longest-established fine wine houses, based near Bangalore.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[India]]
> - **Region:** [[Nandi Hills]], [[Karnataka]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Syrah]]
> - **Styles:** [[Bordeaux blend]]

## About
The estate sits in the Nandi Hills area of Karnataka and has long worked with French winemaking advice. The reds are the calling card, often built on Cabernet and Shiraz and given oak ageing.

## Known for
- La Réserve
- Chêne Grand Réserve
- Vijay Amritraj Reserve
