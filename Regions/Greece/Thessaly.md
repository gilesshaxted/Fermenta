# Thessaly

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The sunny Thessalian plain and the slopes of Mount Olympus, home of Rapsani, a blend of three local grapes.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Greece]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, schist, granite
> - **Main grapes:** [[Xinomavro]], [[Krasato]], [[Stavroto]], [[Roditis]]

## Landscape and topography
A large plain surrounded by mountains, with hilly vineyards on the slopes of Mount Olympus.

## Soil and geology
Clay, limestone, schist and granite.

## Climate
Continental, with cold winters and hot summers.

## History
Rapsani, a blend of Xinomavro, Krasato and Stavroto, was among the first Greek appellations, and one of the few designations for a field blend.

## Production and wine styles
Reds and whites, with Rapsani as the standout.

## Key wines
- Rapsani
- Anchialos
- Thessalian Roditis

## What to look for
- Savoury reds with herb and spice.
- A small but interesting area.
