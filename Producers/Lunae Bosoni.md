# Lunae Bosoni

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family estate on the Ligurian border with Tuscany, known for sophisticated Vermentino.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Liguria]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Vermentino]]

## About
The Bosoni family works in the Colli di Luni, close to the sea. The Vermentino is aromatic and saline with surprising weight.

## Known for
- Vermentino Colli di Luni Etichetta Nera
- Vermentino Cavagino
- Numero Chiuso
