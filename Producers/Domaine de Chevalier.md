# Domaine de Chevalier

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A classic Pessac-Léognan estate prized for both elegant reds and age-worthy dry whites.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Pessac-Léognan]], [[Bordeaux]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Sémillon]]
> - **Styles:** [[Bordeaux blend]]

## About
Domaine de Chevalier is set in the pine forest south of Bordeaux. Its whites are among the most respected dry whites of the region, and the reds are fine and lightly structured.

- Domaine de Chevalier Rouge
- Domaine de Chevalier Blanc
