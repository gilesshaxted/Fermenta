# Aglianico

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Dark, tannic and ageworthy. Often called the Barolo of the south.

## Where it's from
Southern Italy, probably brought by the Greeks. The name may come from Hellenic.

## Where it grows
- **Taurasi, Campania:** cool, high hills
- **Vulture, Basilicata:** volcanic slopes

## What it tastes like
- Black cherry, tar, smoke and iron

## Classic wines
- Taurasi
- Aglianico del Vulture
- Aglianico del Taburno

## Pairs with
- Roast lamb
- Braised beef
- Aged pecorino

## Good to know
- Ripens late, often in November.
- Good ones age for decades.
