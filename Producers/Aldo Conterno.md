# Aldo Conterno

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Monforte d'Alba estate with a name for powerful, long lived Bussia Barolo.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Barolo]], [[Piedmont]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Nebbiolo]], [[Barbera]], [[Chardonnay]]

## About
A family estate working in the Bussia area, with Barolo that mixes tradition with a measured use of oak. The top wines are structured and need time.

## Known for
- Barolo Bussia Soprana
- Barolo Granbussia
- Barolo Cicala
