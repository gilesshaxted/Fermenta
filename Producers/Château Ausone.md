# Château Ausone

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A tiny Saint-Émilion estate on the limestone slope, famed for fine, mineral and long-lived reds.

> - **Country:** [[France]]

## About
Ausone is a small, top-ranked estate with vineyards on the limestone plateau and slopes below the town. The wine is tight and elegant when young and ages superbly.
