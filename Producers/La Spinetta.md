# La Spinetta

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family estate known for single vineyard Barbaresco and a benchmark Moscato d'Asti.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Barbaresco]], [[Asti]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Nebbiolo]], [[Barbera]]
> - **Styles:** [[Asti and Moscato d'Asti]]

## About
Started in the Asti area and now with sites in Barbaresco, the family makes ambitious reds as well as sweet, lightly sparkling Moscato. The style is modern, polished and richly fruited.

## Known for
- Barbaresco Gallina
- Barbaresco Starderi
- Barbaresco Vürsù
- Moscato d'Asti Biancospino
