# Navarra

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A varied region next to Rioja, known for Garnacha rosado and a mix of grapes.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, gravel, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grenache|Garnacha]], [[Tempranillo]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Chardonnay]]
> - **Styles:** [[Rosé]]

## Landscape and topography
Varied landscapes, from Pyrenean foothills in the north to the dry Ebro plains in the south.

## Soil and geology
Clay, limestone and gravel, with sandy soils.

## Climate
A mix of Atlantic, Mediterranean and continental influences.

## History
Navarra was a royal kingdom, and its rosados were famous. The region had long been seen as an alternative to Rioja.

## Production and wine styles
Garnacha, Tempranillo, and plenty of international grapes. Rosado (rosé) is a speciality.

## Key wines
- Navarra Rosado
- Navarra Garnacha
- Navarra Reserva

## What to look for
- Good rosado and juicy Garnacha.
- Values are common.
