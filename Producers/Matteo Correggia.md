# Matteo Correggia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A leading Roero estate making sandy soil Nebbiolo and fine Arneis.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Roero]], [[Piedmont]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Nebbiolo]], [[Arneis]], [[Barbera]]

## About
Founded by the late Matteo Correggia in Canale, the estate showed the potential of Roero. The reds are aromatic and supple, and the Arneis is fresh and floral.

## Known for
- Roche d'Ampsej
- Roero Riserva Ròche d'Ampsèj
- Roero Arneis
