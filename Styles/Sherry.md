# Sherry

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Fortified wine from the Sherry Triangle in southern Spain, from bone dry to very sweet.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Category:** Fortified
> - **Colour:** Varies
> - **Sweetness:** Varies
> - **Country:** Spain
> - **Main grapes:** [[Palomino]], [[Pedro Ximénez]], [[Muscat]]
> - **Regions:** [[Jerez]]

## How it's made
Palomino is fermented dry, fortified and aged in a solera system. Fino and Manzanilla age under a layer of flor yeast, which protects them from air. Oloroso ages without flor and oxidises.

## Where it's made
The triangle of Jerez, Sanlúcar de Barrameda and El Puerto de Santa María. Manzanilla comes only from Sanlúcar.

## What it tastes like
- Fino and Manzanilla: almond, salt, green apple
- Amontillado: hazelnut, tobacco
- Oloroso: walnut, dried fig, caramel
- Pedro Ximénez: raisin, treacle, coffee

## Pairs with
- Fino with olives and almonds
- Manzanilla with seafood
- Oloroso with game
- PX over ice cream

## Good to know
- Dry styles should be served cold and drunk fresh.
- Pedro Ximénez is the sweetest style, made from sun dried grapes.
