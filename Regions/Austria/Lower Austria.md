# Lower Austria

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Austria's biggest wine state and home of its great Grüner Veltliner and Riesling.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Austria]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** loess, gneiss, granite, primary rock, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grüner Veltliner]], [[Riesling]], [[Zweigelt]], [[Blaufränkisch]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling hills, terraces and river valleys along the Danube and its tributaries, northeast of Vienna.

## Soil and geology
Loess in lower areas, gneiss, granite and primary rock on steeper terraces.

## Climate
Continental, with Pannonian warmth from the east and cool air from the Waldviertel forest in the north-west. Big day-night swings.

## History
Monastic orders planted much of the land, and Krems and Melk were key wine centres. The 1985 glycol scandal prompted Austria's strict wine laws, leading to a quality surge.

## Production and wine styles
Grüner Veltliner and Riesling are the stars, with Zweigelt and other reds. The DAC system names wines by region and style.

## Key wines
- Wachau Smaragd
- Kamptal DAC
- Kremstal DAC
- Wagram
- Weinviertel DAC

## What to look for
- Grüner Veltliner is peppery, citrusy and savoury.
- DAC wines name the region, giving more transparency.
