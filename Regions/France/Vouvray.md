# Vouvray

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Chenin Blanc in every style, from bone dry to lusciously sweet to sparkling, on the Loire's north bank.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Loire Valley]]
> - **Climate:** Maritime
> - **Soils:** tuffeau, clay, flint
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chenin Blanc]]

## Landscape and topography
A plateau cut by small valleys on the north bank of the Loire, east of Tours, with caves cut into the rock.

## Soil and geology
Tuffeau limestone with clay and flint, giving structure and minerality.

## Climate
Maritime to semi-continental. Autumn mist helps noble rot in the sweet vintages.

## History
Vouvray has had appellation status since 1936. Cellars in the cliffs have been used for ageing wine for centuries.

## Production and wine styles
100% Chenin Blanc. Labels say sec (dry), demi-sec, moelleux (sweet) or mousseux/pétillant for sparkling.

## Key wines
- Vouvray sec
- Vouvray demi-sec
- Vouvray moelleux
- Vouvray Mousseux

## What to look for
- Quince, honey, lanolin and a lively acidity.
- Check the sweetness word on the label before you buy.
