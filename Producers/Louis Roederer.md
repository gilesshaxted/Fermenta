# Louis Roederer

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family-owned house in Reims, best known for the prestige cuvée Cristal.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Champagne]], [[Montagne de Reims]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Pinot Meunier]]
> - **Styles:** [[Champagne (style)]]

## About
Roederer owns a large share of its own vineyards and farms an increasing amount organically and biodynamically. The wines are precise and elegant with a firm, mineral backbone.

## Known for
- Cristal
- Brut Premier
- Blanc de Blancs
