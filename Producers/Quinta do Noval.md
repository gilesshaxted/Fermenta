# Quinta do Noval

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A famous quinta in the Cima Corgo, best known for the rare, ungrafted Nacional Vintage Port.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Portugal]]
> - **Region:** [[Port]], [[Douro]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Touriga Nacional]], [[Touriga Franca]], [[Tinta Barroca]], [[Tinto Cão]]
> - **Styles:** [[Port (style)]]

## About
Quinta do Noval owns a small plot of ungrafted vines which gives its prized Nacional. The wider range is classically styled and age worthy.

## Known for
- Nacional
- Vintage Port
- 20 Year Old Tawny
