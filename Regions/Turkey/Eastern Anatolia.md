# Eastern Anatolia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Turkey's high eastern interior, the home of the native reds Öküzgözü and Boğazkere.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Turkey]]
> - **Part of:** [[Asia]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, basalt, gravel
> - **Main grapes:** [[Öküzgözü]], [[Boğazkere]]

## Landscape and topography
Vineyards at high altitude, from about 700 to 1,200 metres or more, around Elazığ, Malatya and Diyarbakır.

## Soil and geology
Clay, limestone and basalt, with gravel.

## Climate
Continental, with harsh winters, very hot summers and low rainfall.

## History
This region is close to the ancient home of wine culture and has grown vines for thousands of years. Local grapes are often blended to balance each other.

## Production and wine styles
Öküzgözü gives soft, fruity red wine, Boğazkere gives deeper, tannic red. They are often blended, and can age well.

## Key wines
- Öküzgözü
- Boğazkere
- Öküzgözü Boğazkere blends

## What to look for
- A blend of soft and firm reds.
- Hard to find outside Turkey.
