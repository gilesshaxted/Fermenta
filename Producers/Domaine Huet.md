# Domaine Huet

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A benchmark Vouvray domaine, biodynamic and famed for long-lived Chenin Blanc in dry, off-dry and sweet styles.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Vouvray]], [[Loire Valley]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chenin Blanc]]

## About
Domaine Huet farms three vineyards on the Vouvray plateau, each with its own character. The wines are pure and tense, and can age for many decades.

## Known for
- Le Haut-Lieu
- Le Mont
- Clos du Bourg
