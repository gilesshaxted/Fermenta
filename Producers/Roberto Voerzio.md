# Roberto Voerzio

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A La Morra producer known for very low yields and rich, polished single vineyard Barolo.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Barolo]], [[Piedmont]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Nebbiolo]], [[Barbera]]

## About
Voerzio works his vines hard to cut yields, aiming for intensity and depth. The wines are opulent and modern in feel, and they are made in tiny quantities.

## Known for
- Barolo Brunate
- Barolo Cerequio
- Barolo Sarmassa
