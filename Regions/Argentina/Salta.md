# Salta

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Some of the highest vineyards in the world, producing aromatic Torrontés and intense Malbec.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Argentina]]
> - **Part of:** [[South America]]
> - **Climate:** Arid continental
> - **Soils:** sand, alluvial, stony
> - **Main grapes:** [[Torrontés]], [[Malbec]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Tannat]]

## Landscape and topography
High desert valleys in the northwest, with vineyards at 1,700 to 3,000 metres.

## Soil and geology
Sandy, stony alluvial soils.

## Climate
Very sunny, with intense UV, cold nights and little rain.

## History
Wine has been made here since the 16th century. Altitude gives unusually fresh fruit in a warm latitude.

## Production and wine styles
Torrontés for whites, Malbec, Cabernet Sauvignon and Tannat for reds.

## Key wines
- Cafayate Torrontés
- Salta Malbec

## What to look for
- Floral whites and deeply coloured reds.
- Spectacular scenery.
