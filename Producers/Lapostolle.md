# Lapostolle

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A French-Chilean estate in Colchagua, best known for the Carmenère-led Clos Apalta.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Chile]]
> - **Region:** [[Colchagua Valley]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Carmenère]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Syrah]]

## About
A biodynamic-minded winery with a gravity-fed cellar and a strong focus on single vineyard quality. The wines are polished and rich, with dark fruit and fine tannins.

## Known for
- Clos Apalta
- Cuvée Alexandre
- Borobo
