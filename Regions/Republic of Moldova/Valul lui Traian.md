# Valul lui Traian

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A warm southern region of black earth and plains, best known for ripe reds and aromatic whites.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Republic of Moldova]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Warm continental
> - **Soils:** black earth, clay, limestone, loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Fetească Neagră]], [[Rară Neagră]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Muscat Ottonel]]

## Landscape and topography
A flat to gently rolling area in the south of the Republic of Moldova, close to the Prut and the Ukrainian and Romanian borders.

## Soil and geology
Rich black earth soils, with some clay and limestone.

## Climate
The warmest part of the country, with long, hot summers, mild autumns and less rainfall.

## History
The name refers to the old earthen ramparts known as Trajan's Wall, which run through the area. Wine was grown here for centuries and the region is now one of the country's three main wine regions.

## Production and wine styles
Reds, led by Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Fetească Neagră, with Rară Neagră and some aromatic whites such as Muscat Ottonel.

## Key wines
- Valul lui Traian Cabernet Sauvignon
- Valul lui Traian Merlot
- Valul lui Traian Fetească Neagră

## What to look for
- Ripe, fruity reds.
- Good value.
