# Ahr

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A northern, steep-sloped red wine island on the edge of the Eifel, known for Spätburgunder.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Germany]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Temperate
> - **Soils:** slate, greywacke, volcanic
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Riesling]]

## Landscape and topography
A narrow, winding valley with steep terraces.

## Soil and geology
Slate, greywacke and volcanic soils.

## Climate
Cool, but the valley is sheltered and warm in places.

## History
Floods in 2021 devastated the valley, and the vineyards are now being rebuilt with courage.

## Production and wine styles
Spätburgunder is dominant, with some Frühburgunder.

## Key wines
- Ahr Spätburgunder
- Frühburgunder

## What to look for
- Silky cherry fruit with minerality.
- A small region of great interest.
