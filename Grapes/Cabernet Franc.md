# Cabernet Franc

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Fresh, leafy and aromatic, a lighter cousin of Cabernet Sauvignon. Raspberries with a pencil-lead edge.

## Where it's from
South-west France. It is a parent of both Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot.

## Where it grows
- **Chinon, Bourgueil and Saumur-Champigny, Loire:** light, fresh and herbal
- **Saint-Émilion, Bordeaux:** blended with Merlot, including Cheval Blanc
- **Bolgheri, Tuscany:** used in Super Tuscan blends
- **Niagara, Canada:** also makes ice wine

## What it tastes like
- **Loire:** raspberry, red pepper, pencil shavings, violet
- **Warmer sites:** riper red and black fruit, graphite, spice

## Classic wines
- Chinon
- Bourgueil
- Saumur-Champigny
- Saint-Émilion blends

## Pairs with
- Roast chicken
- Pork
- Goat's cheese
- Herby dishes
- Charcuterie

## Good to know
- A parent of both Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot.
- Loire versions are often slightly chilled in summer.
