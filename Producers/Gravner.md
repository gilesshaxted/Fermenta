# Gravner

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The Oslavia estate that led the modern revival of long skin contact white wine made in buried clay amphorae.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Collio]], [[Friuli-Venezia Giulia]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Ribolla Gialla]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]]
> - **Styles:** [[Orange wine]], [[Natural wine]]

## About
Josko Gravner abandoned modern techniques and now ferments and ages his wines in Georgian style qvevri buried in the cellar. The wines are amber coloured, tannic and long lived, and have been highly influential.
