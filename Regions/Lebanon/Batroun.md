# Batroun

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A coastal district north of Beirut whose vineyards climb into the hills behind the sea.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Lebanon]]
> - **Part of:** [[Asia]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, stony soils
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Merwah]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills rise quickly from the Mediterranean coast, with vineyards at a range of altitudes inland from the town of Batroun.

## Soil and geology
Limestone and clay, often stony.

## Climate
Mediterranean, with sea breezes and cooler conditions at altitude than on the coast.

## History
Batroun is an ancient Phoenician port town, and its wine heritage is part of the lineage of the region. Newer estates have been planted here in recent decades.

## Production and wine styles
Fresh whites and elegant reds, including Syrah, Cabernet Sauvignon, Sauvignon Blanc and Chardonnay.

## Key wines
- Batroun Syrah
- Batroun white blends

## What to look for
- Cooler, fresher wines than the Bekaa.
- A newer area to watch.
