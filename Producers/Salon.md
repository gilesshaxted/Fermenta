# Salon

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A tiny Le Mesnil-sur-Oger house that makes one wine, a blanc de blancs, only in the best years.

## About
Salon is a single-vineyard, single-grape Champagne from the grand cru village of Le Mesnil. It is steely and very long lived, often needing years in bottle to open up.
