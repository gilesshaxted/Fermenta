# Colli Orientali del Friuli

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Eastern hills north of Collio, known for sweet Picolit and local reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Friuli-Venezia Giulia]]
> - **Climate:** Temperate
> - **Soils:** ponca, marl, sandstone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Friulano]], [[Ribolla Gialla]], [[Refosco]], [[Schioppettino]]
> - **Styles:** [[Orange wine]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills east of Udine, rising to the foothills of the Alps.

## Soil and geology
Ponca marl and sandstone.

## Climate
Temperate, with warm days and cool nights.

## History
Picolit, a rare sweet wine, was prized by European courts in the 18th century.

## Production and wine styles
Whites from Friulano and Ribolla, reds from Refosco and Schioppettino, and sweet Picolit.

## Key wines
- Colli Orientali Friulano
- Picolit
- Refosco dal Peduncolo Rosso
- Schioppettino

## What to look for
- Local reds are peppery and savoury.
- Picolit is a rare dessert wine.
