# Domaine Pierre Overnoy

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Pupillin cellar, regarded as a founder of the modern natural wine movement.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Arbois and Château-Chalon]], [[Jura]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Poulsard]], [[Savagnin]], [[Chardonnay]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]], [[Vin Jaune]]

## About
Pierre Overnoy and his successor Emmanuel Houillon make wines with almost no sulphur and little intervention. The reds are light and delicate, the whites tense and often oxidative.

## Known for
- Arbois-Pupillin Ploussard
- Vin Jaune
