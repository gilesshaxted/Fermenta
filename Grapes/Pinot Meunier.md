# Pinot Meunier

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The third Champagne grape, fruity and early to ripen.

## Where it's from
A mutation of Pinot Noir. Its name refers to the flour-dusted look of the leaves (meunier means miller).

## Where it grows
- **Champagne, France:** especially the Marne Valley
- **England:** increasingly planted for sparkling wine

## What it tastes like
- **In blends:** soft red fruit and approachable roundness
- **On its own:** apple, red cherry, savoury notes

## Classic wines
- Champagne blends
- Meunier-only cuvées
- English sparkling wine

## Pairs with
- Smoked salmon
- Roast chicken
- Canapés

## Good to know
- Buds late and ripens early, so it copes with frost better than Pinot Noir.
- Often adds fruit and early appeal to Champagne.
