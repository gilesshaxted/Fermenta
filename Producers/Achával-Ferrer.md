# Achával-Ferrer

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A quality focused Mendoza winery famous for powerful but polished single vineyard Malbec.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Argentina]]
> - **Region:** [[Luján de Cuyo]], [[Mendoza]]

## About
Founded as a boutique project in Mendoza with old vine vineyards as the core. The wines are rich and concentrated, with a clear sense of place from vineyard to vineyard.
