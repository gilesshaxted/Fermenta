# Château Palmer

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Margaux Third Growth loved for its Merlot-rich, velvety wines and biodynamic approach.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Margaux]], [[Bordeaux]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Petit Verdot]]
> - **Styles:** [[Bordeaux blend]]

## About
Palmer is a Margaux estate that regularly rivals the First Growths in quality. The wines are seductive and aromatic, with a high share of Merlot that is unusual for the Médoc.
