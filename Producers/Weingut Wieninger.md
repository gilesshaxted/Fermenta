# Weingut Wieninger

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The leading name for Vienna's traditional field blend, Wiener Gemischter Satz.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Austria]]
> - **Region:** [[Vienna]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]], [[Pinot Blanc]], [[Chardonnay]]

## About
A family estate in Stammersdorf and on the Nussberg, farming organically and biodynamically. The wines include the city's signature mixed planting blends and fresh, bright whites.

## Known for
- Wiener Gemischter Satz
