# Florio

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A historic Marsala house with a long record of fortified Sicilian wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Marsala]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grillo]], [[Catarratto]]
> - **Styles:** [[Marsala (style)]], [[Passito]]

## About
One of the most famous names of Marsala, with ageing cellars on the coast. It makes a range of Marsala styles as well as some sweet wines from Pantelleria.

## Known for
- Targa Riserva 1840
- Baglio Florio
- Terre Arse
- Morsi di Luce
