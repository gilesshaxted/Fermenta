# Carmenère

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Chile's signature red: soft, spicy and plummy, with a savoury green edge when underripe.

## Where it's from
Once a major Bordeaux grape, it was largely lost after phylloxera. It survived in Chile, where it was mistaken for Merlot until the 1990s.

## Where it grows
- **Colchagua and Cachapoal, Chile:** the best ripening conditions
- **Maipo, Chile:** often in blends

## What it tastes like
- Blackcurrant, plum and warm spice
- Green pepper if picked early
- Soft, rounded tannins

## Classic wines
- Chilean Carmenère
- Carmenère blends with Cabernet Sauvignon

## Pairs with
- Grilled meat
- Empanadas
- Stews

## Good to know
- Needs a long, warm season to ripen fully.
- The accent is sometimes written Carménère.
