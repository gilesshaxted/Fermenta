# Ugni Blanc

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The high-acid grape behind Cognac and Armagnac brandy. Neutral and sharp.

## Where it's from
The Italian Trebbiano Toscano, brought to France and now widely planted in the Charente.

## Where it grows
- **Cognac and Armagnac, France:** the base for brandy
- **Provence, France:** a blending white

## What it tastes like
- Neutral, lemony and high in acid, ideal for distilling

## Classic wines
- Cognac
- Armagnac
- Côtes de Gascogne

## Pairs with
- Seafood (as a simple white)

## Good to know
- Its high acidity and low alcohol suit distillation.
- Rarely seen as a quality table wine.
