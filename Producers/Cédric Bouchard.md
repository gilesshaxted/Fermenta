# Cédric Bouchard

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Côte des Bar grower, under the Roses de Jeanne label, making single-vineyard, single-vintage, single-grape cuvées.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Champagne]], [[Côte des Bar]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]]
> - **Styles:** [[Champagne (style)]], [[Natural wine]]

## About
Bouchard farms organically with low yields and bottles at a lower pressure for a soft, creamy mousse. The wines are pure, intense and very site specific.

- Val Vilaine
- La Bolorée
