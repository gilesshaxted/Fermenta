# Xinjiang

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A remote north-western region on the old Silk Road, with a long grape-growing tradition and dry, sunny conditions.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[China]]
> - **Part of:** [[Asia]]
> - **Climate:** Semi-arid
> - **Soils:** sand, gravel, loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Cabernet Gernischt]]

## Landscape and topography
Basins and valleys at the foot of the Tian Shan, such as Turpan, Manas and Yili.

## Soil and geology
Sand, gravel and loam, with irrigation from mountain meltwater.

## Climate
Very dry and sunny, with hot summers and cold winters, so vines need irrigation and often winter protection.

## History
Grapes have been grown in the oases for centuries, mostly for raisins and table fruit. Wine for export and for the quality market has grown in recent decades.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Chardonnay in a ripe style, along with sweet wines from local grapes.

## Key wines
- Xinjiang Cabernet Sauvignon
- Manas wines

## What to look for
- Ripe and sunny in style.
- Often hard to find outside China.
