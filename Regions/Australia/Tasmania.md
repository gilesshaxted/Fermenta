# Tasmania

> [!abstract] At a glance
> An island state with Australia's coolest climate and some of its best sparkling wine and Pinot Noir.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Australia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Oceania]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** dolerite, clay, sandstone, alluvial
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Riesling]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]]

## Landscape and topography
A mountainous island with vineyards in the Tamar Valley, Coal River Valley and the east coast.

## Soil and geology
Dolerite, clay and sandstone soils.

## Climate
Cool, with a long ripening period and high acidity.

## History
Vines were first planted in the 1820s, and the modern industry started in the 1950s and 1970s.

## Production and wine styles
Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, sparkling wine, Riesling and Sauvignon Blanc.

## Key wines
- Tasmanian sparkling wine
- Tasmanian Pinot Noir

## What to look for
- Pure, tense and fresh.
- World-class sparkling.
