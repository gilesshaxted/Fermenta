# Central Anatolia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A high, dry plateau with cold winters and hot summers, home to some of Turkey's best native grapes, including Kalecik Karası, Narince and Emir.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Turkey]]
> - **Part of:** [[Asia]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** volcanic tuff, clay, limestone, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Kalecik Karası]], [[Narince]], [[Emir]], [[Syrah]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## Landscape and topography
A vast plateau at 800 to 1,200 metres or more, with volcanic hills around Cappadocia and valleys near Ankara and Tokat.

## Soil and geology
Volcanic tuff in Cappadocia, plus clay and limestone elsewhere.

## Climate
Continental, with cold, snowy winters, hot dry summers and big day to night swings.

## History
Anatolia is one of the oldest winegrowing regions in the world, and Cappadocia has ancient rock-cut cellars. Wine here is now an important part of tourism.

## Production and wine styles
Elegant red Kalecik Karası, and white Emir and Narince, plus Syrah and international varieties.

## Key wines
- Kalecik Karası
- Emir
- Narince
- Cappadocia wines

## What to look for
- Try Emir and Narince.
- Cappadocia is a great place to visit.
