# Somló

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Hungary's smallest wine region, an extinct volcano with mineral, long lived whites.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Hungary]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** basalt, volcanic, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Furmint]], [[Hárslevelű]], [[Welschriesling]]

## Landscape and topography
A steep basalt hill rising out of a flat plain, with vines on its slopes.

## Soil and geology
Basalt and volcanic tuff over clay.

## Climate
Continental, with a warm, dry microclimate on the volcano.

## History
Somló wine was once said to bring fertility, and was given to Hungarian brides on their wedding night. It was prized by the Habsburg court.

## Production and wine styles
Full, mineral whites from Furmint, Hárslevelű and Olaszrizling.

## Key wines
- Somlói Furmint
- Somlói Hárslevelű
- Somlói Juhfark

## What to look for
- Smoky, mineral, high acid whites.
- Wines need time to open up.
