# Henschke

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family run Eden Valley estate behind Hill of Grace, one of Australia's most prized single vineyard Shirazes.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Australia]]
> - **Region:** [[Eden Valley]]
> - **Type:** Major producer

## About
A multi-generational winery in the cool, high Eden Valley, known for old vines and thoughtful farming. The reds are elegant and savoury, and the Rieslings are fine and long-lived.
