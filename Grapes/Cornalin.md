# Cornalin

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A dark, tannic Valais red, long grown in the canton and now one of its flagship reds.

## Where it's from
A native Valais grape, once called Rouge du Pays, and revived after nearly disappearing. It is not related to the Aosta Valley's grape of the same name.

## Where it grows
- **Valais, Switzerland:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Black cherry, violet, pepper and spice

## Classic wines
- Cornalin du Valais

## Pairs with
- Cured meat
- Game
- Hard cheese

## Good to know
- Not the same as Humagne Rouge.
- Ripens late and needs a good site.
