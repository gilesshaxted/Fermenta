# Nelson

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small, sunny, artisan region at the top of the South Island.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[New Zealand]]
> - **Part of:** [[Oceania]]
> - **Climate:** Maritime
> - **Soils:** clay, gravel, loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Riesling]]

## Landscape and topography
Coastal plains and foothills near Tasman Bay.

## Soil and geology
Clay and gravel soils.

## Climate
Sunny, with a mild coastal climate.

## History
Small family-run estates have shaped the area since the 1970s.

## Production and wine styles
Sauvignon Blanc, Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and aromatic whites.

## Key wines
- Nelson Sauvignon Blanc
- Nelson Pinot Noir

## What to look for
- Small scale, characterful wines.
- Good aromatic whites.
