# Vidal

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A hybrid grape that survives hard winters and is Canada's classic icewine variety.

## Where it's from
Bred in France in the 1930s by crossing Ugni Blanc with Rayon d'Or. Its thick skin and winter hardiness make it ideal for icewine in Ontario.

## Where it grows
- **Niagara Peninsula, Canada:** icewine and sparkling icewine
- **Finger Lakes, USA:** small plantings

## What it tastes like
- Peach, apricot, honey and tropical fruit

## Classic wines
- Vidal Icewine
- Ontario late harvest

## Pairs with
- Fruit tart
- Blue cheese
- Foie gras

## Good to know
- A hybrid, not a pure Vitis vinifera.
- Also called Vidal Blanc.
