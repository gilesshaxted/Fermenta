# Neusiedlersee

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Around the shallow Lake Neusiedl, famed for its fog-aided dessert wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Austria]]
> - **Part of:** [[Burgenland]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** sand, gravel, clay, loess
> - **Main grapes:** [[Zweigelt]], [[Welschriesling]], [[Chardonnay]]
> - **Styles:** [[Icewine and Eiswein]], [[Noble rot wines]]

## Landscape and topography
Very flat land around a vast, shallow steppe lake, with reed beds and low vineyards.

## Soil and geology
Sandy soils, gravel and clay.

## Climate
Warm, dry and sunny, with lake humidity that encourages botrytis in autumn.

## History
Illmitz and Apetlon are famous for sweet wines. The area is a UNESCO World Heritage cultural landscape with Hungary.

## Production and wine styles
Sweet Beerenauslese and Trockenbeerenauslese wines from Welschriesling, Chardonnay and Scheurebe, plus Zweigelt reds.

## Key wines
- Neusiedlersee DAC
- Trockenbeerenauslese
- Ausbruch

## What to look for
- Honey, apricot and rich acidity.
- Wines of great sweetness and balance.
