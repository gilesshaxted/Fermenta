# Negotin

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A sandy eastern region on the Danube and Romanian border, with warm conditions and traditional cellars.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Serbia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** sand, loess, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Prokupac]], [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling land by the Danube in the east of Serbia, near the borders with Romania and Bulgaria.

## Soil and geology
Sandy and loess soils.

## Climate
Warmer and drier than most of the country, with long ripening.

## History
Negotin Krajina is known for its old stone cellars (the Rajac wine cellars), and has long been a wine village area.

## Production and wine styles
Reds from Prokupac, Merlot and Cabernet Sauvignon, plus some whites.

## Key wines
- Negotinska Krajina
- Rajac cellars

## What to look for
- A small region with old cellars.
- Warm style reds.
