# San Juan

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Argentina's second largest wine region, warmer than Mendoza and known for Syrah.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Argentina]]
> - **Part of:** [[South America]]
> - **Climate:** Arid continental
> - **Soils:** alluvial, sand, gravel
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Bonarda]], [[Malbec]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## Landscape and topography
River valleys such as Tulum, Ullum and Pedernal, at 600 to 1,400 metres.

## Soil and geology
Sandy and alluvial soils.

## Climate
Hot, very dry and sunny.

## History
A historic area for wine and also for brandy and raisins.

## Production and wine styles
Syrah, Bonarda and Malbec.

## Key wines
- San Juan Syrah

## What to look for
- Spicy, peppery Syrah.
- Pedernal valley has cool, high-altitude sites.
