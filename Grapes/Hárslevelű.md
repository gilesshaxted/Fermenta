# Hárslevelű

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A soft, perfumed Hungarian white, Furmint's partner in Tokaj.

## Where it's from
Its name means linden leaf, from the shape of its leaves. It is the second grape of Tokaj, adding aroma and softness to Furmint's acidity.

## Where it grows
- **Tokaj, Hungary:** in sweet and dry blends
- **Somló, Hungary:** rich, full whites

## What it tastes like
- Pear, honey, linden blossom and spice

## Classic wines
- Tokaji Aszú
- Somló Hárslevelű

## Pairs with
- Roast chicken
- Spiced dishes
- Soft cheese

## Good to know
- Rounder and more aromatic than Furmint.
- Often blended.
