# Royal Tokaji

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A pioneering estate that helped restore Tokaj's reputation for sweet Aszú wines after the fall of communism.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Hungary]]
> - **Region:** [[Tokaj]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Furmint]], [[Hárslevelű]], [[Muscat]]
> - **Styles:** [[Tokaji]], [[Noble rot wines]]

## About
A large Tokaj estate with holdings in a number of classified vineyards. It makes dry Furmint and sweet Aszú in a classical style.

## Known for
- Mézes Mály
- Betsek
- Aszú 5 Puttonyos
