# Transylvania

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A high, hilly plateau ringed by the Carpathians, with cool-climate, aromatic whites and sparkling wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Romania]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, marl, sand, volcanic
> - **Main grapes:** [[Fetească Regală]], [[Riesling]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Gewürztraminer|Traminer]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling hills and valleys within the Carpathian arc, with vineyards on south-facing slopes, often at 250 to 500 metres.

## Soil and geology
Clay, marl, limestone and sand, with some volcanic soils.

## Climate
Cooler continental than the rest of Romania, with cold winters, warm summers and good acidity.

## History
Germanic Saxon settlers brought wine culture to the area in the Middle Ages. Under communism the wine industry was state-run, and modern private estates have emerged since 1990.

## Production and wine styles
Mostly whites: Fetească Regală, Riesling, Sauvignon Blanc, Pinot Gris, Muscat Ottonel and Traminer, plus sparkling wine.

## Key wines
- Târnave
- Alba Iulia
- Aiud
- Sebeș-Apold

## What to look for
- Crisp, aromatic whites.
- Good value.
