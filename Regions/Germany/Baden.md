# Baden

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Germany's warmest and longest region, and a source of its best Pinot Noir and Pinot varieties.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Germany]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Temperate
> - **Soils:** volcanic, loess, limestone, gneiss, granite
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Pinot Blanc]], [[Müller-Thurgau]], [[Riesling]]

## Landscape and topography
A long, narrow strip from Lake Constance to Heidelberg, along the Rhine and the Black Forest.

## Soil and geology
Volcanic soils at Kaiserstuhl, loess, limestone and granite elsewhere.

## Climate
Warm and sunny, the closest German wine area to Alsace.

## History
Baden grows more red wine than other German regions and has a strong cooperative tradition.

## Production and wine styles
Spätburgunder (Pinot Noir), Grauburgunder and Weissburgunder, plus Müller-Thurgau and Riesling.

## Key wines
- Baden Spätburgunder
- Baden Grauburgunder
- Kaiserstuhl wines

## What to look for
- Cherry-fruited Pinot Noir.
- Often excellent value.
