# Tokaj

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Hungary's most famous wine region, home of the legendary sweet Tokaji Aszú and a rising line of dry Furmint.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Hungary]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** volcanic, loess, clay, rhyolite
> - **Main grapes:** [[Furmint]], [[Hárslevelű]], [[Muscat]]
> - **Styles:** [[Tokaji]], [[Noble rot wines]]

## Landscape and topography
A region of extinct volcanoes and rolling hills where the Bodrog and Tisza rivers meet, with vineyards on south facing slopes.

## Soil and geology
Volcanic rock, clay and loess, with deep cellars carved into the volcanic tuff.

## Climate
Continental, with warm summers and misty autumns that encourage noble rot.

## History
Tokaji wine was prized by European courts, and in 1737 Tokaj became one of the first places in the world to formally classify its vineyards. It is a UNESCO World Heritage cultural landscape.

## Production and wine styles
Aszú wine is made from botrytised berries added to base wine. The scale of puttonyos was dropped in 2013. Szamorodni, Eszencia and dry Furmint are also made.

## Key wines
- Tokaji Aszú
- Tokaji Szamorodni
- Tokaji Eszencia
- Dry Tokaji Furmint

## What to look for
- Apricot, honey and orange marmalade in sweet wines.
- Dry Furmint is mineral and age worthy.
