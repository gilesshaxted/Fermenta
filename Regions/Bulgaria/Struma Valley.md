# Struma Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A hot, sunny region in the far south-west, where the native Melnik grows on sandy slopes below the Pirin mountains.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Bulgaria]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Warm mediterranean-influenced
> - **Soils:** sand, sandstone, clay, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Melnik]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Syrah]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
The valley of the Struma river in the south-west, ringed by the Pirin and Rila mountains, with sandstone pyramids around the town of Melnik.

## Soil and geology
Sandy, sandstone and clay soils, with some limestone.

## Climate
The warmest part of Bulgaria, with a Mediterranean influence from the south, long hot summers and mild winters.

## History
Melnik is a small, old wine town with a long winemaking tradition. Many of the cellars there are cut into the rock.

## Production and wine styles
Mostly powerful, structured reds. The local Melnik grape makes spicy, long-lived reds, alongside Cabernet and Merlot.

## Key wines
- Melnik wines
- Struma Valley Cabernet Sauvignon
- Struma Valley Merlot

## What to look for
- Ripe, spicy, age-worthy reds.
- Melnik is the grape to look for.
