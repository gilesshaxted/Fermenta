# Portugal

Part of [[Europe]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> An Atlantic country with over 250 native grapes, the home of Port and Madeira, and an increasing number of fine dry wines.

## How Portuguese wine is organised
- **DOC** is the main quality tier, and **Vinho Regional** (formerly IGP) allows more freedom.
- **Reserva** and **Garrafeira** denote higher quality and longer ageing.
- Port and Madeira have their own style categories: Ruby, Tawny, Vintage, and Sercial to Malmsey.
- Many wines are field blends of local grapes.
