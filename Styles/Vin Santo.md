# Vin Santo

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Italian dessert wine from dried grapes, aged for years in small barrels.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Category:** Sweet
> - **Colour:** Varies
> - **Sweetness:** Sweet
> - **Country:** Italy
> - **Main grapes:** [[Trebbiano]], [[Malvasia]], [[Sangiovese]], [[Nosiola]]
> - **Regions:** [[Tuscany]], [[Chianti]], [[San Gimignano]], [[Trentino]]

## How it's made
Grapes are dried on racks or hung from rafters, pressed, then slowly fermented and aged in small barrels called caratelli for at least three years.

## Where it's made
Mostly Tuscany, with Vino Santo Trentino made from Nosiola.

## What it tastes like
- Toasted hazelnut, dried apricot, caramel
- Sweet, nutty, amber colour

## Pairs with
- Cantucci biscuits
- Almond cake
- Hard cheese

## Good to know
- Dunking cantucci in Vin Santo is the classic Tuscan finish.
- Occhio di Pernice is made from Sangiovese.
