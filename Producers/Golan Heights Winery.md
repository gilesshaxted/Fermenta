# Golan Heights Winery

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A pioneering Israeli winery that helped raise the country's quality standards, with its Yarden, Gamla and Hermon labels.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Israel]]
> - **Region:** [[Golan Heights]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Merlot]]

## About
Based in Katzrin in the Golan Heights, the winery uses high-altitude volcanic vineyards. It makes a wide range, including premium Yarden wines and some sparkling.

## Known for
- Yarden Cabernet Sauvignon
- Yarden Katzrin
- Yarden Blanc de Blancs
