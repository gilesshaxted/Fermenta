# Mas de Daumas Gassac

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A famous Languedoc estate that showed how fine wine could be made in the south, often called the Grand Cru of the Languedoc.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Languedoc]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Syrah]]

## About
A family estate in the Hérault valley, making a Cabernet-led red on a cool, unusual soil, alongside a white from many varieties. The red is elegant and ages well.

## Known for
- Mas de Daumas Gassac Rouge
- Mas de Daumas Gassac Blanc
- Rosé Frizant
