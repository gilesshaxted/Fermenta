# Álvaro Palacios

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of the leaders of the modern Priorat revival, now also working in Rioja.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Region:** [[Priorat]], [[Rioja]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grenache]], [[Carignan]], [[Tempranillo]]

## About
Álvaro Palacios was one of the group of growers who helped revive Priorat in the 1990s. His wines come from steep slate slopes and are fragrant, mineral and deep, with L'Ermita as the icon.

## Known for
- L'Ermita
- Finca Dofí
- Les Terrasses
