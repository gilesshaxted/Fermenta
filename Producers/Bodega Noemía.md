# Bodega Noemía

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small Patagonian estate making old vine Malbec from the Río Negro valley.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Argentina]]
> - **Region:** [[Patagonia]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Malbec]]

## About
Set up around very old Malbec vines in the cool, windswept south of the country. The wines are structured and aromatic, with more freshness than many northern Malbecs.
