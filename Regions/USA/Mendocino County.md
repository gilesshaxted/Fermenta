# Mendocino County

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A rural, organic-minded county north of Sonoma, with a cool coastal valley and warm inland areas.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[North America]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** clay, loam, gravel, sandstone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Zinfandel]], [[Syrah]], [[Riesling]]

## Landscape and topography
Wooded ridges, river valleys and a rugged coastline.

## Soil and geology
Clay, loam and gravel with sandstone and shale.

## Climate
Cool in Anderson Valley, warmer inland.

## History
A long tradition of small farms and family wineries, and one of the most organic regions in the US.

## Production and wine styles
Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and sparkling wine from Anderson Valley, with Zinfandel, Syrah and Rhône varieties inland.

## Key wines
- Anderson Valley Pinot Noir
- Anderson Valley sparkling wine
- Mendocino Zinfandel

## What to look for
- Often good value and small scale.
- Anderson Valley has an Alsace-style Riesling and Gewürztraminer tradition.
