# Dealu Mare

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Romania's premier red wine region, on south-facing hills in the foothills of the Carpathians.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Romania]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, gravel, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Fetească Neagră]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Fetească Regală]]

## Landscape and topography
A line of hills south of the Carpathians, with vineyards at about 200 to 400 metres facing south.

## Soil and geology
Clay and limestone, with gravel and sandy soils.

## Climate
Warm continental, with sunny, long summers and a long ripening season.

## History
Dealu Mare means Big Hill and has been a winegrowing region for centuries. Recas and Prince Stirbey are among important producers.

## Production and wine styles
Red wines from Fetească Neagră, Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Pinot Noir. Whites include Fetească Regală and Sauvignon Blanc.

## Key wines
- Dealu Mare Fetească Neagră
- Dealu Mare Cabernet Sauvignon
- Dealu Mare Pinot Noir

## What to look for
- Ripe red and black fruit, and savoury spice.
- Fetească Neagră is the native red to try.
