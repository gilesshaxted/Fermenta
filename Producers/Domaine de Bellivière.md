# Domaine de Bellivière

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A leading estate of Jasnières and the Coteaux du Loir, making tense, mineral Chenin Blanc.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Loire Valley]]

> - **Main grapes:** [[Chenin Blanc]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]]

## About
Bellivière farms organically and biodynamically on the limestone of the northern Loire. The Chenin Blanc is taut and long lived and has a strong following among natural wine drinkers.
