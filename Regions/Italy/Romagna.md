# Romagna

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The sunny eastern hills of Emilia-Romagna, home of Sangiovese di Romagna and the white Albana.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Emilia-Romagna]]
> - **Climate:** Temperate
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sangiovese]], [[Trebbiano]], [[Albana]]

## Landscape and topography
Foothills of the Apennines running to the Adriatic coast.

## Soil and geology
Clay, limestone and sand, with a variety of microzones.

## Climate
Warmer and more maritime than Emilia.

## History
Romagna has been recognised as a Sangiovese area with named subzones since 2011, and Albana was the first white wine in Italy to get DOCG.

## Production and wine styles
Sangiovese di Romagna (with subzones), Albana di Romagna and Trebbiano.

## Key wines
- Sangiovese di Romagna Superiore
- Romagna Riserva
- Albana di Romagna

## What to look for
- A fruitier, earthier take on Sangiovese than Chianti.
- Very good value.
