# Auxerrois

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A soft, rounded white, often fuller and richer than Pinot Blanc, and a speciality of Luxembourg.

## Where it's from
A very old grape from northeastern France. Auxerrois is widely grown in Luxembourg and Alsace, and it is often sold as Auxerrois Blanc.

## Where it grows
- **Moselle, Luxembourg:** a leading grape
- **Alsace, France:** also grown

## What it tastes like
- Ripe apple, pear, honey and almond

## Classic wines
- Luxembourg Auxerrois
- Alsace Auxerrois

## Pairs with
- Fish
- Chicken
- Creamy dishes

## Good to know
- Often confused with Pinot Blanc.
- Not related to Auxerre or to Malbec.
