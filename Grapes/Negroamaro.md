# Negroamaro

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Dark, earthy and slightly bitter. The main red grape of Salento.

## Where it's from
Puglia, in the Salento peninsula. The name roughly means black bitter.

## Where it grows
- **Salento, Italy:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Black cherry, earth and a bitter twist

## Classic wines
- Salice Salentino
- Copertino

## Pairs with
- Grilled meats
- Orecchiette with ragù
- Aged cheese

## Good to know
- Makes excellent rosato.
- Often blended with Malvasia Nera.
