# Vale do São Francisco

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A tropical region in north east Brazil where vines can give two or more harvests a year.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Brazil]]
> - **Part of:** [[South America]]
> - **Climate:** Tropical semi-arid
> - **Soils:** sandy, alluvial, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Muscat]], [[Chenin Blanc]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## Landscape and topography
A semi-arid valley along the São Francisco river around Petrolina, close to the equator, using irrigation.

## Soil and geology
Sandy and alluvial soils.

## Climate
Hot and sunny all year, with irrigation. Growers can time pruning to shape harvest dates.

## History
The valley began growing table grapes and later added wine grapes, becoming one of the world's unusual tropical wine regions.

## Production and wine styles
Fresh whites, rosé, reds and sparkling wines made in a warm climate style.

## Key wines
- Vale do São Francisco Syrah
- Vale do São Francisco sparkling

## What to look for
- A rare tropical wine region.
- Several harvests per year are possible.
