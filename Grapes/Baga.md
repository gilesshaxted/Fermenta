# Baga

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A thick-skinned, tannic red that is Bairrada's signature grape.

## Where it's from
Bairrada, near the Atlantic coast of Portugal.

## Where it grows
- **Bairrada, Portugal:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Red cherry, earth and tough tannin

## Classic wines
- Bairrada Garrafeira
- Bairrada Baga

## Pairs with
- Suckling pig
- Roast meats

## Good to know
- Needs ageing or rich food.
- Quality has risen with older vines and gentler handling.
