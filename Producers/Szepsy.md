# Szepsy

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small, much admired Tokaj producer of site-specific Furmint and Aszú.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Hungary]]
> - **Region:** [[Tokaj]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Furmint]], [[Hárslevelű]]
> - **Styles:** [[Tokaji]], [[Noble rot wines]]

## About
A family estate led by a vigneron regarded as one of Tokaj's finest. The wines are vineyard-driven, precise and very age worthy.
