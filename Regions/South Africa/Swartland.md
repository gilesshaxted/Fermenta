# Swartland

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The leader of South Africa's new wave: dry-farmed old bush vines, low-intervention winemaking and exciting blends.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[South Africa]]
> - **Part of:** [[Africa]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** shale, granite, schist, iron-rich clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chenin Blanc]], [[Syrah]], [[Grenache]], [[Cinsault]], [[Mourvèdre]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling wheat country and low hills north of Cape Town, with the Kasteelberg and Perdeberg.

## Soil and geology
Weathered shale, schist and granite, with iron-rich clay.

## Climate
Hot, dry summers, with little rain and cooling Atlantic winds.

## History
Long a wheat region. A new generation, including Eben Sadie, revived old vines from the 1990s and founded the Swartland Revolution.

## Production and wine styles
Chenin Blanc, Syrah, Grenache, Cinsault, Mourvèdre and field blends.

## Key wines
- Swartland Chenin Blanc
- Swartland Syrah
- Swartland red blends

## What to look for
- Savoury, fresh and often wild-fermented.
- Old bush vines are a major draw.
