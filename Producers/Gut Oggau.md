# Gut Oggau

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A biodynamic Burgenland estate that became a symbol of Austrian natural wine, with wines named as members of a family.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Austria]]
> - **Region:** [[Leithaberg]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Blaufränkisch]], [[Grüner Veltliner]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]]

## About
A biodynamic estate in Oggau working with minimal intervention. Each cuvée is given a family-member name and label, and the wines are lively, savoury and distinctive.
