# Marcel Deiss

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Bergheim biodynamic estate that champions the idea of terroir over grape variety.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Alsace]], [[Alsace Grand Cru]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Gewürztraminer]], [[Pinot Noir]]

## About
Deiss plants and co-ferments mixed varieties in the same vineyard, labelling the wines by place rather than grape. The wines are textured, complex and unusual.

## Known for
- Altenberg de Bergheim
- Mambourg
