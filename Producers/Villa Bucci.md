# Villa Bucci

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small, revered estate making some of Italy's most ageworthy Verdicchio.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Verdicchio dei Castelli di Jesi]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer

## About
A family estate with old vines and a long standing mixed farming tradition. The wines are deep and complex, and often improve over many years.
