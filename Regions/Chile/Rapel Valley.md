# Rapel Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A big zone divided into Cachapoal and Colchagua, home of Chile's modern premium reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Chile]]
> - **Part of:** [[South America]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** alluvial, clay, granite, loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Carmenère]], [[Merlot]], [[Syrah]]

## Landscape and topography
Broad valleys between the Andes and the coastal range.

## Soil and geology
Alluvial, clay and granite soils.

## Climate
Warm and dry Mediterranean climate.

## History
A key area of modern wine investment since the 1990s.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon, Carmenère, Merlot and Syrah.

## Key wines
- Colchagua Carmenère
- Cachapoal Cabernet Sauvignon

## What to look for
- Full-bodied reds.
- Carmenère does particularly well here.
