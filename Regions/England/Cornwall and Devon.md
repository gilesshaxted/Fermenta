# Cornwall and Devon

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The mild, coastal south-west, with sheltered valleys, slate soils and a distinctive maritime character.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[England]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Mild maritime
> - **Soils:** slate, shale, clay, greensand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Seyval Blanc]], [[Bacchus]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]]
> - **Styles:** [[English sparkling wine]]

## Landscape and topography
Coastal valleys and sheltered slopes close to the sea in Cornwall and Devon, including the Camel Valley.

## Soil and geology
Slate, shale and clay, with some greensand in east Devon.

## Climate
Mild and maritime, with ocean influence and lots of rain, though sheltered south-facing sites do well.

## History
Camel Valley in Cornwall has won international awards for its sparkling and still wines. Wine making here began in the 1970s.

## Production and wine styles
Sparkling and still whites, plus rosé. Seyval Blanc and Bacchus do well.

## Key wines
- Camel Valley Bacchus
- Cornish sparkling
- Devon sparkling

## What to look for
- Fresh, crisp and bright.
- A good match for local seafood.
