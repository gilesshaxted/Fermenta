# Domaine de Trévallon

> [!abstract] At a glance
> An iconic estate in Les Baux de Provence, famed for its unusual Syrah and Cabernet blend.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Provence]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## About
A family domaine in the Alpilles that chose to make wine outside the local appellation rules. The red is firm, savoury and long-lived, and there is a small amount of white.

## Known for
- Trévallon Rouge
- Trévallon Blanc
