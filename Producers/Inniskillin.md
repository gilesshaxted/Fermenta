# Inniskillin

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Canada's most famous icewine house, based in Niagara-on-the-Lake.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Canada]]
> - **Region:** [[Niagara-on-the-Lake]], [[Niagara Peninsula]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Vidal]], [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Riesling]]
> - **Styles:** [[Icewine and Eiswein]]

## About
Inniskillin was one of the first estate wineries in Ontario after Prohibition. The icewines are luscious and bright.

## Known for
- Vidal Icewine
- Cabernet Franc Icewine
- Sparkling Vidal Icewine
