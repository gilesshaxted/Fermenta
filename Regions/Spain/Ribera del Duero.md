# Ribera del Duero

> [!abstract] At a glance
> High-altitude Tempranillo (Tinto Fino) on Spain's central plateau, powerful and dark.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, sand, gravel
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tempranillo]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]]

## Landscape and topography
A high plateau along the Duero river, with vineyards at roughly 750 to 1,000 metres.

## Soil and geology
Clay, limestone and sand, with alluvial gravels near the river.

## Climate
Continental, with very cold winters, hot summers and big day-night swings that keep acidity.

## History
Vega Sicilia has made wine here since the 1860s, and the DO was only created in 1982, after which many new estates opened.

## Production and wine styles
Tempranillo (Tinto Fino) with a little Bordeaux grape allowed. Crianza, Reserva and Gran Reserva follow the Rioja pattern.

## Key wines
- Ribera del Duero Crianza
- Ribera del Duero Reserva
- Vega Sicilia Único
- Pingus

## What to look for
- Black fruit, liquorice and firm tannins, with notes of oak.
- More concentrated than Rioja.
