# Georges Laval

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small Cumières grower, among the early champions of organic farming in Champagne.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Champagne]], [[Vallée de la Marne]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Meunier]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]]
> - **Styles:** [[Champagne (style)]], [[Natural wine]]

## About
Laval ferments in old oak with native yeasts and bottles with minimal intervention. The wines are savoury, vinous and distinctive.

## Known for
- Cumières Premier Cru
- Les Chênes
- Les Hautes Chèvres
