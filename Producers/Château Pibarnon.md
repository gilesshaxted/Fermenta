# Château Pibarnon

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A high altitude Bandol estate making structured Mourvèdre and fresh rosé.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Bandol]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Mourvèdre]], [[Grenache]], [[Cinsault]]
> - **Styles:** [[Rosé]]

## About
An estate with terraced vineyards on limestone and clay above the sea. The reds are firm, ageworthy and savoury, and the rosé is pale and food friendly.

- Bandol Rouge
- Bandol Rosé
