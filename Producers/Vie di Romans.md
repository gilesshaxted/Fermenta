# Vie di Romans

> [!abstract] At a glance
> An Isonzo family estate known for intense, mineral whites, especially Sauvignon Blanc.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Friuli-Venezia Giulia]]

## About
Gianfranco Gallo's family farms in the stony Isonzo plain. The wines are concentrated and precise, with a strong sense of place.

- Dessimis Pinot Grigio
