# Vilana

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Crete's most widely planted white, light and fresh.

## Where it's from
Native to Crete and the island's main white grape. It is the base of Peza white.

## Where it grows
- **Peza, Crete:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Apple, herbs, citrus and white flowers

## Classic wines
- Peza white

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Salads
- Grilled fish

## Good to know
- Often blended.
- Best young.
