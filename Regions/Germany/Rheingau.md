# Rheingau

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A south-facing stretch of the Rhine, known for stately dry Riesling and historic estates.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Germany]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Temperate
> - **Soils:** slate, quartzite, loess, loam, marl
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]], [[Pinot Noir]]
> - **Styles:** [[Icewine and Eiswein]]

## Landscape and topography
A short, sunny stretch where the Rhine flows east to west, with vineyards on south-facing slopes opposite the Taunus hills.

## Soil and geology
Slate and quartzite on the steepest sites, loess and loam on lower slopes.

## Climate
Mild, protected by the Taunus hills and moderated by the river.

## History
Monks at Kloster Eberbach planted vines in the 12th century. Spätlese was said to be discovered here in 1775, when a messenger was late with permission to harvest. The Charta of 1984 defined dry styles.

## Production and wine styles
Riesling and some Spätburgunder (Pinot Noir) near Assmannshausen. Many are dry (trocken), with sweet Prädikat styles too.

## Key wines
- Rheingau Riesling Erstes Gewächs
- Rüdesheimer Berg
- Assmannshäuser Spätburgunder

## What to look for
- Rich yet precise Riesling.
- Historic estates such as Schloss Johannisberg.
