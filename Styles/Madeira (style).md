# Madeira (style)

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Long lived fortified wine from a volcanic island, deliberately heated while ageing.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Category:** Fortified
> - **Colour:** Varies
> - **Sweetness:** Varies
> - **Country:** Portugal
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sercial]], [[Verdelho]], [[Boal]], [[Malvasia]], [[Tinta Negra]]
> - **Regions:** [[Madeira]]

## How it's made
Madeira is warmed during ageing, in estufa tanks or in warm lofts, which gives its caramelised flavour and makes it almost indestructible. The four noble grapes set the sweetness: Sercial is driest, then Verdelho, Boal and Malvasia (Malmsey).

## Where it's made
The island of Madeira in the Atlantic.

## What it tastes like
- Burnt caramel, orange peel, walnut
- High acidity keeps it fresh
- Dry to very sweet

## Pairs with
- Soup
- Cheese
- Fruit cake
- Nuts

## Good to know
- An opened bottle can last for months or even years.
- Most basic Madeira is made from Tinta Negra.
