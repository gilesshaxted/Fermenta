# Domaine Rives-Blanques

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A quality led Limoux estate making both still wines and traditional method sparkling.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Limoux]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Mauzac]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Chenin Blanc]]
> - **Styles:** [[Traditional method]], [[Crémant]]

## About
A family domaine in the cool hills above Limoux, making sparkling wines from Mauzac as well as Chardonnay and Chenin Blanc. The wines are fresh, floral and good value.

## Known for
- Blanquette de Limoux
- Crémant de Limoux
- Odyssée Chardonnay
- Dédicace Chenin
