# Nemea

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Home of Agiorgitiko (St George), a soft, plummy red, grown at altitude in the Peloponnese.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Greece]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, marl
> - **Main grapes:** [[Agiorgitiko]]

## Landscape and topography
Valleys and hillsides at 250 to 900 metres in the northeastern Peloponnese, near ancient Nemea.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, clay and marl, with sandy soils in the lower area.

## Climate
Mediterranean, with altitude cooling the higher vineyards, helping to retain freshness.

## History
Hercules is said to have fought the Nemean lion here. The wine was known in antiquity as the Blood of Hercules.

## Production and wine styles
100% Agiorgitiko, in styles from fresh and fruity to oak-aged and structured.

## Key wines
- Nemea Agiorgitiko
- Nemea Reserve
- Agiorgitiko rosé

## What to look for
- Plum, cherry and spice, with soft tannins.
- Great food wine.
