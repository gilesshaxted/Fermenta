# Golden Mile Bench

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A warm, dry strip in the south Okanagan, noted for powerful reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Canada]]
> - **Part of:** [[Okanagan Valley]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** sand, gravel, alluvial
> - **Main grapes:** [[Merlot]], [[Syrah]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
A bench on the western side of the valley south of Oliver, with a fan of gravel and sand.

## Soil and geology
Gravel, sand and alluvial fans.

## Climate
Hot and dry, close to desert conditions.

## History
Canada's first sub-appellation within the Okanagan Valley.

## Production and wine styles
Merlot, Syrah, Cabernet Sauvignon and Chardonnay.

## Key wines
- Golden Mile Syrah
- Golden Mile Merlot
- Golden Mile Cabernet

## What to look for
- Bold, ripe reds.
- Warmest part of Canada's wine country.
