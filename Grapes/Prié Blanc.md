# Prié Blanc

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of the highest vineyards in Europe makes this light, crisp Alpine white.

## Where it's from
A rare grape of the Alps, grown at around 1,200 metres around Morgex and La Salle, on ungrafted vines.

## Where it grows
- **Morgex et La Salle, Italy:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Lemon, apple, herbs and mineral

## Classic wines
- Blanc de Morgex et de La Salle

## Pairs with
- Alpine cheese
- Trout
- Light starters

## Good to know
- Ungrafted vines grown low to the ground.
- Fresh and zesty.
