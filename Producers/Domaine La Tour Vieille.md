# Domaine La Tour Vieille

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small, highly regarded Collioure domaine working the steep slopes above the sea.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Collioure and Banyuls]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grenache]], [[Carignan]], [[Mourvèdre]]
> - **Styles:** [[Vin doux naturel]]

## About
A family estate making wines of freshness and depth from low yielding old vines. The range includes dry reds and whites and traditional Banyuls.
