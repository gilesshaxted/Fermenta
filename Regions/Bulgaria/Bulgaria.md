# Bulgaria

Part of [[Europe]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of Europe's old wine countries, with ancient Thracian roots and its own native red grapes, from Mavrud to Melnik.

## How wine is organised here
- Bulgarian wine is classified under EU rules, as PDO wines and PGI wines.
- The country is often described in five wine regions: Danube Plain, Black Sea, Thracian Valley, Struma Valley and Rose Valley.
- Native grapes such as Mavrud, Melnik, Rubin, Gamza and Dimiat are an important part of the country's identity.
