# Domaine de la Bongran

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Clessé estate famous for rich, late-harvested Mâcon whites, a world apart from typical Mâconnais wine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Mâconnais]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]]
> - **Styles:** [[Noble rot wines]]

## About
Jean Thévenet picks late and lets botrytis develop, so the wines carry some residual sugar and real depth. They age very well and are often unlike anything else in Burgundy.

## Known for
- Cuvée Tradition
- Quintaine
- Cuvée Levroutée
