# Château Latour

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A First Growth Pauillac estate famed for dense, long-lived and structured Cabernet Sauvignon.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Pauillac]], [[Bordeaux]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]]
> - **Styles:** [[Bordeaux blend]]

## About
Latour sits close to the Gironde estuary in Pauillac and is known for deep, powerful wines that need years in the cellar. A second wine and a third label are made from younger vines and declassified lots.
