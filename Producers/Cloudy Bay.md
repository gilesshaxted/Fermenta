# Cloudy Bay

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The Marlborough winery that made New Zealand Sauvignon Blanc famous around the world.

> - **Country:** [[New Zealand]]

## About
A large, long established producer in the Wairau Valley. The signature Sauvignon is bright, zesty and aromatic, and the range also includes richer Chardonnay and sparkling wine.

## Known for
- Sauvignon Blanc
- Te Koko
- Pelorus
- Te Wahi Pinot Noir
