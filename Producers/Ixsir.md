# Ixsir

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A modern Lebanese winery, with vineyards at altitude and a base in the Batroun hills.

> - **Country:** [[Lebanon]]

## About
Founded in the 2000s, Ixsir blends fruit from different altitudes. The wines are polished and modern in style.
