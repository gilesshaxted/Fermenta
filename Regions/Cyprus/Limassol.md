# Limassol

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The country's biggest wine district, running from the coast up to the hillside villages.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Cyprus]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, chalk, clay, volcanic
> - **Main grapes:** [[Mavro]], [[Xynisteri]], [[Maratheftiko]], [[Syrah]], [[Grenache]]

## Landscape and topography
A district from the coast of southern Cyprus up into the southern slopes of the Troodos, with vineyards on hills and terraces.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, chalk and clay, with some volcanic soils higher up.

## Climate
Hot and dry on the coast and cooler higher up, with plenty of sunshine.

## History
This district has long been at the centre of Cypriot winemaking, and Commandaria comes from its hillside villages. Modern wineries now work with old local grapes as well as international varieties.

## Production and wine styles
Mavro and Xynisteri, with Maratheftiko, Syrah and Grenache. Dry reds, whites and rosés are all made.

## Key wines
- Limassol Xynisteri
- Limassol Maratheftiko
- Limassol Syrah

## What to look for
- Sun-ripened reds and fresh whites.
- Hills above the coast do best.
