# Raventós i Blanc

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family estate making organic and biodynamic sparkling wines in the Conca del Riu Anoia.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Region:** [[Penedès]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Xarel·lo]], [[Macabeo]], [[Parellada]]
> - **Styles:** [[Traditional method]]

## About
The family left the Cava denomination to focus on a vineyard led identity. The wines are fine, savoury, and made by the traditional method.

## Known for
- De la Finca
- De Nit
- L'Hereu
