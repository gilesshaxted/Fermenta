# Hemel-en-Aarde

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A valley above Hermanus, South Africa's top address for Pinot Noir and Chardonnay.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[South Africa]]
> - **Part of:** [[Walker Bay]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** clay, shale, granite
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
A valley whose name means Heaven and Earth, between steep slopes.

## Soil and geology
Clay-rich shale, with granite on the slopes.

## Climate
Cool and breezy, with Atlantic influence.

## History
Hamilton Russell, Bouchard Finlayson and others have defined the style since the 1970s.

## Production and wine styles
Pinot Noir and Chardonnay.

## Key wines
- Hemel-en-Aarde Pinot Noir

## What to look for
- Silky, savoury Pinot.
- Mineral Chardonnay.

## Producers
- [[Hamilton Russell Vineyards]]
- [[Newton Johnson]]
