# Dr. Loosen

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A leading Mosel Riesling house known for old vine, steep slate sites around Bernkastel.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Germany]]
> - **Region:** [[Mosel]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]]

## About
Based on the middle Mosel, working classic sites such as Ürzig and Erden with old, ungrafted vines. The wines range from delicate, low alcohol Kabinett and Spätlese to rich sweet styles and dry bottlings.

## Known for
- Erdener Prälat
- Ürziger Würzgarten
- Wehlener Sonnenuhr
