# Château Musar

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Lebanon's most famous winery, in the Bekaa Valley, known for long-lived reds and whites.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Lebanon]]
> - **Region:** [[Bekaa Valley]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Cinsault]], [[Carignan]], [[Obaideh]], [[Merwah]]

## About
A family estate, with vineyards at altitude in the Bekaa and a distinctive, low-intervention approach. The reds age for many years, and the whites use the native Obaideh and Merwah grapes.
