# Arnaldo Caprai

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The estate most associated with the modern rise of Sagrantino di Montefalco.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Montefalco]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sagrantino]]

## About
A family estate in Montefalco that worked to tame Sagrantino's famously heavy tannins. The wines are dark, powerful and made for the cellar.

## Known for
- Sagrantino di Montefalco 25 Anni
- Sagrantino di Montefalco Collepiano
- Montefalco Rosso
