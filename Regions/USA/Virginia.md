# Virginia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> East coast wine country with rolling Blue Ridge foothills and a flair for Cabernet Franc and Viognier.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[North America]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** granite, clay, loam, slate
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Viognier]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Petit Verdot]], [[Merlot]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling hills and Blue Ridge foothills near Charlottesville and Middleburg.

## Soil and geology
Granite, clay, loam and slate.

## Climate
Warm and humid summers, with disease pressure, and mild winters.

## History
Thomas Jefferson tried to grow European vines at Monticello. The modern industry took off from the 1970s.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Franc, Viognier, Chardonnay, Petit Verdot and Merlot.

## Key wines
- Virginia Viognier
- Virginia Cabernet Franc

## What to look for
- Elegant, fresh wines.
- A region still finding its feet.
