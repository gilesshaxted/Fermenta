# Cahors

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The original home of Malbec, known historically as the black wine of Cahors.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[South-West France]]
> - **Climate:** Maritime
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, gravel, alluvium
> - **Main grapes:** [[Malbec]]

## Landscape and topography
River terraces and limestone plateaux along the winding Lot valley.

## Soil and geology
Alluvial gravel terraces near the river, and limestone plateaux (causses) above, with iron-rich clay.

## Climate
Maritime to semi-continental, warmer and drier than Bordeaux.

## History
Famously exported to England and Russia in the Middle Ages. Phylloxera and a catastrophic frost in 1956 nearly ended the region, but replanting saved it.

## Production and wine styles
Minimum 70% Malbec (locally called Auxerrois or Côt), blended with Merlot and Tannat. Wines are structured, dark and long-lived.

## Key wines
- Cahors

## What to look for
- Plum, violets, black cherry and firm tannin.
- Great with duck confit and steak.
