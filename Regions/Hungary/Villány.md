# Villány

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Hungary's warm south, known for powerful reds and Cabernet Franc.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Hungary]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Warm continental
> - **Soils:** limestone, loess, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Blaufränkisch]]

## Landscape and topography
Low limestone hills facing south, near the Croatian border.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, loess and clay.

## Climate
Hot summers with a Mediterranean influence, the warmest of Hungary's wine regions.

## History
Villány was a wine region in Roman times. German settlers brought new grapes after the Ottomans left. The region was renewed with private estates after 1990.

## Production and wine styles
Full bodied reds from Cabernet Franc, Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Kékfrankos. The Villányi Franc is a signature style.

## Key wines
- Villány Cabernet Franc
- Villány Cuvée
- Villány Kékfrankos

## What to look for
- Cabernet Franc is the signature red.
- Rich, ripe and oaked reds.
