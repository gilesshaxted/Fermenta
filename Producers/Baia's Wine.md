# Baia's Wine

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small Imereti estate led by Baia Abuladze, known for natural wines from local varieties.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Georgia]]
> - **Region:** [[Imereti]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tsolikouri]], [[Tsitska]], [[Krakhuna]]
> - **Styles:** [[Qvevri wine]], [[Natural wine]], [[Orange wine]]

## About
Organic farming and minimal intervention in the Imeretian style, using qvevri. The wines are fresh, textural and shaped by gentle skin contact.
