# Dalmatia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The long Adriatic coast and its islands, home to Plavac Mali and some of the oldest vine ancestry in Europe.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Croatia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, karst, clay, terra rossa
> - **Main grapes:** [[Plavac Mali]], [[Pošip]], [[Babić]], [[Vugava]], [[Grk]]

## Landscape and topography
A narrow strip of coast and many islands along the Adriatic, with vineyards on steep, rocky slopes and sheltered plains.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, karst and terra rossa, often poor and stony.

## Climate
Hot and dry Mediterranean, with strong sunshine, sea breezes and little rain in summer.

## History
Greek colonists planted vines on Dalmatian islands in the ancient world. The family of Plavac Mali includes Tribidrag, known elsewhere as Zinfandel and Primitivo.

## Production and wine styles
Full-bodied reds from Plavac Mali and Babić, and whites such as Pošip, Vugava and Grk.

## Key wines
- Dingač
- Postup
- Pelješac
- Primošten Babić

## What to look for
- Rich reds from steep, sun-baked vineyards.
- Island whites are crisp and salty.
