# Tinta Barroca

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A soft, rich Douro red that adds sweetness and body to Port.

## Where it's from
Grown on north-facing Douro slopes, as it dislikes excessive heat.

## Where it grows
- **Douro, Portugal:** in Port blends

## What it tastes like
- Plum, raisin, fig and spice

## Classic wines
- Port
- Douro red

## Pairs with
- Dessert
- Chocolate
- Cheese

## Good to know
- Ripens early.
- Adds richness.
