# Valle de Parras

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A high desert valley in Coahuila, home of Casa Madero, often described as the oldest winery in the Americas.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Mexico]]
> - **Part of:** [[North America]]
> - **Climate:** Semi-arid
> - **Soils:** limestone, sandy loam, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Cinsault]], [[Petite Sirah]], [[Chenin Blanc]], [[Tempranillo]]

## Landscape and topography
A valley in the high plateau of northern Mexico, at altitude, surrounded by desert mountains.

## Soil and geology
Limestone based soils with sandy loam and clay.

## Climate
Dry and sunny with big differences between day and night because of the altitude.

## History
Casa Madero was founded in the late sixteenth century and is often cited as the oldest winery in the Americas. The Parras area has a very long wine history linked to Spanish missions.

## Production and wine styles
Warm climate reds and some whites, made mostly by a small number of larger producers.

## Key wines
- Casa Madero wines
- Parras Cabernet Sauvignon
- Parras Chenin Blanc

## What to look for
- Old history in the New World.
- Altitude gives fresh acidity.
