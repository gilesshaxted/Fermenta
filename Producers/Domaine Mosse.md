# Domaine Mosse

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A much-loved Anjou domaine making vibrant, low intervention Chenin Blanc and a famous pet-nat.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Anjou]], [[Loire Valley]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chenin Blanc]], [[Gamay]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]], [[Pétillant naturel]]

## About
Agnès and René Mosse farm organically and make wine with minimal additions in the Anjou schist hills. The wines are energetic, textural and precise, and widely admired by natural wine fans.

## Known for
- Bonnes Blanches
- Arcane
- Moussamoussettes
