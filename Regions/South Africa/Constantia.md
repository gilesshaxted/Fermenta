# Constantia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> South Africa's oldest wine area, a cool, green valley on the Cape peninsula, with Sauvignon Blanc and historic sweet wine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[South Africa]]
> - **Part of:** [[Africa]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** granite, decomposed granite, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Sémillon]], [[Muscat]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## Landscape and topography
A small valley on the eastern slopes of Table Mountain, close to Cape Town.

## Soil and geology
Decomposed granite and clay.

## Climate
Cool and breezy, with sea air from False Bay.

## History
Founded in 1685 by Simon van der Stel. Vin de Constance, a sweet Muscat wine, was prized across Europe in the 18th and 19th centuries.

## Production and wine styles
Sauvignon Blanc, Sémillon, Muscat de Frontignan and Bordeaux varieties.

## Key wines
- Constantia Sauvignon Blanc
- Vin de Constance

## What to look for
- Crisp, complex Sauvignon.
- Legendary sweet wine.
