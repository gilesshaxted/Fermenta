# Merlot

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Soft, plummy and easy to like. Plush fruit with gentler tannins than Cabernet.

## Where it's from
Bordeaux, where it leads the Right Bank. DNA work shows Cabernet Franc is one of its parents.

## Where it grows
- **Pomerol and Saint-Émilion, France:** the heartland, rich and velvety
- **Washington State, USA:** ripe and structured
- **Colchagua Valley, Chile:** soft, ripe and good value
- **Bolgheri, Italy:** used in Super Tuscan blends
- **Veneto and Friuli, Italy:** lighter, everyday styles

## What it tastes like
- **Cooler sites:** red plum, cherry, bay leaf, herbs
- **Warmer sites:** black plum, fig, chocolate
- **With oak:** mocha, vanilla, clove

## Classic wines
- Pomerol
- Saint-Émilion
- Right Bank Bordeaux blends
- Washington State Merlot
- Chilean Merlot

## Pairs with
- Roast chicken
- Duck
- Pasta with a meat ragù
- Mushroom dishes

## Good to know
- Ripens earlier than Cabernet Sauvignon and does well on cooler clay soils.
- Soft tannins make it approachable young.
