# Asti

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Home of sweet, lightly fizzy Moscato d'Asti and the everyday red Barbera d'Asti.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Piedmont]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** clay, marl, sand, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Muscat|Moscato]], [[Barbera]]
> - **Styles:** [[Asti and Moscato d'Asti]], [[Charmat method]]

## Landscape and topography
Gently rolling hills around the town of Asti and Canelli, in the Monferrato.

## Soil and geology
Calcareous clay, marl and sand.

## Climate
Continental, with warm summers.

## History
Asti Spumante is one of Italy's best-known sparkling wines, and Moscato d'Asti is a lighter, more delicate relative.

## Production and wine styles
Moscato Bianco for Asti and Moscato d'Asti, Barbera for Barbera d'Asti.

## Key wines
- Moscato d'Asti
- Asti Spumante
- Barbera d'Asti

## What to look for
- Moscato d'Asti is low in alcohol and gently fizzy, with peach and orange blossom.
- Barbera d'Asti is juicy and acidic.
