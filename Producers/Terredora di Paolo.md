# Terredora di Paolo

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family estate making reliable Taurasi, Fiano and Greco from across Irpinia.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Taurasi]], [[Campania]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Aglianico]], [[Fiano]], [[Greco]]

## About
A family firm with vineyards in several parts of Avellino province. The wines are clean and typical, and offer good value.

## Known for
- Taurasi Fatica Contadina
- Fiano di Avellino Terre di Dora
- Greco di Tufo Loggia della Serra
