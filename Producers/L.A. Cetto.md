# L.A. Cetto

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Mexico's largest wine producer, based in Baja California's Valle de Guadalupe.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Mexico]]
> - **Region:** [[Valle de Guadalupe]]

## About
L.A. Cetto is a family-run company with a large network of vineyards in Baja. Its range runs from easy everyday bottles to varietal reds, with Nebbiolo and Petite Sirah as local specialities.
