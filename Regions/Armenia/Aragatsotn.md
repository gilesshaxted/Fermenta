# Aragatsotn

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A highland province on the slopes of Mount Aragats, with cooler, higher vineyards and growing interest in quality wine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Armenia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Asia]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** volcanic, basalt, stony loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Areni]], [[Voskehat]]

## Landscape and topography
Slopes of the extinct volcano Mount Aragats, with vineyards on volcanic terrain at fairly high altitude.

## Soil and geology
Volcanic soils, with basalt and stony loam.

## Climate
Continental, with warm summers and cold winters, cooler than the Ararat Valley.

## History
The area around Ashtarak and Karmrashen has a long viticultural history and is home to local wineries.

## Production and wine styles
Mainly reds and whites from native grapes such as Areni and Voskehat.

## Key wines
- Aragatsotn red
- Aragatsotn white

## What to look for
- Volcanic soils give mineral character.
- A region to watch.
