# Domaine de Fontsainte

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A respected Corbières estate, best known for its Gris de Gris rosé.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Corbières]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Carignan]], [[Grenache]], [[Syrah]], [[Mourvèdre]]
> - **Styles:** [[Rosé]]

## About
A family domaine with a modern but traditional approach to Corbières, including old vine Carignan. The Gris de Gris is pale, dry and very food friendly.

## Known for
- Gris de Gris
- Corbières La Demoiselle
- Corbières Réserve
