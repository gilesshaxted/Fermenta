# Pétrus

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The most celebrated estate in Pomerol, producing rich, silky Merlot from a clay-rich plateau.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Pomerol]], [[Bordeaux]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Franc]]
> - **Styles:** [[Bordeaux blend]]

## About
Pétrus is a small property whose vineyard is almost entirely Merlot, planted on distinctive blue clay. The wine is dense, plush and very long lived, and is one of the most sought-after reds in the world.
