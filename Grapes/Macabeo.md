# Macabeo

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A light, fresh grape used in Cava and white Rioja, known as Viura in Rioja.

## Where it's from
Northeastern Spain. In Rioja it is called Viura, and in France it is called Macabeu.

## Where it grows
- **Penedès, Spain:** one of the three Cava grapes
- **Rioja, Spain:** as Viura, for white Rioja

## What it tastes like
- Apple, lemon and a slight almond note

## Classic wines
- Cava
- White Rioja

## Pairs with
- Tapas
- Seafood
- Light salads

## Good to know
- Ages well in oak in traditional white Rioja.
- Also grown in Roussillon.
