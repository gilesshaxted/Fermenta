# Canelones

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Uruguay's main wine region, close to Montevideo, and the heartland of Tannat.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Uruguay]]
> - **Part of:** [[South America]]
> - **Climate:** Maritime
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, loam, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tannat]], [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
Gently rolling land north of Montevideo, with many small family farms and vineyards.

## Soil and geology
Clay and limestone based soils with some sand and loam.

## Climate
Maritime, with humid conditions and rain at harvest, so site and vintage matter.

## History
Wine making here goes back to the nineteenth century when Basque and southern European settlers brought vines. Tannat arrived in the 1870s.

## Production and wine styles
Most of the country's wine is made here, from structured Tannat to fresh whites and rosé.

## Key wines
- Tannat
- Tannat blends
- Canelones Sauvignon Blanc

## What to look for
- About two thirds of Uruguay's vineyards lie here.
- Small family wineries are common.
