# Weingut Sepp Muster

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A biodynamic Styrian producer known for skin contact whites matured in amphora and large oak.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Austria]]
> - **Region:** [[Südsteiermark]], [[Styria]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Pinot Blanc]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]], [[Orange wine]]

## About
Based on the steep slopes of Südsteiermark and one of the pioneers of low intervention and orange wine in Austria. The wines are textural, earthy and individual.
