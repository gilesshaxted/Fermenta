# Furmint

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Hungary's great white grape, behind both Tokaji Aszú and a growing line of dry wines.

## Where it's from
Native to Hungary and grown in Tokaj for centuries. It ripens late, holds high acidity and takes noble rot easily, which is why it suits sweet wine so well.

## Where it grows
- **Tokaj, Hungary:** sweet Aszú and dry wines
- **Somló and Badacsony, Hungary:** mineral dry whites
- **Burgenland, Austria:** small plantings
- **Slovenia:** as Šipon

## What it tastes like
- Green apple, quince, honey and smoke

## Classic wines
- Tokaji Aszú
- Dry Tokaji Furmint
- Tokaji Szamorodni

## Pairs with
- Smoked fish
- Pork
- Creamy sauces
- Blue cheese (sweet wines)

## Good to know
- Dry versions are crisp and mineral, and age well.
- Sweet versions are among the longest lived wines in the world.
