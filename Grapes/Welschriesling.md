# Welschriesling

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A light, fresh white widely grown in central Europe, with no relation to Riesling.

## Where it's from
The name suggests a link to Riesling, but there is none. It is widely planted from Austria to Croatia, Hungary and Slovenia.

## Where it grows
- **Burgenland and Styria, Austria:** for dry whites and sweet wines
- **Croatia and Hungary:** as Graševina and Olaszrizling

## What it tastes like
- Green apple, lemon, herbs and white flowers

## Classic wines
- Neusiedlersee sweet wines
- Styrian Welschriesling

## Pairs with
- Fried fish
- Salads
- Light starters

## Good to know
- Also used for botrytis wines near Lake Neusiedl.
- Fresh and easy when young.
