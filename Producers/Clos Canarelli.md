# Clos Canarelli

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A quality led estate in Figari in the far south of Corsica.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Corsica]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Vermentino]], [[Sciaccarello]]

## About
A family domaine working organically in the south of the island. The whites are fresh and mineral, and the reds are savoury and spiced.

## Known for

- Figari Rouge
