# Quinta do Crasto

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A well known Douro estate on the north bank, with old vine field blends and a modern touch.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Portugal]]
> - **Region:** [[Douro]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Touriga Nacional]], [[Touriga Franca]], [[Tinto Cão]], [[Tempranillo]]

## About
Quinta do Crasto makes both Port and table wines from dramatic, terraced schist vineyards. The old vine wines are the stars.

- Maria Teresa
- Vinha da Ponte
