# Wynns Coonawarra Estate

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A classic Coonawarra producer, famous for Cabernet Sauvignon grown on red terra rossa soil.

> [!info] Quick facts

## About
A long established estate in Coonawarra, with some of the older vineyards in the region. The wines are cool and savoury, with fine structure and good ageing potential.
