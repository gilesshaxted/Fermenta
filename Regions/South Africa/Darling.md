# Darling

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cool coastal area near the Atlantic, with dry-farmed bush vines and fresh Sauvignon Blanc.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[South Africa]]
> - **Part of:** [[Africa]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** granite, shale, sandy loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Syrah]], [[Cinsault]], [[Chenin Blanc]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling hills close to the Atlantic coast, north of Cape Town.

## Soil and geology
Granite and shale, with sandy loams.

## Climate
Cool, with cold Atlantic air, mist and low rainfall.

## History
A small, rising area of boutique wine.

## Production and wine styles
Sauvignon Blanc, Syrah, Cinsault and Chenin Blanc.

## Key wines
- Darling Sauvignon Blanc

## What to look for
- Salty, fresh Sauvignon.
- Small production.
