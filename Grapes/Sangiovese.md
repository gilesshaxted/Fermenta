# Sangiovese

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Savoury, high-acid cherry red with grippy tannins. The backbone of Tuscany.

## Where it's from
Central Italy. Italy's most planted red grape, and the name is often said to come from sanguis Jovis, the blood of Jupiter.

## Where it grows
- **Chianti and Chianti Classico, Tuscany:** the heartland, savoury and food-friendly
- **Montalcino, Tuscany:** Brunello is 100% Sangiovese, aged for years
- **Montepulciano, Tuscany:** Vino Nobile, called Prugnolo Gentile locally
- **Scansano, Tuscany:** Morellino, fruitier and warmer
- **Romagna, Emilia-Romagna:** lighter, everyday styles

## What it tastes like
- **Fruit:** sour cherry, red plum
- **Savoury:** tomato leaf, dried herbs, balsamic
- **With age:** leather, tea leaf, tobacco

## Classic wines
- Chianti Classico
- Brunello di Montalcino
- Rosso di Montalcino
- Vino Nobile di Montepulciano
- Morellino di Scansano

## Pairs with
- Tomato-based pasta
- Pizza
- Roast meats
- Pecorino and parmesan

## Good to know
- Vino Nobile di Montepulciano is made from Sangiovese in the Tuscan town of Montepulciano. It has nothing to do with the Montepulciano grape of Abruzzo.
- High acidity is why it works so well with food.
