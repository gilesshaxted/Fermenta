# Upper Galilee

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The highest and coolest part of the Galilee, with mountain vineyards near Mount Meron.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Israel]]
> - **Part of:** [[Galilee]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** basalt, terra rossa, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]]

## Landscape and topography
A mountainous area in the north with vineyards at around 500 to 1,000 metres.

## Soil and geology
Rocky terra rossa, limestone and basalt.

## Climate
Cooler than the lower Galilee, with real seasonal contrasts and cold nights.

## History
Plantings at altitude were developed by Israel's quality-focused wineries from the 1980s on.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot, with Chardonnay and Sauvignon Blanc.

## Key wines
- Upper Galilee Cabernet Sauvignon
- Upper Galilee Chardonnay

## What to look for
- Cooler fruit, more freshness.
- Mountain whites are worth seeking.
