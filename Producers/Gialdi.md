# Gialdi

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A well known Ticino house, much associated with Merlot made in the southern Mendrisiotto.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Switzerland]]
> - **Region:** [[Ticino]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Merlot]]

## About
Based in Mendrisio, Gialdi works with Ticino's signature variety. Its wines are generally ripe and smooth, in a Merlot-focused style.

## Known for
- Sassi Grossi
- Merlot del Ticino
