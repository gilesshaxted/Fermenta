# Broglia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A respected Gavi estate whose La Meirana vineyard produces a refined, mineral white.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Gavi]], [[Piedmont]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cortese]]

## About
The Broglia family has long farmed Cortese in the Gavi hills. Their wines are crisp and floral with a stony edge, and they can improve with a few years of age.
