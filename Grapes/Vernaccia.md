# Vernaccia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The white grape of San Gimignano, with a notable almond-like finish.

## Where it's from
Vernaccia di San Gimignano was one of the first wines to be given DOC status in Italy, in 1966. The name may relate to vernacular, meaning local.

## Where it grows
- **San Gimignano, Italy:** the home

## What it tastes like
- Almond, lemon, apple and mineral

## Classic wines
- Vernaccia di San Gimignano

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Light pasta
- Tuscan antipasti

## Good to know
- Not related to the other Vernaccias of Italy.
- Riserva styles can age.
