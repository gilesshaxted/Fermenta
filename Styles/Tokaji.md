# Tokaji

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Hungary's famed sweet wine, made from botrytised Furmint and Hárslevelű.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Category:** Sweet
> - **Colour:** White
> - **Sweetness:** Sweet
> - **Country:** Hungary
> - **Main grapes:** [[Furmint]], [[Hárslevelű]], [[Muscat]]
> - **Regions:** [[Tokaj]]

## How it's made
Individually picked, noble rotted aszú berries are macerated in base wine or must. Sweetness was historically measured in puttonyos, a scale dropped in 2013, and Aszú now needs a minimum sugar level. Eszencia is the extremely sweet free run juice, and Szamorodni is made from whole bunches.

## Where it's made
The Tokaj hills in north east Hungary, with a few vineyards across the border in Slovakia.

## What it tastes like
- Apricot, honey, orange marmalade
- Very high acidity
- Long ageing

## Pairs with
- Foie gras
- Blue cheese
- Almond desserts

## Good to know
- Dry Furmint from Tokaj is a rising style of its own.
- Tokaj was one of the first places in the world to classify its vineyards, in 1737.
