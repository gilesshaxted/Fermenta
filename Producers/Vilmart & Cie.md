# Vilmart & Cie

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Rilly-la-Montagne grower known for rich, oak-fermented Champagne.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Champagne]], [[Montagne de Reims]]

> - **Styles:** [[Champagne (style)]]

## About
Vilmart ferments its wines in large oak foudres and avoids malolactic fermentation. The result is a full, toasty yet fresh style.

## Known for
- Grand Cellier
- Coeur de Cuvée
- Grand Cellier d'Or
