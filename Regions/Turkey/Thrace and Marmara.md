# Thrace and Marmara

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Turkey's largest wine region by output, in the northwest around the Sea of Marmara, with a cooler, more European feel.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Turkey]]
> - **Part of:** [[Asia]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, sand, loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Cinsault]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling hills and plains in the far northwest of Turkey, near the Greek and Bulgarian borders, along the Sea of Marmara.

## Soil and geology
Clay, limestone and sandy loam.

## Climate
Mediterranean with maritime and continental influences, cooler than the south, with moderate rainfall.

## History
The area has a long winemaking history from Thracian and Greek times. Modern wineries in Tekirdağ and Şarköy now make international-style wines.

## Production and wine styles
Dry reds and whites, led by international varieties such as Syrah, Merlot, Cabernet Sauvignon and Chardonnay, with some native grapes such as Papazkarası.

## Key wines
- Thrace red blends
- Şarköy wines
- Tekirdağ whites

## What to look for
- Good value reds.
- An easy introduction to Turkish wine.
