# Klein Constantia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Constantia estate best known for Vin de Constance, a famous sweet wine with centuries of history.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[South Africa]]
> - **Region:** [[Constantia]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Muscat]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]]

## About
A cool-climate winery close to Table Mountain, making both sweet and dry wines. The sweet wine is rich, fresh and long-lived.

- Metis Sauvignon Blanc
