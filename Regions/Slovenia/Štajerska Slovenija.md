# Štajerska Slovenija

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Slovenian Styria, a hilly district of steep vineyards and white wines in the style of neighbouring Austria.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Slovenia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Podravje]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** marl, sandstone, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Furmint]], [[Welschriesling]], [[Riesling]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Pinot Gris]]

## Landscape and topography
Steep, green hills around Maribor, Ptuj and Ormož, with vineyards on south-facing slopes.

## Soil and geology
Marl, sandstone and clay.

## Climate
Continental, with warm summers, cold winters and misty autumns that favour sweet wines.

## History
Winemaking here dates back to Roman times, and the area was part of the Austro-Hungarian Empire. Ormož and Jeruzalem are well known sub-areas.

## Production and wine styles
White wines from Welschriesling, Furmint (Šipon), Riesling, Sauvignon Blanc and Pinot Gris, plus sparkling and sweet wines.

## Key wines
- Štajerska Šipon
- Štajerska Laški Rizling
- Štajerska Rizling

## What to look for
- Crisp, aromatic whites.
- Sweet wines are well worth finding.
