# Rathfinny

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A large, modern, sustainability minded vineyard near the Sussex coast, making sparkling wine.

## About
Rathfinny sits on the South Downs above Cuckmere Haven, with chalk and flint soils. It makes traditional method sparkling wines with fine bubbles and lively acidity.

## Known for
- Classic Cuvée
- Blanc de Blancs
- Rosé
