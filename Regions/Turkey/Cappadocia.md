# Cappadocia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A dramatic volcanic landscape of fairy chimneys and cave cellars, and the home of the Emir grape.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Turkey]]
> - **Part of:** [[Central Anatolia]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** volcanic tuff, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Emir]], [[Kalecik Karası]], [[Syrah]]

## Landscape and topography
A high plateau of volcanic hills and valleys around Nevşehir and Ürgüp, at about 1,000 metres.

## Soil and geology
Volcanic tuff and ash, with clay.

## Climate
Dry continental, with cold winters and hot summers.

## History
Winemaking is very old here, and caves cut from the soft rock were used for storage. The tradition revived with modern wineries.

## Production and wine styles
Mineral, fresh Emir, and some reds, such as Kalecik Karası and Syrah.

## Key wines
- Cappadocia Emir
- Cappadocia red

## What to look for
- Crisp, mineral whites.
- Cave cellars to visit.
