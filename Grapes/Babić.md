# Babić

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Dalmatian red grown on the rocky, terraced slopes around Primošten.

## Where it's from
Grown around Primošten, where the stone-walled vineyards are protected as a landscape of rare beauty.

## Where it grows
- **Primošten, Croatia:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Black cherry, dried fruit, herbs and pepper

## Classic wines
- Primošten Babić

## Pairs with
- Grilled meat
- Stews
- Aged cheese

## Good to know
- Can be tannic and powerful.
- The terraced vineyards are very picturesque.
