# Serra do Sudeste

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A hilly southern region with granite soils and a fresher climate.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Brazil]]
> - **Part of:** [[South America]]
> - **Climate:** Humid subtropical
> - **Soils:** granite, sandy loam, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tannat]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Touriga Nacional]]

## Landscape and topography
Low hills in southern Rio Grande do Sul, between the coast and the Uruguayan border.

## Soil and geology
Granite based soils with sand and clay.

## Climate
Warm summers and moderate rain, with cooler nights than the lowlands.

## History
A newer area for wine, with plantings expanding in recent decades.

## Production and wine styles
Structured reds and some whites.

## Key wines
- Serra do Sudeste Tannat

## What to look for
- Often grouped with Campanha.
- A small but growing area.
