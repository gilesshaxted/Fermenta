# Château Mercian

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of Japan's biggest and best-known wine houses, with a Katsunuma base and a Nagano vineyard source.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Japan]]
> - **Region:** [[Katsunuma]], [[Yamanashi]], [[Nagano]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Koshu]], [[Merlot]], [[Muscat Bailey A]]

## About
Mercian is the wine arm of a large drinks group and has deep roots in Katsunuma. It makes everything from accessible Koshu to ambitious Nagano Merlot.

## Known for
- Kikyogahara Merlot
- Mariko Merlot
- Mercian Koshu
