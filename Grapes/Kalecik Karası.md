# Kalecik Karası

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A light, elegant Turkish red, sometimes compared with Pinot Noir.

## Where it's from
Named after the town of Kalecik, near Ankara, and revived after nearly dying out. It now makes some of Turkey's most elegant reds.

## Where it grows
- **Kalecik, Turkey:** the heartland
- **Cappadocia, Turkey:** also grown

## What it tastes like
- Red cherry, strawberry, spice and violet

## Classic wines
- Kalecik Karası

## Pairs with
- Poultry
- Lamb
- Mezze

## Good to know
- Often called Turkey's Pinot Noir.
- Best from cool sites.
