# Crémant

> [!abstract] At a glance
> French traditional method sparkling wine from outside Champagne.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Category:** Sparkling
> - **Colour:** Varies
> - **Sweetness:** Dry
> - **Country:** France
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Blanc]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chenin Blanc]], [[Mauzac]]
> - **Regions:** [[Alsace]], [[Burgundy]], [[Loire Valley]], [[Limoux]], [[Jura]], [[Bordeaux]]

## How it's made
Made by the same bottle fermentation as Champagne but with local grapes. Rules require hand harvesting, whole bunch pressing and at least nine months on lees.

## Where it's made
Each region has its own Crémant: Alsace, Burgundy, the Loire, Limoux, Jura, Bordeaux and others.

## What it tastes like
- Apple, pear, citrus
- Brioche from lees ageing
- Creamy and refined

## Pairs with
- Aperitif
- Shellfish
- Roast chicken
- Soft cheese

## Good to know
- Often much better value than Champagne.
- Crémant de Loire usually leads with Chenin Blanc.
