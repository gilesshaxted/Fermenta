# Cleto Chiarli

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of the oldest and best known Lambrusco houses, working in the area around Modena.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Modena and Reggio Emilia]], [[Emilia-Romagna]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Lambrusco]]
> - **Styles:** [[Lambrusco (style)]]

## About
The estate makes dry, fresh Lambrusco, including Sorbara and Grasparossa. It has helped show that Lambrusco can be a serious food wine.

## Known for
- Vecchia Modena Premium
- Lambrusco di Sorbara
- Brut de Noir
