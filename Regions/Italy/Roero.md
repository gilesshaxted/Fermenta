# Roero

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Sandy hills on the left bank of the Tanaro, home of Arneis whites and lighter Nebbiolo.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Piedmont]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** sand, marl, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Arneis]], [[Nebbiolo]]

## Landscape and topography
Steep, eroded hills and ravines on the left bank of the Tanaro.

## Soil and geology
Sandy, loose soils with marl and clay.

## Climate
Continental, with a slightly warmer feel than Barolo.

## History
Roero was promoted to DOCG in 2004.

## Production and wine styles
Roero Arneis (white) and Roero Rosso (Nebbiolo, lighter and earlier than Barolo).

## Key wines
- Roero Arneis
- Roero Rosso

## What to look for
- Arneis is peachy, floral and almond-edged.
- Nebbiolo from sand is soft and perfumed.

## Producers
- [[Matteo Correggia]]
- [[Vietti]]
