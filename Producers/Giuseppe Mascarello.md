# Giuseppe Mascarello

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Monchiero based estate whose Monprivato vineyard is a Barolo legend.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Barolo]], [[Piedmont]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Nebbiolo]], [[Barbera]], [[Dolcetto]]

## About
A traditional family estate that holds the Monprivato vineyard in Castiglione Falletto. Its Barolos are finely detailed and long lived, in a classic mould.

## Known for
- Barolo Monprivato
- Barolo Villero
- Barbera d'Alba Superiore
