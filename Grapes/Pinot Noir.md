# Pinot Noir

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Pale, perfumed and silky. Red fruit and earthiness, with high acidity and light tannin.

## Where it's from
Burgundy, where it has been grown for centuries. A thin-skinned, fussy grape that shows its site clearly.

## Where it grows
- **Burgundy, France:** the benchmark, from Gevrey-Chambertin to Volnay
- **Champagne, France:** a main grape alongside Chardonnay and Pinot Meunier
- **Willamette Valley, USA:** Oregon's signature red
- **Central Otago, New Zealand:** darker, riper fruit
- **Germany:** known as Spätburgunder
- **Adelaide Hills, Australia:** cool sites suit it

## What it tastes like
- **Cooler sites:** red cherry, cranberry, raspberry, violet
- **Warmer sites:** black cherry, plum, spice
- **With age:** mushroom, forest floor, truffle, game

## Classic wines
- Red Burgundy
- Champagne and Crémant
- Willamette Valley Pinot
- Central Otago Pinot
- German Spätburgunder

## Pairs with
- Salmon
- Roast duck
- Roast chicken
- Mushrooms
- Charcuterie

## Good to know
- Thin skins make it light in colour and tannin, and sensitive to where it grows.
- Called Pinot Nero in Italy and Spätburgunder in Germany.
