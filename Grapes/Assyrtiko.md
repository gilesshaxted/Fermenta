# Assyrtiko

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Santorini's volcanic grape, with piercing acidity and a salty edge.

## Where it's from
Santorini, where ungrafted vines are trained into basket shapes.

## Where it grows
- **Santorini, Greece:** the heartland
- **Macedonia and mainland Greece:** new plantings

## What it tastes like
- Lemon, sea salt, smoke and wet stone

## Classic wines
- Santorini Assyrtiko
- Nykteri

## Pairs with
- Grilled fish
- Seafood
- Feta

## Good to know
- Keeps high acidity even in heat.
- Ages brilliantly.
