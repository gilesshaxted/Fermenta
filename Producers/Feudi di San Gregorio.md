# Feudi di San Gregorio

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A large, ambitious Campania winery known for polished Taurasi and white wines from Irpinia.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Fiano di Avellino]], [[Taurasi]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Aglianico]], [[Fiano]], [[Greco]]

## About
A modern estate in the hills of Irpinia, with a wide range from fresh whites to serious reds. The style is clean, ripe and carefully made.

## Known for
- Serpico
- Taurasi Piano di Montevergine
- Fiano di Avellino Pietracalda
- Greco di Tufo Cutizzi
