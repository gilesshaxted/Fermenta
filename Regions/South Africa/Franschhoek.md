# Franschhoek

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A French-named valley in a spectacular mountain bowl, known for Sémillon, Cap Classique and boutique estates.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[South Africa]]
> - **Part of:** [[Africa]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** granite, shale, alluvial, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sémillon]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Syrah]]

## Landscape and topography
A tight valley surrounded by mountains, east of Stellenbosch.

## Soil and geology
Granite and shale on slopes, with alluvial soils on the floor.

## Climate
Warm, with mountain cooling, and rain on the slopes.

## History
Huguenot refugees arrived in 1688, bringing wine skills. Names such as La Motte and Boschendal date from this era.

## Production and wine styles
Sémillon, Cabernet, Chardonnay, Syrah and Cap Classique sparkling wine.

## Key wines
- Franschhoek Sémillon
- Cap Classique

## What to look for
- Waxy, honeyed Sémillon.
- Food and wine tourism is a big draw.
