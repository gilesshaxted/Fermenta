# Friulano

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Friuli's signature white, with almond, pear and a bitter finish.

## Where it's from
Friuli, formerly called Tocai Friulano.

## Where it grows
- **Collio and Colli Orientali, Italy:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Almond, pear and green herbs

## Classic wines
- Collio Friulano
- Colli Orientali Friulano

## Pairs with
- Prosciutto di San Daniele
- Asparagus
- Fish

## Good to know
- Renamed from Tocai in 2007 after a dispute with Hungary.
- Underrated.
