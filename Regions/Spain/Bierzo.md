# Bierzo

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Mountain-ringed vineyards in northwest Spain, famed for aromatic, lighter red Mencía.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** slate, clay, quartzite, alluvium
> - **Main grapes:** [[Mencía]], [[Godello]]

## Landscape and topography
A valley surrounded by mountains in León, with steep slate slopes and vines at 450 to 900 metres.

## Soil and geology
Slate and quartzite on hillsides, clay and alluvial soils in the valley.

## Climate
Continental, with Atlantic influence and sheltered conditions.

## History
Romans mined gold here. Álvaro Palacios and his nephew Ricardo Pérez Palacios helped to revive the region in the late 1990s.

## Production and wine styles
Mencía for red, Godello for white. Old bush vines on slate produce complex wines.

## Key wines
- Bierzo Mencía
- Bierzo Godello
- Bierzo Villa wines

## What to look for
- Raspberry, violets and a mineral edge.
- Often compared with Cabernet Franc or northern Rhône Syrah.
