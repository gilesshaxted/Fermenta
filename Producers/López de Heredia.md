# López de Heredia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A very traditional Haro house, loved by wine lovers for its pale, haunting, slow released reds and whites.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Region:** [[Rioja Alta]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tempranillo]], [[Grenache]], [[Graciano]], [[Carignan]], [[Macabeo]]

## About
Wines are aged in old oak and bottle for many years before release, from historic vineyards such as Tondonia. The reds are light in colour but intense and savoury, and the white Tondonia Reserva is a cult favourite.

## Known for
- Viña Tondonia Reserva
- Viña Bosconia
- Viña Gravonia
- Viña Tondonia Blanco Reserva
