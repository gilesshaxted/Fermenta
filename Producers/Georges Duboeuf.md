# Georges Duboeuf

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The best known négociant in Beaujolais, who did a great deal to make Beaujolais Nouveau famous.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Beaujolais]], [[Beaujolais Crus]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Gamay]]
> - **Styles:** [[Carbonic maceration]]

## About
A large house that buys from growers across the region and bottles a wide range, from simple Beaujolais to the ten crus. The wines are fruity, soft and easy to enjoy.

## Known for
- Beaujolais Nouveau
- Beaujolais-Villages
- Fleurie
- Moulin-à-Vent
