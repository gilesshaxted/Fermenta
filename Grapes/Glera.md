# Glera

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The grape behind Prosecco. Light, fresh and fruity, with green apple, pear and white flowers.

## Where it's from
North-east Italy. It was long called Prosecco, and renamed Glera in 2009 so that Prosecco could be protected as a place name.

## Where it grows
- **Veneto, Italy:** the heartland of Prosecco DOC
- **Friuli-Venezia Giulia, Italy:** Prosecco DOC also covers this area
- **Conegliano Valdobbiadene, Veneto:** the hills of the higher-quality DOCG

## What it tastes like
- **Fruit:** green apple, pear, white peach
- **Floral:** honeysuckle, acacia
- **Fresh:** citrus, almond, soft bubbles

## Classic wines
- Prosecco Spumante
- Prosecco Frizzante
- Conegliano Valdobbiadene Prosecco Superiore
- Cartizze

## Pairs with
- Aperitif
- Antipasti
- Light seafood
- Fried foods
- Sushi

## Good to know
- Mostly made by the tank (Charmat) method, which keeps it fresh and fruity.
- Spumante is fully sparkling, Frizzante lightly sparkling.
