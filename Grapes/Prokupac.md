# Prokupac

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Serbia's best known native red grape, grown in Župa and the south.

## Where it's from
An old Serbian grape grown for centuries, and the main red grape of the country. It is also found in Macedonia and Bulgaria in small amounts.

## Where it grows
- **Župa, Serbia:** the heartland
- **Negotin, Serbia:** also grown

## What it tastes like
- Red cherry, plum, spice and earth

## Classic wines
- Župa Prokupac

## Pairs with
- Grilled meat
- Stews
- Hard cheese

## Good to know
- Light to medium body.
- Old vines are valued by producers.
