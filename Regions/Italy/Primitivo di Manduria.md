# Primitivo di Manduria

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Powerful, jammy Primitivo from the Manduria area, a close relative of California Zinfandel.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Puglia]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** terra rossa, limestone, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Zinfandel|Primitivo]]

## Landscape and topography
Flat, sun-drenched land around Manduria in the Salento peninsula.

## Soil and geology
Red terra rossa and limestone.

## Climate
Hot, dry Mediterranean.

## History
Primitivo was shown to be genetically the same as Zinfandel in the 1990s, tracing back to Croatia's Crljenak Kaštelanski.

## Production and wine styles
100% Primitivo. Dolce Naturale is a sweet version.

## Key wines
- Primitivo di Manduria
- Primitivo di Manduria Dolce Naturale

## What to look for
- Plum, fig, spice and high alcohol.
- Great with barbecued meat.
