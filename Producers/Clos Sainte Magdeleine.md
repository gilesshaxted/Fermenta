# Clos Sainte Magdeleine

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A leading Cassis estate, set on the cliffs above the sea and known for its white wine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Cassis]]

## About
A family estate with vines high above the Mediterranean. The white is dry, mineral and saline, and works well with fish.
