# Vršac

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A hill range in the Banat near the Romanian border, with a long tradition of white wine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Serbia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** loess, sand, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Welschriesling]], [[Riesling]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Merlot]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills of the Vršac mountains in the Banat, rising from the plain.

## Soil and geology
Loess, sand and clay.

## Climate
Continental, with warm summers and cold winters.

## History
Vršac has a long winegrowing history, and was a centre of wine under the Habsburgs. It borders Romania's Banat.

## Production and wine styles
Mostly white wines, including Welschriesling, Riesling and Pinot Gris, plus some reds.

## Key wines
- Vršac Welschriesling
- Vršac Riesling

## What to look for
- Crisp, aromatic whites.
- An old wine town.
