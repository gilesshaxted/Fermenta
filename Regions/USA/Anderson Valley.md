# Anderson Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cool, narrow valley running towards the Pacific, known for Pinot Noir and sparkling wine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[Mendocino County]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** clay, loam, sandstone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Gewürztraminer]], [[Riesling]]

## Landscape and topography
A narrow, winding valley, 10 to 25 miles from the coast.

## Soil and geology
Clay loam and sandy soils with marine influence.

## Climate
Cool, with fog and Pacific winds, and large day to night swings.

## History
Roederer Estate planted here in the 1980s for sparkling wine, and Pinot Noir has since taken hold.

## Production and wine styles
Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, sparkling wine and aromatic whites.

## Key wines
- Anderson Valley Pinot Noir
- Roederer Estate sparkling

## What to look for
- Bright acidity and red fruit.
- Fine sparkling wine.
