# Château Tirecul La Gravière

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small, quality-led Monbazillac estate making rich, concentrated sweet wines.

> - **Country:** [[France]]

## About
Tirecul La Gravière is a family property regarded as one of the finest in Monbazillac, run on organic principles. The botrytised wines are deep and honeyed yet balanced by good acidity.
