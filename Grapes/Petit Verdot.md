# Petit Verdot

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Deep, inky and floral. A small but valuable part of Bordeaux blends.

## Where it's from
Bordeaux, where it ripens late and is used in tiny amounts.

## Where it grows
- **Médoc, France:** a touch in the Left Bank blend
- **Margaret River, Australia:** ripens better in the warm climate

## What it tastes like
- Dark blueberry, violet and firm tannin

## Classic wines
- Bordeaux blends
- Margaret River blends

## Pairs with
- Roast beef
- Lamb

## Good to know
- Adds colour, tannin and perfume to a blend.
- Does better in warm climates.
