# Trousseau

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Jura red with more colour and spice than Poulsard.

## Where it's from
Native to the Jura, and the same as Bastardo in Portugal. It ripens late and likes warm sites.

## Where it grows
- **Jura, France:** around Arbois
- **Portugal:** known as Bastardo

## What it tastes like
- Red cherry, spice, pepper and herbs

## Classic wines
- Arbois Trousseau

## Pairs with
- Game
- Charcuterie
- Comté

## Good to know
- Known as Bastardo in Portugal.
- Can age well.
