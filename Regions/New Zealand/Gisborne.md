# Gisborne

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A warm, sunny east-coast region, called the Chardonnay capital of New Zealand.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[New Zealand]]
> - **Part of:** [[Oceania]]
> - **Climate:** Warm maritime
> - **Soils:** alluvial, clay, silt
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Gewürztraminer]], [[Viognier]]

## Landscape and topography
Fertile coastal plains around Gisborne and the Poverty Bay flats.

## Soil and geology
Alluvial silts and clays.

## Climate
Sunny and warm, with high humidity.

## History
Vines have been planted since the 1870s, and Gisborne was once focused on bulk wine.

## Production and wine styles
Chardonnay, Pinot Gris, Gewürztraminer and Viognier.

## Key wines
- Gisborne Chardonnay

## What to look for
- Ripe, round and fruity.
- Often great value.
