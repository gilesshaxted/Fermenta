# Savagnin

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The Jura grape behind vin jaune, nutty and intense.

## Where it's from
The Jura and neighbouring eastern France. It is a very old grape related to Traminer.

## Where it grows
- **Jura, France:** the home and focus
- **Australia and New Zealand:** small plantings sometimes confused with Albariño

## What it tastes like
- **Ouillé:** fresh apple and mineral
- **Sous voile:** walnut, curry spice, dry sherry notes

## Classic wines
- Vin Jaune
- Château-Chalon
- Arbois Savagnin

## Pairs with
- Comté
- Chicken in cream and morels
- Mushroom dishes

## Good to know
- Vin jaune ages six years and three months under a veil of yeast.
- Needs food.
