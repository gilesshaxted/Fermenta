# Marsanne

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Rich, waxy and nutty. Often blended with Roussanne.

## Where it's from
The northern Rhône, around Hermitage and Saint-Joseph.

## Where it grows
- **Hermitage and Crozes-Hermitage, France:** the classic home
- **Victoria, Australia:** old vines in Goulburn Valley
- **Languedoc, France:** used in blends

## What it tastes like
- Almond, pear, honey and a waxy texture
- Gains complexity with age

## Classic wines
- Hermitage blanc
- Saint-Joseph blanc
- Crozes-Hermitage blanc
- Cassis

## Pairs with
- Roast chicken
- Creamy sauces
- Salmon

## Good to know
- Low acidity makes it feel rich and weighty.
- Good examples can age for decades.
