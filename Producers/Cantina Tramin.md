# Cantina Tramin

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cooperative in Tramin, the village that gave Gewürztraminer its name, known for aromatic whites.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Alto Adige]], [[Trentino-Alto Adige]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Gewürztraminer]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Lagrein]]

## About
Member growers supply fruit from some of the region's best known vineyards. The wines are precise and expressive, with floral and spicy character.

- Nussbaumer Gewürztraminer
