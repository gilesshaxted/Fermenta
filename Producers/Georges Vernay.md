# Georges Vernay

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A benchmark Condrieu estate, often credited with helping to save the appellation.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Condrieu]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Viognier]], [[Syrah]]

## About
A family domaine on the steep terraces of Condrieu, making Viognier that is perfumed but balanced rather than heavy. It also makes some Côte-Rôtie and Saint-Joseph.

## Known for
- Coteau de Vernon
- Les Chaillées de l'Enfer
- Maison Rouge
