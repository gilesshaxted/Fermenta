# Slovenska Istra

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small stretch of Adriatic coast and hinterland, known for Malvazija and Refosco.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Slovenia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Primorska]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** flysch, marl, sandstone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Malvasia]], [[Refosco]], [[Merlot]], [[Muscat]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills and coastal slopes around Koper and Izola, beside the Adriatic.

## Soil and geology
Flysch, marl and sandstone.

## Climate
Mediterranean, warm and sunny with sea breezes.

## History
Part of the wider Istrian wine tradition, shared with Croatia and Italy. Malvazija and Refosco are the main grapes.

## Production and wine styles
Malvazija for whites and Refosco for reds, with some Merlot and Muscat.

## Key wines
- Slovenska Istra Malvazija
- Slovenska Istra Refosk

## What to look for
- Fresh, citrusy whites.
- Good with seafood.
