# Elqui Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A high-altitude, desert valley at the edge of the Atacama, making intense Syrah and fresh whites.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Chile]]
> - **Part of:** [[South America]]
> - **Climate:** Arid
> - **Soils:** alluvial, granite, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Carmenère]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## Landscape and topography
A narrow Andean valley with vineyards at 400 to 2,000 metres.

## Soil and geology
Alluvial and granite soils.

## Climate
Sunny and dry, with clear skies, strong UV and cool nights.

## History
Pisco has long been made here, and quality wine plantings started in the 1990s.

## Production and wine styles
Syrah, Carmenère, Sauvignon Blanc and Cabernet.

## Key wines
- Elqui Syrah

## What to look for
- Peppery, concentrated Syrah.
- Altitude gives freshness.
