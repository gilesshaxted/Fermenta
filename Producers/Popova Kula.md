# Popova Kula

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family winery at Demir Kapija, known for local grapes and its distinctive tower cellar.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[North Macedonia]]
> - **Region:** [[Povardarie]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Vranac]], [[Chardonnay]]

## About
Set in the Povardarie region in the Vardar valley, this estate works with Vranec and with local varieties such as Stanušina. The wines are fruit-driven and good value.

## Known for
- Stanušina
- Vranec
