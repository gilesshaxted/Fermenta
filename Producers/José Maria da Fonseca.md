# José Maria da Fonseca

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A historic family company in the Setúbal peninsula, known for Periquita and Moscatel de Setúbal.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Portugal]]
> - **Region:** [[Portugal]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Castelão]], [[Muscat]]

## About
The company has long been a leading name for Setúbal's fortified Muscat and for the red Periquita. The cellars hold some very old wines.

## Known for
- Periquita
- Moscatel de Setúbal
- Colecção Privada Domingos Soares Franco
