# Coonawarra

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A narrow strip of red earth over limestone, Australia's finest Cabernet Sauvignon country.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Australia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Oceania]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** terra rossa, limestone, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Syrah]], [[Merlot]]
> - **Styles:** [[Bordeaux blend]]

## Landscape and topography
A flat, narrow strip of land about 15 km long, in the far south-east of South Australia.

## Soil and geology
A thin layer of red terra rossa soil over limestone.

## Climate
Cool maritime, with a long, slow growing season.

## History
John Riddoch planted vines in the 1890s, and the region became known for Cabernet in the mid-20th century.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon above all, with Shiraz and Merlot.

## Key wines
- Coonawarra Cabernet Sauvignon

## What to look for
- Blackcurrant, mint and fine tannins.
- Ageworthy.

- [[Wynns Coonawarra Estate]]
