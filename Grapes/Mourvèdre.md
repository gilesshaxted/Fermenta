# Mourvèdre

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Dark, meaty and tannic, with violets and wild herbs. Needs plenty of heat to ripen.

## Where it's from
Eastern Spain, where it is called Monastrell. The French name is usually linked to Murviedro, an old name for the town of Sagunto near Valencia.

## Where it grows
- **Bandol, Provence:** the star grape, made into long-lived reds
- **Southern Rhône, France:** the M in GSM blends
- **Jumilla and Alicante, Spain:** as Monastrell, big and ripe
- **Barossa Valley, Australia:** known as Mataro

## What it tastes like
- **Fruit:** blackberry, black plum, dark cherry
- **Savoury:** game, leather, earth, black pepper
- **Floral:** violet, wild herbs

## Classic wines
- Bandol rouge
- Jumilla Monastrell
- GSM blends

## Pairs with
- Game
- Lamb stew
- Cassoulet
- Barbecued meat

## Good to know
- Late ripening and heat-loving, so it thrives in hot, dry Mediterranean sites.
- Bandol rouge must contain at least 50% Mourvèdre.
