# Soalheiro

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A pioneering Alvarinho producer in Monção e Melgaço, in northern Vinho Verde.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Portugal]]
> - **Region:** [[Vinho Verde]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Albariño]]

## About
Soalheiro helped show that Alvarinho could make serious, mineral, age worthy white wine. The range includes still and sparkling wines.

## Known for
- Alvarinho Primeiras Vinhas
- Alvarinho Reserva
- Nature
