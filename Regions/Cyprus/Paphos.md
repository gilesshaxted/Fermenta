# Paphos

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A western district of hill villages, and the home area of the rare native red Maratheftiko.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Cyprus]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, chalk, clay, volcanic
> - **Main grapes:** [[Maratheftiko]], [[Xynisteri]], [[Mavro]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Syrah]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills and valleys in the west of Cyprus, running from the coast up to the Troodos foothills.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, chalk and clay soils, with volcanic soils in the Troodos foothills.

## Climate
Mediterranean, with hot summers and cooler nights at altitude, and a little more rainfall in the hills.

## History
The district is among the most important for the revival of local varieties in Cyprus. Maratheftiko, once nearly lost, has been a focus for growers here.

## Production and wine styles
Reds from Maratheftiko, Cabernet Sauvignon and Syrah, and whites from Xynisteri.

## Key wines
- Paphos Maratheftiko
- Paphos Xynisteri
- Paphos Cabernet Sauvignon

## What to look for
- Deep, spicy reds from Maratheftiko.
- Local grape revival.
