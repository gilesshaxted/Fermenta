# Valdespino

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A historic, traditional sherry house known for characterful, dry wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Region:** [[Jerez]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer

## About
Valdespino is respected for dry, vineyard based sherries, including fino from the Macharnudo estate. The wines are long aged and thoughtfully made.

## Known for
- Fino Inocente
- Amontillado Tío Diego
- Oloroso Don Gonzalo
