# Recaredo

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family run, biodynamic producer making long aged, bone dry sparkling wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Region:** [[Cava]], [[Penedès]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Xarel·lo]], [[Macabeo]], [[Parellada]]

## About
Recaredo disgorges by hand and ages its wines for years on the lees, aiming for depth and precision. Cava at its most serious.
