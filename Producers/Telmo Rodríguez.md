# Telmo Rodríguez

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A hugely influential winemaker who revived forgotten vineyards across Spain, from Rioja to Galicia and Toro.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Region:** [[Rioja Alavesa]], [[Spain]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tempranillo]], [[Grenache]], [[Godello]], [[Mencía]]

## About
Telmo Rodríguez works with old vines in several regions, emphasising local grapes and a fresher, less oaky style. The Remelluri link keeps him close to Rioja while his projects stretch across the country.

## Known for
- Lanzaga
