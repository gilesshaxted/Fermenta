# Le Macchiole

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small Bolgheri estate known for single variety Super Tuscans, especially Cabernet Franc.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Bolgheri]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Merlot]], [[Syrah]]
> - **Styles:** [[Super Tuscan]]

## About
A family estate that showed Bolgheri could do more than Cabernet Sauvignon blends. The wines are finely made and show real individual character.

- Messorio
- Paleo Rosso
