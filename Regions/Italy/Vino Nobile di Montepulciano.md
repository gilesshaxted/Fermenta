# Vino Nobile di Montepulciano

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Vino Nobile di Montepulciano: Sangiovese (here called Prugnolo Gentile) from a Renaissance hill town.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Tuscany]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** clay, sand, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sangiovese]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills around the walled town of Montepulciano in southern Tuscany.

## Soil and geology
Sand and clay with limestone, and some marine fossils.

## Climate
Mediterranean with a touch of altitude.

## History
Praised by Francesco Redi in the 17th century as the king of all wines. DOCG status arrived in 1980.

## Production and wine styles
Minimum 70% Sangiovese (Prugnolo Gentile). Not to be confused with the grape Montepulciano used in Abruzzo.

## Key wines
- Vino Nobile di Montepulciano
- Rosso di Montepulciano

## What to look for
- Plum, cherry, violet and tea leaf, with firm tannins.
- Often better value than Brunello.

- [[Avignonesi]]
- [[Poliziano]]
