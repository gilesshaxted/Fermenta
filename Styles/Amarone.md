# Amarone

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Rich, powerful red made from dried grapes in Valpolicella.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Category:** Still
> - **Colour:** Red
> - **Sweetness:** Dry
> - **Country:** Italy
> - **Main grapes:** [[Corvina]], [[Rondinella]], [[Molinara]]
> - **Regions:** [[Valpolicella]], [[Veneto]]

## How it's made
Corvina, Rondinella and Molinara are dried for months until they lose up to 40% of their weight, then fermented to dryness. The wine is full bodied and high in alcohol. Ripasso is made by refermenting regular Valpolicella on the used Amarone skins.

## Where it's made
The hills of Valpolicella in Veneto.

## What it tastes like
- Dried cherry, fig, chocolate
- Spice and bitter almond
- Full bodied and warming

## Pairs with
- Braised beef
- Aged cheese
- Game

## Good to know
- Alcohol is often 15% or more.
- Ripasso is nicknamed the baby Amarone.
