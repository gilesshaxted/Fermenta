# Limarí Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A dry, cool northern valley with limestone soils and some of Chile's best Chardonnay.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Chile]]
> - **Part of:** [[South America]]
> - **Climate:** Arid maritime
> - **Soils:** limestone, calcareous clay, alluvial
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Syrah]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Pinot Noir]]

## Landscape and topography
A valley with coastal influence and hills of limestone.

## Soil and geology
Calcareous soils, a rare feature in Chile.

## Climate
Very dry, sunny, with cool ocean influence.

## History
Famed for the Quebrada Seca vineyard, with limestone soils that give mineral wines.

## Production and wine styles
Chardonnay, Syrah, Sauvignon Blanc and Pinot Noir.

## Key wines
- Limarí Chardonnay
- Limarí Syrah

## What to look for
- Salty minerality.
- Great acidity.
