# Domaine de la Mordorée

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A well regarded southern Rhône domaine making serious Tavel rosé alongside Châteauneuf and Lirac.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Tavel]], [[Châteauneuf-du-Pape]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grenache]], [[Cinsault]], [[Syrah]], [[Mourvèdre]]
> - **Styles:** [[Rosé]]

## About
A family domaine farming organically and biodynamically. Its Tavel is a full-bodied, food friendly rosé that shows how substantial the style can be.

## Known for
- La Dame Rousse
- Châteauneuf-du-Pape Reine des Bois
- Lirac
