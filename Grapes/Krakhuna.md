# Krakhuna

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A fuller, aromatic Imeretian white, often blended with Tsolikouri.

## Where it's from
A native grape of Imereti, and often used alongside Tsolikouri and Tsitska.

## Where it grows
- **Imereti, Georgia:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Peach, apricot, white flowers and citrus

## Classic wines
- Imereti Krakhuna

## Pairs with
- Poultry
- Fish
- Soft cheese

## Good to know
- Gives body to blends.
- Often made with some skin contact.
