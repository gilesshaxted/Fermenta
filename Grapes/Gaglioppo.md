# Gaglioppo

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Calabria's main red grape, with firm tannins and savoury character.

## Where it's from
Grown in Calabria since ancient times, mainly around Cirò on the Ionian coast. The wine was said to be given to Olympic athletes in antiquity, though this is a tradition more than a proven fact.

## Where it grows
- **Cirò, Italy:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Red cherry, dried herbs, earth and spice

## Classic wines
- Cirò Rosso

## Pairs with
- Grilled meat
- Hard cheese
- Pasta with ragù

## Good to know
- Pale in colour, yet firm in tannin.
- Often compared with Nebbiolo.
