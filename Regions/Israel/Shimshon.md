# Shimshon

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A central region between the coast and the Judean foothills, also called Samson, with warm conditions.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Israel]]
> - **Part of:** [[Asia]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, sand, loess
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Carignan]], [[Argaman]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
A transition zone of foothills and plains between Tel Aviv and Jerusalem.

## Soil and geology
Clay, limestone, loess and sandy soils.

## Climate
Warmer than the hills, with hot summers, some sea influence and lower rainfall.

## History
The area includes the Latrun valley and other places with long viticultural histories. Modern wineries use fruit from the region and elsewhere in Israel.

## Production and wine styles
Reds from Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Carignan and Argaman, with some whites.

## Key wines
- Shimshon Cabernet Sauvignon
- Shimshon red blends

## What to look for
- Riper, rounder reds.
- A place to meet Argaman.
