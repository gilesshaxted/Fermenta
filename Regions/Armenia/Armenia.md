# Armenia

Part of [[Asia]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of the world's oldest winemaking places, with high-altitude vineyards, native grapes like Areni and a strong brandy tradition.

## How wine is organised here
- Wines are mostly labelled by grape and by region, such as Vayots Dzor or Aragatsotn.
- Areni is the main native red grape, and Voskehat the main native white.
- Armenia is also well known for its brandy.
