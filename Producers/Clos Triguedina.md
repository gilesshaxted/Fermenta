# Clos Triguedina

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A historic Cahors estate known for traditional black wine in a rich, structured style.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Cahors]], [[South-West France]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Malbec]], [[Merlot]], [[Tannat]]

## About
Clos Triguedina is a long-established family property in the Lot valley. It is best known for its dark, tannic Malbec made in the old Cahors manner, including cuvées for long ageing.
