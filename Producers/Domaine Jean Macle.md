# Domaine Jean Macle

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Château-Chalon estate regarded as a standard-bearer for the Jura's famous vin jaune.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Arbois and Château-Chalon]], [[Jura]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Savagnin]], [[Chardonnay]]
> - **Styles:** [[Vin Jaune]]

## About
Macle ages Savagnin under a veil of yeast in barrel for many years. The vin jaune is nutty, saline and very long lived.
