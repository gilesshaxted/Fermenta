# Barossa Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Australia's most celebrated Shiraz region, with some of the oldest productive vines in the world.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Australia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Oceania]]
> - **Climate:** Warm mediterranean
> - **Soils:** red-brown earth, sandy loam, clay, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Grenache]], [[Mourvèdre]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Sémillon]]
> - **Styles:** [[GSM]]

## Landscape and topography
A broad valley with rolling hills, north-east of Adelaide, at 200 to 350 metres.

## Soil and geology
Varied: red-brown earths, sandy loams and clay, with some limestone.

## Climate
Warm and dry, with hot summers and cool nights.

## History
German-speaking Silesian settlers arrived in the 1840s, planting vines that survive today. Ancient vineyards escaped phylloxera because South Australia was spared.

## Production and wine styles
Shiraz (Syrah) leads, with Grenache, Mourvèdre (Mataro), Cabernet Sauvignon and Sémillon.

## Key wines
- Barossa Shiraz
- GSM blends
- Barossa Semillon

## What to look for
- Rich dark fruit, pepper and chocolate.
- Old-vine wines are the highlight.
