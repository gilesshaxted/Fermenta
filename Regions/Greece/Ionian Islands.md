# Ionian Islands

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Islands off the west coast with cool maritime conditions and Robola of Cephalonia.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Greece]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Robola]], [[Mavrodaphne]], [[Vertzami]], [[Tsaoussi]]

## Landscape and topography
Mountainous islands such as Cephalonia, Corfu and Zakynthos, with vineyards on high limestone slopes.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, clay and sandy soils.

## Climate
Mediterranean, with cool sea breezes and heavier rainfall than elsewhere in Greece.

## History
Venetian rule shaped the islands' culture. Robola of Cephalonia is the best known wine.

## Production and wine styles
Robola for a mineral, citrusy white, and Mavrodaphne, Vertzami and Tsaoussi among others.

## Key wines
- Robola of Cephalonia
- Mavrodaphne of Cephalonia

## What to look for
- Crisp, mineral whites.
- A small, scenic area.
