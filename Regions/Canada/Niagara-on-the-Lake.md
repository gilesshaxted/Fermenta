# Niagara-on-the-Lake

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The lakeside heart of the Niagara Peninsula, with many of its best known icewine estates.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Canada]]
> - **Part of:** [[Niagara Peninsula]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** clay, loam, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Vidal]], [[Riesling]], [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Merlot]]
> - **Styles:** [[Icewine and Eiswein]]

## Landscape and topography
Flat land right beside Lake Ontario, near the mouth of the Niagara River.

## Soil and geology
Clay and loam with sand.

## Climate
Warmest part of the peninsula, with long lake moderated autumns.

## History
The first estates of the modern Ontario wine industry were planted here.

## Production and wine styles
Icewine, late harvest and table wines from Riesling, Chardonnay and Cabernet Franc.

## Key wines
- Niagara-on-the-Lake Icewine
- Cabernet Franc
- Chardonnay

## What to look for
- Home to many icewine producers.
- Visit in winter for the harvest.
