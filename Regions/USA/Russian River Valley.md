# Russian River Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A foggy, cool river valley celebrated for Pinot Noir and Chardonnay.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[Sonoma County]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** loam, sandy loam, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Zinfandel]]

## Landscape and topography
A broad valley along the Russian River, open to cool Pacific air through the Petaluma Gap and river gap.

## Soil and geology
Well-drained sandy and clay loam, including the Goldridge series.

## Climate
Morning fog and cool afternoons, with warmer areas upriver.

## History
Became an AVA in 1983 and a Pinot Noir hub for producers like Williams Selyem.

## Production and wine styles
Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and old-vine Zinfandel.

## Key wines
- Russian River Pinot Noir
- Russian River Chardonnay

## What to look for
- Ripe red cherry, cola and spice.
- Rich but fresh Chardonnay.

## Producers
- [[Kistler Vineyards]]
- [[Williams Selyem]]
