# Nino Negri

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The best known name in Valtellina, working the steep terraced slopes of the Alps with Nebbiolo.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]

> - **Main grapes:** [[Nebbiolo]]
> - **Styles:** [[Passito]]

## About
Negri farms and buys fruit on the stony terraces above the Adda river. The wines are lighter and more fragrant than Barolo, and the dried grape Sfursat is a local speciality.

## Known for
- Sfursat 5 Stelle
- Inferno Mazér
- Quadrio
