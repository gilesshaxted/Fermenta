# Domaine le Sang des Cailloux

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A leading Vacqueyras domaine, known for generous but fresh southern Rhône reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Vacqueyras]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer

## About
A family estate working organically, making wines with good concentration and fine tannins that often compare well with Gigondas.
