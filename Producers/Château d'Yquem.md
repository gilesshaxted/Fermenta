# Château d'Yquem

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The benchmark of Sauternes, a sweet wine of great richness, acidity and longevity.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Sauternes]], [[Bordeaux]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sémillon]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]]
> - **Styles:** [[Sauternes (style)]], [[Noble rot wines]]

## About
Yquem is the only Premier Cru Supérieur of the 1855 classification of Sauternes. Grapes are picked selectively, berry by berry, as noble rot develops. The wines are luscious yet vibrant and can age for many decades.
