## About
Hush Heath makes traditional method wines in the Weald of Kent, alongside still wines and cider. The sparkling rosé is a signature, with fresh red berry fruit.

## Known for
- Balfour Brut Rosé
- Balfour Blanc de Blancs
- Leslie's Reserve
