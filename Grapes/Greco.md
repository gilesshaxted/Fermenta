# Greco

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Structured, mineral white from Campania, thought to come from ancient Greece.

## Where it's from
Campania, probably brought by the Greeks.

## Where it grows
- **Tufo, Campania:** the best-known home

## What it tastes like
- Peach, almond and flinty mineral

## Classic wines
- Greco di Tufo

## Pairs with
- Shellfish
- Roast fish
- Mozzarella

## Good to know
- Sulphur-rich soils give a mineral tang.
- Ages well.
