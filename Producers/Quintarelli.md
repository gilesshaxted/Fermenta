# Quintarelli

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A legendary Negrar estate that made traditional Amarone famous among collectors.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Valpolicella]], [[Veneto]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Corvina]], [[Rondinella]], [[Molinara]], [[Cabernet Franc]]
> - **Styles:** [[Amarone]]

## About
Giuseppe Quintarelli worked slowly, drying grapes for long periods and ageing in large old casks. The wines are intense, complex and made with great patience.

## Known for
- Amarone della Valpolicella Classico
- Alzero
- Recioto della Valpolicella
- Rosso Ca' del Merlo
