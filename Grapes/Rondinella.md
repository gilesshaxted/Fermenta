# Rondinella

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A supporting red grape of the Verona region, adding colour and freshness to Valpolicella and Bardolino.

## Where it's from
Grown around Verona for a long time. It works well in drying for Amarone.

## Where it grows
- **Valpolicella, Italy:** blended with Corvina
- **Bardolino, Italy:** a main partner

## What it tastes like
- Cherry, spice, almond and herbs

## Classic wines
- Valpolicella
- Amarone
- Bardolino

## Pairs with
- Pasta
- Roast pork
- Soft cheese

## Good to know
- Rarely bottled on its own.
- Dries well for Amarone.
