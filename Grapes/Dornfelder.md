# Dornfelder

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A dark, fruity red grape created in Germany, easy to drink and widely planted.

## Where it's from
Developed in 1955 at Weinsberg in Württemberg, and named after Immanuel Dornfeld. It became popular in the 1990s.

## Where it grows
- **Pfalz and Rheinhessen, Germany:** the biggest plantings

## What it tastes like
- Black cherry, plum, spice and violet

## Classic wines
- Pfalz Dornfelder

## Pairs with
- Sausages
- Roast pork
- Pizza

## Good to know
- Deeply coloured and low in tannin.
- Quality varies from simple to oak-aged.
