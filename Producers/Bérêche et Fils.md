# Bérêche et Fils

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Ludes family grower admired for fine, lively wines from the northern Montagne de Reims.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Champagne]], [[Montagne de Reims]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Pinot Meunier]]
> - **Styles:** [[Champagne (style)]]

## About
The Bérêche brothers farm their parcels with care and ferment partly in oak. The wines are fresh, expressive and finely drawn.

## Known for
- Brut Réserve
- Le Cran
- Rilly La Montagne
- Campania Remensis Rosé
