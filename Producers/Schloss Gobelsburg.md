# Schloss Gobelsburg

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A historic monastery-linked estate that became a modern leader in the Kamptal.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Austria]]
> - **Region:** [[Kamptal]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grüner Veltliner]], [[Riesling]]

## About
Based at an old abbey estate near Langenlois, with vines in the best Kamptal sites. The Tradition range revives older winemaking approaches, and the wines are elegant and mineral.

- Tradition
- Heiligenstein Riesling
