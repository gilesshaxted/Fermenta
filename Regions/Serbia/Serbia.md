# Serbia

Part of [[Europe]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A country with a long winemaking past, led by the native red Prokupac and aromatic whites from the north.

## How wine is organised here
- Wine is grouped into regions and sub-areas, with Vojvodina in the north and Central Serbia to the south.
- Labels show the grape variety, vintage and often the area, such as Župa or Fruška Gora.
