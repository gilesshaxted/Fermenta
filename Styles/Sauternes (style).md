# Sauternes (style)

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Luscious Bordeaux dessert wine from botrytised Sémillon.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Category:** Sweet
> - **Colour:** White
> - **Sweetness:** Sweet
> - **Country:** France
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sémillon]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Muscadelle]]
> - **Regions:** [[Sauternes]], [[Bordeaux]], [[Bergerac and Monbazillac]]

## How it's made
Autumn mists encourage noble rot, which shrivels the grapes and concentrates sugar and acidity. Grapes are picked in several passes. Wines are fermented and aged in oak.

## Where it's made
The communes of Sauternes, Barsac, Bommes, Fargues and Preignac, on the Garonne in Bordeaux.

## What it tastes like
- Apricot, honey, saffron, orange peel
- Sweetness balanced by acidity
- Long ageing potential

## Pairs with
- Foie gras
- Blue cheese
- Fruit tarts
- Roquefort

## Good to know
- Château d'Yquem is the most famous estate.
- Monbazillac, in Bergerac, makes a similar style at lower prices.
