# Vacqueyras

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Gigondas's slightly softer neighbour, offering good value and a spicy, generous style.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Rhône Valley]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, sand, galets roulés
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grenache]], [[Syrah]], [[Mourvèdre]]
> - **Styles:** [[GSM]]

## Landscape and topography
Terraces and plains between the Dentelles de Montmirail and the Ouvèze river.

## Soil and geology
Sand, clay and limestone, with pebbly terraces.

## Climate
Mediterranean.

## History
Promoted to Cru status in 1990.

## Production and wine styles
Grenache-led red blends, plus rosé and a small amount of white.

## Key wines
- Vacqueyras rouge
- Vacqueyras blanc

## What to look for
- Ripe fruit, spice and garrigue, a little softer than Gigondas.
- Often excellent value.
