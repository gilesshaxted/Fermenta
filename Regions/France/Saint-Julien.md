# Saint-Julien

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Small, consistent and prized for its balance between Pauillac's power and Margaux's finesse.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Bordeaux]]
> - **Climate:** Maritime
> - **Soils:** gravel, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Petit Verdot]]

## Landscape and topography
Compact, gentle gravel slopes between Pauillac and Margaux, running down to the Gironde.

## Soil and geology
Deep gravel over clay and sand, with good drainage.

## Climate
Maritime, with the estuary helping to even out vintages.

## History
There are no First Growths here, but there is a high proportion of Second Growths, including three Léoville estates.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon leads, with Merlot and smaller amounts of Cabernet Franc and Petit Verdot. Red only.

## Key wines
- Léoville-Las Cases
- Ducru-Beaucaillou
- Gruaud-Larose
- Léoville Barton

## What to look for
- Known for consistency from year to year.
- Cedar, blackcurrant and a polished texture.
