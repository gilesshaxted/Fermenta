# Guerrouane

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A designated appellation near Meknès, known for its sturdy reds and rosés.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Morocco]]
> - **Part of:** [[Meknès]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Grenache]], [[Cinsault]]

## Landscape and topography
Vineyards on gentle hills near Meknès.

## Soil and geology
Clay and limestone.

## Climate
Warm and dry, with cooler nights.

## History
Guerrouane is one of Morocco's older quality designations, with a reputation for red and rosé wines.

## Production and wine styles
Reds, rosés and some whites, mostly from Grenache, Cinsault, Cabernet Sauvignon and Syrah.

## Key wines
- Guerrouane red
- Guerrouane rosé

## What to look for
- Ripe, rustic reds.
- A classic Moroccan name.
