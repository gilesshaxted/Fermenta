# Podravje

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Slovenia's largest wine region in the north east, with aromatic, crisp whites and a strong sweet wine tradition.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Slovenia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** marl, sandstone, clay, gravel, loess
> - **Main grapes:** [[Welschriesling]], [[Furmint]], [[Riesling]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling hills and steep slopes along the Drava river, bordering Austria, Hungary and Croatia, with the Pannonian plain to the east.

## Soil and geology
Marl, sandstone, clay and loess, with gravel in places.

## Climate
Continental, with warm summers, cold winters and cooler hill sites that keep acidity.

## History
Maribor has one of the oldest vines in the world on its riverfront, and the region has a long history of white winemaking. It sits beside Austrian Styria and shares many styles.

## Production and wine styles
Dry, aromatic whites from Welschriesling, Riesling, Sauvignon Blanc and Furmint (Šipon), plus sparkling wine and sweet wines made from botrytised grapes.

## Key wines
- Štajerska Slovenija
- Prekmurje
- Maribor

## What to look for
- Crisp, fruity whites, and sweet wines of real quality.
- Furmint is called Šipon here.
