# Cinti Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A high valley in Chuquisaca known for Singani and small scale wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Bolivia]]
> - **Part of:** [[South America]]
> - **Climate:** Semi-arid
> - **Soils:** sandy, alluvial, stony
> - **Main grapes:** [[Muscat of Alexandria]], [[Malbec]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## Landscape and topography
A dry valley at altitude north of Tarija, with terraced vineyards.

## Soil and geology
Sandy, alluvial and stony soils.

## Climate
Dry with strong sun and cool nights.

## History
Cinti has long produced grapes and Singani, and is a traditional centre for the spirit.

## Production and wine styles
Small volumes of wine alongside Singani.

## Key wines
- Cinti Singani
- Cinti reds

## What to look for
- Better known for Singani.
- Very high altitude vines.
