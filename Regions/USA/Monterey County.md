# Monterey County

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A large, windy coastal county, producing much of California's Chardonnay and Pinot.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[North America]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** gravel, granite, loam, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Syrah]], [[Riesling]]

## Landscape and topography
Long valleys running from Monterey Bay inland, with hillsides and benches.

## Soil and geology
Granitic, gravelly and sandy soils, varying by area.

## Climate
Cool and windy, with a long growing season.

## History
Large plantings began in the 1960s. Santa Lucia Highlands has since become a quality focus.

## Production and wine styles
Chardonnay, Pinot Noir, Syrah and Riesling.

## Key wines
- Santa Lucia Highlands Pinot Noir
- Monterey Chardonnay

## What to look for
- Ripe, bright fruit and good acidity.
- Plenty of everyday value.
