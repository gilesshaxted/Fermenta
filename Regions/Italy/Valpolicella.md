# Valpolicella

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Home of Amarone, a rich dried-grape red, and the lighter Valpolicella and Ripasso.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Veneto]]
> - **Climate:** Temperate
> - **Soils:** limestone, basalt, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Corvina]]
> - **Styles:** [[Passito]], [[Amarone]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills and valleys north of Verona, rising towards the Lessini Mountains.

## Soil and geology
Limestone and clay with some volcanic basalt.

## Climate
Temperate, with cool mountain air and Lake Garda's influence.

## History
Recioto, a sweet dried-grape wine, is the oldest style. Amarone appeared as a dry version in the 20th century, and the DOCG was recognised in 2010.

## Production and wine styles
Corvina, Corvinone, Rondinella and sometimes Molinara. Grapes are dried for months (appassimento) for Amarone and Recioto. Ripasso is Valpolicella re-fermented on Amarone skins.

## Key wines
- Amarone della Valpolicella
- Valpolicella Ripasso
- Recioto della Valpolicella
- Valpolicella Classico

## What to look for
- Amarone is rich, dark, dried fruit and chocolate, often 15% alcohol or more.
- Ripasso is a more affordable mini-Amarone.
