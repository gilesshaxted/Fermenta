# Altesse

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A fine, aromatic white from the Alps, known in Savoie as Roussette.

## Where it's from
A Savoie speciality, said by legend to have come from Cyprus. It makes the wine called Roussette de Savoie.

## Where it grows
- **Savoie, France:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Almond, honey, citrus and herbs

## Classic wines
- Roussette de Savoie
- Roussette de Savoie Marestel

## Pairs with
- Cheese fondue
- Trout
- Poultry

## Good to know
- Also called Roussette.
- A rare Alpine white.
