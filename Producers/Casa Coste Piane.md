# Casa Coste Piane

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A tiny Valdobbiadene grower reviving the old col fondo style of cloudy, bottle fermented Prosecco.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Prosecco]], [[Veneto]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Glera]]
> - **Styles:** [[Prosecco (style)]], [[Natural wine]]

## About
Run on traditional lines with minimal intervention, this small estate bottles Prosecco that finishes its fermentation in the bottle. The wines are dry, savoury and very different from mainstream Prosecco.

## Known for
- Prosecco Col Fondo
- Prosecco Brut
