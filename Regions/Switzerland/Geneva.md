# Geneva

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Switzerland's third largest wine canton, ringed by France and varied in its grape plantings.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Switzerland]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** gravel, moraine, clay, molasse
> - **Main grapes:** [[Gamay]], [[Chasselas]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Gamaret]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
Gentle hills and plains around the city and the lake, mostly low lying, in several separate pockets.

## Soil and geology
Glacial moraine, gravel, clay and molasse.

## Climate
Mild for Switzerland, with a lake influence and relatively low rainfall.

## History
Geneva has grown vines for many centuries, and its wines were long drunk locally. Modern growers have added plenty of newer varieties.

## Production and wine styles
Reds, led by Gamay and Pinot Noir, with Gamaret and Merlot, plus Chasselas, Chardonnay and aromatic whites. Many wines are blends.

## Key wines
- Geneva Gamay
- Geneva Chasselas
- Gamaret

## What to look for
- Soft, fruity Gamay.
- More variety than elsewhere.
