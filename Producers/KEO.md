# KEO

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A historic Limassol house, famous for Commandaria and for Cyprus's best known everyday wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Cyprus]]
> - **Region:** [[Commandaria]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Xynisteri]], [[Mavro]]
> - **Styles:** [[Vin doux naturel]]

## About
One of the island's oldest and largest producers, it buys grapes from villages across the hills. It makes sweet Commandaria and a wide table wine range.
