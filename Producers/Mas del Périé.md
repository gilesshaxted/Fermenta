# Mas del Périé

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small Cahors estate that has helped bring a fresher, low intervention style of Malbec to the fore.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Cahors]], [[South-West France]]

> - **Main grapes:** [[Malbec]]

## About
Mas del Périé is a family domaine farmed organically and with minimal intervention in the cellar. The wines show juicy fruit and drinkability alongside Cahors structure.
