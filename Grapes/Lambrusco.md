# Lambrusco

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family of grapes behind fizzy, dry red wine.

## Where it's from
Emilia-Romagna, around Modena. The grape family is ancient.

## Where it grows
- **Modena and Reggio Emilia, Italy:** Sorbara, Grasparossa and Salamino

## What it tastes like
- Cherry, violets and fresh, frothy acidity

## Classic wines
- Lambrusco di Sorbara
- Lambrusco Grasparossa

## Pairs with
- Prosciutto
- Parmesan
- Tortellini

## Good to know
- Dry (secco) is the style to look for.
- Serve slightly chilled.
