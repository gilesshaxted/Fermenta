# Kent

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The Garden of England, with some of the warmest, sunniest conditions in the country and a fast-growing wine scene.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[England]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** chalk, clay, greensand, sandstone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Pinot Meunier]], [[Bacchus]]
> - **Styles:** [[English sparkling wine]]

## Landscape and topography
Gentle chalk and clay hills near the Channel, including the North Downs and the Weald.

## Soil and geology
Chalk, clay, greensand and sandstone.

## Climate
One of the warmest and driest areas of England, with mild conditions in the south-east.

## History
Chapel Down and Gusbourne helped establish Kent as a leading region. Orchards and hop gardens have been joined by vineyards.

## Production and wine styles
Mostly sparkling wine, plus still Bacchus, Chardonnay and Pinot Noir.

## Key wines
- Kent sparkling
- Kent Bacchus
- Kent Pinot Noir

## What to look for
- Fresh, apple-fruited sparkling.
- Good Bacchus has elderflower and gooseberry.
