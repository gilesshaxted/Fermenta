# Ribolla Gialla

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A crisp, high-acid white from Friuli and across the border into Slovenia, also made as orange wine.

## Where it's from
Grown on the hills of Friuli and western Slovenia for many centuries. Today it is a flagship of the Collio, where skin-contact versions have made it fashionable.

## Where it grows
- **Collio, Italy:** the heartland
- **Colli Orientali del Friuli, Italy:** fresh, mineral styles
- **Slovenia:** where it is called Rebula

## What it tastes like
- Lemon, white flowers, green apple and mineral

## Classic wines
- Collio Ribolla Gialla
- Orange Ribolla

## Pairs with
- Prosciutto
- Fish
- Light cheese

## Good to know
- Skin-contact Ribolla is a Friulian speciality.
- Also makes good sparkling wine.
