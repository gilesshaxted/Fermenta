# Louis-Antoine Luyt

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A pioneer of the Chilean natural wine movement and champion of old País and Carignan vines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Chile]]
> - **Region:** [[Maule Valley]], [[Itata Valley]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[País]], [[Carignan]], [[Muscat]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]]

## About
A French-born winemaker working with old, dry-farmed vines in the south of Chile. The wines are made with very little intervention, often released as juicy, easy-drinking reds and fragrant whites.

## Known for
- Pipeño
- Trequilemu
- Quella
