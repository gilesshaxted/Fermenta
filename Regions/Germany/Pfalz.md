# Pfalz

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Germany's sunniest, warmest wine area, with rich, dry whites and rising Pinot Noir.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Germany]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Temperate
> - **Soils:** limestone, sandstone, loess, clay, basalt
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]], [[Pinot Blanc]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Dornfelder]]
> - **Styles:** [[Icewine and Eiswein]]

## Landscape and topography
A long strip along the Haardt mountains, with vines on the foothills facing east over the Rhine plain.

## Soil and geology
A mix of limestone, sandstone, loess and clay, with basalt and others in certain sites.

## Climate
Warm and dry for Germany, shielded by the Haardt and Vosges mountains, so almonds and figs also grow.

## History
Wines were exported by Roman times. The 'Pfälzer' dry style has risen since the 1990s, with the Mittelhaardt a centre of Riesling.

## Production and wine styles
Riesling, Pinot Gris and Blanc, Pinot Noir and some Dornfelder. Most are dry, with powerful bodies.

## Key wines
- Pfalz Riesling
- Grauburgunder
- Weissburgunder
- Spätburgunder

## What to look for
- Peach, apricot and spice, with weight.
- A strong choice for dry German wine.
