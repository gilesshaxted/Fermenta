# Vipava Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A windswept valley where the bora blows, home to the rare local whites Pinela and Zelen.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Slovenia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Primorska]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** flysch, marl, sandstone, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinela]], [[Zelen]], [[Ribolla Gialla]], [[Merlot]], [[Refosco]]
> - **Styles:** [[Orange wine]]

## Landscape and topography
A valley running east to west below the Trnovo plateau, with vineyards on the valley floor and the lower slopes.

## Soil and geology
Flysch and marl on the slopes, with limestone gravel and alluvium in places.

## Climate
A blend of Mediterranean warmth and Alpine cool, with the strong, cold bora wind which dries the grapes and cuts disease.

## History
The valley has a long wine history, and its local grapes were nearly forgotten in the last century before being revived by growers. Pinela and Zelen are almost unknown outside the area.

## Production and wine styles
Local whites Pinela and Zelen, as well as Rebula, Malvazija, Sauvignon Blanc, Merlot and Refosco.

## Key wines
- Vipavska Dolina Pinela
- Vipavska Dolina Zelen
- Vipava Merlot
- Vipava white blends

## What to look for
- A good place to try Pinela and Zelen.
- Fresh, mineral whites.
