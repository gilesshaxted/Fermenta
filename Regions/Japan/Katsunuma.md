# Katsunuma

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The best known wine town in Yamanashi, with many small wineries clustered on the slopes.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Japan]]
> - **Part of:** [[Yamanashi]]
> - **Climate:** Humid continental
> - **Soils:** volcanic, alluvial, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Koshu]], [[Muscat Bailey A]], [[Delaware]]

## Landscape and topography
A sloping area on the eastern side of the Kofu basin, with vineyards facing south and east.

## Soil and geology
Free draining volcanic and alluvial soils.

## Climate
Warm summers with rain, though the slopes give some drainage and airflow.

## History
Katsunuma is regarded as the cradle of Japanese wine and is where many of the country's oldest wineries are based.

## Production and wine styles
Dry Koshu is the main white, sometimes aged on lees. Sweeter styles from Delaware and red from Muscat Bailey A are also made.

## Key wines
- Katsunuma Koshu
- Koshu sur lie

## What to look for
- Many wineries offer tastings.
- A good place to see Koshu styles side by side.
