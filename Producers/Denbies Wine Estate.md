# Denbies Wine Estate

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The largest single vineyard estate in England, based in the Surrey Hills.

> - **Country:** [[England]]
> - **Region:** [[Surrey]]
> - **Type:** Major producer

## About
Denbies farms chalk slopes on the North Downs and makes a broad range of still and sparkling wines. It is also a well known visitor destination.
