# Galilee

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Israel's northern wine region, with cooler, higher vineyards on basalt and limestone and some of the country's best wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Israel]]
> - **Part of:** [[Asia]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** basalt, terra rossa, limestone, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Syrah]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills and mountains in the north, with vineyards from a few hundred metres up to about 1,000 metres.

## Soil and geology
Basalt, terra rossa and limestone, with clay in the valleys.

## Climate
Mediterranean, but cooler and wetter than the south, with more of a diurnal swing at altitude.

## History
Wine has been made in the area since ancient times. Modern development began in the late twentieth century with high-altitude plantings of international varieties.

## Production and wine styles
Bordeaux style reds and Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc and other whites. Many wines are kosher.

## Key wines
- Galilee Cabernet Sauvignon
- Galilee Chardonnay
- Galilee red blends

## What to look for
- Altitude gives fresher, more structured wines.
- A good place to start with Israeli wine.
