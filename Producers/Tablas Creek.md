# Tablas Creek

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Paso Robles estate founded by the Perrin family of Beaucastel and the Haas family.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Region:** [[Paso Robles]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grenache]], [[Mourvèdre]], [[Syrah]], [[Roussanne]]
> - **Styles:** [[GSM]]

## About
Tablas Creek uses cuttings from Château de Beaucastel and farms organically. The wines are Rhône inspired and sit between old world and new world.

## Known for
- Esprit de Tablas
- Esprit de Tablas Blanc
- Patelin de Tablas
