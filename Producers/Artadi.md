# Artadi

> [!abstract] At a glance
> An Alavesa estate that helped lead the modern, single vineyard wave in Rioja.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Region:** [[Rioja Alavesa]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tempranillo]]

## About
Working from Laguardia, Artadi focuses on old vine Tempranillo and expressive single vineyard bottlings. The wines are more modern and fruit driven than the classic Rioja houses but still carry a sense of place.

## Known for
- Viñas de Gain
- Pagos Viejos
- El Pisón
