# Naramata Bench

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A sloping bench on the eastern shore of Okanagan Lake, with some of the valley's most refined wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Canada]]
> - **Part of:** [[Okanagan Valley]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** glacial, sand, gravel
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Merlot]], [[Syrah]]

## Landscape and topography
A narrow strip of terraced land above Okanagan Lake.

## Soil and geology
Glacial sand and gravel.

## Climate
Warm and dry, moderated by the lake.

## History
A small sub-appellation with many boutique wineries.

## Production and wine styles
Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, Merlot and Syrah.

## Key wines
- Naramata Pinot Noir
- Naramata Chardonnay
- Naramata Merlot

## What to look for
- Boutique, quality focused wines.
