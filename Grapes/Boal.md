# Boal

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A rich, medium-sweet style of Madeira.

## Where it's from
A noble grape of Madeira, also called Bual. It makes medium-rich wines, between Verdelho and Malmsey.

## Where it grows
- **Madeira, Portugal:** the warmer south

## What it tastes like
- Caramel, orange peel, nuts and honey

## Classic wines
- Boal Madeira

## Pairs with
- Dessert
- Cheese
- Nuts

## Good to know
- Also spelled Bual.
- Warm, nutty and long-lived.
