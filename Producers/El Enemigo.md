# El Enemigo

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Mendoza label from a well known family-linked project, focused on high altitude Malbec and Cabernet Franc.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Argentina]]
> - **Region:** [[Uco Valley]], [[Mendoza]]

## About
Based in Mendoza with vineyards in the Uco Valley, notably in Gualtallary. The wines are elegant and restrained, with fruit that does not feel over ripe.
