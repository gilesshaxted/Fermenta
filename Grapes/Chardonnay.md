# Chardonnay

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A versatile white that takes on the character of where it grows and how it is made. Anything from crisp and flinty to rich and buttery.

## Where it's from
Burgundy, a cross between Pinot and Gouais Blanc. It is now grown in almost every wine country.

## Where it grows
- **Chablis and Burgundy, France:** from steely and flinty to rich Meursault
- **Champagne, France:** the principal grape in Blanc de Blancs, although other permitted white varieties may be used
- **Languedoc, France:** ripe, fruity and good value
- **California, USA:** ripe, often oaked and buttery
- **Adelaide Hills, Australia:** cool-climate, elegant and citrusy

## What it tastes like
- **Cooler sites:** green apple, lemon, flint
- **Warmer sites:** peach, melon, pineapple
- **With oak or malolactic:** butter, toast, vanilla, hazelnut

## Classic wines
- Chablis
- Meursault
- Pouilly-Fuissé
- Blanc de Blancs Champagne
- Californian Chardonnay

## Pairs with
- Roast chicken
- Creamy pasta
- Lobster and crab
- Oysters (Chablis)
- Soft cheeses

## Good to know
- A blank canvas: oak and malolactic fermentation shape the style as much as the grape.
- Chablis is Chardonnay, even though the label does not say so.
