# Quinta do Vale Meão

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Douro Superior estate with a strong reputation for structured, ageworthy reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Portugal]]
> - **Region:** [[Douro]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Touriga Nacional]], [[Touriga Franca]], [[Tinto Cão]]

## About
The estate was once linked to the legendary Barca Velha, and its wines combine power with finesse. Its vineyards sit on hot, dry slopes in the Douro Superior.
