# Casablanca Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cool coastal valley near Valparaíso, Chile's first big white wine region.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Chile]]
> - **Part of:** [[South America]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** granite, clay, loam, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Pinot Noir]]

## Landscape and topography
A low valley open to the sea, with hills on either side.

## Soil and geology
Granitic and clay soils.

## Climate
Cool and foggy mornings with Pacific breezes.

## History
Vines were first planted in 1982, and the region took off in the 1990s.

## Production and wine styles
Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc and Pinot Noir.

## Key wines
- Casablanca Sauvignon Blanc
- Casablanca Chardonnay

## What to look for
- Crisp, aromatic whites.
- Pinot Noir with good acidity.

## Producers
- [[Errázuriz]]
- [[Kingston Family Vineyards]]
