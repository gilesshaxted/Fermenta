# Basilicata

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Italy's instep, with Aglianico del Vulture grown on the slopes of an extinct volcano.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** volcanic, clay, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Aglianico]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills and mountain land, with Monte Vulture rising to over 1,300 metres.

## Soil and geology
Volcanic soils on Vulture, clay and limestone elsewhere.

## Climate
Continental-influenced, with warm days and cool nights at altitude.

## History
Aglianico may have been brought by the Greeks. Vulture's volcanic caves were used for wine ageing.

## Production and wine styles
Aglianico del Vulture DOC and DOCG Superiore, with long ageing.

## Key wines
- Aglianico del Vulture
- Aglianico del Vulture Superiore

## What to look for
- Smoky dark fruit, minerals and firm tannin.
- Great value for ageworthy red.
