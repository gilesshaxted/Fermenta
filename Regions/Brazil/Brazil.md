# Brazil

Part of [[South America]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Brazil is best known for sparkling wine and Moscatel from the south, with new regions from the tropics to the highlands.

## How wine is organised here
- Brazil has indications of origin such as Vale dos Vinhedos, its first geographical indication for wine.
- Espumante is the local term for sparkling wine, and Moscatel espumante is a popular style.
