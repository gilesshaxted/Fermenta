# Marlborough

> [!abstract] At a glance
> New Zealand's largest region, which made the world fall for pungent, zesty Sauvignon Blanc.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[New Zealand]]
> - **Part of:** [[Oceania]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** gravel, silt, loam, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Riesling]]

## Landscape and topography
Open, flat river valleys on the north-east of the South Island, ringed by hills.

## Soil and geology
Stony river gravels and silt on the Wairau floor, with clay in the south.

## Climate
Sunny, dry and cool, with large day to night swings and very high sunshine hours.

## History
Wine began in the 1970s, and Cloudy Bay's 1985 Sauvignon Blanc made an international splash.

## Production and wine styles
Sauvignon Blanc mostly, with Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and aromatic whites.

## Key wines
- Marlborough Sauvignon Blanc
- Marlborough Pinot Noir
- Marlborough sparkling wine

## What to look for
- Passion fruit, grapefruit and cut grass.
- Sub-regions give different styles.
