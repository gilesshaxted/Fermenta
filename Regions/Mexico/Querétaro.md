# Querétaro

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A high altitude central Mexican region known for sparkling wine and reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Mexico]]
> - **Part of:** [[North America]]
> - **Climate:** Semi-arid
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, volcanic, sandy loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tempranillo]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Chenin Blanc]], [[Grenache]]

## Landscape and topography
A plateau region north west of Mexico City, with vineyards in the Tequisquiapan and Ezequiel Montes area.

## Soil and geology
Clay, limestone and volcanic soils.

## Climate
Dry highland climate with warm days and cooler nights.

## History
Wine making grew here in the twentieth century, and the region became a centre for sparkling wine and for wine tourism.

## Production and wine styles
Sparkling wines made by the traditional method and still wines from Spanish and French varieties.

## Key wines
- Querétaro sparkling wine
- Querétaro Tempranillo

## What to look for
- A popular wine tourism area.
- Good for sparkling wine.
