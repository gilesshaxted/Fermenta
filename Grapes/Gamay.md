# Gamay

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Juicy, bright and low in tannin. Serve lightly chilled.

## Where it's from
A natural cross of Pinot Noir and the old white grape Gouais Blanc, from the Burgundy area. Philip the Bold banned it from the Côte d'Or in 1395 to protect Pinot Noir.

## Where it grows
- **Beaujolais, France:** the heartland, on granite hills
- **Loire Valley, France:** light reds in Touraine and Anjou
- **Savoie, France:** fresh alpine reds

## What it tastes like
- **Simple Beaujolais:** red berries, candy and banana from carbonic maceration
- **Cru Beaujolais:** darker cherry, violet, spice and mineral grip

## Classic wines
- Fleurie
- Morgon
- Moulin-à-Vent
- Beaujolais-Villages

## Pairs with
- Charcuterie
- Roast chicken
- Salmon
- Soft cheeses

## Good to know
- Moulin-à-Vent and Morgon can age and begin to taste like Burgundy.
- One of the best reds to drink slightly cool.
