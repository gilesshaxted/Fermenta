# Three Lakes

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Vineyards beside Lakes Neuchâtel, Biel and Morat, in western Switzerland, known for light whites and a pink Pinot Noir.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Switzerland]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** limestone, marl, clay, gravel
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chasselas]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Gamay]]

## Landscape and topography
Gentle slopes along the shores of three lakes, with the Jura mountains to the north.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, marl and clay.

## Climate
Moderate continental, with the lakes moderating temperatures and extending the ripening season.

## History
Winegrowing around Neuchâtel, Biel and Vully has medieval roots. The lakes are shared by the cantons of Neuchâtel, Bern, Fribourg and Vaud.

## Production and wine styles
Fresh Chasselas and Pinot Noir, including Oeil de Perdrix, a pale Pinot Noir rosé from Neuchâtel, plus Pinot Gris and Chardonnay.

## Key wines
- Neuchâtel Chasselas
- Oeil de Perdrix
- Vully
- Lake Biel Chasselas

## What to look for
- Light, crisp whites.
- Oeil de Perdrix is the local rosé.
