# Prince Edward County

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A limestone island county on Lake Ontario, with cool-climate Pinot Noir and Chardonnay.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Canada]]
> - **Part of:** [[North America]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Gamay]], [[Riesling]]

## Landscape and topography
A peninsula and island in eastern Lake Ontario, with flat land and limestone ridges.

## Soil and geology
Shallow limestone over clay.

## Climate
Cool, with cold winters. Vines are buried each winter to survive.

## History
A young region where the first wineries were planted in the late 1990s, drawn by the Burgundy-like limestone.

## Production and wine styles
Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, Gamay and Riesling, plus sparkling wines.

## Key wines
- Prince Edward County Pinot Noir
- Prince Edward County Chardonnay

## What to look for
- Compared with Burgundy.
- Vines are buried against winter.
