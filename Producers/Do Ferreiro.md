# Do Ferreiro

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family Albariño producer in Rías Baixas, known for serious, long lived white wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Region:** [[Spain]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Albariño]]

## About
Working with old vines in the Salnés valley, Do Ferreiro makes textured, mineral Albariño that rewards ageing. Cepas Vellas is the flagship.

## Known for
- Cepas Vellas
- Do Ferreiro Albariño
