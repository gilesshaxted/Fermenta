# Radikon

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family estate in Oslavia and a leading light of the orange wine movement.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Collio]], [[Friuli-Venezia Giulia]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Ribolla Gialla]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]]
> - **Styles:** [[Orange wine]], [[Natural wine]]

## About
Radikon macerates white grapes on their skins for weeks or months and uses no added sulphur in most bottlings. The wines are savoury, structured and built to age.

## Known for
- Ribolla Gialla
- Oslavje
- Jakot
- Slatnik
