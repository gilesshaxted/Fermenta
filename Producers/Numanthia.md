# Numanthia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A leading Toro estate making deep, powerful reds from very old, ungrafted Tinta de Toro vines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Region:** [[Toro]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tempranillo]]

## About
Toro's hot, dry conditions give dark, muscular red wines, and Numanthia handles them with a polished touch. The vines are old and the wines are built to age.
