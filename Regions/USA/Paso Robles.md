# Paso Robles

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A sunny, large inland area of the Central Coast, famous for ripe reds and Rhône blends.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[North America]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, calcareous clay, shale, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Zinfandel]], [[Syrah]], [[Grenache]], [[Mourvèdre]]
> - **Styles:** [[GSM]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling hills and plateau between the Santa Lucia Range and the Salinas valley.

## Soil and geology
Calcareous clay and shale soils on the west, with alluvial soils elsewhere.

## Climate
Hot days and cool nights, with large diurnal swings.

## History
Zinfandel grapes were planted in the 1880s. Rhône varieties pioneers such as Tablas Creek arrived in the 1990s.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon, Zinfandel, Syrah and Rhône blends.

## Key wines
- Paso Robles Cabernet Sauvignon
- Paso Robles Rhône blends
- Paso Robles Zinfandel

## What to look for
- Ripe, rounded reds with soft tannins.
- Calcareous areas in the west give more structure.
