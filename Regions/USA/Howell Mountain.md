# Howell Mountain

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A high-elevation mountain district above the fog line, making dense, tannic wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[Napa Valley]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** volcanic, tufa, red clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Zinfandel]], [[Petite Sirah]]

## Landscape and topography
Mountain slopes mostly above 1,400 feet, above the morning fog.

## Soil and geology
Reddish volcanic tuff, thin and well drained.

## Climate
Cooler nights and warm sunny days, with less fog than the valley.

## History
Zinfandel vineyards were planted here in the 1880s, and the AVA was one of California's first, in 1983.

## Production and wine styles
Powerful Cabernet Sauvignon, Zinfandel and Petite Sirah.

## Key wines
- Howell Mountain Cabernet Sauvignon
- Howell Mountain Zinfandel

## What to look for
- Dense fruit and firm tannins.
- Needs cellar time.
