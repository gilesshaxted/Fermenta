# Rară Neagră

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A native red of Moldova, with fresh acidity, red fruit and an elegant, spicy style.

## Where it's from
The Moldovan name means rare black. It is the same variety as the Romanian Băbească Neagră and is grown on both sides of the Prut. It is made as a varietal red or in blends.

## Where it grows
- **Republic of Moldova:** Codru and Ștefan Vodă
- **Romania:** as Băbească Neagră

## What it tastes like
- Red cherry, plum, spice and violet

## Classic wines
- Rară Neagră
- Negru de Purcari blends

## Pairs with
- Roast pork
- Grilled meats
- Soft cheese

## Good to know
- Also called Băbească Neagră.
- Lighter in style than Fetească Neagră.
