# Margaux

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The most southerly of the great Médoc communes, known for perfumed, silky and elegant Cabernet blends.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Bordeaux]]
> - **Climate:** Maritime
> - **Soils:** gravel, sand, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Petit Verdot]]
> - **Styles:** [[Bordeaux blend]]

## Landscape and topography
Low gravel ridges close to the estuary, with a more dispersed, varied landscape than Pauillac.

## Soil and geology
Some of the thinnest, poorest gravel in the Médoc, mixed with sand and limestone in places. The low fertility stresses the vines and concentrates flavour.

## Climate
Maritime, with the Gironde moderating temperatures. The gravel warms quickly and holds the heat.

## History
Château Margaux was ranked a First Growth in 1855, and the commune has more classified estates than any other in the Médoc.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon with Merlot, Cabernet Franc and Petit Verdot. A small amount of white, Pavillon Blanc, is made from Sauvignon Blanc and sold as Bordeaux AOC.

## Key wines
- Château Margaux (First Growth)
- Château Palmer
- Château Rauzan-Ségla

## What to look for
- Perfume, violets and fine tannins rather than sheer power.
- Often more approachable earlier than Pauillac.
