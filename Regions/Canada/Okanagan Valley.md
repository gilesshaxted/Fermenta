# Okanagan Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> British Columbia's wine heartland, a long lakeside valley with warm days and cool nights.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Canada]]
> - **Part of:** [[North America]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** glacial, sand, gravel, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Merlot]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Riesling]]
> - **Styles:** [[Icewine and Eiswein]]

## Landscape and topography
A 250 km valley running north to south, with lakes and hills on both sides.

## Soil and geology
Glacial deposits, sand, gravel and clay.

## Climate
Warm, dry continental, with some of Canada's driest conditions in the south. Cool nights keep acidity.

## History
Modern wine began in the 1970s. The valley has grown into over 80% of BC's wine production.

## Production and wine styles
Merlot, Pinot Gris, Chardonnay and Pinot Noir lead. Syrah and Bordeaux blends do well in the south.

## Key wines
- Okanagan Merlot
- Okanagan Pinot Gris
- Okanagan Pinot Noir

## What to look for
- Bordeaux reds from the south, aromatic whites from the north.
- Icewine also made.
