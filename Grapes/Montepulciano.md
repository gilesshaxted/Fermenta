# Montepulciano

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Soft, plummy and juicy with ripe fruit and rustic spice. Abruzzo's everyday hero.

## Where it's from
Central Italy. It is most closely linked with Abruzzo, and is widely grown along the Adriatic coast.

## Where it grows
- **Abruzzo:** Montepulciano d'Abruzzo, the benchmark
- **Marche:** Rosso Conero, and Rosso Piceno blended with Sangiovese
- **Molise and Puglia:** everyday reds

## What it tastes like
- **Fruit:** black cherry, plum, blackberry
- **Spice:** black pepper, liquorice, dried herbs
- **Savoury:** earthy, a little tar when more serious

## Classic wines
- Montepulciano d'Abruzzo
- Rosso Conero
- Rosso Piceno
- Cerasuolo d'Abruzzo (rosé)

## Pairs with
- Pizza
- Pasta with ragù
- Grilled lamb
- Sausages
- Pecorino

## Good to know
- The grape is not the town. Vino Nobile di Montepulciano in Tuscany is made from Sangiovese.
- Deep colour and soft tannins make it very food-friendly and good value.
