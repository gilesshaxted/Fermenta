# Loureiro

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A floral, citrusy white and a key grape of Vinho Verde.

## Where it's from
Northwest Portugal, where it is named after laurel, because of its aroma.

## Where it grows
- **Vinho Verde, Portugal:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Lime, orange blossom and bay laurel

## Classic wines
- Vinho Verde Loureiro

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Salads
- Sushi

## Good to know
- Often blended with Arinto and Trajadura.
- Best drunk young.
