# Gewürztraminer

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Unmistakable: lychee, roses and spice. Full, oily and often off-dry.

## Where it's from
Linked to the village of Tramin in Alto Adige, northern Italy. Alsace is now the benchmark. A pink-skinned grape.

## Where it grows
- **Alsace, France:** the benchmark, from dry to sweet
- **Alto Adige, Italy:** cooler, more elegant
- **Germany:** aromatic, often off-dry
- **Gisborne, New Zealand:** ripe, perfumed
- **Anderson Valley, California:** cool-climate, aromatic

## What it tastes like
- **Aromatic:** lychee, rose petal, Turkish delight
- **Spice:** ginger, grapefruit, exotic spice
- **Sweeter styles:** honey, apricot, ginger

## Classic wines
- Alsace Gewurztraminer
- Alsace Vendange Tardive
- Alto Adige Gewürztraminer

## Pairs with
- Thai and Indian food
- Moroccan dishes
- Munster cheese
- Pâté

## Good to know
- Gewürz means spice in German.
- Low acidity and high alcohol make it rich and oily.
