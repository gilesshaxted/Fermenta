# Nitra

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A central-west region around the town of Nitra, with plenty of white wine and some reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Slovakia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Warm continental
> - **Soils:** loess, loam, clay, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Welschriesling]], [[Müller-Thurgau]], [[Grüner Veltliner]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Riesling]]

## Landscape and topography
Gentle hills and plains in the west of Slovakia, to the north-east of Bratislava, along the Nitra river.

## Soil and geology
Loess and loam, with clay and limestone.

## Climate
Warm continental, with long, dry summers and cold winters.

## History
The area has a long agricultural history, and winegrowing was gradually replanted after 1989.

## Production and wine styles
Mainly easy-drinking whites, including Welschriesling and Müller-Thurgau, plus Pinot Gris and Grüner Veltliner.

## Key wines
- Nitra Welschriesling
- Nitra Pinot Gris

## What to look for
- Easy, fruity whites.
- Good value.
