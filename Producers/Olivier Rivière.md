# Olivier Rivière

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A French born winemaker in Rioja making fresh, low intervention wines from old vineyards.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Region:** [[Rioja]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tempranillo]], [[Grenache]], [[Macabeo]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]]

## About
He works with small parcels of old vines and a hands off approach in the cellar, aiming for lighter, more transparent wines than traditional Rioja. The wines are often seen as part of the region's new wave.

## Known for
- Rayos Uva
- Mirando al Sur
