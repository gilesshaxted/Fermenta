# Boğazkere

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A deep, tannic Turkish red, whose name means throat burner.

## Where it's from
Grown around Diyarbakır in southeast Turkey, where it makes dark, powerful wine.

## Where it grows
- **Diyarbakır, Turkey:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Blackberry, tar, spice and dried fruit

## Classic wines
- Boğazkere
- Öküzgözü Boğazkere blends

## Pairs with
- Lamb
- Kebabs
- Rich stews

## Good to know
- Name means throat burner, for its tannin.
- Often blended with Öküzgözü.
