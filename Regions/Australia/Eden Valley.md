# Eden Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A higher, cooler neighbour to the Barossa, famed for lime-scented Riesling and elegant Shiraz.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Australia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Barossa Valley]]
> - **Climate:** Cool continental
> - **Soils:** sandy loam, clay, quartz, schist
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]], [[Syrah]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## Landscape and topography
Hilly country at 400 to 550 metres on the eastern ridge of the Barossa Ranges.

## Soil and geology
Thin sandy loam with clay and decomposed rocks.

## Climate
Cooler, with higher rainfall than the Barossa floor.

## History
Pewsey Vale and Henschke's Hill of Grace have long been showpieces.

## Production and wine styles
Riesling, Shiraz and Cabernet Sauvignon.

## Key wines
- Eden Valley Riesling
- Hill of Grace Shiraz

## What to look for
- Lime, steel and bottle age in Riesling.
- Elegant, peppery Shiraz.

## Producers
- [[Henschke]]
- [[Yalumba]]
