# Cabernet Sauvignon

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Structured, dark-fruited red with firm tannins and a long life ahead of it. The classic backbone of Bordeaux blends.

## Where it's from
A natural cross of Cabernet Franc and Sauvignon Blanc that arose in south-west France, probably in the 17th century.

## Where it grows
- **Bordeaux Left Bank, France:** Médoc, Pauillac and Margaux, usually blended with Merlot
- **Napa Valley, USA:** ripe, powerful and often generously oaked
- **Coonawarra, Australia:** red terra rossa soil, blackcurrant and mint
- **Maipo Valley, Chile:** good value, ripe cassis
- **Bolgheri, Italy:** a key grape in the Super Tuscan blends

## What it tastes like
- **Cooler sites:** blackcurrant, green pepper, graphite
- **Warmer sites:** black cherry, plum, mocha
- **With oak:** cedar, vanilla, cigar box

## Classic wines
- Pauillac and Médoc
- Napa Valley Cabernet
- Coonawarra Cabernet
- Super Tuscans

## Pairs with
- Roast lamb
- Grilled steak
- Mature Cheddar and hard cheeses

## Good to know
- Thick skins and small berries give high tannin and deep colour, which is why it ages so well.
- Often blended: with Merlot in Bordeaux, with Syrah in Australia.
