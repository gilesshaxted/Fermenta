# Golan Heights

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A high volcanic plateau in the north-east, one of Israel's coolest and most important quality areas.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Israel]]
> - **Part of:** [[Galilee]]
> - **Climate:** Cool mediterranean
> - **Soils:** volcanic, basalt, stony soils
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Syrah]], [[Merlot]]

## Landscape and topography
A basalt plateau with vineyards at about 400 to 1,200 metres above sea level.

## Soil and geology
Volcanic basalt soils, often stony and rich in minerals.

## Climate
Cool for the region, with snow in winter and a long ripening season in summer.

## History
Modern viticulture began in the 1970s and 1980s, and the Golan Heights Winery, whose Yarden wines became known worldwide, was founded in 1983.

## Production and wine styles
Reds from Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Syrah, whites from Chardonnay and Sauvignon Blanc, and sparkling wine.

## Key wines
- Yarden
- Golan Heights Cabernet Sauvignon
- Golan Heights Sauvignon Blanc

## What to look for
- High-altitude, structured wines.
- A disputed area, so labels may be sensitive.
