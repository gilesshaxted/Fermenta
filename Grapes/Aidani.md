# Aidani

> [!abstract] At a glance
> An aromatic white of Santorini that adds perfume to blends.

## Where it's from
A native of the Aegean, grown on Santorini, Naxos and Paros, especially for Vinsanto.

## Where it grows
- **Santorini, Greece:** in blends and Vinsanto

## What it tastes like
- Jasmine, citrus, pear and herbs

## Classic wines
- Santorini white
- Vinsanto

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Salads

## Good to know
- Perfumed and soft.
- Part of the Vinsanto blend.
