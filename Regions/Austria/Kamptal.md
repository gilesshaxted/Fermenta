# Kamptal

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The Kamp Valley around Langenlois, with the famous Heiligenstein vineyard.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Austria]]
> - **Part of:** [[Lower Austria]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** loess, gneiss, volcanic
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grüner Veltliner]], [[Riesling]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling hills and terraces around the Kamp river, and the Heiligenstein, a steep Riesling site.

## Soil and geology
Loess, with a unique volcanic desert sandstone at Heiligenstein.

## Climate
Warm Pannonian days and cool nights from the forest.

## History
Kamptal DAC was created in 2008. Producers such as Bründlmayer and Loimer have made it known abroad.

## Production and wine styles
Grüner Veltliner and Riesling, in classic and Reserve styles.

## Key wines
- Kamptal DAC Grüner Veltliner
- Heiligenstein Riesling

## What to look for
- Peppery Grüner Veltliner with spicy depth.
- Riesling with a rich, mineral character.

- [[Weingut Bründlmayer]]
- [[Weingut Hirsch]]
