# Barbadillo

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The leading Sanlúcar de Barrameda house, known for Manzanilla Solear.

> - **Country:** [[Spain]]

## About
Barbadillo is one of the main producers of manzanilla, aged under flor in the coastal cellars of Sanlúcar. The range stretches from fresh manzanilla to rare old wines.

## Known for
- Manzanilla Solear
- Solear en Rama
- Obispo Gascón Palo Cortado
- Príncipe Amontillado
