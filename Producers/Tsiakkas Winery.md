# Tsiakkas Winery

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family winery in Pelendri, in the Limassol hills, working with local grapes.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Cyprus]]
> - **Region:** [[Limassol]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Xynisteri]], [[Maratheftiko]], [[Mavro]]

## About
Estate-focused and traditional in feel, it makes both indigenous and international varieties at altitude. The style is fresh and modest in alcohol.
