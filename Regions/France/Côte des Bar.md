# Côte des Bar

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The southern Aube, with Kimmeridgian clay and limestone and Pinot Noir-led wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Champagne]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, marl
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]]
> - **Styles:** [[Champagne (style)|Champagne style]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling hills around Bar-sur-Seine and Bar-sur-Aube, closer to Chablis than to Reims.

## Soil and geology
Kimmeridgian marl and limestone, the same geology as Chablis.

## Climate
Cool and continental, a touch warmer and more sheltered than the Marne.

## History
Its place in Champagne was disputed in the early 20th century, leading to riots in 1911, before it was fully recognised in 1927.

## Production and wine styles
Pinot Noir dominates, giving fruity, rounder wines used by many houses. The area also produces Rosé des Riceys, a still rosé.

## Key wines
- Rosé des Riceys (still)
- Grower Champagnes
- Pinot Noir cuvées

## What to look for
- Often fruit-led and generous, at friendly prices.
- Home to many small, growing grower producers.
