# Sardinia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A rugged island with Vermentino whites, spicy Cannonau (Grenache) and a long-lived population.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** granite, limestone, sand, schist
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grenache|Cannonau]], [[Vermentino]], [[Carignan|Carignano]], [[Monica]]
> - **Styles:** [[Passito]]

## Landscape and topography
Mountainous and windswept island, with granite hills in the north and limestone plateaux elsewhere.

## Soil and geology
Granite in the north (Gallura), limestone and schist elsewhere, with sandy soils on the coast.

## Climate
Hot and dry Mediterranean, with strong winds and sea breezes.

## History
Cannonau is said to be the Sardinian name for Grenache and may have arrived from Spain. Sardinia is one of the world's Blue Zones, with some of the longest-living people, and Cannonau is associated with longevity.

## Production and wine styles
Vermentino di Gallura (the only DOCG), Cannonau di Sardegna, Carignano del Sulcis, Monica and Vernaccia di Oristano.

## Key wines
- Vermentino di Gallura
- Cannonau di Sardegna
- Carignano del Sulcis
- Vernaccia di Oristano

## What to look for
- Vermentino is zesty and saline.
- Cannonau is warm, spicy and rich.
