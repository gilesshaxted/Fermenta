# Malagousia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> An aromatic white that was almost extinct until revived in the 1970s.

## Where it's from
Rescued from near extinction by Vangelis Gerovassiliou in the 1970s and 1980s in Macedonia.

## Where it grows
- **Macedonia, Greece:** Epanomi and Halkidiki
- **Attica, Greece:** also grown

## What it tastes like
- Peach, jasmine, citrus and herbs

## Classic wines
- Malagousia

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Salads
- Spicy dishes

## Good to know
- Smells of peach and jasmine.
- Now widely planted.
