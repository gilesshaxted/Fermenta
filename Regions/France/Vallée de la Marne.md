# Vallée de la Marne

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The river valley west of Épernay, home of Pinot Meunier and soft, fruit-forward Champagne.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Champagne]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** chalk, clay, marl, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Meunier]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]]
> - **Styles:** [[Champagne (style)|Champagne style]]

## Landscape and topography
Slopes along the Marne between Épernay and Château-Thierry, often south-facing on the north bank.

## Soil and geology
Chalk with clay, marl and sand. Heavier clay and sand suit Pinot Meunier.

## Climate
Cool and continental. Pinot Meunier ripens early, which suits the frost-prone valley.

## History
Hautvillers abbey, where Dom Pérignon worked, overlooks the valley. Aÿ is a Grand Cru village for Pinot Noir.

## Production and wine styles
Pinot Meunier thrives here and adds fruit and early approachability to blends.

## Key wines
- Meunier-led cuvées
- Aÿ Pinot Noir

## What to look for
- Rounder, fruitier Champagne for earlier drinking.
- Some growers now bottle Meunier on its own.
