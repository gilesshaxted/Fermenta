# Sémillon

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Waxy and lemony when young, honeyed and toasty with age. The grape behind Sauternes.

## Where it's from
Bordeaux, where it is blended with Sauvignon Blanc for both dry and sweet wines.

## Where it grows
- **Sauternes and Barsac, Bordeaux:** sweet, botrytis-affected, with Sauvignon Blanc
- **Graves, Bordeaux:** dry white blends
- **Hunter Valley, Australia:** low-alcohol, ages for decades
- **Barossa Valley, Australia:** richer, riper styles

## What it tastes like
- **Young:** lemon, lime, waxy texture
- **With age:** honey, toast, lanolin, lemon curd
- **Sauternes:** apricot, marmalade, saffron, honey

## Classic wines
- Sauternes
- Barsac
- Hunter Valley Sémillon
- White Graves

## Pairs with
- Roast chicken
- Seafood
- Sauternes with blue cheese or foie gras

## Good to know
- Thin skins make it prone to noble rot, the basis of Sauternes.
- Hunter Valley Sémillon is low in alcohol and develops toasty notes without oak.
