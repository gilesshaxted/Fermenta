# Jumilla

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A hot, dry southeastern region and the home of powerful Monastrell (Mourvèdre).

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Mourvèdre|Monastrell]]

## Landscape and topography
A high, arid plateau in Murcia at about 400 to 800 metres.

## Soil and geology
Chalky limestone, clay and sandy soil, with very low yields.

## Climate
Hot and very dry, with little rain.

## History
Phylloxera largely missed the region's sandy soils, so many ungrafted vines remain.

## Production and wine styles
Monastrell, in powerful reds and some rosé and sweet wines.

## Key wines
- Jumilla Monastrell
- Jumilla Crianza

## What to look for
- Dark fruit, herbs and warmth.
- Great value for bold flavours.
