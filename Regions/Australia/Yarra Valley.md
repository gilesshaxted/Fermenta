# Yarra Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cool, scenic valley near Melbourne, making refined Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and sparkling wine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Australia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Oceania]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** clay, loam, volcanic, sandstone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Syrah]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling foothills and hills of the Great Dividing Range, at 50 to 400 metres.

## Soil and geology
Grey clay loams in the lower areas, with red volcanic soils in the upper valley.

## Climate
Cool, with large temperature swings and moderate rainfall.

## History
Vines were planted in the 1830s, pulled out in the 1920s and revived from the 1960s.

## Production and wine styles
Chardonnay, Pinot Noir, sparkling wine, Syrah and Cabernet.

## Key wines
- Yarra Valley Chardonnay
- Yarra Valley Pinot Noir

## What to look for
- Elegant and fresh styles.
- Good sparkling producers such as Domaine Chandon.
