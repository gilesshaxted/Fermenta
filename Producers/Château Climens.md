# Château Climens

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Barsac estate making pure, fresh and long-lived sweet wines from nearly all Sémillon.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Sauternes]], [[Bordeaux]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sémillon]]
> - **Styles:** [[Sauternes (style)]], [[Noble rot wines]]

## About
Climens is a leading name in Barsac, farmed biodynamically. The wines are known for purity and a lively, citrusy acidity rather than heavy richness.
