# Marsala (style)

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Fortified wine from western Sicily, used in both cooking and drinking.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Category:** Fortified
> - **Colour:** Varies
> - **Sweetness:** Varies
> - **Country:** Italy
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grillo]], [[Inzolia]], [[Catarratto]]
> - **Regions:** [[Marsala]], [[Sicily]]

## How it's made
White grapes are fortified and aged, sometimes in a solera like system. Styles range from dry (secco) to sweet (dolce), and from Fine to Vergine and Riserva by ageing.

## Where it's made
The area around the town of Marsala in western Sicily.

## What it tastes like
- Brown sugar, dried apricot, nuts
- Vinous sweetness with a bitter edge

## Pairs with
- Aged cheese
- Tiramisu
- Chicken Marsala
- Dried fruit

## Good to know
- Cooking Marsala is a lower grade than Vergine and Riserva.
- English merchants in the 18th century helped start the trade.
