# Roda

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Haro bodega that built a modern, elegant style of Rioja around careful vineyard selection.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Region:** [[Rioja Alta]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tempranillo]], [[Grenache]], [[Graciano]]

## About
Roda makes polished reds with fresh fruit and French oak influence, taking a more contemporary line than the old Haro houses. The range builds from Sela up to the flagship Roda I.

- Roda I
