# Robertson

> [!abstract] At a glance
> An inland valley of limestone soils, long known for Chardonnay, value wines and horses.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[South Africa]]
> - **Part of:** [[Africa]]
> - **Climate:** Warm continental
> - **Soils:** limestone, alluvial, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Syrah]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## Landscape and topography
A long, narrow valley, irrigated by the Breede River.

## Soil and geology
Limestone-rich soils.

## Climate
Warm and dry, with cool nights.

## History
A long-established wine and fruit area.

## Production and wine styles
Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc, Syrah and Cabernet Sauvignon.

## Key wines
- Robertson Chardonnay
- Robertson Syrah

## What to look for
- Ripe, generous styles.
- Good value.
