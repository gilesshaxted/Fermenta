# Hidalgo La Gitana

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family sherry house in Sanlúcar, famous for Manzanilla La Gitana.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Region:** [[Jerez]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Palomino]], [[Pedro Ximénez]]
> - **Styles:** [[Sherry]]

## About
Hidalgo has long been a leading name for fresh manzanilla. Its Pastrana is a single vineyard manzanilla pasada.

## Known for
- Manzanilla La Gitana
- Manzanilla Pasada Pastrana
- Napoleon Amontillado
