# Czechia

Part of [[Europe]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cool-climate wine country whose fresh whites, peppery Grüner Veltliner and home-bred Pálava are little known abroad.

## How wine is organised here
- There are two wine regions, Moravia and Bohemia, which Moravia dominates by a large margin.
- Quality wine labels can carry terms like Pozdní sběr and Výběr z hroznů, which are linked to the ripeness of the grapes at harvest.
- The Czech name for Grüner Veltliner is Veltlínské zelené and the name for Blaufränkisch is Frankovka.
