# Torres

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The best known family wine business in Spain, based in Vilafranca del Penedès.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Region:** [[Penedès]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Tempranillo]], [[Grenache]], [[Carignan]], [[Chardonnay]]

## About
Torres makes a wide range from everyday wines to single vineyard icons, with a strong emphasis on sustainability. It also has vineyards in other regions and countries.
