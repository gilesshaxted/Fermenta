# Domaine Henri Bourgeois

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A large family domaine at Chavignol and one of the best-known names in Sancerre.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Sancerre]], [[Loire Valley]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Pinot Noir]]

## About
Henri Bourgeois is a family-run estate making a wide range of Sancerre from different soils. The whites are crisp, mineral and reliable, with top cuvées from old vines.

## Known for
- Sancerre La Côte des Monts Damnés
- Sancerre d'Antan
- Sancerre Les Baronnes
