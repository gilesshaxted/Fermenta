# Albariño

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Fresh and zesty, with peach, citrus and a salty sea-spray edge. A seafood white.

## Where it's from
Galicia in north-west Spain and northern Portugal, where it is called Alvarinho. Thick skins help it cope with the damp Atlantic climate.

## Where it grows
- **Rías Baixas, Galicia:** the benchmark, cool and Atlantic
- **Monção e Melgaço, Portugal:** called Alvarinho, in Vinho Verde
- **Vinho Verde, Portugal:** often blended

## What it tastes like
- **Fruit:** peach, apricot, grapefruit, lemon
- **Floral:** orange blossom, jasmine
- **Savoury:** sea salt, almond, a touch of minerality

## Classic wines
- Rías Baixas Albariño
- Monção e Melgaço Alvarinho

## Pairs with
- Shellfish
- Octopus and scallops
- Grilled fish
- Tapas

## Good to know
- Vines are often trained high on pergolas to let air circulate in the humid climate.
- Alvarinho in Portugal, Albariño in Spain.
