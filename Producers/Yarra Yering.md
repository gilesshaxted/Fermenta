# Yarra Yering

> [!abstract] At a glance
> An influential Yarra Valley winery, known for Bordeaux and Rhône style reds named simply as Dry Red.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Australia]]
> - **Region:** [[Yarra Valley]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Syrah]], [[Pinot Noir]]
> - **Styles:** [[Bordeaux blend]]

## About
A small, long established estate that helped shape the modern Yarra Valley. The wines are savoury, structured and often very distinctive.

## Known for
- Dry Red No. 1
- Dry Red No. 2
- Underhill Shiraz
