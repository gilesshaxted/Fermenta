# Domaine Faiveley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A large family-run domaine and négociant based in Nuits-Saint-Georges, with extensive vineyards across Burgundy.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Côte de Nuits]], [[Côte Chalonnaise]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]]

## About
Faiveley owns parcels from the Côte de Nuits down to the Côte Chalonnaise. The wines range from reliable village bottlings to fine grands crus.
