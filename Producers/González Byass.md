# González Byass

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The family house behind Tío Pepe, one of the world's best known fino sherries.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Region:** [[Jerez]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Palomino]], [[Pedro Ximénez]]
> - **Styles:** [[Sherry]], [[Solera system]]

## About
A major Jerez producer with a very broad range, from crisp fino to very old rare sherries. The Palmas and old VORS sherries show the house at its best.

## Known for
- Tío Pepe
- Matusalem
- Noé
- Apóstoles
