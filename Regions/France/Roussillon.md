# Roussillon

> [!abstract] At a glance
> France's sun-baked southwest corner, with old-vine reds and a rich tradition of fortified sweet wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** schist, limestone, granite, clay, gneiss
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grenache]], [[Carignan]], [[Syrah]], [[Mourvèdre]], [[Muscat]]
> - **Styles:** [[Vin doux naturel]]

## Landscape and topography
Terraced slopes between the Pyrenees and the sea, with the Agly, Têt and Tech valleys.

## Soil and geology
Schist, limestone, granite, gneiss and clay, giving great variety.

## Climate
Hot, dry Mediterranean, with the Tramontane wind and low rainfall, so concentrated fruit.

## History
A Catalan region historically, it was Spanish until 1659 and still has Catalan culture. Vin doux naturel (fortified sweet wine) has been made here since the 13th century.

## Production and wine styles
Dry reds and whites, plus vin doux naturel from Grenache or Muscat, where fermentation is stopped with grape spirit.

## Key wines
- Côtes du Roussillon
- Côtes du Roussillon-Villages
- Collioure
- Maury
- Banyuls
- Rivesaltes

## What to look for
- Old-vine Grenache and Carignan are the stars.
- Banyuls is a classic partner for chocolate.
