# Arequipa

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A high desert area in southern Peru with volcanic soils and strong sun.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Peru]]
> - **Part of:** [[South America]]
> - **Climate:** Arid subtropical
> - **Soils:** volcanic, sandy, alluvial
> - **Main grapes:** [[Malbec]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Muscat of Alexandria]]

## Landscape and topography
River valleys and terraces below volcanic peaks in southern Peru.

## Soil and geology
Volcanic, sandy and alluvial soils.

## Climate
Dry and sunny, with cool nights because of altitude.

## History
Vines were planted here under Spanish rule, and the region has a long tradition of grape and pisco production.

## Production and wine styles
Small scale still wines and pisco grapes.

## Key wines
- Arequipa reds

## What to look for
- A small, little known region.
- Volcanic desert terroir.
