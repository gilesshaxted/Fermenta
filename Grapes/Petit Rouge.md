# Petit Rouge

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The main red grape of the Aosta Valley, grown at very high altitude.

## Where it's from
A native of the Alps and the base of many Valle d'Aosta reds.

## Where it grows
- **Valle d'Aosta, Italy:** high vineyards

## What it tastes like
- Red cherry, herbs, pepper and spice

## Classic wines
- Torrette
- Enfer d'Arvier

## Pairs with
- Alpine cheese
- Cured meat

## Good to know
- Often blended with local grapes.
- Light, fresh and peppery.
