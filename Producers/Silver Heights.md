# Silver Heights

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small family winery in Ningxia that helped show what Chinese wine could do.

> - **Country:** [[China]]

## About
Silver Heights is a boutique estate at the foot of the Helan Mountain, led by a family winemaker trained in Bordeaux. The reds are Bordeaux-style, with careful oak and a polished, restrained feel.

- The Summit
- Family Reserve
