# Chenin Blanc

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Bright acidity with apple, quince and honey. From bone dry to sweet to sparkling, one of the most versatile white grapes.

## Where it's from
The Loire Valley in France, around Anjou and Touraine. In South Africa it is called Steen.

## Where it grows
- **Vouvray, Savennières and Anjou, Loire:** dry, off-dry, sweet and sparkling
- **Swartland, South Africa:** old bush vines, rich and textured
- **Stellenbosch, South Africa:** fresh, fruity and good value

## What it tastes like
- **Young:** apple, pear, lemon, white flowers
- **Ripe styles:** quince, honey, ginger
- **With age:** honey, nuts, beeswax, wet wool

## Classic wines
- Vouvray
- Savennières
- Coteaux du Layon (sweet)
- Crémant de Loire
- Swartland Chenin

## Pairs with
- Pork
- Chicken in cream sauce
- Mild Asian dishes
- Soft cheese

## Good to know
- On Vouvray labels, sec means dry and moelleux means sweet.
- The most planted grape in South Africa.
