# Domaine Ostertag

> [!abstract] At a glance
> An Epfig estate, led by André Ostertag, known for biodynamic farming and a creative approach.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Alsace]], [[Alsace Grand Cru]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Sylvaner]]

## About
Ostertag works on a range of soils and uses a mix of oak and stainless steel. The wines are vivid, individual and often fascinating.
