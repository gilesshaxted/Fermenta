# Manchuela

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A high plateau east of La Mancha, home to old-vine Bobal, a local red grape.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Bobal]], [[Tempranillo]], [[Grenache|Garnacha]], [[Syrah]]

## Landscape and topography
A high plateau around 600 to 1,000 metres, with the Júcar river valley and hills.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, clay and sand, with chalky soils.

## Climate
Continental, with Mediterranean influence from the east.

## History
Bobal was once used mostly for bulk wine and rosé. A younger generation has rediscovered old bush vines.

## Production and wine styles
Bobal-led reds, plus Garnacha, Tempranillo and Syrah. Many are made by small producers.

## Key wines
- Manchuela Bobal
- Manchuela Rosado

## What to look for
- Dark fruit with fresh acidity and spice.
- A good-value find for old-vine wines.
