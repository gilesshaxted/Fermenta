# Hebei

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A province around Beijing, with the Huailai valley and the coastal Changli area as its main wine zones.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[China]]
> - **Part of:** [[Asia]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** sandy loam, gravel, clay, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Marselan]], [[Cabernet Gernischt]]

## Landscape and topography
River valleys and low hills north-west of Beijing, plus coastal plains at Changli.

## Soil and geology
Sandy loam, gravel, clay and limestone.

## Climate
Continental, with hot summers and cold, dry winters, and a risk of frost. Changli is more maritime.

## History
Sino-French Huailai joint ventures were established in the 1980s, and the area was among the first modern wine regions in China.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Chardonnay and Marselan wines, along with sparkling wine.

## Key wines
- Huailai Cabernet Sauvignon
- Changli Cabernet Sauvignon
- Huailai Chardonnay

## What to look for
- Close to Beijing, so good for visits.
- A bright, fruity style.
