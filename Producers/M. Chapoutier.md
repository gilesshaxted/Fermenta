# M. Chapoutier

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A major Rhône négociant and landowner, with a large holding on the hill of Hermitage and a focus on biodynamic farming.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Hermitage]], [[Rhône Valley]], [[Châteauneuf-du-Pape]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Marsanne]], [[Grenache]], [[Roussanne]]

## About
A family house based in Tain l'Hermitage, making single plot wines from the northern Rhône alongside a wide range from across the south. Many of the wines carry Braille on the label.

## Known for
- Hermitage Le Pavillon
- Hermitage L'Ermite
- Hermitage Monier de la Sizeranne
- Crozes-Hermitage Les Meysonniers
