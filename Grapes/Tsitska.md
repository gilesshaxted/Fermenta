# Tsitska

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A high-acid Imeretian white, often blended with Tsolikouri.

## Where it's from
A native grape of Imereti, valued for acidity and used for still and sparkling wines.

## Where it grows
- **Imereti, Georgia:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Lemon, green apple, herbs and mineral

## Classic wines
- Imereti Tsitska

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Salads
- Light cheese

## Good to know
- Frequently blended.
- Good freshness.
