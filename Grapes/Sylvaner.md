# Sylvaner

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Subtle, earthy and food-friendly. Underrated and often overlooked.

## Where it's from
A cross between Traminer and Österreichisch Weiss, probably from Central Europe.

## Where it grows
- **Alsace, France:** an everyday white, with one Grand Cru (Zotzenberg)
- **Franken, Germany:** its best expression, in the Bocksbeutel bottle

## What it tastes like
- Green apple, herbs and an earthy tone

## Classic wines
- Alsace Sylvaner
- Franken Silvaner

## Pairs with
- White asparagus
- Sauerkraut
- Roast pork

## Good to know
- Silvaner is the German and Austrian spelling.
- A traditional match for white asparagus.
