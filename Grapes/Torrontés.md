# Torrontés

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Argentina's aromatic white, with perfumed floral notes and a dry palate.

## Where it's from
Argentina. DNA work shows it is a cross of Muscat of Alexandria and Criolla Chica (Mission).

## Where it grows
- **Salta and Cafayate, Argentina:** high-altitude vineyards give the freshest wines

## What it tastes like
- Rose petal, peach, citrus and spice

## Classic wines
- Torrontés Riojano from Cafayate

## Pairs with
- Spicy Asian food
- Ceviche
- Salads

## Good to know
- Smells sweet but tastes dry.
- Best drunk young and chilled.
