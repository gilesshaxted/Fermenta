# La Mancha

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Europe's biggest vineyard area, a high plateau that is shifting from bulk wine to quality.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tempranillo]], [[Airén]], [[Grenache|Garnacha]], [[Macabeo]]

## Landscape and topography
A vast, flat high plateau of about 600 to 800 metres in central Spain.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, clay and sandy soil, with low fertility.

## Climate
Continental with very hot summers and little rain.

## History
Don Quixote was set here. Airén, a drought-resistant white, once dominated and was mostly distilled.

## Production and wine styles
Tempranillo (called Cencibel) now leads. Large cooperatives and a few ambitious estates share the region.

## Key wines
- La Mancha Tempranillo
- Vino de Pago wines

## What to look for
- Value-priced reds and whites.
- Look for Vino de Pago estates for quality.
