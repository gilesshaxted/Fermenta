# Rivera

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A northern frontier region with sandy soils and a warmer climate.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Uruguay]]
> - **Part of:** [[South America]]
> - **Climate:** Humid subtropical
> - **Soils:** sandstone, sand, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tannat]], [[Marselan]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## Landscape and topography
Gentle hills on the Brazilian border, at higher elevation than the south.

## Soil and geology
Sandy and sandstone soils with clay.

## Climate
Warmer and more humid than the south, with a long season.

## History
A newer area for vines, where northern Uruguay has seen new plantings in recent decades.

## Production and wine styles
Tannat and other reds with ripe fruit, made mostly by a small number of producers.

## Key wines
- Rivera Tannat

## What to look for
- A small but growing region.
- Warmer than the coast.
