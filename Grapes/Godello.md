# Godello

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A textured, mineral white from northwest Spain.

## Where it's from
Galicia, especially Valdeorras, where it was nearly lost before a revival in the 20th century.

## Where it grows
- **Valdeorras, Spain:** the heartland
- **Bierzo, Spain:** on slate

## What it tastes like
- Apple, peach and citrus with a stony edge

## Classic wines
- Godello Valdeorras
- Bierzo Godello

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Roast chicken
- Creamy dishes

## Good to know
- Compared with Chardonnay or white Burgundy.
- Can age in oak.
