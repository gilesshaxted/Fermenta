# Domaine Leflaive

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Puligny-Montrachet estate and a benchmark for white Burgundy.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Côte de Beaune]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]]

## About
Leflaive has farmed biodynamically for many years and holds parcels of premier and grand cru Chardonnay. The wines are pure, mineral and age beautifully.

## Known for
- Puligny-Montrachet Les Pucelles
- Bâtard-Montrachet
- Chevalier-Montrachet
