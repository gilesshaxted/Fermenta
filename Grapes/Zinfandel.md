# Zinfandel

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Big, jammy and spicy, often high in alcohol. California's signature red, and the same grape as Italy's Primitivo.

## Where it's from
Croatia, where it is known as Crljenak Kaštelanski or Tribidrag. DNA testing showed it is identical to Zinfandel and Primitivo.

## Where it grows
- **Lodi, Sonoma and Paso Robles, California:** ripe, jammy, often old vines
- **Puglia, Italy:** called Primitivo, including Manduria and Gioia del Colle

## What it tastes like
- **Fruit:** blackberry jam, raspberry, black cherry
- **Spice:** black pepper, sweet spice, liquorice
- **Ripe styles:** dried fruit, chocolate, high alcohol warmth

## Classic wines
- Old-vine California Zinfandel
- Primitivo di Manduria
- Gioia del Colle Primitivo

## Pairs with
- Barbecue ribs
- Burgers
- Pizza
- Spicy sausage
- Tomato-based pasta

## Good to know
- Zinfandel and Primitivo are the same grape. Alcohol is often 14% or more.
- White Zinfandel is a pink, off-dry wine made from the same red grape.
