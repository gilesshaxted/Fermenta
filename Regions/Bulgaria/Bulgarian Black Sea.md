# Bulgarian Black Sea

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cooler coastal region for fresh, aromatic whites, led by the local Dimiat and Muscat varieties.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Bulgaria]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Moderate maritime
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, sand, loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Dimiat]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Muscat Ottonel]], [[Misket Cherven]], [[Pinot Noir]]

## Landscape and topography
A strip of coast and low hills along the Black Sea in eastern Bulgaria, around Varna and Burgas.

## Soil and geology
Clay, limestone, sand and loam.

## Climate
Moderate, with the sea tempering summer heat and keeping winters mild, which helps whites keep their freshness.

## History
Ancient Greek colonists made wine on this coast, and Varna and the Black Sea area have long been white wine country. Modern producers have mostly focused on whites and sparkling wine.

## Production and wine styles
Mostly fresh, dry whites from Dimiat, Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc and Muscat Ottonel, as well as some Pinot Noir and sparkling wine.

## Key wines
- Black Sea Dimiat
- Black Sea Chardonnay
- Black Sea Muscat Ottonel

## What to look for
- Light, fresh and aromatic whites.
- Good with seafood.
