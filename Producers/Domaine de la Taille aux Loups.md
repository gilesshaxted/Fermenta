# Domaine de la Taille aux Loups

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Jacky Blot's domaine in Montlouis and Vouvray, known for refined Chenin Blanc in several styles.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Vouvray]], [[Loire Valley]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chenin Blanc]]

## About
La Taille aux Loups helped establish Montlouis as a serious appellation alongside Vouvray. The wines range from dry to sweet and from sparkling to still, and are known for purity and ageing ability.

## Known for
- Remus
- Clos Michet
- Triple Zero
