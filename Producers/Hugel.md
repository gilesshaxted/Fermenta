# Hugel

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A very old Riquewihr family house, one of the leading names in Alsace wine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Alsace]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Gewürztraminer]]
> - **Styles:** [[Noble rot wines]]

## About
Hugel blends its own vineyards with grapes bought from growers and is known for late-harvest wines. The house style is clean, dry and food-friendly.

## Known for
- Gentil
- Jubilee
