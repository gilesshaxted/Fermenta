# Albillo

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A soft white grape with a Spanish heartland around Ribera del Duero and the Canaries.

## Where it's from
Albillo Mayor is native to Castile and Albillo Criollo is grown in the Canaries. They are different grapes.

## Where it grows
- **Castile, Spain:** as Albillo Mayor
- **Canary Islands, Spain:** Albillo Criollo

## What it tastes like
- Apple, herbs, honey and mineral

## Classic wines
- Albillo
- Ribera del Duero blends

## Pairs with
- Roast pork
- Seafood

## Good to know
- Different forms share the name.
- Used in some Ribera blends.
