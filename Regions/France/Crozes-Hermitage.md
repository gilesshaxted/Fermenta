# Crozes-Hermitage

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The large, friendly northern Rhône appellation for Syrah, offering good value and approachable style.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Rhône Valley]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** granite, clay, gravel, loess
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Marsanne]], [[Roussanne]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling hills and plains around Tain-l'Hermitage, much larger than Hermitage itself.

## Soil and geology
Granite on the hillsides, with clay, gravel and loess on the plains below.

## Climate
Continental, a touch cooler and more varied than Hermitage.

## History
The area expanded in the 20th century, and top parcels now rival Hermitage in quality.

## Production and wine styles
Syrah for red and Marsanne with Roussanne for white.

## Key wines
- Crozes-Hermitage rouge
- Crozes-Hermitage blanc

## What to look for
- Blackberry, pepper and olive, often earlier-drinking than Hermitage.
- Excellent with grilled meat and stews.

- [[Domaine Alain Graillot]]
- [[Paul Jaboulet Aîné]]
