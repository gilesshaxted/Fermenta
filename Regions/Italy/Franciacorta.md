# Franciacorta

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Italy's finest traditional-method sparkling wine, from glacial hills beside Lake Iseo.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Lombardy]]
> - **Climate:** Temperate
> - **Soils:** moraine, gravel, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Pinot Blanc]]
> - **Styles:** [[Franciacorta (style)|Franciacorta style]]

## Landscape and topography
Low hills of glacial moraine south of Lake Iseo, near Brescia.

## Soil and geology
Glacial deposits of gravel, sand and clay.

## Climate
Temperate, with the lake moderating temperatures.

## History
Guido Berlucchi and Franco Ziliani began making sparkling wine in the 1960s. Franciacorta became DOCG in 1995 and rules out the word spumante.

## Production and wine styles
Traditional method. Styles include non-vintage, Satèn (Chardonnay-led and softer), Rosé and Millesimato.

## Key wines
- Franciacorta Brut
- Franciacorta Satèn
- Franciacorta Rosé
- Franciacorta Millesimato

## What to look for
- Brioche, apple and citrus.
- Satèn is a creamier style, with less fizz pressure.
