# Antinori

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of Italy's best known wine families, central to the story of modern Tuscan wine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Chianti Classico]], [[Tuscany]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sangiovese]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]
> - **Styles:** [[Super Tuscan]]

## About
A large, family-run company with estates across Tuscany and beyond. It helped lead the Super Tuscan movement and also makes classic Chianti Classico. The wines range from approachable everyday bottles to serious age-worthy reds.

## Known for
- Tignanello
- Solaia
- Badia a Passignano Chianti Classico Gran Selezione
- Pèppoli Chianti Classico
