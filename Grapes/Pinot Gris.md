# Pinot Gris

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Light, dry and easy in Italy as Pinot Grigio. Richer, rounder and spicier in Alsace as Pinot Gris.

## Where it's from
Burgundy, where it arose as a colour mutation of Pinot Noir. It has a greyish-pink skin.

## Where it grows
- **Veneto, Friuli and Alto Adige, Italy:** known as Pinot Grigio, crisp and light
- **Alsace, France:** known as Pinot Gris, rich and often off-dry
- **Oregon, USA:** fruity and fresh
- **Germany:** called Grauburgunder, dry and weighty

## What it tastes like
- **Pinot Grigio:** pear, green apple, lemon, almond
- **Alsace Pinot Gris:** white peach, honeysuckle, ginger, spice

## Classic wines
- Pinot Grigio delle Venezie
- Alto Adige Pinot Grigio
- Alsace Pinot Gris
- Ramato Pinot Grigio

## Pairs with
- Light fish
- Seafood
- Risotto
- Salads
- Antipasti

## Good to know
- Same grape, two styles. Grigio is usually light and crisp, Gris richer and rounder.
- Ramato is a copper-coloured Pinot Grigio made with skin contact, mainly in Friuli.
