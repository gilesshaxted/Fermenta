# Pierre Péters

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Le Mesnil-sur-Oger family grower known for taut, mineral blanc de blancs.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Champagne]], [[Côte des Blancs]]

> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]]
> - **Styles:** [[Champagne (style)]]

## About
Pierre Péters works mainly with grand cru Chardonnay, blending in a reserve perpetual to add depth. The wines are linear and saline with great ageing potential.

## Known for
- Cuvée de Réserve
- L'Esprit
- Les Chétillons
