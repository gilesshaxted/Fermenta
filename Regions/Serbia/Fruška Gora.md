# Fruška Gora

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A low mountain on the Vojvodina plain, Serbia's best known historic wine hills, with monasteries and old cellars.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Serbia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** loess, sand, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Welschriesling]], [[Riesling]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Merlot]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## Landscape and topography
A low, forested ridge above the Danube, rising from the flat plain of Vojvodina, with vineyards on its slopes.

## Soil and geology
Loess, sand and clay.

## Climate
Warm continental, with hot summers and cold winters, moderated by the Danube.

## History
Fruška Gora has been a wine area since Roman times, and Roman emperor Probus is said to have planted vines here. Monasteries and the town of Sremski Karlovci have long wine traditions.

## Production and wine styles
Aromatic whites such as Welschriesling and Riesling, alongside Chardonnay and red wines from Merlot and Pinot Noir.

## Key wines
- Sremski Karlovci
- Fruška Gora white
- Sremski Karlovci Bermet

## What to look for
- Mostly fresh whites.
- Bermet is a historic spiced fortified wine from Sremski Karlovci.
