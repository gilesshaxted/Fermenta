# Ar.Pe.Pe.

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small family grower in Valtellina making traditional, age worthy mountain Nebbiolo.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Valtellina]], [[Lombardy]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Nebbiolo]]

## About
Based in Sondrio, Ar.Pe.Pe. uses large old casks and long ageing in the cellar. The wines are delicate, savoury and need time.

## Known for
- Sassella Rocce Rosse Riserva
- Grumello Rocce Rosse Riserva
- Sfursat Ultimi Raggi
