# Vaud

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Switzerland's second largest canton by vineyard area, with a long line of vineyards on the northern shore of Lake Geneva.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Switzerland]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** limestone, molasse, clay, gravel, moraine
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chasselas]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Gamay]], [[Gamaret]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
Vineyards run along the lakeshore and the slopes above it, with some inland areas near the Jura foothills.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, molasse sandstone, clay and glacial moraine, depending on the area.

## Climate
Moderate continental, softened by the lake, which reflects light and holds warmth into autumn.

## History
Monks, especially Cistercians, built much of the terracing in the Middle Ages. Chasselas has been the main grape for centuries.

## Production and wine styles
Mostly Chasselas, which reflects its site very clearly, plus Pinot Noir, Gamay and Gamaret, and some Chardonnay.

## Key wines
- Dézaley
- Calamin
- Yvorne
- Epesses
- La Côte

- Chasselas shows terroir very clearly here.
