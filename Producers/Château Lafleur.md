# Château Lafleur

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A tiny, quality-led Pomerol estate making intensely mineral and age-worthy wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Pomerol]], [[Bordeaux]]

## About
Lafleur is a very small family estate with a large reputation, farmed with great care. The wine is more structured and mineral than the typical plush Pomerol style.
