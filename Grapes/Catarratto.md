# Catarratto

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Sicily's most planted white grape, used for Marsala and everyday dry wine.

## Where it's from
Grown in western Sicily for centuries and used heavily in Marsala. Today better versions come from high-altitude vineyards.

## Where it grows
- **Western Sicily, Italy:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Lemon, almond, herbs and white flowers

## Classic wines
- Marsala
- Catarratto

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Salads
- Pasta

## Good to know
- Several forms exist, including Catarratto Lucido.
- Quality ranges from basic to quite fine.
