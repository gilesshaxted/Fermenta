# Tannat

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Dark, tannic and powerful. The signature grape of Madiran and Uruguay.

## Where it's from
South-West France, around Madiran in the foothills of the Pyrenees.

## Where it grows
- **Madiran, France:** the original home
- **Uruguay:** the national grape, softer in style

## What it tastes like
- Blackberry, tar and strong tannin

## Classic wines
- Madiran
- Irouléguy
- Uruguayan Tannat

## Pairs with
- Duck confit
- Cassoulet
- Grilled steak

## Good to know
- One of the highest tannin grapes.
- Micro-oxygenation was developed to soften it.
