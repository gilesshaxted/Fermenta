# Kuenhof

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small family estate in the Valle Isarco, admired for mineral Sylvaner and Riesling.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Alto Adige]], [[Trentino-Alto Adige]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sylvaner]], [[Riesling]], [[Pinot Blanc]]

## About
Peter Pliger works steep terraces in the cool Isarco valley, farming with great care. The wines are bright, saline and taut, and benefit from some bottle age.

## Known for
- Sylvaner
- Riesling
- Kaiton Riesling
