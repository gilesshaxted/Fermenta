# Calabria

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The toe of Italy, one of Italy's oldest wine lands, with Cirò the best-known zone.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Gaglioppo]], [[Greco Bianco]]

## Landscape and topography
Mountains and hills running down the length of the toe, with the Ionian and Tyrrhenian seas on each side.

## Soil and geology
Clay, limestone and sandy soils.

## Climate
Hot Mediterranean.

## History
The Greeks called Calabria Oenotria, the land of vines. Cirò may be one of Italy's oldest wines.

## Production and wine styles
Gaglioppo reds and rosés from Cirò, and sweet Greco di Bianco.

## Key wines
- Cirò Rosso
- Greco di Bianco

## What to look for
- Light-coloured, tannic reds with red fruit.
- Rare outside Italy.
