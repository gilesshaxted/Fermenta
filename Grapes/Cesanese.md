# Cesanese

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The red grape of the hills south-east of Rome.

## Where it's from
Grown in the area around Rome for centuries, mostly as Cesanese di Affile and Cesanese del Piglio.

## Where it grows
- **Lazio, Italy:** the hills of Frosinone

## What it tastes like
- Red cherry, spice, violet and herbs

## Classic wines
- Cesanese del Piglio
- Cesanese di Affile

## Pairs with
- Roman pasta
- Roast lamb

## Good to know
- A rare, characterful grape.
- Cesanese di Olevano Romano is also made.
