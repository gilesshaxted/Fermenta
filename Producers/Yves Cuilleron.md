# Yves Cuilleron

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A leading northern Rhône grower, best known for his Viognier from Condrieu and for characterful Saint-Joseph.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Condrieu]], [[Saint-Joseph]], [[Côte-Rôtie]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Viognier]], [[Syrah]], [[Marsanne]]

## About
A family domaine in the northern Rhône with vineyards across several appellations. The whites are aromatic and textured, and the reds are peppery and fresh.

## Known for
- Condrieu Les Chaillets
- Condrieu Vertige
- Saint-Joseph Les Serines
