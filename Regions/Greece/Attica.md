# Attica

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The region around Athens, with Savatiano vines in the Mesogeia plain, the source of traditional retsina.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Greece]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, schist, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Savatiano]], [[Assyrtiko]], [[Malagousia]], [[Agiorgitiko]]
> - **Styles:** [[Retsina]]

## Landscape and topography
Plains and hills around Athens, from the Mesogeia plain to the mountains and the sea.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, clay and sandy soils, with low fertility.

## Climate
Hot, dry Mediterranean, with sea breezes.

## History
Retsina, a wine flavoured with Aleppo pine resin, dates back to antiquity, when resin sealed wine amphorae. It was long seen as cheap, but quality versions have appeared.

## Production and wine styles
Savatiano is the main grape, used in retsina and dry white wines, with Malagousia and others.

## Key wines
- Retsina
- Attica Savatiano
- Malagousia

## What to look for
- Retsina is resinous, with citrus and pine.
- Quality Savatiano can age well.
