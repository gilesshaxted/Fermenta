# Château Le Puy

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A long-established family estate in the Côtes de Francs working organically and biodynamically with little intervention.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Bordeaux]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Cabernet Franc]]
> - **Styles:** [[Bordeaux blend]], [[Natural wine]]

## About
Le Puy is a small, old family estate that has farmed without chemicals for a very long time. The wines are fine, light on their feet and made with minimal additions.
