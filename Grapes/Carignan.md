# Carignan

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Rustic, dark and high in acidity. Old vines turn it into something special.

## Where it's from
Probably from Aragon in Spain, where it is called Cariñena or Mazuelo. It spread widely through the Mediterranean.

## Where it grows
- **Languedoc and Roussillon, France:** old bush vines make concentrated reds
- **Priorat, Spain:** old-vine Cariñena on slate
- **Maule Valley, Chile:** old dry-farmed vines

## What it tastes like
- **Young vines:** harsh, high yield and simple
- **Old vines:** dark fruit, herbs, spice and savoury depth

## Classic wines
- Corbières
- Minervois
- Priorat blends
- Faugères

## Pairs with
- Grilled lamb
- Cassoulet
- Barbecue

## Good to know
- Once used for bulk wine, now a quiet hero when sourced from old vines.
- Usually blended with Grenache, Syrah and Mourvèdre.
