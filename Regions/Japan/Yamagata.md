# Yamagata

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A northern prefecture with a long tradition of fruit growing and a growing number of wineries.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Japan]]
> - **Part of:** [[Asia]]
> - **Climate:** Humid continental
> - **Soils:** alluvial, volcanic, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Delaware]], [[Muscat Bailey A]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Merlot]]

## Landscape and topography
Vineyards lie in basins between mountain ranges, around Kaminoyama, Takahata and Tendo.

## Soil and geology
Alluvial and volcanic soils.

## Climate
Cold winters with heavy snow and warm summers.

## History
Yamagata is better known for fruit than wine, but it has produced wine for many decades and now has a number of respected producers.

## Production and wine styles
Mostly fresh whites and light reds, with Delaware, Chardonnay and Muscat Bailey A common.

## Key wines
- Yamagata Chardonnay
- Yamagata Delaware

## What to look for
- A small but expanding region.
- Look for local grape wines.
