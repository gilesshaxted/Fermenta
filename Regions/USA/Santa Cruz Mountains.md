# Santa Cruz Mountains

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A mountainous area south of San Francisco, making steely Chardonnay and elegant Pinot Noir.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[North America]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** shale, sandstone, limestone, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## Landscape and topography
Steep, forested ridges above Silicon Valley and the Pacific coast.

## Soil and geology
Shale, sandstone and some limestone, thin and well drained.

## Climate
Cool, with fog and altitude giving a long growing season.

## History
Ridge Vineyards' Monte Bello and Mount Eden Vineyards led the region's reputation, and Paul Masson's wines came from here.

## Production and wine styles
Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and Cabernet Sauvignon, in small lots.

## Key wines
- Ridge Monte Bello
- Santa Cruz Mountains Pinot Noir

## What to look for
- Mineral, restrained styles.
- Ridge Monte Bello was in the 2006 Judgment of Paris rematch.
