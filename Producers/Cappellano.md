# Cappellano

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A traditional Serralunga d'Alba estate famous for its Barolo Chinato and ungrafted Nebbiolo.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Barolo]], [[Piedmont]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Nebbiolo]], [[Barbera]]

## About
A small family operation that keeps to old methods. Alongside Barolo it makes a celebrated aromatised Barolo Chinato and a bottling from ungrafted vines.

## Known for
- Barolo Chinato
- Barolo Rupestris
- Barolo Gabutti
