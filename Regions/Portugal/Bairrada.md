# Bairrada

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Home of Baga, a tannic red grape, and Portugal's traditional-method sparkling wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Portugal]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Maritime
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Baga]], [[Arinto]], [[Fernão Pires|Maria Gomes]]

## Landscape and topography
Gentle hills and plains near the Atlantic coast, south of Porto.

## Soil and geology
Clay and limestone, with sandy soils near the coast.

## Climate
Maritime, with Atlantic rain and cooler conditions, so harvests can be late and challenging.

## History
Baga was once seen as rustic, but producers like Luís Pato have made it a serious grape. Sparkling wine and suckling pig are regional specialities.

## Production and wine styles
Baga for reds, Arinto and Maria Gomes for whites, plus Espumante made by the traditional method.

## Key wines
- Bairrada Baga
- Bairrada Espumante
- Bairrada Garrafeira

## What to look for
- High acid, firm tannin and red fruit.
- Great with local roast suckling pig.
