# Zelen

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A local white grape of the Vipava Valley with crisp acidity, and its name means green.

## Where it's from
A Vipava Valley native, grown for a long time and nearly lost before being revived.

## Where it grows
- **Vipava Valley, Slovenia:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Green apple, citrus, herbs and mineral

## Classic wines
- Vipavska Dolina Zelen

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Salads
- Fresh cheese

## Good to know
- The name means green in Slovene.
- Best drunk young.
