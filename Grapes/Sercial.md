# Sercial

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The driest and sharpest style of Madeira, from one of its noble grapes.

## Where it's from
A noble grape of Madeira, grown at high altitude and on the north coast. It ripens late.

## Where it grows
- **Madeira, Portugal:** the cooler north

## What it tastes like
- Lemon, almond, salt and citrus

## Classic wines
- Sercial Madeira

## Pairs with
- Olives
- Soup
- Salted nuts

## Good to know
- High acidity is the key.
- Ages for decades.
