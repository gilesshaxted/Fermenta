# Frankland Estate

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family estate in the Frankland River area of Great Southern, with a reputation for single vineyard Riesling.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Australia]]
> - **Region:** [[Great Southern]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]], [[Syrah]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## About
A small, organically minded estate with a cool climate focus. The Rieslings are crisp and mineral, and the reds are savoury and structured.

## Known for
- Isolation Ridge Riesling
- Olmo's Reward
- Poison Hill Riesling
