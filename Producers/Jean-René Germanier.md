# Jean-René Germanier

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A leading family house in Vétroz, Valais, known for Syrah and for local grapes such as Petite Arvine and Cornalin.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Switzerland]]
> - **Region:** [[Valais]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Petite Arvine]], [[Cornalin]]

## About
Family-run, the house works with growers and its own vines across the Valais. Its wines range from fresh, mineral whites to rich, peppery Syrah.

- Balavaud Syrah
