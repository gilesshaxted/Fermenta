# Bruno Giacosa

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A revered Langhe name known for deeply structured, traditional Barolo and Barbaresco.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Barolo]], [[Barbaresco]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Nebbiolo]], [[Barbera]], [[Arneis]]

## About
Giacosa built his name on buying fruit from the best vineyards and later farming his own. The red label Riserva wines are made only in the finest vintages and are held up as classics of the region.

## Known for
- Barolo Falletto Vigna Le Rocche
- Barbaresco Asili
- Barbaresco Rabajà
