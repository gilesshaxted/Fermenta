# Maharashtra

> [!abstract] At a glance
> India's main wine state, centred on Nashik on the Deccan plateau.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[India]]
> - **Part of:** [[Asia]]
> - **Climate:** Tropical
> - **Soils:** basalt, clay, red loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chenin Blanc]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Syrah]], [[Viognier]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## Landscape and topography
An upland plateau in western India, at about 600 metres above sea level.

## Soil and geology
Dark volcanic basalt and clay, with red loam in places.

## Climate
Tropical, but moderated by altitude, with a monsoon season and a dry, sunny growing period. Vines are managed to avoid the rains.

## History
Modern commercial winemaking began in the late twentieth century and took off with the arrival of producers such as Sula, founded in 1997. The Nashik district is the heart of the industry.

## Production and wine styles
Whites like Chenin Blanc, Sauvignon Blanc and Viognier, reds such as Syrah and Cabernet Sauvignon, and sparkling wine.

## Key wines
- Sula wines
- Nashik Chenin Blanc
- Nashik Syrah

## What to look for
- Fruity, easy-drinking wines with fresh acidity.
- Good with spicy food.
