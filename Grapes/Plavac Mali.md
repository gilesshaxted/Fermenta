# Plavac Mali

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Dalmatia's best known red grape, a cross of Zinfandel's ancestor Tribidrag and Dobričić, grown on steep, sunny slopes.

## Where it's from
A natural cross between Tribidrag (Zinfandel) and Dobričić, discovered by DNA testing. It is the main red of Dalmatia.

## Where it grows
- **Pelješac, Croatia:** Dingač and Postup
- **Hvar, Croatia:** Ivan Dolac
- **Brač, Croatia:** also grown

## What it tastes like
- Black cherry, dried fig, spice and herbs

## Classic wines
- Dingač
- Postup
- Pelješac Plavac Mali

## Pairs with
- Grilled lamb
- Stews
- Hard cheese

## Good to know
- Related to Zinfandel.
- Can be powerful and tannic.
