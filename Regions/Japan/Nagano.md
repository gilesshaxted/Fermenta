# Nagano

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A high, cooler mountain prefecture often called Shinshu, with a fast growing wine scene.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Japan]]
> - **Part of:** [[Asia]]
> - **Climate:** Cool continental
> - **Soils:** volcanic, clay, gravel, alluvial
> - **Main grapes:** [[Merlot]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]], Niagara, [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## Landscape and topography
Vineyards sit on high valley floors and slopes in the Japanese Alps, at some of the highest elevations in the country.

## Soil and geology
Volcanic soils, gravel and clay depending on the valley.

## Climate
Cooler and drier than Yamanashi, with big day to night temperature swings and less summer rain.

## History
Shinshu has a long record of fruit growing, and wine making expanded here in the twentieth century. Recent decades have seen many new small wineries.

## Production and wine styles
Merlot is a standout red, and Chardonnay and Niagara are widely grown. Cooler sites suit Pinot Noir and aromatic whites.

## Key wines
- Shinshu Merlot
- Shinshu Chardonnay
- Shinshu Niagara

## What to look for
- Often called Shinshu on labels.
- The Chikuma river valley is a key area.
