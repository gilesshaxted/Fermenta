# Southern Oregon

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A warmer, more varied region than the Willamette, with Syrah, Tempranillo and Viognier alongside Pinot.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[North America]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** granite, alluvial, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Syrah]], [[Tempranillo]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Viognier]]

## Landscape and topography
Interlinked valleys in the south of the state, such as the Rogue, Applegate and Umpqua.

## Soil and geology
Granitic and alluvial soils.

## Climate
Warmer and drier, with big day to night differences.

## History
Wine pioneers planted here from the 1960s, and the region has since broadened its range.

## Production and wine styles
Pinot Noir, Syrah, Tempranillo, Cabernet Sauvignon and Viognier.

## Key wines
- Rogue Valley Syrah
- Umpqua Pinot Noir

## What to look for
- Warm, ripe fruit and spice.
- A region for variety.
