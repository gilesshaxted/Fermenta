# Emidio Pepe

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A pioneering organic and biodynamic Abruzzo family famous for very long lived natural wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Abruzzo]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Montepulciano]], [[Trebbiano]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]]

## About
A family estate in Torano Nuovo, using hand work, wild yeasts and decades of cellar ageing before release. The reds and whites are pure, earthy and age extraordinarily well.

## Known for
- Montepulciano d'Abruzzo
- Trebbiano d'Abruzzo
- Pecorino
