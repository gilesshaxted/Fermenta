# Surrey

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The North Downs near London, with chalk slopes and a growing number of boutique vineyards.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[England]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** chalk, greensand, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Pinot Meunier]], [[Bacchus]]
> - **Styles:** [[English sparkling wine]]

## Landscape and topography
The steep chalk escarpment of the North Downs and the greensand ridges of the Surrey Hills.

## Soil and geology
Chalk and greensand.

## Climate
Cool, a little sheltered and warmer than other inland areas.

## History
Denbies in Dorking is England's largest single estate vineyard, planted on south-facing chalk slopes from 1986.

## Production and wine styles
Mostly sparkling wine, with still whites and Pinot Noir.

## Key wines
- Denbies sparkling
- Surrey Hills still wines

## What to look for
- Crisp, apple-fruited wines.
- Easy to visit on a day out from London.
