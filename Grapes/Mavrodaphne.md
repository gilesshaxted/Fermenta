# Mavrodaphne

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A dark red grape, best known for the sweet fortified wine of Patras.

## Where it's from
The name means black laurel. It is grown in the Peloponnese and Ionian Islands, and it makes sweet fortified wine in the style of Port.

## Where it grows
- **Patras, Greece:** for sweet wine
- **Cephalonia, Greece:** also grown

## What it tastes like
- Raisin, plum, spice and chocolate

## Classic wines
- Mavrodaphne of Patras
- Mavrodaphne of Cephalonia

## Pairs with
- Chocolate
- Blue cheese
- Nuts

## Good to know
- Also found in dry blends.
- Aged versions are rich and long-lived.
