# Bonarda

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Juicy, easy red that is Argentina's second most planted red grape.

## Where it's from
The Argentine Bonarda is identified with the French Douce Noire (Charbono in California), unrelated to the Italian Bonarda grapes.

## Where it grows
- **Mendoza, Argentina:** widely planted, especially in the east

## What it tastes like
- Black cherry, plum and violet
- Soft tannins and a fruity style

## Classic wines
- Mendoza Bonarda
- Malbec and Bonarda blends

## Pairs with
- Pizza
- Pasta
- Barbecue

## Good to know
- Often an inexpensive alternative to Malbec.
- Old vines give more depth.
