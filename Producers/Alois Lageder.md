# Alois Lageder

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Bolzano based family estate that leads Alto Adige in biodynamic farming.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Alto Adige]], [[Trentino-Alto Adige]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Blanc]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Gewürztraminer]], [[Lagrein]]

## About
Lageder farms organically and biodynamically and works with growers who follow the same principles. The wines are pure and precise, and the range covers many local grapes.

## Known for
- Löwengang Chardonnay
- Cor Römigberg
- Krafuss Pinot Noir
- Benefizium Porer
