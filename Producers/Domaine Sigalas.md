# Domaine Sigalas

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A leading Santorini producer of mineral, volcanic Assyrtiko.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Greece]]
> - **Region:** [[Santorini]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Assyrtiko]], [[Aidani]], [[Athiri]]

## About
A family run estate working old, low-trained basket vines on volcanic soil. The wines are dry, saline and long lived, with some barrel aged styles.

## Known for
- Santorini Assyrtiko
- Nykteri
- Kavalieros
