# Vayots Dzor

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Armenia's best known wine region, home to the Areni grape and some of the highest vineyards in the world.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Armenia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Asia]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** volcanic, limestone, clay, stony loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Areni]], [[Voskehat]]

## Landscape and topography
A mountain province in the south, around the village of Areni, with vineyards at high altitude, often above 1,000 metres.

## Soil and geology
Volcanic and stony soils, with limestone and clay.

## Climate
Dry continental, with hot summers, cold winters and big day and night temperature swings.

## History
The region is home to the Areni-1 cave, where evidence of ancient winemaking has been found, dating back thousands of years.

## Production and wine styles
Fresh, spicy reds from Areni, along with white wines from Voskehat and other local grapes.

## Key wines
- Areni red
- Vayots Dzor Voskehat

## What to look for
- Areni is the native red to try.
- High-altitude freshness.
