# Campania

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Ancient volcanic vineyards around Naples, with Aglianico reds and some of Italy's best southern whites.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** volcanic, tuff, clay, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Aglianico]], [[Fiano]], [[Greco]], [[Falanghina]], [[Piedirosso]]

## Landscape and topography
Coastal plains, volcanic slopes around Vesuvius and high inland hills in the Irpinia area, up to 700 metres.

## Soil and geology
Volcanic ash, tuff and lava around Vesuvius, and clay and limestone inland.

## Climate
Mediterranean on the coast, cooler in the high inland hills.

## History
Known to the Romans as Campania Felix, home of famous Falernian wine. Phylloxera never fully damaged some of the old vines, which grow on their own roots.

## Production and wine styles
Taurasi (Aglianico), Fiano di Avellino, Greco di Tufo and Falanghina. Lacryma Christi is grown on Vesuvius.

## Key wines
- Taurasi
- Fiano di Avellino
- Greco di Tufo
- Falanghina del Sannio
- Lacryma Christi del Vesuvio

## What to look for
- Ancient grapes with real character.
- The whites age better than most expect.
