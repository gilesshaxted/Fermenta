# E. Guigal

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A large, family-run Rhône house based in Ampuis, famous for its single vineyard Côte-Rôties.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Côte-Rôtie]], [[Rhône Valley]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Viognier]], [[Grenache]]

## About
Guigal built its name on Côte-Rôtie and has since grown to make wines across the whole Rhône. The top reds are aged for a long time in new oak and are rich, smoky and powerful, while the entry wines are widely available and reliable.

## Known for
- La Mouline
- La Landonne
- La Turque
- Côtes du Rhône
