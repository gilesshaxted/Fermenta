# Lagrein

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Dark, plummy and floral Alpine red from around Bolzano.

## Where it's from
The Bolzano area of Alto Adige.

## Where it grows
- **Alto Adige, Italy:** around Bolzano

## What it tastes like
- Plum, violet, dark chocolate and a touch of spice

## Classic wines
- Alto Adige Lagrein
- Lagrein Riserva

## Pairs with
- Game
- Speck
- Hearty stews

## Good to know
- A close relative of Teroldego, both from the Alpine south.
- Lagrein rosé is called Kretzer.
