# Luxembourg

Part of [[Europe]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A tiny Moselle wine country with crisp whites and Crémant de Luxembourg, mostly drunk at home.

## How wine is organised here
- The Moselle Luxembourgeoise is the only wine region, with AOP status.
- Crémant de Luxembourg is the protected traditional method sparkling wine.
- Labels usually show the grape variety and the village or vineyard.
