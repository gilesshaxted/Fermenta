# Nashik

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The wine capital of India, a high plateau district with many of the country's best known wineries.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[India]]
> - **Part of:** [[Maharashtra]]
> - **Climate:** Tropical
> - **Soils:** basalt, clay, red loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chenin Blanc]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Syrah]], [[Viognier]]

## Landscape and topography
A plateau with vineyards at about 600 metres around Nashik city and the Godavari river.

## Soil and geology
Dark volcanic basalt soils and clay.

## Climate
Warm, with monsoon rains from June to September, so vines are pruned and managed around the rainy season. Harvest takes place in the dry season, often in February and March.

## History
Grapes were first planted here for table fruit. Wine took hold in the 1990s, and the area is now known for wine tourism as well as production.

## Production and wine styles
Fresh whites from Chenin Blanc and Sauvignon Blanc, reds from Syrah, and sparkling wines.

## Key wines
- Nashik Chenin Blanc
- Nashik Sauvignon Blanc
- Nashik Syrah

## What to look for
- Chenin Blanc is a signature grape.
- Vineyards are open to visitors.
