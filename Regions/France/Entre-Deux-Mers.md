# Entre-Deux-Mers

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Between the two rivers: fresh, dry whites of Sauvignon Blanc and Sémillon.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Bordeaux]]
> - **Climate:** Maritime
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, gravel
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Sémillon]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling hills and plateaux between the Garonne and the Dordogne.

## Soil and geology
Clay and limestone with some gravel and sand.

## Climate
Maritime, with mild, humid conditions that suit fresh white wines.

## History
The name means between two tides, referring to the tidal Garonne and Dordogne.

## Production and wine styles
Only white wines can use the Entre-Deux-Mers name. Reds from the area are sold as Bordeaux or Bordeaux Supérieur.

## Key wines
- Entre-Deux-Mers

## What to look for
- Crisp, citrusy and easy. A classic match for oysters and seafood.
- Usually inexpensive and best drunk young.
