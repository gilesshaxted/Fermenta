> [!abstract] At a glance

## About
A long established Rhône house based at Tain l'Hermitage, with vineyards on the hill and across Crozes-Hermitage. The wines range from approachable Crozes to long-lived Hermitage.

## Known for
- Hermitage La Chapelle
- Crozes-Hermitage Domaine de Thalabert
- Crozes-Hermitage Les Jalets
