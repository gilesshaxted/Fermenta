# Dr. Heger

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A leading Kaiserstuhl estate known for Burgundian grapes grown on volcanic soils at Ihringen.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Germany]]
> - **Region:** [[Baden]]

## About
A family estate on the warm Kaiserstuhl volcanic hill in Baden. The wines are ripe yet precise, notably the Pinot Noir and Grauburgunder from the Winklerberg site.

## Known for
- Ihringer Winklerberg
- Winklerberg Grauburgunder
