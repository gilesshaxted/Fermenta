# Bobal

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Deeply coloured, juicy and fresh, with plenty of acidity. One of Spain's most planted reds.

## Where it's from
Eastern Spain, around Valencia and Utiel-Requena. Many vineyards are old bush vines.

## Where it grows
- **Utiel-Requena, Valencia:** the heartland, reds and rosados
- **Manchuela, Castilla-La Mancha:** high-altitude, fresher styles
- **Valencia, Spain:** everyday reds and rosados

## What it tastes like
- **Fruit:** blackberry, blueberry, plum, dark cherry
- **Fresh:** bright acidity, violet, dried herbs
- **Savoury:** earthy, spicy, a touch of liquorice

## Classic wines
- Utiel-Requena Bobal
- Manchuela Bobal
- Bobal rosado

## Pairs with
- Tapas
- Chorizo
- Grilled lamb
- Tomato-based dishes
- Roast pork

## Good to know
- Deeply coloured but not heavy, so it makes fresh reds and good rosados.
- Fresh acidity and good value make it a popular modern Spanish red.
