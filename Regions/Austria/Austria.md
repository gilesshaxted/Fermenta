# Austria

Part of [[Europe]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small, high-quality producer with crisp whites, Grüner Veltliner above all, and fine reds from Blaufränkisch and Zweigelt.

## How Austrian wine is organised
- **DAC** areas label wine by style of the region, such as Weinviertel or Kamptal.
- **Qualitätswein** and **Prädikatswein** follow ripeness levels, as in Germany.
- In the Wachau, **Steinfeder**, **Federspiel** and **Smaragd** describe increasing ripeness and weight.
- Most wine is white, but red is a growing force.
