# Duras

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A peppery red of Gaillac, with firm tannin.

## Where it's from
Grown around Gaillac since the Middle Ages, and thought to be a relative of Syrah.

## Where it grows
- **Gaillac, France:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Black pepper, blackberry, violet and spice

## Classic wines
- Gaillac Rouge

## Pairs with
- Duck
- Sausages
- Hard cheese

## Good to know
- Not related to Durif.
- Spicy and tannic.
