# Dr. Bürklin-Wolf

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A leading Pfalz estate in Wachenheim, biodynamic and known for classed-site dry Riesling.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Germany]]
> - **Region:** [[Pfalz]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]]

## About
A large, historic estate with extensive holdings in top vineyards of the central Pfalz. The wines are structured and age worthy, and the estate uses a Burgundy-style classification of its sites.
