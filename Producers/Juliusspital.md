# Juliusspital

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of Germany's great charitable wine estates, centred on Würzburg and known for Franconian Silvaner.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Germany]]
> - **Region:** [[Franken]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sylvaner]], [[Riesling]]

## About
A historic estate run for the benefit of a hospital foundation, with vineyards around Würzburg. It is a benchmark for dry Silvaner and Riesling sold in the traditional Bocksbeutel bottle.

## Known for
- Würzburger Stein
- Silvaner
