# Montenidoli

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A pioneering estate in San Gimignano making characterful, textured Vernaccia.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[San Gimignano]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Vernaccia]]

## About
A small estate run for decades by Elisabetta Fagiuoli, farmed organically. Its Vernaccia has more depth and age than most, with a savoury, saline feel.

## Known for
- Vernaccia di San Gimignano Fiore
- Vernaccia di San Gimignano Carato
- Vernaccia di San Gimignano Tradizionale
