# Walker Bay

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cool coastal region near Hermanus, making Burgundy-like wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[South Africa]]
> - **Part of:** [[Africa]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** clay, shale, granite, sandstone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Syrah]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills and valleys by the sea, with whales visible in the bay.

## Soil and geology
Clay-rich shale and granite over sandstone.

## Climate
Cool and maritime, with winds and fog.

## History
Hamilton Russell planted here in 1975, and sparked the region's wine reputation.

## Production and wine styles
Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc and Syrah.

## Key wines
- Hemel-en-Aarde Pinot Noir
- Walker Bay Chardonnay

## What to look for
- Elegant, structured Pinot and Chardonnay.
- Cool-climate Syrah.
