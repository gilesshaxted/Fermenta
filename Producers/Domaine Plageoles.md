# Domaine Plageoles

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A pioneering Gaillac family estate devoted to rescuing old local grape varieties.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Gaillac]], [[South-West France]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention

> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]]

## About
The Plageoles family farm organically and have championed forgotten varieties such as Mauzac and Ondenc. They make still, sparkling and sweet wines in a traditional, non-interventionist way.
