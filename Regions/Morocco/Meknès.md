# Meknès

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Morocco's main wine region, in the foothills of the Middle Atlas, with most of the country's best-known wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Morocco]]
> - **Part of:** [[Africa]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, sand, gravel
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Grenache]], [[Cinsault]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
A plateau and foothills between Fès and the Middle Atlas, at about 500 to 600 metres.

## Soil and geology
Clay, limestone and sandy soils, with gravel in places.

## Climate
Warm Mediterranean, with hot summers and a cooling effect from altitude.

## History
Vines have a long history in Morocco, and plantings expanded under the French protectorate. After independence the industry shrank and later revived with private investment.

## Production and wine styles
Reds from Syrah, Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Grenache, plus Cinsault. Whites and rosés are also made, in a fruity, easy style.

## Key wines
- Meknès Syrah
- Meknès Cabernet Sauvignon
- Guerrouane

## What to look for
- Warm, fruity reds.
- Good value.
