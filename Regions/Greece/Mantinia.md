# Mantinia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A high plateau in the Peloponnese known for aromatic Moschofilero, a pink-skinned grape.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Greece]]
> - **Part of:** [[Peloponnese]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Moschofilero]]

## Landscape and topography
A high plateau at about 650 metres in Arcadia.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, clay and sand.

## Climate
Cool, continental, with high altitude and big day-night temperature swings.

## History
Moschofilero, with its greyish-pink berries, is made as a white wine and also a sparkling and rosé.

## Production and wine styles
100% Moschofilero or blends, in fresh, aromatic styles.

## Key wines
- Mantinia Moschofilero
- Moschofilero sparkling

## What to look for
- Rose, citrus and spice, with fresh acidity.
- A great aperitif.

- [[Tselepos]]
