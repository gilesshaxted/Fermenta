# Orvieto

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A white wine from the volcanic hills around Orvieto, once sweeter and now mostly dry and crisp.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Umbria]]
> - **Climate:** Temperate
> - **Soils:** tuff, clay, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grechetto]], [[Trebbiano]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills around the cliff-top town of Orvieto, on volcanic tuff.

## Soil and geology
Volcanic tuff, clay and limestone.

## Climate
Temperate.

## History
Orvieto was once famous for sweet, noble-rot wines, and is known for its cathedral and underground caves.

## Production and wine styles
Dry white blends from Grechetto, Procanico (Trebbiano) and others. Orvieto Classico is the core zone.

## Key wines
- Orvieto Classico
- Orvieto Superiore

## What to look for
- Peach, almond and citrus.
- A simple, refreshing white.
