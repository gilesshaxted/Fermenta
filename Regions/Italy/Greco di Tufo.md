# Greco di Tufo

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Mineral, structured white from sulphur-rich clay near the village of Tufo.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Campania]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** volcanic, sulphur-rich clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Greco]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills around Tufo in Irpinia.

## Soil and geology
Clay and volcanic soils rich in sulphur.

## Climate
Continental-influenced, with high altitude.

## History
Greco is among the oldest grape varieties in Italy, brought by the Greeks.

## Production and wine styles
100% Greco, with quality focusing on low yields.

## Key wines
- Greco di Tufo

## What to look for
- Peach, almond and a saline, mineral edge.
- Complex enough to age.

## Producers
- [[Pietracupa]]
