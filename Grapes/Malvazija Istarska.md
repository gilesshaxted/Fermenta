# Malvazija Istarska

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Istria's signature white, from crisp and floral to rich, oak-aged and skin-contact styles.

## Where it's from
Grown in Istria for a long time and the main grape of the peninsula. Known in Italy as Malvasia Istriana.

## Where it grows
- **Istria, Croatia:** the heartland
- **Slovenska Istra, Slovenia:** also grown

## What it tastes like
- Green apple, citrus, acacia and almond

## Classic wines
- Istrian Malvazija

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Truffles
- Asparagus

## Good to know
- Often shown in three styles: fresh, oak-aged and skin-contact.
- Not the same as Malvasia of southern Italy.
