# Valentini

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cult Abruzzo estate whose Trebbiano d'Abruzzo is regarded as one of Italy's great white wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Abruzzo]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Trebbiano]], [[Montepulciano]]

## About
A tiny family estate that makes wines in a very traditional way, with minimal fuss and long ageing in old casks. The wines are intense, savoury and slow to evolve.

## Known for
- Trebbiano d'Abruzzo
- Montepulciano d'Abruzzo
- Cerasuolo d'Abruzzo
