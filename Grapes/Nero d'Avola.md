# Nero d'Avola

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Sicily's main red: dark, plummy and sun-ripened.

## Where it's from
Sicily, around Avola, in the south-east of the island.

## Where it grows
- **Sicily, Italy:** across the island, especially the south-east

## What it tastes like
- Black cherry, plum, herbs and soft spice

## Classic wines
- Nero d'Avola
- Cerasuolo di Vittoria
- Noto

## Pairs with
- Grilled meats
- Aubergine dishes
- Tomato pasta

## Good to know
- Often blended with Frappato for a lighter style.
- Also called Calabrese.
