# Maipú

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A warm area east of Mendoza city with some of the oldest vines in the country.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Argentina]]
> - **Part of:** [[Mendoza]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** alluvial, sand, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Malbec]], [[Syrah]], [[Bonarda]]

## Landscape and topography
A flat valley floor at around 700 to 900 metres.

## Soil and geology
Sand and clay alluvium.

## Climate
Warm and dry.

## History
Many old family vineyards survive here, and the area was an early heartland of the industry.

## Production and wine styles
Malbec, Syrah and Bonarda.

## Key wines
- Maipú Malbec

## What to look for
- Rich, ripe and fruity.
- Old vine wines have more depth.
