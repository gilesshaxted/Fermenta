# Dog Point Vineyard

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small Marlborough family estate known for restrained Sauvignon Blanc and the barrel-fermented Section 94.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[New Zealand]]

## About
A quality focused, sustainable producer in the Brancott Valley. The wines are drier and more textured than many in the region.
