# Itata Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of Chile's oldest wine regions, now celebrated for old vine Cinsault, País and Muscat.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Chile]]
> - **Part of:** [[South America]]
> - **Climate:** Cool mediterranean
> - **Soils:** granite, clay, volcanic
> - **Main grapes:** [[País]], [[Muscat]], [[Cinsault]], [[Carignan]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling hills on the coast range, with dry-farmed bush vines.

## Soil and geology
Granite, clay and some volcanic soils.

## Climate
Cooler and wetter than central Chile.

## History
Spanish colonists planted País and Muscat here in the 16th century, and old vines were rediscovered by a new generation.

## Production and wine styles
Old vine País, Cinsault, Carignan and Muscat of Alexandria.

## Key wines
- Itata Cinsault
- Itata País
- Itata Muscat

## What to look for
- Light, fresh reds and fragrant whites.
- A natural wine hotspot.

## Producers
- [[De Martino]]
- [[Louis-Antoine Luyt]]
