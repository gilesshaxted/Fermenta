# Icewine and Eiswein

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Intensely sweet wine from grapes frozen on the vine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Category:** Sweet
> - **Colour:** Varies
> - **Sweetness:** Sweet
> - **Country:** Various
> - **Main grapes:** [[Vidal]], [[Riesling]], [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Welschriesling]]
> - **Regions:** [[Niagara Peninsula]], [[Niagara-on-the-Lake]], [[Okanagan Valley]], [[Mosel]], [[Pfalz]], [[Rheingau]], [[Burgenland]], [[Neusiedlersee]]

## How it's made
Grapes are picked while frozen, at minus 7 degrees or colder in Germany and Austria and minus 8 in Canada, and pressed immediately. The water stays as ice, and the concentrated juice makes tiny amounts of very sweet, high acid wine.

## Where it's made
Germany and Austria use the name Eiswein, and Canada makes more icewine than anywhere else. Riesling is the classic German grape, while Ontario leans on the hardy hybrid Vidal.

## What it tastes like
- Peach, apricot, lime
- Lively acidity
- Honeyed, concentrated

## Pairs with
- Fruit desserts
- Blue cheese
- Foie gras

## Good to know
- Yields are very low, so prices are high.
- Cabernet Franc icewine is a rare, pink and strawberry style.
