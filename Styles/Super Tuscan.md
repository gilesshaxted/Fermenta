# Super Tuscan

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Tuscan reds made outside the traditional rules, often with Bordeaux grapes.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Category:** Still
> - **Colour:** Red
> - **Sweetness:** Dry
> - **Country:** Italy
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sangiovese]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Syrah]]
> - **Regions:** [[Bolgheri]], [[Maremma]], [[Tuscany]]

## How it's made
Producers in the 1970s broke from local rules and blended Cabernet and Merlot with or instead of Sangiovese. Most wines were declassified to table wine, though they have since become icons.

## Where it's made
Coastal Tuscany: Bolgheri and the Maremma, plus other parts of the region.

## What it tastes like
- Black cherry, cedar, tobacco
- Structured, full bodied
- Polished oak

## Pairs with
- Grilled steak
- Roast lamb
- Aged cheese

## Good to know
- Sassicaia began the trend and now has its own DOC.
- Many are labelled IGT Toscana.
