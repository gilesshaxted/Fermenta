# Château Léoville-Las Cases

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Saint-Julien Second Growth with a reputation as a super second for powerful, age-worthy reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]

## About
Léoville-Las Cases is a large, walled vineyard in Saint-Julien next to Latour. The wines are deep, structured and classically built, usually needing patience.
