# Toro

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Powerful, dark red wine from the Tinta de Toro clone of Tempranillo on old, ungrafted vines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** sand, clay, limestone, gravel
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tempranillo]]

## Landscape and topography
A high plateau west of Ribera del Duero, around the Duero river, at about 600 to 750 metres.

## Soil and geology
Sandy soils, which helped some vines survive phylloxera, along with clay and gravel.

## Climate
Dry, hot continental, with extreme heat and cold.

## History
Wines from Toro were praised in the Middle Ages, and Christopher Columbus supposedly took some on his voyage. Modern quality arrived in the 1990s.

## Production and wine styles
Tinta de Toro (Tempranillo), with Garnacha. Wines are dark and structured.

## Key wines
- Toro
- Toro Reserva

## What to look for
- Black fruit, spice and sturdy tannins.
- Great value compared with Ribera del Duero.
