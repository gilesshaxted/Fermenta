# Madiran

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The heartland of Tannat, one of the world's most tannic grapes, made in a rich, serious style.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[South-West France]]
> - **Climate:** Maritime
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, gravel, pebbles
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tannat]], [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## Landscape and topography
Gentle hills in the foothills of the Pyrenees, with a Pyrenean backdrop.

## Soil and geology
Clay and limestone, with stony areas that drain well.

## Climate
Maritime, with warm summers and Atlantic rain, and a foehn-type wind from the Pyrenees.

## History
Monks on the pilgrimage to Santiago made wine here. Micro-oxygenation (microbullage) was developed here to soften Tannat.

## Production and wine styles
Minimum 40% Tannat, often much more, with Cabernet Franc and Cabernet Sauvignon. Tannin is high and ageing is needed.

## Key wines
- Madiran
- Pacherenc du Vic-Bilh (white)

## What to look for
- Black fruit, tar, spice and big tannin.
- Needs rich food, such as confit or cassoulet.
