# Alexander Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A warm, open valley with soft, ripe Cabernet and generous Chardonnay.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[Sonoma County]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** gravel, loam, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Merlot]], [[Zinfandel]]

## Landscape and topography
A wide valley along the Russian River, running northeast of Healdsburg.

## Soil and geology
Gravelly and loamy alluvial soils, with hillside vineyards on volcanic and clay soils.

## Climate
Warmer than Russian River, with less fog.

## History
Land was granted to Cyrus Alexander in the 1840s. Ranching and orchards gave way to vineyards in the 20th century.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon is the star, with Merlot, Zinfandel and Chardonnay.

## Key wines
- Alexander Valley Cabernet Sauvignon

## What to look for
- Soft tannins and ripe dark fruit.
- More approachable than many Napa Cabernets.
