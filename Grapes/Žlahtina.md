# Žlahtina

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A light, fresh white from the island of Krk, centred on the town of Vrbnik.

## Where it's from
Grown on Krk for centuries, and the best known wine of the Kvarner Gulf.

## Where it grows
- **Vrbnik, Krk, Croatia:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Citrus, green apple, herbs and almond

## Classic wines
- Vrbnička Žlahtina

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Fish
- Light salads

## Good to know
- Light and refreshing.
- Rarely seen abroad.
