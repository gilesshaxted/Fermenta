# Nandi Hills

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Hills north of Bangalore, where the altitude gives some of India's best-known vineyard sites.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[India]]
> - **Part of:** [[Karnataka]]
> - **Climate:** Tropical
> - **Soils:** red loam, granite, sandy loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Syrah]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Chenin Blanc]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling hills at roughly 800 to 900 metres, with vineyards on gentle slopes.

## Soil and geology
Red loam and granite-derived sandy soils.

## Climate
Moderate for the tropics, with cooler nights and a defined dry season.

## History
A well known example is the Nandi Hills vineyard of Grover Zampa, which brought international grapes to the region.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon and Syrah reds, with Sauvignon Blanc and Chenin Blanc whites.

## Key wines
- Nandi Hills Cabernet Sauvignon
- Nandi Hills Sauvignon Blanc

## What to look for
- A cooler setting for a tropical country.
- Look for Bangalore-region wines.
