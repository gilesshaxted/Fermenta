> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Valle de Guadalupe estate with an angel-themed range and a popular guest stay.

## About
Adobe Guadalupe is a family-run vineyard and winery in Baja. The reds are blended from Rhône and Bordeaux grapes and named after archangels.
