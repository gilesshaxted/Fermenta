# Usakhelauri

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A rare, dark Georgian red grape, used for semi-sweet wine in Lechkhumi.

## Where it's from
A local red from the Lechkhumi area. It makes some of Georgia's rarest and most prized semi-sweet reds.

## Where it grows
- **Racha-Lechkhumi, Georgia:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Dark cherry, blackcurrant, spice and earth

## Classic wines
- Usakhelauri

## Pairs with
- Dessert
- Hard cheese
- Spiced dishes

## Good to know
- A very rare grape.
- Usually semi-sweet.
