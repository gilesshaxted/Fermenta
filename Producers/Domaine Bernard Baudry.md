# Domaine Bernard Baudry

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family Chinon domaine known for fresh, precise and gastronomic Cabernet Franc.

> [!info] Quick facts

## About
Baudry works vineyards on gravel, sand and limestone around Cravant-les-Coteaux. The wines are pure, aromatic and finely structured rather than heavy.

- Le Clos Guillot
