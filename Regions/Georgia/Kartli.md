# Kartli

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A central region around Tbilisi and Gori, known for light, crisp whites and sparkling wine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Georgia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Asia]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, sandy loam, alluvial
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chinuri]], [[Tavkveri]], [[Mtsvane]], [[Saperavi]]
> - **Styles:** [[Qvevri wine]]

## Landscape and topography
A broad valley along the Mtkvari river in central Georgia, around Tbilisi and Gori, with vineyards on the valley floor and gentler slopes.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, clay and sandy loam, with alluvial soils near the river.

## Climate
Continental and drier than the west, with warm summers and cold winters.

## History
Kartli has been a winegrowing area for centuries, and was historically an important centre of Georgian power. It has a tradition of sparkling wine from Chinuri.

## Production and wine styles
Light, fresh whites from Chinuri and Mtsvane, base wines for sparkling, and some reds from Tavkveri and Saperavi.

## Key wines
- Atenuri
- Kartli Chinuri
- Kartli sparkling

## What to look for
- Crisp, high acid whites.
- Look for Chinuri sparkling.
