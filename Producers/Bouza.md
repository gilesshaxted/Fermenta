# Bouza

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small family bodega near Montevideo, known for Tannat and fresh Albariño.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Uruguay]]
> - **Region:** [[Montevideo]]
> - **Type:** Major producer

## About
Bouza is a family-run, quality-focused estate that also keeps a classic car collection on site. The wines are well made and food friendly.

## Known for
- Bouza Tannat A6
- Bouza Albariño
- Monte Vide Eco Tannat
