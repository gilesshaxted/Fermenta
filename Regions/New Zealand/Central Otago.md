# Central Otago

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The world's southernmost wine region, with alpine scenery and powerful Pinot Noir.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[New Zealand]]
> - **Part of:** [[Oceania]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** schist, loess, gravel, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Riesling]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
Dramatic, mountainous high-country basin with vineyards on lake terraces and schist hillsides.

## Soil and geology
Schist-based soils with windblown loess and glacial gravels.

## Climate
Dry and sunny, with the most continental climate in New Zealand: hot days, cold nights, frost risk.

## History
Gold Rush settlers planted vines in the 1860s, and modern plantings began in the 1980s.

## Production and wine styles
Pinot Noir leads, with Pinot Gris, Riesling and Chardonnay.

## Key wines
- Central Otago Pinot Noir
- Central Otago Riesling

## What to look for
- Dark cherry, thyme and spice.
- Bright acidity.
