# Württemberg

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A red-wine heartland near Stuttgart, with Trollinger, Lemberger and Riesling, mostly drunk locally.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Germany]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Temperate
> - **Soils:** keuper, limestone, marl, muschelkalk
> - **Main grapes:** [[Trollinger]], [[Riesling]], [[Blaufränkisch|Lemberger]], [[Pinot Noir]]

## Landscape and topography
Gentle valleys and steep terraced slopes along the Neckar river.

## Soil and geology
Keuper marl and clay, limestone and sandstone.

## Climate
Temperate and warm, with hilly sheltered slopes.

## History
The Swabians drink most of their wine at home, so little is exported. Younger winemakers are bringing a sense of excitement.

## Production and wine styles
Trollinger, Lemberger (Blaufränkisch) and Spätburgunder for reds, Riesling for whites.

## Key wines
- Württemberg Lemberger
- Trollinger
- Württemberg Riesling

## What to look for
- Light, fruity reds.
- Lemberger offers peppery depth.
