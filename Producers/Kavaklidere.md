# Kavaklidere

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of Turkey's oldest and best known wineries, a pioneer of local grape varieties.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Turkey]]
> - **Region:** [[Central Anatolia]], [[Aegean Turkey]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Öküzgözü]], [[Boğazkere]], [[Kalecik Karası]], [[Narince]], [[Emir]]

## About
Based in Ankara and sourcing from several regions, Kavaklidere champions native grapes alongside international ones. The range runs from everyday bottles to premium reds.

- Pendore
