# Negev

> [!abstract] At a glance
> An arid desert region in the south, where vines grow with careful irrigation and cool nights.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Israel]]
> - **Part of:** [[Asia]]
> - **Climate:** Semi-arid
> - **Soils:** loess, sand, limestone, gravel
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Syrah]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]]

## Landscape and topography
High desert plateau and valleys, with vineyards at around 500 to 900 metres.

## Soil and geology
Loess, sand and stony soils.

## Climate
Dry and hot by day, with big temperature drops at night and almost no rainfall.

## History
Vines were grown here in ancient times by the Nabataeans, whose terraced farms in the desert can still be seen. Modern plantings began in the 1990s.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Syrah and Chardonnay from small, irrigated vineyards.

## Key wines
- Negev Cabernet Sauvignon
- Negev red blends

## What to look for
- Sun-ripened fruit with cool-night freshness.
- A desert wine region.
