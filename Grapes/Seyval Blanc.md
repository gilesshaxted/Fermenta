# Seyval Blanc

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A hybrid grape that thrives in cool, damp places such as England.

## Where it's from
A French hybrid created in the 1920s, it ripens early and resists disease. It is widely planted in England, and also in New York.

## Where it grows
- **England:** in the south-west and Cotswolds
- **New York, USA:** also planted

## What it tastes like
- Lemon, green apple, herbs and grass

## Classic wines
- English still whites
- Sparkling blends

## Pairs with
- Fish
- Salads
- Soft cheese

## Good to know
- Many French appellations exclude hybrid varieties, although permitted varieties depend on the individual appellation rules.
- Fresh, light wines.
