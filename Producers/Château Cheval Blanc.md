# Château Cheval Blanc

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A top-ranked Saint-Émilion estate known for a rare Cabernet Franc-led, silky red.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Saint-Émilion]], [[Bordeaux]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Merlot]]
> - **Styles:** [[Bordeaux blend]]

## About
Cheval Blanc sits on the border with Pomerol on a mix of gravel and clay. Its red is perfumed, plush and unusually Cabernet Franc dominated for the right bank.
