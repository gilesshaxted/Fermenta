# Italy

Part of [[Europe]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Twenty regions, over 500 grape varieties and a wine culture tied closely to food. The world's biggest producer by volume, with more local grapes than anywhere else.

## How Italian wine is organised
- **DOCG** is the top tier, **DOC** the next, **IGT** allows more freedom (many Super Tuscans sit here), and **Vino** is the simplest.
- Labels use place names (Chianti, Barolo) or grape plus place (Barbera d'Asti, Montepulciano d'Abruzzo). Learn both patterns.
- Riserva and Superiore usually mean longer ageing or higher alcohol, and Classico points to the historic heartland.
