# Bacchus

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Sauvignon-like, aromatic white that thrives in England.

## Where it's from
Bred in Germany in 1933 by crossing Silvaner and Riesling, followed by a further cross with Müller-Thurgau. It has been widely adopted in England.

## Where it grows
- **England:** widely planted for still whites
- **Germany:** the origin

## What it tastes like
- Elderflower, gooseberry and grapefruit

## Classic wines
- English Bacchus

## Pairs with
- Fish and chips
- Seafood
- Goat's cheese

## Good to know
- Often called England's Sauvignon Blanc.
- Drink young and fresh.
