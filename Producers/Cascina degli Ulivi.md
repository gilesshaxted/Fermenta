# Cascina degli Ulivi

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A biodynamic pioneer in Gavi, making characterful Cortese far from the commercial norm.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Gavi]], [[Piedmont]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cortese]], [[Barbera]], [[Dolcetto]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]]

## About
Stefano Bellotti farms biodynamically near Novi Ligure and bottles low intervention wines with a distinct personality. The Gavi here is textured and ages surprisingly well.

## Known for
- Filagnotti
- Montemarino
- Semplicemente Bianco
