# Mavro

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The most planted grape in Cyprus, a dark-skinned variety used for light reds, rosés and Commandaria.

## Where it's from
The name simply means black. It has long been the island's workhorse variety, and old bush vines in the hills are valued today. The wines are often light, but it is blended into Commandaria.

## Where it grows
- **Troodos, Cyprus:** old bush vines
- **Limassol, Cyprus:** the main area

## What it tastes like
- Red cherry, plum, raisin and earth

## Classic wines
- Commandaria
- Cypriot rosé

## Pairs with
- Grilled meats
- Kebabs
- Mezze

## Good to know
- Black in Greek.
- Used in Commandaria with Xynisteri.
