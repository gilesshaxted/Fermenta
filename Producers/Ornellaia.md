# Ornellaia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A leading Bolgheri estate making polished Bordeaux style reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Bolgheri]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Petit Verdot]]
> - **Styles:** [[Super Tuscan]], [[Bordeaux blend]]

## About
Founded as part of the early wave of Bolgheri estates on the Tuscan coast. The wines are rich, aromatic and refined, and are among the region's most collected.
