# Lisbon

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Atlantic-cooled vineyards around the capital, home of Colares, Bucelas and light, fresh wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Portugal]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Maritime
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Arinto]], [[Fernão Pires]], [[Touriga Nacional]], [[Castelão]]

## Landscape and topography
Gently rolling hills and valleys, and coastal sands, north of Lisbon, in a cooler Atlantic climate.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, clay and sandy soil. Colares' sandy, ungrafted vines are unique.

## Climate
Cool and maritime, with sea breezes and wind.

## History
Bucelas is famed for Arinto, and Colares has ungrafted vines planted in sand, which protected them from phylloxera.

## Production and wine styles
Arinto and Fernão Pires for whites, Castelão and Touriga Nacional for reds. Wines range from cheap to quite fine.

## Key wines
- Bucelas Arinto
- Colares
- Lisboa tinto

## What to look for
- Fresh and crisp, with sea air.
- Colares is a rare, tannic red.
