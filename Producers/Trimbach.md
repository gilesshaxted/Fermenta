# Trimbach

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family estate in Ribeauvillé known for dry, taut, long-lived Alsace Riesling.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Alsace]], [[Alsace Grand Cru]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Gewürztraminer]]

## About
Trimbach aims for dry, precise wines and often avoids malolactic fermentation. The Rieslings in particular are steely and age for decades.

## Known for
- Clos Sainte Hune
- Cuvée Frédéric Émile
- Riesling Réserve
