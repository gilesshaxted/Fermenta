# Kaiserstuhl

> [!abstract] At a glance
> An extinct volcano in the Rhine plain, one of Germany's warmest places, making rich Pinot wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Germany]]
> - **Part of:** [[Baden]]
> - **Climate:** Temperate
> - **Soils:** volcanic, loess, basalt
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Pinot Blanc]]

## Landscape and topography
A rounded volcanic hill rising from the Rhine plain, with terraced vineyards.

## Soil and geology
Volcanic rock (tephrite and phonolite) with loess.

## Climate
One of Germany's warmest and driest sites, with sun and heat storage in the rocks.

## History
Terraces were rebuilt in the 1970s through the Flurbereinigung land consolidation, a controversial process.

## Production and wine styles
Spätburgunder and Grauburgunder, in mineral, powerful styles.

## Key wines
- Kaiserstuhl Spätburgunder
- Kaiserstuhl Grauburgunder

## What to look for
- Ripe cherry and mineral spice for Pinot Noir.
- Full-bodied whites.
