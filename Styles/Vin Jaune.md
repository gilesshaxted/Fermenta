# Vin Jaune

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Jura's famous oxidative white, aged under a veil of yeast for at least six years and three months.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Category:** Still
> - **Colour:** White
> - **Sweetness:** Dry
> - **Country:** France
> - **Main grapes:** [[Savagnin]]
> - **Regions:** [[Jura]], [[Arbois and Château-Chalon]]

## How it's made
Savagnin ferments dry, then ages in old barrels that are not topped up. A film of yeast (voile) forms on the surface, as with Fino, and the wine develops walnut and curry spice notes. It is bottled in the 62 cl clavelin.

## Where it's made
The Jura in eastern France, especially Arbois and Château-Chalon.

## What it tastes like
- Walnut, curry spice, dried apricot
- Sharp and savoury
- Nutty finish

## Pairs with
- Comté
- Poultry in cream and morels
- Walnuts

## Good to know
- Comté cheese is the classic partner.
- Vin Jaune can last for decades.
