# Verdicchio

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Zesty, nutty white from the Marche that can age for a decade.

## Where it's from
The Marche, on the Adriatic side of central Italy.

## Where it grows
- **Castelli di Jesi, Italy:** the best-known zone
- **Matelica, Italy:** a cooler, more mineral inland zone

## What it tastes like
- Lemon, almond and a bitter, saline finish

## Classic wines
- Verdicchio dei Castelli di Jesi Classico
- Verdicchio di Matelica

## Pairs with
- Fish
- Risotto
- Roast chicken

## Good to know
- Can resemble white Burgundy with age.
- Great value.
