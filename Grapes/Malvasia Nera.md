# Malvasia Nera

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A dark, aromatic red used in blends in Salento.

## Where it's from
Different from the white Malvasias, it is grown mainly in Puglia, where it is blended with Negroamaro.

## Where it grows
- **Salento, Italy:** with Negroamaro

## What it tastes like
- Black cherry, plum, spice and violet

## Classic wines
- Salice Salentino
- Salento Rosso

## Pairs with
- Pasta
- Grilled meat

## Good to know
- Adds perfume to Negroamaro.
- Also grown in Piedmont.
