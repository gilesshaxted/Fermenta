# Mexico

Part of [[North America]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Mexico has one of the oldest wine histories in the Americas, with Baja California now leading in quality.

## How wine is organised here
- Wines are mostly labelled by producer and grape or blend rather than by a formal appellation system.
- Valle de Guadalupe in Baja California is the best known area and has many boutique estates.
