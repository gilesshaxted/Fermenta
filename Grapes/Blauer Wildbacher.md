# Blauer Wildbacher

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A high-acid red used for Schilcher, Styria's famous pink wine.

## Where it's from
A native of western Styria, where it makes the rosé called Schilcher.

## Where it grows
- **Western Styria, Austria:** on steep slopes

## What it tastes like
- Sour cherry, red berries, herbs and earth

## Classic wines
- Schilcher

## Pairs with
- Pumpkin seed oil dishes
- Cold cuts
- Grilled sausages

## Good to know
- Schilcher is a salmon-pink wine with sharp acidity.
- A true local speciality.
