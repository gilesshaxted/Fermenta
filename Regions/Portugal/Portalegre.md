# Portalegre

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The cool, high north of the Alentejo, with granite soils and elegant wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Portugal]]
> - **Part of:** [[Alentejo]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** granite, schist
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grenache]], [[Touriga Nacional]], [[Alicante Bouschet]], [[Arinto]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling highlands around the Serra de São Mamede, up to 700 metres.

## Soil and geology
Granite and schist.

## Climate
Cooler than the rest of the Alentejo, with altitude giving fresher acidity.

## History
Old vineyards of mixed varieties survive in this region, and the wines have a sense of place.

## Production and wine styles
Reds and whites with freshness and finesse.

## Key wines
- Portalegre tinto
- Portalegre branco

## What to look for
- Elegant red and black fruit.
- A cooler counterpoint to the warm south.
