# Condrieu

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Home of Viognier at its most perfumed and opulent, on a handful of steep granite terraces.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Rhône Valley]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** granite, mica schist, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Viognier]]

## Landscape and topography
Steep terraces on the right bank of the Rhône south of Côte-Rôtie.

## Soil and geology
Decomposed granite (known locally as arzelle) mixed with mica and sand.

## Climate
Continental. The terraces collect sun while the river moderates conditions.

## History
Viognier nearly vanished here in the 1960s, with only a few hectares left, before a revival.

## Production and wine styles
100% Viognier, in styles from dry to rich, with some oak.

## Key wines
- Condrieu
- Château-Grillet

## What to look for
- Apricot, peach, honeysuckle and white blossom.
- Serve cool, not cold, with shellfish and creamy dishes.

## Producers
- [[Georges Vernay]]
- [[Yves Cuilleron]]
