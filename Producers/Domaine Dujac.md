# Domaine Dujac

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Morey-Saint-Denis domaine known for perfumed, elegant red Burgundy, often using whole bunches.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Côte de Nuits]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]]

## About
Dujac farms organically and biodynamically and uses a notable proportion of whole-bunch fermentation. The wines are aromatic, silky and graceful.

## Known for
- Clos de la Roche
- Clos Saint-Denis
