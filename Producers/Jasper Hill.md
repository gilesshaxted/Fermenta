# Jasper Hill

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small organic Heathcote estate, known for Georgia's Paddock and Emily's Paddock Shiraz.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Australia]]
> - **Region:** [[Heathcote]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]]

## About
A family-run vineyard farmed organically on old Cambrian soils. The wines are powerful, spicy and age very well.

## Known for
- Georgia's Paddock Shiraz
- Emily's Paddock
- Cambrian Rise
