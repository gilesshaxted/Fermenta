# Aleksandrović Winery

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A well known Serbian estate on the slopes of Oplenac, known for its sparkling and reserve reds.

## About
Based around Topola in central Serbia, this is a family-run winery with a modern cellar. It is best known for a traditional method sparkling wine and for oak-aged red blends.
