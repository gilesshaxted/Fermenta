# Auckland

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Small vineyards around New Zealand's biggest city, including Waiheke Island and Kumeu.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[New Zealand]]
> - **Part of:** [[Oceania]]
> - **Climate:** Warm maritime
> - **Soils:** clay, volcanic, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Syrah]], [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## Landscape and topography
Coastal hills, valleys and islands in the north.

## Soil and geology
Clay, volcanic and sandy soils.

## Climate
Warm and humid, with a sea influence.

## History
Croatian and Dalmatian immigrants planted early vineyards in the 19th century.

## Production and wine styles
Chardonnay, Syrah, Merlot and Cabernet blends.

## Key wines
- Waiheke Island Syrah
- Kumeu Chardonnay

## What to look for
- Rich Bordeaux-style blends.
- Waiheke Island is a popular day trip.
