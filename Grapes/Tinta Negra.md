# Tinta Negra

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The most planted grape on Madeira, used for the majority of everyday Madeira wine.

## Where it's from
The main grape for most Madeira. Its parentage is only partly known; ==it is probably of Spanish origin and related to Negramoll==

## Where it grows
- **Madeira, Portugal:** across the island

## What it tastes like
- Caramel, nuts, dried fruit and spice

## Classic wines
- Madeira (Seco, Meio Seco, Doce)

## Pairs with
- Dessert
- Cheese
- Nuts

## Good to know
- Produces the full range of styles.
- Used in most basic and 5 year old wines.
