# Mavrud

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Bulgaria's most prized native red, grown in the Thracian Valley and known for deep colour and firm tannins.

## Where it's from
A native of southern Bulgaria, grown around Asenovgrad and the Thracian Valley for a long time. It ripens late and needs a hot, dry summer.

## Where it grows
- **Thracian Valley, Bulgaria:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Blackberry, plum, spice and earth

## Classic wines
- Thracian Valley Mavrud

## Pairs with
- Grilled lamb
- Stews
- Hard cheese

## Good to know
- Late ripening and deeply coloured.
- Can age well in good years.
