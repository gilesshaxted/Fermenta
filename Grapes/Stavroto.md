# Stavroto

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A native of Mount Olympus and a rare partner to Xinomavro in Rapsani.

## Where it's from
A very old variety, almost unknown outside Rapsani, planted in tiny quantities.

## Where it grows
- **Rapsani, Greece:** the only home

## What it tastes like
- Red cherry, herbs, spice and earth

## Classic wines
- Rapsani

## Pairs with
- Lamb
- Hard cheese

## Good to know
- Rarely grown elsewhere.
- Adds soft fruit to the blend.
