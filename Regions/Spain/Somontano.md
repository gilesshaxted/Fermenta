# Somontano

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Foothills of the Pyrenees in Aragón, making fresh whites and reds from a mix of grapes.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tempranillo]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Gewürztraminer]]

## Landscape and topography
Foothill terraces below the Pyrenees, at about 350 to 650 metres.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, clay and sandy soils.

## Climate
Continental, with cold winters, warm summers and Pyrenean influence.

## History
Somontano was among the first to experiment with international varieties in Spain. Its DO was created in 1984.

## Production and wine styles
Both Spanish and international grapes, with Gewürztraminer and Chardonnay for whites.

## Key wines
- Somontano Tempranillo
- Somontano Gewürztraminer

## What to look for
- Fresh, fruit-led wines.
- A good value region to explore.
