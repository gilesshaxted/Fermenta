# CVNE

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A long established Haro family company, pronounced 'coo-nay', with a broad range from fresh Crianzas to age worthy Gran Reservas.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Region:** [[Rioja Alta]]

## About
A classic Rioja bodega working from the Haro station district, with several labels under one roof. Its wines generally balance fruit with traditional oak and cellar ageing.

- Viña Real
