# Bodega Colomé

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of the oldest wineries in Argentina, set at extreme altitude in the Calchaquí Valleys of Salta.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Argentina]]
> - **Region:** [[Salta]], [[Cafayate]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Malbec]], [[Torrontés]], [[Tannat]]

## About
An estate winery with some of the highest vineyards in the world, giving dark, intense reds and aromatic whites. Wines are known for strong structure and clear mountain character.
