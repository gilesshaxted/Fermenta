# Domaine Vincent Dauvissat

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Chablis domaine known for deep, mineral wines from the Les Clos and La Forest vineyards.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Chablis]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]]

## About
Dauvissat is a traditional family estate working mainly in old barrels. The wines are rich yet tense and long lived.

## Known for
- Chablis Les Clos
- Chablis La Forest
- Chablis Séchet
