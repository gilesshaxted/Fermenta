# Martinborough

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Wairarapa's best-known district, a village surrounded by vineyards.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[New Zealand]]
> - **Part of:** [[Wairarapa]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** gravel, silt, stony loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]]

## Landscape and topography
A flat terrace, around the village of Martinborough.

## Soil and geology
Stony, free-draining river terraces.

## Climate
Cool, dry and windy.

## History
A pioneer of New Zealand Pinot Noir from the early 1980s.

## Production and wine styles
Pinot Noir, and some Sauvignon Blanc.

## Key wines
- Martinborough Pinot Noir

## What to look for
- Rich yet savoury.
- Close to Wellington for a visit.
