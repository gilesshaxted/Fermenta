# Domaine de la Tournelle

> [!abstract] At a glance
> An Arbois domaine run by Evelyne and Pascal Clairet and an early name in natural Jura.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Arbois and Château-Chalon]], [[Jura]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Poulsard]], [[Savagnin]], [[Trousseau]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]]

## About
The wines are made with little intervention and low sulphur. They show the lightness of Jura reds and the savoury edge of the whites.

## Known for
- Fleur de Savagnin
- Uva Arbosiana
