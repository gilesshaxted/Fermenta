# Yakima Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Washington's oldest AVA, with a wide range of grapes and a strong tradition for Riesling.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[Columbia Valley]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** loess, silt, sandy loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Merlot]], [[Syrah]]

## Landscape and topography
A broad valley along the Yakima River, with irrigated vineyards.

## Soil and geology
Silty loams over basalt and gravel.

## Climate
Dry, with hot days and cool nights.

## History
Washington's first AVA, established in 1983, and a hub for the early industry.

## Production and wine styles
Riesling, Chardonnay, Merlot, Syrah and Cabernet.

## Key wines
- Yakima Valley Riesling
- Yakima Valley Syrah

## What to look for
- Crisp fruit and acidity.
- Good value.
