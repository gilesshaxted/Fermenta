# Envínate

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A four-person collective making fresh, saline, low intervention wines from old vines on Tenerife and beyond.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Region:** [[Canary Islands]], [[Spain]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Listán Negro]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]]

## About
Envínate works with tiny plots of old vines on volcanic soils and keeps winemaking light. The Canary wines have become reference points for the natural scene.

## Known for
- Táganan
- Benje
- Albahaca
- Palo Blanco
