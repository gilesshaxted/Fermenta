# Larmandier-Bernier

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Vertus grower and a reference point for biodynamic, low dosage Côte des Blancs Champagne.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Champagne]], [[Côte des Blancs]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]]
> - **Styles:** [[Champagne (style)]]

## About
Larmandier-Bernier farms biodynamically and bottles most wines with very little sugar. The style is precise, chalky and salty, with real depth.

## Known for
- Longitude
- Terre de Vertus
- Latitude
- Vieille Vigne du Levant
