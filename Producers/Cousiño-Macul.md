# Cousiño-Macul

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of Chile's oldest family-run wine producers, known for classic Maipo Cabernet.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Chile]]
> - **Region:** [[Maipo Valley]]

## About
A long established estate in the Maipo Valley, with a traditional approach. The wines are restrained, savoury and often good value as they age.
