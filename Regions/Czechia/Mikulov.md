# Mikulov

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A sunny sub-region around the Pálava hills, near the Austrian border, known for aromatic whites.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Czechia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Moravia]]
> - **Climate:** Warm continental
> - **Soils:** limestone, loess, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pálava]], [[Grüner Veltliner]], [[Riesling]], [[Welschriesling]], [[Pinot Blanc]]

## Landscape and topography
Chalky hills and slopes around the town of Mikulov, in the south of Moravia.

## Soil and geology
Limestone and loess.

## Climate
Among the warmest and driest areas of the country.

## History
The sub-region is named after the town of Mikulov, a wine town with Habsburg-era cellars. The Pálava grape is named for the nearby hills.

## Production and wine styles
Aromatic whites, including Pálava, Riesling and Pinot Blanc, as well as Grüner Veltliner.

## Key wines
- Mikulov Pálava
- Mikulov Riesling

## What to look for
- Floral, aromatic whites.
- A good place to try Pálava.
