# Montefalco

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The only home of Sagrantino, one of the world's most tannic grapes.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Umbria]]
> - **Climate:** Temperate
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sagrantino]], [[Sangiovese]]

## Landscape and topography
Gentle hills around the hill town of Montefalco, with views over the Umbrian valley.

## Soil and geology
Clay and limestone, with sandy layers.

## Climate
Temperate, with warm days and cool nights.

## History
Sagrantino was once used for sweet passito wine. A dry version appeared in the 1970s and the DOCG was granted in 1992.

## Production and wine styles
Montefalco Sagrantino is 100% Sagrantino, aged at least 37 months. Montefalco Rosso blends Sangiovese with Sagrantino.

## Key wines
- Montefalco Sagrantino
- Montefalco Sagrantino Passito
- Montefalco Rosso

## What to look for
- Blackberry, tar and drying tannin.
- Needs rich food or years in the cellar.

- [[Arnaldo Caprai]]
- [[Paolo Bea]]
