# Pessac-Léognan

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Bordeaux's southern gravels beside the city, producing refined reds and some of the best dry whites in the region.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Bordeaux]]
> - **Climate:** Maritime
> - **Soils:** gravel, sand, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Sémillon]]
> - **Styles:** [[Bordeaux blend]]

## Landscape and topography
Low gravel hills (croupes) just south of Bordeaux city, partly swallowed by suburbs.

## Soil and geology
Ancient river gravel over clay and sand. The stones hold heat and help ripening.

## Climate
Maritime, slightly warmer than the Médoc thanks to the shelter of the pine forest and the heat of the city.

## History
Château Haut-Brion was ranked a First Growth in 1855, the only one from outside the Médoc. The appellation was created in 1987, splitting from Graves.

## Production and wine styles
Red wines are Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot blends. Dry whites are Sauvignon Blanc and Sémillon, often barrel-fermented.

## Key wines
- Château Haut-Brion
- La Mission Haut-Brion
- Domaine de Chevalier
- Château Smith Haut Lafitte

## What to look for
- Reds show smoke, tobacco and warm earth.
- Whites are waxy, toasty and long-lived.
