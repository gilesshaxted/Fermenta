# Szekszárd

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A warm region with deep loess soils, famous for Kadarka and silky red blends.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Hungary]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Warm continental
> - **Soils:** loess, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Kadarka]], [[Blaufränkisch]], [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling hills on the west bank of the Danube, with vineyards on steep loess ridges.

## Soil and geology
Deep loess over clay.

## Climate
Warm continental, with long, warm autumns.

## History
A wine region since Roman times. The Szekszárd region revived Kadarka and has a strong reputation for family estates.

## Production and wine styles
Reds, from Kadarka and Kékfrankos to Bordeaux varieties. Szekszárdi Bikavér is a regional blend.

## Key wines
- Szekszárd Kadarka
- Szekszárdi Bikavér
- Szekszárd Kékfrankos

## What to look for
- Kadarka is the grape to try here.
- Soft, spicy and friendly reds.
