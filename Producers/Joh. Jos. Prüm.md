# Joh. Jos. Prüm

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A benchmark Mosel family estate famed for long-lived, fine boned Riesling from Wehlen.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Germany]]
> - **Region:** [[Mosel]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]]

## About
A family run estate in Wehlen with holdings in the great steep sites around Bernkastel. The wines are light in alcohol, delicate and precise, and are famous for ageing very well in bottle.

## Known for
- Wehlener Sonnenuhr
- Graacher Himmelreich
