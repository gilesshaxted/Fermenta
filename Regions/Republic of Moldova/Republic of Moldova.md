# Republic of Moldova

Part of [[Europe]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small country with a huge share of land under vines, fresh whites, Soviet-era underground cellars and growing quality in its native reds.

## How wine is organised here
- Wine is made under Moldovan geographical indication rules, with PGI regions such as Codru, Ștefan Vodă and Valul lui Traian.
- Labels often name the grape variety and the region, and can include Fetească, Rară Neagră and Aligoté.
- The Republic of Moldova is a separate country from the Moldova region of Romania, although they share a history and some grapes.
