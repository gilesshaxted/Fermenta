# Fiano

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Waxy, nutty and age-worthy. One of southern Italy's top whites.

## Where it's from
Campania, around Avellino.

## Where it grows
- **Irpinia, Campania:** the heartland
- **Puglia and Sicily, Italy:** smaller plantings

## What it tastes like
- Hazelnut, pear, honey and smoke

## Classic wines
- Fiano di Avellino

## Pairs with
- Shellfish
- White meats
- Soft cheeses

## Good to know
- Often ages better than expected.
- Mastroberardino revived it.
