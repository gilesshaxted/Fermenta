# Leithaberg

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Limestone hills on the northwest of Lake Neusiedl, with elegant, mineral wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Austria]]
> - **Part of:** [[Burgenland]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** limestone, schist, slate
> - **Main grapes:** [[Blaufränkisch]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Blanc]]

## Landscape and topography
A hilly ridge, the Leitha mountains, along the western shore of Lake Neusiedl.

## Soil and geology
Limestone and schist, giving structure and minerality.

## Climate
Warm, with cooling influence from the hills.

## History
Leithaberg DAC was introduced in 2009 for white and red wines of mineral style.

## Production and wine styles
Blaufränkisch for red, and Chardonnay, Pinot Blanc or Neuburger for white.

## Key wines
- Leithaberg DAC Rot
- Leithaberg DAC Weiss

## What to look for
- Elegant, mineral and fresh.
- A serious Austrian style.
