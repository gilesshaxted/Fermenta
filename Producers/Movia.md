# Movia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A biodynamic estate straddling the Slovenian and Italian border, a pioneer of natural wine in the area.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Slovenia]]
> - **Region:** [[Goriška Brda]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Ribolla Gialla]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Merlot]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]], [[Orange wine]], [[Traditional method]]

## About
A family estate led by Aleš Kristančič, farming biodynamically in Brda. The wines range from skin contact whites and reds to the unfiltered, disgorgement-free sparkling Puro.
