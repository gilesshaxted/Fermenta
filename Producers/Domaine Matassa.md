# Domaine Matassa

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A natural leaning Roussillon domaine by an English winemaker, known for lean, mineral whites and reds.

## About
A small domaine making wines with little intervention from old vines on schist and limestone, in a restrained and savoury style.
