# Pedro Ximénez

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Sun-dried grapes make a dark, syrupy sweet wine.

## Where it's from
Andalusia, possibly of Canary or Rhineland origin according to old legends.

## Where it grows
- **Jerez, Spain:** for sweet Sherry
- **Montilla-Moriles, Spain:** also for dry wines

## What it tastes like
- Raisin, fig, toffee and coffee

## Classic wines
- Pedro Ximénez Sherry
- Montilla-Moriles

## Pairs with
- Ice cream
- Blue cheese
- Chocolate desserts

## Good to know
- Pour it over vanilla ice cream.
- Grapes are dried in the sun before pressing.
