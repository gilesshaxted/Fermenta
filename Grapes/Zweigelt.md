# Zweigelt

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Austria's most planted red, soft and fruity with fresh cherry notes.

## Where it's from
A cross of Blaufränkisch and St. Laurent, created in 1922 by Dr. Fritz Zweigelt.

## Where it grows
- **Burgenland and Lower Austria, Austria:** widely grown

## What it tastes like
- Sour cherry, plum and a peppery spice

## Classic wines
- Carnuntum Zweigelt
- Zweigelt Reserve

## Pairs with
- Schnitzel
- Roast chicken
- Pasta

## Good to know
- Easy-drinking and food-friendly.
- Serve slightly cool.
