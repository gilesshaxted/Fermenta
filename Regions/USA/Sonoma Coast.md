# Sonoma Coast

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Wild ridge and coastal vineyards producing some of California's most distinctive cool-climate Pinot Noir.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[Sonoma County]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** sandstone, clay, loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
Steep ridges close to the Pacific, often above the fog line.

## Soil and geology
Sandy, rocky and thin soils, with marine sediments.

## Climate
Cold and windy, with strong Pacific influence.

## History
A recent frontier for wine growing, with new plantings from the 1990s onwards.

## Production and wine styles
Pinot Noir and Chardonnay with high natural acidity.

## Key wines
- Sonoma Coast Pinot Noir
- Sonoma Coast Chardonnay

## What to look for
- Tension and freshness, red fruit and spice.
- Often small production.
