# San Gimignano

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A medieval skyline of towers above the vines of Vernaccia, Tuscany's best-known white.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Tuscany]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** sand, clay, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Vernaccia]]
> - **Styles:** [[Vin Santo]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills around the medieval hill town of San Gimignano, famous for its towers.

## Soil and geology
Sandy, marine-fossil rich soils over clay.

## Climate
Mediterranean, with a drier feel than Chianti.

## History
Vernaccia di San Gimignano was the first Italian wine to gain DOC status, in 1966, and DOCG in 1993.

## Production and wine styles
Vernaccia di San Gimignano, a dry white with nutty, citrus character.

## Key wines
- Vernaccia di San Gimignano
- Vernaccia Riserva

## What to look for
- Lemon, almond and a slight bitterness.
- Good with seafood and light pasta.
