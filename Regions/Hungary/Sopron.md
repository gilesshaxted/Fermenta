# Sopron

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cool western region near the Austrian border, known for Kékfrankos.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Hungary]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** slate, clay, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Blaufränkisch]], [[Zweigelt]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills on the western side of Lake Neusiedl, linked in character to Austria's Burgenland.

## Soil and geology
Slate, clay and sand.

## Climate
Moderate continental, with the lake keeping humidity and warmth.

## History
Sopron has a long tradition of red wine and shares history with Burgenland across the border.

## Production and wine styles
Red wine, led by Kékfrankos (Blaufränkisch), with Zweigelt and Cabernet Sauvignon.

## Key wines
- Soproni Kékfrankos

## What to look for
- Peppery, medium bodied reds.
- Compare with Burgenland.
