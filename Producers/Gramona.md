# Gramona

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family house focused on long aged Cava and local grapes, especially Xarel·lo.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Region:** [[Cava]], [[Penedès]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer

## About
Gramona is known for long lees ageing and organic farming, with some of the region's most complex sparkling wines. The best cuvées can age well.
