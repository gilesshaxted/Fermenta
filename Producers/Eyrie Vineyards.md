# Eyrie Vineyards

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The pioneering winery that planted Pinot Noir and Pinot Gris in the Willamette Valley.

## About
Eyrie helped put Oregon Pinot on the map. The wines are restrained, delicate and age well.
