# Argaman

> [!abstract] At a glance
> An Israeli red crossing, bred from Carignan and Souzao, with deep colour and juicy fruit.

## Where it's from
Bred in Israel in the 1970s from Souzao and Carignan. The name means purple in Hebrew, for its colour.

## Where it grows
- **Shimshon, Israel:** a popular area for it
- **Galilee, Israel:** also planted

## What it tastes like
- Blackberry, plum, spice and violet

## Classic wines
- Argaman varietal
- Argaman blends

## Pairs with
- Grilled meat
- Stews
- Hard cheese

## Good to know
- Very deeply coloured.
- Often used to add colour to blends.
