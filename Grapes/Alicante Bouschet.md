# Alicante Bouschet

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A rare red-fleshed grape, giving very dark, tannic wines, particularly in Alentejo.

## Where it's from
Created in France in the 19th century by Henri Bouschet as a cross of Petit Bouschet and Grenache. It is one of few grapes with red flesh.

## Where it grows
- **Alentejo, Portugal:** one of its best homes
- **Languedoc and California:** also grown

## What it tastes like
- Blackberry, plum, spice and earth

## Classic wines
- Alentejo red

## Pairs with
- Grilled meat
- Stews
- Hard cheese

## Good to know
- The juice is red, unlike most grapes.
- Gives deep colour to blends.
