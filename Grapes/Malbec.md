# Malbec

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Inky, plush and juicy, with dark fruit and a hint of violets. Argentina's flagship red.

## Where it's from
South-west France, where it is called Côt or Auxerrois in Cahors. It found its great success in Argentina.

## Where it grows
- **Mendoza, Argentina:** the flagship, ripe and generous
- **Uco Valley, Argentina:** high altitude, fresher and more structured
- **Salta, Argentina:** some of the highest vineyards in the world
- **Cahors, France:** darker and more rustic, the original home

## What it tastes like
- **Argentina:** blackberry, plum, violet, cocoa, soft spice
- **Cahors:** darker, firmer, more earthy and tannic

## Classic wines
- Mendoza Malbec
- Uco Valley Malbec
- Cahors

## Pairs with
- Grilled steak
- Barbecue and asado
- Burgers
- Spiced sausages
- Hard cheese

## Good to know
- Argentina is now the leading Malbec producer, although the grape began in France.
- Argentine Malbec is plusher and rounder, Cahors firmer and more rustic.
