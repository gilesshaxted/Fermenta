# Barbera

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Juicy, high in acidity and low in tannin. Piedmont's everyday red.

## Where it's from
Piedmont, in the hills around Asti and Alba, where it is the most planted red grape.

## Where it grows
- **Asti and Alba, Italy:** the heartland, juicy and fresh
- **California, USA:** warm-climate versions
- **Argentina:** small plantings

## What it tastes like
- **Unoaked:** tart cherry, plum, fresh and zippy
- **With oak:** darker fruit, vanilla and extra weight

## Classic wines
- Barbera d'Asti
- Barbera d'Alba
- Nizza

## Pairs with
- Pizza and tomato sauces
- Cured meats
- Pasta with ragù

## Good to know
- High acidity makes it a great food wine.
- Nizza is the top-tier Barbera subzone.
