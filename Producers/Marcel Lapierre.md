# Marcel Lapierre

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The Morgon domaine of the late Marcel Lapierre, the figure most associated with the revival of natural Beaujolais.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Beaujolais Crus]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Gamay]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]], [[Carbonic maceration]]

## About
A family estate in Morgon whose wines helped to define natural Gamay. They are fresh, juicy and drinkable, and the domaine is widely copied.

## Known for
- Morgon
- Morgon Cuvée Camille
- Raisins Gaulois
