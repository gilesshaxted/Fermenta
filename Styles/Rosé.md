# Rosé

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Pink wine made by limited skin contact with black grapes.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Category:** Still
> - **Colour:** Rosé
> - **Sweetness:** Dry
> - **Country:** Various
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grenache]], [[Cinsault]], [[Mourvèdre]], [[Syrah]]
> - **Regions:** [[Provence]], [[Côtes de Provence]], [[Tavel]], [[Cassis]], [[Bandol]], [[Navarra]], [[Languedoc]]

## How it's made
Black grapes are pressed soon after crushing, or the juice is bled off a red ferment (saignée). A few hours of skin contact gives the colour, so lighter shades mean less contact.

## Where it's made
Provence is the benchmark for pale, dry rosé. Tavel in the Rhône makes a deeper, more structured style, and Navarra and Rioja make rosado.

## What it tastes like
- Strawberry, watermelon, citrus
- Dry, crisp and fresh
- Deeper versions: cherry and spice

## Pairs with
- Salade niçoise
- Grilled fish
- Mediterranean food
- Picnics

## Good to know
- Most rosé is best drunk within a year or two.
- Bandol rosé can age.
