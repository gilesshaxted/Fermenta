# Fernão Pires

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Portugal's most planted white grape, aromatic and floral, also called Maria Gomes in Bairrada.

## Where it's from
Widely grown in the Lisbon, Tejo and Bairrada regions. In Bairrada it is known as Maria Gomes.

## Where it grows
- **Lisbon and Tejo, Portugal:** the main areas
- **Bairrada, Portugal:** as Maria Gomes

## What it tastes like
- Orange blossom, peach, citrus and spice

## Classic wines
- Lisbon white
- Bairrada Espumante

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Salads
- Light starters

## Good to know
- Has a lot of aroma.
- Also used for sparkling wine.
