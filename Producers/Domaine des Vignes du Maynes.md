# Domaine des Vignes du Maynes

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Cruzille biodynamic domaine leading the natural wine scene in the Mâconnais.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Mâconnais]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Gamay]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]]

## About
The estate makes low sulphur wines from old vines in granite and limestone soils. The whites are full and textured, the reds fresh and lively.

- Mâcon Cruzille Rouge
