# Viognier

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Perfumed, rich and full, with peach and apricot. Low acidity and a big floral nose.

## Where it's from
The Rhône Valley. Condrieu is its home. It nearly vanished in the 1960s before being revived.

## Where it grows
- **Condrieu, northern Rhône:** the benchmark, rich and floral
- **Languedoc, France:** widely planted, fruity and good value
- **Barossa Valley, Australia:** ripe and generous
- **Virginia, USA:** a signature white

## What it tastes like
- **Fruit:** peach, apricot, ripe pear
- **Floral:** orange blossom, honeysuckle
- **Spice:** ginger, nutmeg, a touch of musk

## Classic wines
- Condrieu
- Languedoc Viognier
- Côte-Rôtie (blended in)

## Pairs with
- Mild curries and Thai food
- Roast chicken
- Pork
- Scallops

## Good to know
- Low acidity means it is usually best young.
- A little is co-fermented with Syrah in Côte-Rôtie for perfume.
