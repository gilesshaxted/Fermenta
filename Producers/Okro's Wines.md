# Okro's Wines

> [!abstract] At a glance
> An Imereti winery known for natural wines from the local white varieties.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Georgia]]
> - **Region:** [[Imereti]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tsolikouri]], [[Tsitska]], [[Krakhuna]]
> - **Styles:** [[Qvevri wine]], [[Natural wine]], [[Orange wine]]

## About
Working in the Imeretian tradition, with shorter skin contact than in Kakheti. The wines tend to be lighter, fresh and more delicate than the amber wines of the east.

## Known for
- Tsolikouri
- Tsitska
- Krakhuna
