# Domaine Chassorney

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A biodynamic Saint-Romain estate, run by Frédéric Cossard, making unfussy natural-leaning Burgundy.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Côte de Beaune]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]]

## About
Cossard farms biodynamically and keeps additions to a minimum, including sulphur. The wines are fresh, savoury and expressive.
