# Lustau

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A leading Jerez house, known for its wide range and its Almacenista selection from small cellars.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Region:** [[Jerez]]
> - **Type:** Major producer

## About
Lustau helped bring attention to individual, small-cellar sherries and offers a wide range across styles. The wines are typically precise and well made.

## Known for
- Emilín Moscatel
- Península Palo Cortado
- Los Arcos Amontillado
- East India Solera
