# Shomron

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A region in the north-central area of the country, covering the Carmel hills and coastal plain, also known as Samaria.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Israel]]
> - **Part of:** [[Asia]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, terra rossa, alluvial, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Syrah]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills, slopes and coastal plain around Mount Carmel, running up to the Samaria hills.

## Soil and geology
Terra rossa, limestone, alluvial soils and sand.

## Climate
Mediterranean, with sea breezes cooling the coast and warmer conditions inland.

## History
The Zichron Yaakov area was planted with vines by Baron Edmond de Rothschild in the 1880s, which led to the founding of the Carmel winery. This is the origin of much of modern Israeli wine.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Chardonnay, with Syrah and Sauvignon Blanc and a wide range of styles.

## Key wines
- Carmel Winery wines
- Shomron red blends

## What to look for
- The historic home of Israeli wine.
- Styles range from easy to serious.
