# Touriga Franca

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The most planted grape of the Douro, with floral and structured wines.

## Where it's from
A Douro native thought to be a cross of Touriga Nacional and Mourisco Tinto. It is easier to grow than Touriga Nacional.

## Where it grows
- **Douro, Portugal:** for Port and dry reds

## What it tastes like
- Blackberry, violet, spice and floral

## Classic wines
- Vintage Port
- Douro red

## Pairs with
- Roast lamb
- Grilled meat

## Good to know
- Often partnered with Touriga Nacional.
- Spicy and floral.
