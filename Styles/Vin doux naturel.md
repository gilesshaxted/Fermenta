# Vin doux naturel

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Sweet French fortified wine where spirit is added during fermentation.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Category:** Fortified
> - **Colour:** Varies
> - **Sweetness:** Sweet
> - **Country:** France
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grenache]], [[Muscat]], [[Carignan]]
> - **Regions:** [[Roussillon]], [[Collioure and Banyuls]], [[Rhône Valley]], [[Languedoc]]

## How it's made
Grape spirit stops fermentation early, keeping natural grape sugar (doux naturel means naturally sweet). Styles range from fresh Muscat to long aged, oxidised Grenache.

## Where it's made
Mainly Roussillon and the southern Rhône, with Banyuls and Maury famous for red styles.

## What it tastes like
- Muscat: orange blossom, honey
- Grenache: dried cherry, fig, chocolate
- Aged styles: walnut, coffee

## Pairs with
- Dark chocolate
- Blue cheese
- Fruit desserts

## Good to know
- Banyuls is a classic match for chocolate.
- Rancio is the name for the oxidised, aged style.
