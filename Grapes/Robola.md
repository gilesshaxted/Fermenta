# Robola

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A crisp, mineral white from the limestone slopes of Cephalonia.

## Where it's from
Native to Cephalonia, where it grows at altitude on limestone soils.

## Where it grows
- **Cephalonia, Greece:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Lemon, lime, herbs and mineral

## Classic wines
- Robola of Cephalonia

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Salads
- Feta

## Good to know
- High acid and good minerality.
- Often compared with Riesling.
