# Tarija

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Bolivia's main wine region, in the south, with some of the highest vineyards in the world.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Bolivia]]
> - **Part of:** [[South America]]
> - **Climate:** Semi-arid
> - **Soils:** sandy loam, alluvial, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tannat]], [[Malbec]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Muscat of Alexandria]], [[Syrah]]

## Landscape and topography
Vineyards in valleys at roughly 1,600 to 2,800 metres above sea level, near the Argentine border.

## Soil and geology
Sandy loam and alluvial soils.

## Climate
Dry and sunny at high altitude, with intense light and cool nights.

## History
Vines arrived in the colonial era. A modern wine industry developed from the late twentieth century, with a focus on altitude wines.

## Production and wine styles
Ripe but fresh reds and aromatic whites, plus Singani, a grape spirit which is not wine.

## Key wines
- Tarija reds
- Tarija Tannat
- Tarija Muscat of Alexandria

## What to look for
- Altitude gives intense colour and aromas.
- Singani is a spirit from Muscat of Alexandria.

## Producers
- [[Aranjuez]]
- [[Campos de Solana]]
- [[Kohlberg]]
