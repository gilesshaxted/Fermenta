# Georgia

Part of [[Asia]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of the oldest wine cultures in the world, famed for qvevri winemaking, amber wines and hundreds of native grapes.

## How wine is organised here
- Wines are labelled by appellation, such as Tsinandali, Mukuzani and Khvanchkara, with Kakheti the biggest region.
- Traditional qvevri wines are made in buried clay vessels, and the method is recognised by UNESCO.
- Many wines are labelled by grape, such as Rkatsiteli or Saperavi.
