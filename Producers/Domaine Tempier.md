# Domaine Tempier

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The most famous estate in Bandol, known for ageworthy Mourvèdre reds and a serious rosé.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Bandol]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Mourvèdre]], [[Grenache]], [[Cinsault]]
> - **Styles:** [[Rosé]]

## About
A family domaine on terraced hillsides above the Mediterranean. Its single plot reds are dark, spicy and long-lived, and the rosé is one of the finest in France.

## Known for
- Bandol Cuvée Cabassaou
- Bandol La Migoua
- Bandol La Tourtine
- Bandol Rosé
