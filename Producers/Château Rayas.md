# Château Rayas

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A legendary Châteauneuf-du-Pape estate, famous for pale, perfumed and unusually elegant Grenache.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Châteauneuf-du-Pape]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grenache]]

## About
A small family-run estate on sandy soils in a wooded corner of the appellation, making wines that are far more delicate than typical Châteauneuf. The reds age remarkably well and are rare and sought after.

## Known for
- Châteauneuf-du-Pape Rouge
- Pignan
- Fonsalette
