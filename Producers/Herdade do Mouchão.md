# Herdade do Mouchão

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A traditional Alentejo estate famous for Alicante Bouschet, aged in large old oak.

> [!info] Quick facts

## About
Mouchão is known for deeply coloured, age worthy red wines made in a traditional way. Its Tonel 3-4 is a long-lived benchmark.
