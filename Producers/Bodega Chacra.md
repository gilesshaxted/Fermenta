# Bodega Chacra

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Patagonian Pinot Noir specialist working biodynamically with very old vines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Argentina]]
> - **Region:** [[Patagonia]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]]

## About
Based in the Río Negro valley, farming old Pinot Noir plantings with a hands off cellar approach. The wines are light, savoury and quietly intense, often with very little added.

## Known for
- Treinta y Dos
- Cincuenta y Cinco
- Barda
- Sin Azufre
