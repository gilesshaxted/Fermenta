# Corbières

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Rugged hill country behind Narbonne, producing rustic red blends and rising quality sub-zones.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Languedoc]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, schist, sandstone, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Carignan]], [[Grenache]], [[Syrah]], [[Mourvèdre]]

## Landscape and topography
Wild, dry hills and valleys with ancient Cathar castles, at the northern foot of the Pyrenees.

## Soil and geology
A complex mix of limestone, schist, sandstone and clay.

## Climate
Hot and dry Mediterranean, strongly influenced by the Tramontane wind.

## History
One of the oldest and largest appellations in the Languedoc, now with a quality-focused Boutenac cru.

## Production and wine styles
Mainly red from Carignan, Grenache and Syrah, with some white and rosé.

## Key wines
- Corbières rouge
- Corbières-Boutenac
- Corbières blanc

## What to look for
- Black fruit, herbs and garrigue, rustic and earthy.
- Excellent with cassoulet.

- [[Domaine de Fontsainte]]
- [[Gérard Bertrand]]
