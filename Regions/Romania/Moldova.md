# Moldova

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Romania's largest wine region, with Cotnari's famous sweet wine and a mixed bag of traditional varieties.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Romania]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, loess, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Fetească Albă]], [[Fetească Regală]], [[Muscat|Tămâioasă Românească]], [[Grasă de Cotnari]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling hills and plains in the east, between the Carpathians and the Prut river.

## Soil and geology
Clay, limestone, loess and sandy soils.

## Climate
Continental, with cold winters, hot summers and drought risk.

## History
The Cotnari vineyards produced sweet wines praised by medieval princes, and were famous throughout Europe. Cotnari lies near the border with the Republic of Moldova.

## Production and wine styles
Fetească Albă, Fetească Regală, Grasă de Cotnari and Tămâioasă for whites, with sweet noble-rot wines from Cotnari.

## Key wines
- Cotnari Grasă
- Cotnari Tămâioasă Românească
- Odobești

## What to look for
- Honeyed, fruity whites.
- Sweet wines are a speciality.
