# Domaine Jean-Louis Chave

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The great family name of Hermitage, making profound, long-lived reds and whites from the hill of Hermitage.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Hermitage]], [[Saint-Joseph]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Marsanne]], [[Roussanne]]

## About
A family estate that has farmed on the hill for many generations, blending plots from different parts of the hill into a single Hermitage. The red is deep yet fine, and the white is one of the longest-lived in France.

## Known for
- Hermitage Rouge
- Hermitage Blanc
- Saint-Joseph Offerus
