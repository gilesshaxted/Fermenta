# Heathcote

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small area on ancient red soil, making concentrated, plush Shiraz.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Australia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Oceania]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** Cambrian red soil, greenstone, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Grenache]]

## Landscape and topography
A ridge line with vineyards at 160 to 320 metres.

## Soil and geology
Ancient red Cambrian soils, known as Cambrian greenstone, which store water.

## Climate
Warm and dry with cool nights.

## History
Vineyards were first planted in the 1860s, and modern development began in the 1970s.

## Production and wine styles
Shiraz primarily, with Cabernet Sauvignon and Grenache.

## Key wines
- Heathcote Shiraz

## What to look for
- Dark fruit with mineral edge.
- Full-bodied but balanced.
