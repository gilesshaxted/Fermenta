# Mâconnais

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Rounded, fruity Chardonnay from limestone hills in the south of Burgundy, usually excellent value.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Burgundy]]
> - **Climate:** Semi-continental
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, granite
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Gamay]]

## Landscape and topography
Gently rolling limestone hills and dramatic rocky outcrops such as Solutré and Vergisson.

## Soil and geology
Limestone and clay, with some marl and granite, giving more variety than the label suggests.

## Climate
Warmer and sunnier than the Côte d'Or, so riper, fuller wines.

## History
Pouilly-Fuissé earned appellation status in 1936. The area has a long, quiet tradition of Chardonnay.

## Production and wine styles
Chardonnay dominates, with a little Gamay and Pinot Noir for reds. Wines range from unoaked to lightly oaked.

## Key wines
- Pouilly-Fuissé
- Saint-Véran
- Viré-Clessé
- Mâcon-Villages

## What to look for
- Peach, white blossom and a plump texture.
- Pouilly-Fuissé can be seriously good, and is often under-priced compared with the Côte d'Or.
