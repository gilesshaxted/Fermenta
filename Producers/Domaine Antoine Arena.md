# Domaine Antoine Arena

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A leading Patrimonio grower, working organically and making some of the island's most admired wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Corsica]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Vermentino]], [[Sciaccarello]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]]

## About
A small family domaine in the Patrimonio area, farming old vines on limestone and clay. The white is textured and saline, and the reds are fresh and spicy.

## Known for
- Carco
- Grotte di Sole
- Morta Maio
