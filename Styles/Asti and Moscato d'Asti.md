# Asti and Moscato d'Asti

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Sweet, low alcohol, grapey Moscato from Piedmont, lightly or fully sparkling.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Category:** Sparkling
> - **Colour:** White
> - **Sweetness:** Sweet
> - **Country:** Italy
> - **Main grapes:** [[Muscat]]
> - **Regions:** [[Asti]], [[Piedmont]]

## How it's made
Fermentation is stopped early by chilling and filtering, leaving sweetness and low alcohol. Moscato d'Asti is lightly fizzy (frizzante), while Asti is fully sparkling.

## Where it's made
The hills around Asti and Alba in Piedmont.

## What it tastes like
- Peach, grape, orange blossom
- Sweet and light
- Low alcohol, around 5 to 7%

## Pairs with
- Fruit tart
- Fresh fruit
- Light desserts
- Breakfast pastries

## Good to know
- Moscato d'Asti is usually the finer wine.
- The grape is Muscat Blanc à Petits Grains, called Moscato in Italy.
