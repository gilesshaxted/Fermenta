# Domaine Arretxea

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small biodynamic Irouléguy domaine producing distinctive wines of the Basque hills.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Irouléguy]], [[South-West France]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tannat]], [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Petit Courbu]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]]

## About
Arretxea is a family estate farmed biodynamically on very steep slopes. The wines are expressive and taut, reflecting both the site and the minimal approach in the cellar.
