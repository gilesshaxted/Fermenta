# North Macedonia

Part of [[Europe]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A sunny, landlocked country of inland vineyards, best known for the dark red Vranec from the Tikveš area.

## How wine is organised here
- Most of the vineyards are in the Povardarie region, in the Vardar river valley.
- Labels usually show the grape variety, with Vranec (Vranac) the most famous.
