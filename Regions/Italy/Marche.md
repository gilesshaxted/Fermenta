# Marche

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Adriatic hills with Verdicchio, a surprisingly ageworthy white, and the Montepulciano grape on Conero.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Temperate
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Verdicchio]], [[Montepulciano]], [[Sangiovese]], [[Pecorino]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills running from the Apennines to the Adriatic, with the Conero peninsula on the coast.

## Soil and geology
Clay and limestone, with sandy soils near the sea.

## Climate
Temperate with Adriatic breezes.

## History
Verdicchio was once sold in amphora-shaped bottles that made it famous in the 1960s and 1970s. Quality is now excellent.

## Production and wine styles
Verdicchio dei Castelli di Jesi and di Matelica, Rosso Conero (Montepulciano) and Rosso Piceno.

## Key wines
- Verdicchio dei Castelli di Jesi
- Verdicchio di Matelica
- Rosso Conero
- Rosso Piceno

## What to look for
- Lemon, almond and a saline, bitter finish.
- Verdicchio can age for a decade or more.
