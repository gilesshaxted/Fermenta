# Catena Zapata

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family winery that did more than any other to show the world what high altitude Mendoza Malbec could be.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Argentina]]
> - **Region:** [[Mendoza]], [[Uco Valley]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Malbec]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## About
Based in Mendoza, with vineyards at some of the highest sites in the Uco Valley. The family has long studied altitude and soil differences, and the wines range from approachable bottles to serious single vineyard releases.

## Known for
- Adrianna Vineyard
- Nicasia Vineyard
- Catena Alta
- Malbec Argentino
