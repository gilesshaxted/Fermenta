# Tavkveri

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A light, fresh Georgian red grape, grown mainly in Kartli.

## Where it's from
A traditional Kartli red, used for rosé, light reds and blends.

## Where it grows
- **Kartli, Georgia:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Red cherry, strawberry, spice and herbs

## Classic wines
- Tavkveri rosé
- Kartli red

## Pairs with
- Grilled vegetables
- Poultry
- Soft cheese

## Good to know
- Used for rosé and light reds.
- Not as tannic as Saperavi.
