# Fetească Albă

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A light, fresh Romanian white, one of the country's oldest native grapes.

## Where it's from
Called the white maiden, a parent of Fetească Regală, and widely grown in Moldova.

## Where it grows
- **Moldova, Romania:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Apple, pear, citrus and white flowers

## Classic wines
- Fetească Albă

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Poultry
- Salads

## Good to know
- Parent of Fetească Regală.
- Best young.
