# Germany

Part of [[Europe]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Thirteen regions, mostly in the cool west and southwest, known for Riesling, steep river slopes and a classification based on ripeness.

## How German wine is organised
- **Prädikatswein** is the quality tier, graded by must weight: Kabinett, Spätlese, Auslese, Beerenauslese, Trockenbeerenauslese and Eiswein.
- **VDP** growers use a vineyard classification: Gutswein, Ortswein, Erste Lage and Grosse Lage.
- **Trocken** means dry, **Halbtrocken** off-dry. Grosses Gewächs (GG) is the top dry wine.
- Labels follow the pattern village plus vineyard plus grape.
