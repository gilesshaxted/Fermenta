# Casa Madero

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Parras winery, widely regarded as the oldest in the Americas.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Mexico]]
> - **Region:** [[Valle de Parras]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Syrah]], [[Merlot]]

## About
Casa Madero is based in the high, dry Valle de Parras in Coahuila. It makes a broad range of wines, with the reds the best known.

## Known for
- Casa Madero 3V
- Casa Madero V Cabernet Sauvignon
