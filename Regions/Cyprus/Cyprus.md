# Cyprus

Part of [[Europe]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> An island with one of the oldest winemaking traditions in the world, now known for fresh Xynisteri whites, the rare Maratheftiko and sweet Commandaria.

## How wine is organised here
- Cypriot wine follows EU rules, with PDO wines such as Commandaria, and PGI wines.
- Vineyards are mostly in the hills of the Troodos mountains and the districts around them.
- Native grapes, mainly Xynisteri, Mavro and Maratheftiko, are central to the island's identity.
