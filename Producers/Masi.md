# Masi

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A big name in Amarone, long associated with the appassimento tradition.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Valpolicella]], [[Veneto]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Corvina]], [[Rondinella]], [[Molinara]]
> - **Styles:** [[Amarone]]

## About
A family led house with deep roots in Valpolicella, Masi is known for developing the ripasso style of Campofiorin as well as many Amarone bottlings. The range covers a lot of ground, from approachable to serious.

## Known for
- Costasera Amarone
- Campofiorin
- Mazzano Amarone
- Serego Alighieri Vaio Armaron
