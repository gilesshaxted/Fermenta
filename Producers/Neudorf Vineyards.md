# Neudorf Vineyards

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family estate in Nelson widely known for the Moutere Chardonnay.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[New Zealand]]
> - **Region:** [[Nelson]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]]

## About
A small, quality-focused winery in the Upper Moutere hills, with Chardonnay at the centre. The wines are rich but fresh, with great length.

## Known for
- Moutere Chardonnay
- Moutere Pinot Noir
- Rosie's Block Chardonnay
