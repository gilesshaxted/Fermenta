# Sauvignon Blanc

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Sharp, zesty and aromatic, with gooseberry, passion fruit and cut grass. Crisp and refreshing.

## Where it's from
France, in the Loire or Bordeaux. The name probably comes from sauvage, meaning wild.

## Where it grows
- **Sancerre and Pouilly-Fumé, Loire:** flinty, mineral and elegant
- **Marlborough, New Zealand:** vivid passion fruit and gooseberry
- **Adelaide Hills, Australia:** cool-climate, crisp and zesty
- **Bordeaux, France:** blended with Sémillon in white Bordeaux and Sauternes
- **Casablanca Valley, Chile:** fresh and citrusy

## What it tastes like
- **Cool climate:** gooseberry, grapefruit, lime, cut grass, elderflower
- **Warmer sites:** passion fruit, peach, tropical fruit
- **Loire:** flint, wet stone, smoke

## Classic wines
- Sancerre
- Pouilly-Fumé
- Marlborough Sauvignon Blanc
- White Bordeaux

## Pairs with
- Goat's cheese
- Asparagus
- Salads
- Shellfish
- Thai and Vietnamese herbs

## Good to know
- Sancerre and Pouilly-Fumé are both Sauvignon Blanc.
- Fumé Blanc is a name for oaked Sauvignon Blanc, made popular in California by Robert Mondavi.
