# Colchagua Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A warm valley producing some of Chile's most powerful, rich reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Chile]]
> - **Part of:** [[Rapel Valley]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** alluvial, clay, granite
> - **Main grapes:** [[Carmenère]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Syrah]], [[Malbec]]

## Landscape and topography
A broad east-west valley with the Tinguiririca River, surrounded by hills.

## Soil and geology
Alluvial soils with clay and granite.

## Climate
Warm, sunny and dry, cooled by Pacific air in the western end (Apalta, Lolol and Marchigüe).

## History
Apalta, a horseshoe-shaped amphitheatre, is known for old vines and premium wine.

## Production and wine styles
Carmenère, Cabernet Sauvignon, Syrah and Malbec.

## Key wines
- Colchagua Carmenère
- Apalta blends

## What to look for
- Ripe, dense reds.
- Lolol and Paredones are cooler.
