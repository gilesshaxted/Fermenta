# Trajadura

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A light, fresh grape used in Vinho Verde blends, known as Treixadura in Spain.

## Where it's from
Native to northwest Iberia, and known as Treixadura in Galicia, where it is key in Ribeiro.

## Where it grows
- **Vinho Verde, Portugal:** in blends
- **Ribeiro, Spain:** as Treixadura

## What it tastes like
- Lemon, peach, herbs and white flowers

## Classic wines
- Vinho Verde
- Ribeiro

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Salads

## Good to know
- Soft and fragrant.
- Blends well with Loureiro.
