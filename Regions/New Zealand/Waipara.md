# Waipara

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The main wine district of Canterbury, sheltered in a basin of limestone hills.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[New Zealand]]
> - **Part of:** [[Canterbury]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, gravel
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Riesling]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
A basin of hills with the Waipara River.

## Soil and geology
Limestone clay and gravel terraces.

## Climate
Dry and sheltered, but with a spring frost risk.

## History
Became the region's centre in the 1980s.

## Production and wine styles
Pinot Noir, Riesling and Chardonnay.

## Key wines
- Waipara Riesling

## What to look for
- Mineral, citrus Riesling.
- Quality Pinot.

- [[Pegasus Bay]]
- [[Pyramid Valley]]
