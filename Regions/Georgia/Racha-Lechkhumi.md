# Racha-Lechkhumi

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small mountain region in northwest Georgia, best known for naturally semi-sweet red wine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Georgia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Asia]]
> - **Climate:** Humid continental
> - **Soils:** slate, limestone, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Aladasturi]], [[Usakhelauri]], [[Tsolikouri]], [[Mtsvane]]

## Landscape and topography
High river valleys in the foothills of the Greater Caucasus, with steep vineyards.

## Soil and geology
Slate and limestone soils, with clay.

## Climate
Cooler and more humid than the east, with a short growing season.

## History
Racha is known for a traditional style of semi-sweet red wine, made by stopping fermentation naturally in the cold of the mountains.

## Production and wine styles
Semi-sweet reds from Aladasturi and Usakhelauri, plus some white wines.

## Key wines
- Khvanchkara
- Usakhelauri
- Tvishi

## What to look for
- Khvanchkara is a famous semi-sweet red.
- Look for Usakhelauri.
