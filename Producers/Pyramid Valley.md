# Pyramid Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A pioneering biodynamic estate in North Canterbury, making low intervention Chardonnay and Pinot Noir.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[New Zealand]]
> - **Region:** [[Waipara]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]]

## About
A tiny, limestone-based vineyard farmed biodynamically, with minimal use of sulphur. The wines are detailed, savoury and individual.

## Known for
- Field of Fire Chardonnay
- Earth Smoke Pinot Noir
- Angel Flower Pinot Noir
