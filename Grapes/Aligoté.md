# Aligoté

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Burgundy's other white grape. Tart, fresh and best young.

## Where it's from
Burgundy, where it is a sibling of Chardonnay (both are offspring of Pinot and Gouais Blanc).

## Where it grows
- **Bouzeron, Burgundy:** the only village appellation for Aligoté
- **Romania:** widely planted

## What it tastes like
- Green apple, lemon and a mineral line
- Light and zippy

## Classic wines
- Bourgogne Aligoté
- Bouzeron
- Kir (with cassis)

## Pairs with
- Oysters
- Gougères
- Fresh goat's cheese

## Good to know
- Traditionally the base of Kir (white wine with crème de cassis).
- Quality is rising with old-vine producers.
