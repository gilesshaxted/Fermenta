# France

Part of [[Europe]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The world's reference point for wine. Fifteen major regions, hundreds of appellations, and a system that names the place rather than the grape.

## How French wine is organised
- French labels usually name the **place**, not the grape. Learn the place and you know the grapes.
- The appellation system (AOC/AOP) controls grapes, yields and methods for each place. Tiers rise from regional to village to Premier Cru and Grand Cru in Burgundy, and by classification in Bordeaux.
- IGP (formerly Vin de Pays) allows more freedom, and Vin de France is the simplest tier.
