# Muscat Ottonel

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A light, fragrant Muscat that ripens early, grown in Central and Eastern Europe.

## Where it's from
Bred in the Loire in the 19th century by Moreau-Robert, as a cross of Chasselas and Muscat de Saumur. It does well in cool climates.

## Where it grows
- **Romania:** for dry and sweet wines
- **Alsace, France:** in small amounts

## What it tastes like
- Grape, orange blossom, elderflower and spice

## Classic wines
- Romanian Muscat Ottonel

## Pairs with
- Fruit
- Asparagus
- Spicy dishes

## Good to know
- Ripens early.
- Often sweet or off-dry.
