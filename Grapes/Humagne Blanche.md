# Humagne Blanche

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A rare Valais white, with a firm, dry, herbal character.

## Where it's from
An old Valais variety, and one of the region's rarest grapes. It is not related to Humagne Rouge.

## Where it grows
- **Valais, Switzerland:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Citrus, herbs, white flowers and mineral

## Classic wines
- Humagne Blanche

## Pairs with
- Fish
- Cheese fondue
- Salads

## Good to know
- Needs a warm site.
- Little leaves Switzerland.
