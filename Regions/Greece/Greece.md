# Greece

Part of [[Europe]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of the oldest wine cultures in the world, with island volcanic vineyards, mountain reds and more than 200 native grapes.

## How Greek wine is organised
- **PDO** (OPAP and OPE) is the top tier, and **PGI** (Topikos Oinos) is below.
- Reserve and Grand Reserve indicate ageing in oak and bottle.
- Retsina is a traditional wine flavoured with pine resin.
- Most grapes are native, and many are only found in Greece.
