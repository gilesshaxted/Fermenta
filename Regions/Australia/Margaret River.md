# Margaret River

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A coastal region in the far south-west, famous for elegant Cabernet blends and Chardonnay.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Australia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Oceania]]
> - **Climate:** Maritime mediterranean
> - **Soils:** gravel, loam, sand, ironstone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Sémillon]], [[Merlot]]
> - **Styles:** [[Bordeaux blend]]

## Landscape and topography
A narrow strip between Cape Naturaliste and Cape Leeuwin, with the Indian Ocean on the west.

## Soil and geology
Gravelly loams and sands over ancient granite and gneiss.

## Climate
Maritime, mild and relatively stable year to year.

## History
Dr Tom Cullity and Dr John Gladstones led the modern planting in the 1960s.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon, Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc and Sémillon blends.

## Key wines
- Margaret River Cabernet Sauvignon
- Margaret River Chardonnay
- SSB white blends

## What to look for
- Fine, structured Cabernet.
- Rich and refined Chardonnay.

- [[Leeuwin Estate]]
