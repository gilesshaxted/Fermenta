# Domaine Comte Georges de Vogüé

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The largest owner of the grand cru Musigny, in Chambolle-Musigny.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Côte de Nuits]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]]

## About
A historic estate that makes graceful red Burgundy from its Musigny and Bonnes-Mares holdings. It also makes a rare white Musigny.
