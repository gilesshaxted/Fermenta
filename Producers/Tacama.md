# Tacama

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A very old estate in the Ica valley, considered the longest-running quality winery in South America.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Peru]]
> - **Region:** [[Ica]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Malbec]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]]

## About
Tacama is a historic winery in a hot, dry desert valley, made workable by irrigation. The range runs from simple to premium.

## Known for
- Tacama Reserva Especial
- Demonio de los Andes
