# Roussanne

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Perfumed and elegant with good acidity. The finer partner for Marsanne.

## Where it's from
The Rhône Valley. The name comes from russet, the colour of ripe berries.

## Where it grows
- **Northern Rhône, France:** blended with Marsanne
- **Châteauneuf-du-Pape, France:** for the whites
- **Savoie, France:** known locally as Bergeron, in Chignin-Bergeron

## What it tastes like
- Pear, apricot, herbal tea and flowers
- A touch more acidity and perfume than Marsanne

## Classic wines
- Hermitage blanc
- Châteauneuf-du-Pape blanc
- Chignin-Bergeron

## Pairs with
- Roast poultry
- Shellfish
- Soft cheeses

## Good to know
- Late ripening and sensitive to disease, so small plantings.
- Ages beautifully.
