# Liguria

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A steep coastal strip on the Italian Riviera, with terraced vines making fresh Vermentino and Pigato.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** schist, sandstone, limestone, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Vermentino]], [[Vermentino|Pigato]], [[Rossese]]

## Landscape and topography
A narrow band between the Ligurian Sea and the Apennines, with terraced vineyards falling into the sea, including the Cinque Terre.

## Soil and geology
Schist, sandstone and clay on rocky terraces.

## Climate
Mediterranean, mild and breezy.

## History
Terraces in the Cinque Terre were built over centuries and are a UNESCO World Heritage site. Production is small because the terraces are hard to work.

## Production and wine styles
Mostly white wines: Vermentino, Pigato, Bosco, Albarola. Rossese di Dolceacqua is the local light red.

## Key wines
- Cinque Terre
- Sciacchetrà (sweet)
- Vermentino Riviera Ligure di Ponente
- Rossese di Dolceacqua

## What to look for
- Salty, herbal whites that suit seafood and pesto.
- Little is exported, so look for it locally.
