# Zorah

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A leading Armenian estate making wine from ancient Areni vineyards, partly in karas clay vessels.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Armenia]]
> - **Region:** [[Vayots Dzor]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Areni]]
> - **Styles:** [[Qvevri wine]]

## About
Based in the village of Rind in Vayots Dzor, run by an Italian trained winemaker. The wines treat Areni with real care and have won it international attention.
