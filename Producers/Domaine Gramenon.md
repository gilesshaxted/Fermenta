# Domaine Gramenon

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A pioneering organic and biodynamic domaine in the Drôme, making juicy, vibrant southern Rhône reds with very little intervention.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Rhône Valley]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grenache]], [[Syrah]], [[Cinsault]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]]

## About
A family estate in the Côtes du Rhône area that became a reference point for natural Grenache. The wines are fresh and lively, with pure fruit and low use of sulphur.

## Known for
- La Mémé
- Poignée de Raisins
- Ceps Centenaires
