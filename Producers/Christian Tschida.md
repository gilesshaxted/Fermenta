> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cult low intervention producer from Illmitz known for expressive blends and skin contact whites.

## About
A very small, hands-off estate by Lake Neusiedl. The wines are natural, often with skin contact, and are in strong demand among natural wine enthusiasts.
