# Clos Mogador

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A founding estate of modern Priorat, farmed organically from slate hillsides.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Region:** [[Priorat]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grenache]], [[Carignan]], [[Syrah]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## About
René Barbier was one of the group who revived Priorat's reputation, and his family continues the estate. The wines are structured and savoury, with real sense of place.
