# Pamid

> [!abstract] At a glance
> An old Bulgarian red that makes light, soft wines with low tannin.

## Where it's from
One of the oldest and most widely grown red varieties of Bulgaria, grown mostly in the Thracian Valley. The wines are light and meant for early drinking.

## Where it grows
- **Thracian Valley, Bulgaria:** the main area

## What it tastes like
- Red cherry, strawberry, herbs and spice

## Classic wines
- Pamid

## Pairs with
- Charcuterie
- Grilled chicken
- Pizza

## Good to know
- Light in colour and body.
- Best served slightly cool.
