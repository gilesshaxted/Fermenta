# Bolivia

Part of [[South America]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Bolivia grows vines at some of the highest altitudes in the world, mostly around Tarija.

## How wine is organised here
- Wine is labelled by producer and grape, with little formal appellation structure.
- Singani is a protected grape spirit and is distinct from wine.
