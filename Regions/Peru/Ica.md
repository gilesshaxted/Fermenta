# Ica

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Peru's main wine region, a desert coastal valley where vines have been grown since colonial times.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Peru]]
> - **Part of:** [[South America]]
> - **Climate:** Arid subtropical
> - **Soils:** sandy, alluvial, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tannat]], [[Malbec]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Muscat of Alexandria]]

## Landscape and topography
A coastal desert oasis south of Lima, with vineyards watered by rivers and irrigation.

## Soil and geology
Sandy and alluvial soils with some clay.

## Climate
Arid, very sunny and warm, with little rain and a cool coastal influence.

## History
Spanish colonists brought vines in the sixteenth century. Ica became the centre for both wine and pisco, and Tacama is one of the oldest wineries in the Americas.

## Production and wine styles
Wine is made alongside pisco, which is a spirit. Still reds, whites and some fortified wines are produced.

## Key wines
- Ica reds
- Tacama wines

## What to look for
- Pisco is a distilled spirit, not wine.
- Irrigation is essential.
