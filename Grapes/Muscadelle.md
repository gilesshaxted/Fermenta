# Muscadelle

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A perfumed grape used in Bordeaux sweet wines and in Australian Topaque.

## Where it's from
Native to south-west France and unrelated to Muscat. It is a small part of Sauternes blends, and in Rutherglen it makes the fortified Topaque.

## Where it grows
- **Rutherglen, Australia:** as Topaque
- **Bordeaux, France:** in small amounts

## What it tastes like
- Raisin, orange peel, honey and toffee

## Classic wines
- Rutherglen Topaque
- Sauternes

## Pairs with
- Dessert
- Nuts
- Sticky toffee pudding

## Good to know
- Not a Muscat.
- Called Tokay in Australia in the past.
