# Isole e Olena

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A much loved Chianti Classico estate known for fine, perfumed wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Chianti Classico]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sangiovese]], [[Syrah]]
> - **Styles:** [[Super Tuscan]], [[Vin Santo]]

## About
A small estate near Barberino Val d'Elsa with a reputation for purity and finesse rather than power. It makes classic Chianti Classico alongside an influential pure Sangiovese and an unusual Syrah.

## Known for
- Cepparello
- Chianti Classico
- Syrah Collezione de Marchi
- Vin Santo
