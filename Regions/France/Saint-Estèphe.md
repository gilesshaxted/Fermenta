# Saint-Estèphe

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The northernmost of the great communes, firmer and more tannic, with more Merlot and a distinctly earthy character.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Bordeaux]]
> - **Climate:** Maritime
> - **Soils:** gravel, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Franc]]

## Landscape and topography
Slightly higher, rolling ground along the Gironde, with views over the estuary.

## Soil and geology
Gravel mixed with more clay, which holds water better in dry years than the gravel further south.

## Climate
Maritime, slightly cooler and later ripening than the communes to the south.

## History
Includes Cos d'Estournel, Montrose and Calon-Ségur among its classified growths.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot in more equal measure than in Pauillac, with some Cabernet Franc. Red only.

## Key wines
- Château Cos d'Estournel
- Château Montrose
- Château Calon-Ségur

## What to look for
- Sturdy and tannic, so they often reward a few years in the cellar.
- Dark fruit, earth and spice.
