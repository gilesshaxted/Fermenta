# Katsunuma Jyozo

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Katsunuma producer behind the Aruga Branca range, known for Koshu with skin contact and oak.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Japan]]
> - **Region:** [[Katsunuma]], [[Yamanashi]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Koshu]], [[Muscat Bailey A]]

## About
Katsunuma Jyozo is a family-run winery working locally with Koshu and Muscat Bailey A. It has been a driving force for more serious, gastronomic Koshu.

## Known for
- Aruga Branca Gris de Koshu
- Aruga Branca Koshu
