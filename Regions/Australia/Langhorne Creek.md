# Langhorne Creek

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A flat, flood-irrigated area on the Bremer River, making soft, generous reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Australia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Oceania]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** alluvial, sandy loam, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Malbec]], [[Verdelho]]

## Landscape and topography
A flat plain near Lake Alexandrina.

## Soil and geology
Deep alluvial soils with sandy loam.

## Climate
Mediterranean, with cooling lake breezes.

## History
Planted since the 1850s, and many of the old vines contribute to famous blends.

## Production and wine styles
Shiraz, Cabernet Sauvignon and Malbec.

## Key wines
- Langhorne Creek Shiraz

## What to look for
- Soft, ripe and plummy.
- Often found in big Australian blends.
