# Vittoria

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Sicily's only DOCG, Cerasuolo di Vittoria, a light and elegant blend of Nero d'Avola and Frappato.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Sicily]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** sand, limestone, red clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Nero d'Avola]], [[Frappato]]

## Landscape and topography
Gentle hills in the south-east of the island, inland from the coast.

## Soil and geology
Red sandy soils over limestone.

## Climate
Hot Mediterranean, tempered by sea breezes.

## History
Granted DOCG status in 2005, a first for Sicily.

## Production and wine styles
A blend of Nero d'Avola (50 to 70%) and Frappato (30 to 50%).

## Key wines
- Cerasuolo di Vittoria

## What to look for
- Strawberry, cherry and floral lift.
- Light enough for fish dishes.
