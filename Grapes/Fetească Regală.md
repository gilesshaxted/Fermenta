# Fetească Regală

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A fragrant, light Romanian white, a cross of Fetească Albă and Grasă.

## Where it's from
Transylvania, created in the 1920s as a cross of two native grapes.

## Where it grows
- **Transylvania, Romania:** the main home

## What it tastes like
- Apple, citrus and a floral edge

## Classic wines
- Transylvania Fetească Regală

## Pairs with
- Salads
- Poultry
- Fish

## Good to know
- Romania's most planted white.
- Also grown in Hungary.
