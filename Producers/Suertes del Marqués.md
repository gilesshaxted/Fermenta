# Suertes del Marqués

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Tenerife estate making expressive, mineral wines from old vines in the Valle de la Orotava.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Region:** [[Canary Islands]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Listán Negro]]

## About
Working with local varieties and traditional vine training, the estate makes fresh reds and whites. The wines have helped lift the profile of Tenerife.
