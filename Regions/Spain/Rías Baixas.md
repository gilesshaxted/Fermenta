# Rías Baixas

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Atlantic Spain's star white: crisp, saline Albariño on granite near the sea.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Maritime
> - **Soils:** granite, sand, alluvium
> - **Main grapes:** [[Albariño]]

## Landscape and topography
Coastal valleys and estuaries (rías) in Galicia, with vines trained overhead on pergolas to avoid damp.

## Soil and geology
Granite and sandy soils, with alluvial deposits near rivers.

## Climate
Maritime and wet, with Atlantic influence, mild temperatures and humidity.

## History
Albariño may have been planted by monks. Rías Baixas became a DO in 1988 and Albariño has grown in popularity since the 1990s.

## Production and wine styles
Mostly Albariño, in stainless steel. Subzones include Val do Salnés, Condado do Tea and O Rosal.

## Key wines
- Rías Baixas Albariño
- Val do Salnés
- Condado do Tea

## What to look for
- Peach, lemon, saline and a floral touch.
- A great match for seafood.
