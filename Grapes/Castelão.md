# Castelão

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A widely grown Portuguese red, savoury and structured, especially in Setúbal.

## Where it's from
Known as Periquita in some places. It is grown across southern Portugal, mainly in Setúbal and Tejo.

## Where it grows
- **Setúbal, Portugal:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Red cherry, plum, herbs and earth

## Classic wines
- Palmela Castelão
- Setúbal red

## Pairs with
- Grilled meat
- Stews
- Pork

## Good to know
- Also called Periquita.
- Can age well when well-made.
