# Saint-Joseph

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A long strip on the right bank offering elegant, fresh Syrah with lovely value.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Rhône Valley]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** granite, schist
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Marsanne]], [[Roussanne]]

## Landscape and topography
A stretch of about 60 km of mostly granite slopes between Condrieu and Cornas.

## Soil and geology
Granite, with some schist and gneiss.

## Climate
Continental.

## History
Extended in 1969 and now a source of fine, lighter Syrah and good whites.

## Production and wine styles
Syrah for red, and Marsanne with Roussanne for white.

## Key wines
- Saint-Joseph rouge
- Saint-Joseph blanc

## What to look for
- Red fruit, pepper and violet, a friendlier style than Cornas.
- Best sites are on the old granite hillsides.

## Producers
- [[Domaine Jean-Louis Chave]]
- [[Yves Cuilleron]]
