# Syrah

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Dark, peppery and savoury, or ripe and jammy when grown warm. Same grape as Shiraz.

## Where it's from
The northern Rhône Valley in France, where it makes the great wines of Hermitage, Côte-Rôtie and Cornas.

## Where it grows
- **Northern Rhône, France:** peppery, savoury, violet-scented
- **Languedoc, France:** warm, spicy and good value, often blended
- **Barossa Valley and McLaren Vale, Australia:** known as Shiraz, rich and ripe
- **Swartland, South Africa:** fresh, spicy, increasingly fashionable
- **Washington State, USA:** dark and structured

## What it tastes like
- **Cooler sites:** black pepper, olive, smoked meat, violet
- **Warmer sites:** blackberry jam, plum, liquorice, chocolate, mint

## Classic wines
- Hermitage
- Côte-Rôtie
- Cornas
- Barossa Shiraz
- Languedoc Syrah

## Pairs with
- Barbecued meat
- Lamb
- Peppered steak
- Game
- Hearty stews

## Good to know
- Syrah and Shiraz are the same grape. The name tends to signal the style: Syrah is usually more peppery, Shiraz usually riper.
- Often blended with Grenache and Mourvèdre (a GSM). In Côte-Rôtie it can be co-fermented with a little Viognier.
