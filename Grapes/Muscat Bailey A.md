# Muscat Bailey A

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Japanese red crossing made for the humid climate, giving light, fruity reds.

## Where it's from
Bred in Japan in the 1920s by Zenbei Kawakami as a cross of Bailey and Muscat Hamburg. It is widely grown for light reds.

## Where it grows
- **Yamanashi, Japan:** the main area
- **Yamagata, Japan:** also planted

## What it tastes like
- Strawberry, red cherry, candy and light spice

## Classic wines
- Muscat Bailey A

## Pairs with
- Grilled chicken
- Yakitori
- Mild dishes

## Good to know
- Light and fruity with a candy note.
- Bred by Zenbei Kawakami.
