# Braida

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The estate that showed Barbera could be a serious, ageworthy wine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Asti]], [[Piedmont]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Barbera]]

## About
Giacomo Bologna's family estate in Rocchetta Tanaro championed Barbera in the Asti area, using oak and careful vineyard selection. The results are rich, fresh and structured.

## Known for
- Barbera d'Asti Bricco dell'Uccellone
- Barbera d'Asti Bricco della Bigotta
- Barbera d'Asti Ai Suma
