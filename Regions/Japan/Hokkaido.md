# Hokkaido

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Japan's northern island, with cool conditions suited to Pinot Noir and German-style whites.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Japan]]
> - **Part of:** [[Asia]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** volcanic, clay, sand, loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Müller-Thurgau]], [[Zweigelt]]

## Landscape and topography
Vineyards are spread across the island, with Yoichi, Ikeda and the Sapporo area among the best known.

## Soil and geology
Volcanic soils, clay and sandy loam.

## Climate
The coolest wine climate in Japan, with cold winters and mild summers. Vines are often buried or protected in winter.

## History
Wine making began in the twentieth century with grapes from cold hardy crosses. Plantings of Pinot Noir and other classic varieties have grown since.

## Production and wine styles
Fresh, aromatic whites and light, elegant reds, plus some sparkling wine.

## Key wines
- Hokkaido Pinot Noir
- Hokkaido Müller-Thurgau
- Yoichi wines

## What to look for
- Cool climate wines with bright acidity.
- Quality has risen quickly in recent years.
