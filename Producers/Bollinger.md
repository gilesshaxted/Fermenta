# Bollinger

> [!abstract] At a glance

## About
Bollinger ferments many of its base wines in old oak and ages its reserves under cork in magnums. The style is full, toasty and long lived.

## Known for
- Special Cuvée
- La Grande Année
- R.D.
- Vieilles Vignes Françaises
