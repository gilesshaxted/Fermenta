# Limnio

> [!abstract] At a glance
> An ancient Greek red, mentioned by Aristotle, and grown on Lemnos and the mainland.

## Where it's from
Grown on the island of Lemnos since antiquity, with Aristotle's Lemnian grape often thought to be the same variety.

## Where it grows
- **Lemnos, Greece:** the heartland
- **Macedonia, Greece:** in blends

## What it tastes like
- Red cherry, herbs, spice and bay leaf

## Classic wines
- Lemnos Limnio

## Pairs with
- Lamb
- Grilled meat
- Hard cheese

## Good to know
- Often blended with Cabernet or Grenache.
- Savoury and herbal.
