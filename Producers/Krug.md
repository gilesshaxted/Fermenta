# Krug

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Reims Champagne house famed for its multi-vintage Grande Cuvée and its tiny single-vineyard wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Champagne]], [[Montagne de Reims]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Pinot Meunier]]
> - **Styles:** [[Champagne (style)]]

## About
Krug ferments its base wines in small oak casks and keeps a huge library of reserve wines. The result is rich, layered, nutty Champagne that is built around blending rather than a single year.

## Known for
- Grande Cuvée
- Clos du Mesnil
- Clos d'Ambonnay
- Krug Rosé
