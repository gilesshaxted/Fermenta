# Ningxia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> China's most acclaimed quality wine region, in the north-west at the foot of the Helan Mountains.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[China]]
> - **Part of:** [[Asia]]
> - **Climate:** Semi-arid
> - **Soils:** gravel, sand, loam, alluvial
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Marselan]], [[Cabernet Gernischt]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
A high, dry plateau on the edge of the Gobi desert, with vineyards at about 1,000 to 1,200 metres along the Yellow River.

## Soil and geology
Gravelly, sandy and alluvial soils with low fertility.

## Climate
Dry continental, with hot summers, very cold winters and little rain, so vines need irrigation from the Yellow River. In winter the vines are buried in soil to protect them from freezing.

## History
Commercial grape growing took off here from the 1980s and modern wineries began to appear in the 2000s. A Bordeaux-inspired classification of estates was introduced in the 2010s, and the region's wines have won awards abroad.

## Production and wine styles
Mostly red Bordeaux style wines from Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Cabernet Gernischt, plus Marselan and Chardonnay.

## Key wines
- Helan Mountain East wines
- Ningxia Cabernet Sauvignon
- Ningxia Marselan

## What to look for
- Often firm, with dark fruit and structure.
- The place to start with Chinese wine.
