# Clos de la Coulée de Serrant

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Nicolas Joly's monopole vineyard in Savennières, a landmark of biodynamic viticulture.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Anjou]], [[Loire Valley]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chenin Blanc]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]]

## About
Coulée de Serrant is a steep, ancient vineyard above the Loire, farmed biodynamically for decades. The Chenin Blanc is intense, savoury and often needs time to open up.
