# Modena and Reggio Emilia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Frothy, dry, red sparkling wine made for cured meats and rich food.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Emilia-Romagna]]
> - **Climate:** Temperate
> - **Soils:** alluvium, clay, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Lambrusco]]
> - **Styles:** [[Lambrusco (style)|Lambrusco style]], [[Charmat method]]

## Landscape and topography
Flat to gently rolling land around Modena, Reggio Emilia and Parma on the Po plain.

## Soil and geology
Alluvial clay, sand and gravel.

## Climate
Temperate, with hot summers and foggy winters.

## History
Lambrusco has been made for thousands of years. A sweet export style in the 1970s damaged its image, and quality dry Lambrusco is now enjoying a revival.

## Production and wine styles
Several Lambrusco grapes: Sorbara (pale, floral), Grasparossa (dark, tannic) and Salamino. Usually made in tank, some by bottle fermentation.

## Key wines
- Lambrusco di Sorbara DOC
- Lambrusco Grasparossa di Castelvetro
- Lambrusco Salamino di Santa Croce

## What to look for
- Cherry, violets and bright acidity.
- Serve chilled, with salumi and tortellini.
