# South Africa

Part of [[Africa]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of the New World's oldest wine countries, with Cape heritage, old vines, Chenin Blanc as its hallmark and a lively new wave.

## How South African wine is organised
- The **Wine of Origin (WO)** system names Regions, Districts and Wards.
- A label with a grape or region must have at least 85% of it.
- **Cape Blend** usually contains Pinotage, and **Cap Classique** is traditional-method sparkling wine.
- **Chenin Blanc** is the most planted grape, often from old vines.
