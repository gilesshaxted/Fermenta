# Mount Lebanon

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The mountainous spine behind Beirut, with high, small vineyard sites and several wineries.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Lebanon]]
> - **Part of:** [[Asia]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, stony soils
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Syrah]], [[Cinsault]], [[Merlot]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
Steep terraces and hillsides rising to well over 1,000 metres above the coast.

## Soil and geology
Limestone and clay, often thin and stony.

## Climate
Mediterranean, but cooler with altitude, with more rain than the Bekaa and snow in winter.

## History
Terraced vineyards have a long history in the mountains, and a number of wineries are based here even where fruit comes from several areas. Chateau Musar has its winery at Ghazir in this range, although its vineyards are in the Bekaa.

## Production and wine styles
Reds from Cabernet Sauvignon, Syrah, Cinsault and Merlot, plus some fresh whites.

## Key wines
- Mount Lebanon red blends
- Mount Lebanon Syrah

## What to look for
- Mountain freshness and moderate alcohol.
- Small production, so hard to find.
