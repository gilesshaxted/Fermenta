# Malvasia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A large family of aromatic grapes found around the Mediterranean, used for dry, sweet and fortified wines.

## Where it's from
Malvasia is a family of related, and some unrelated, varieties named after the port of Monemvasia in Greece. Venetian traders spread it around the Mediterranean.

## Where it grows
- **Lazio, Italy:** Malvasia di Candia and Malvasia Puntinata
- **Madeira, Portugal:** Malvasia, also known as Malmsey
- **Canary Islands, Spain:** Malvasía Aromática and Volcánica

## What it tastes like
- Apricot, orange blossom, honey and spice

## Classic wines
- Frascati
- Malmsey Madeira
- Malvasía de Lanzarote

## Pairs with
- Blue cheese
- Fruit desserts
- Nuts

## Good to know
- Several distinct grapes carry the name.
- Sweet Madeira Malmsey is a rich, long-lived wine.
