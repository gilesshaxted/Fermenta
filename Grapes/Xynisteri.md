# Xynisteri

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Cyprus's main native white grape, with fresh, crisp wines when grown at altitude.

## Where it's from
Grown across Cyprus and the most important white variety on the island. The best wines come from high vineyards in the Troodos, and it is also used in Commandaria.

## Where it grows
- **Troodos, Cyprus:** the best vineyards
- **Limassol, Cyprus:** also grown

## What it tastes like
- Lemon, green apple, white flowers and herbs

## Classic wines
- Troodos Xynisteri
- Commandaria

## Pairs with
- Halloumi
- Grilled fish
- Salads

## Good to know
- Also used in Commandaria.
- Drink young for freshness.
