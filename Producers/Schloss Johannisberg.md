# Schloss Johannisberg

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A historic Rheingau estate with a strong claim to be the birthplace of Riesling as a quality grape.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Germany]]
> - **Region:** [[Rheingau]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]]

## About
A large, hilltop estate above the Rhine, planted almost entirely to Riesling. The wines range from dry to sweet and are marked by capsule colours that signal the style.

## Known for
- Gelblack
- Rotlack
