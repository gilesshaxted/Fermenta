# Bodega Garzón

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A modern, high-spec winery near the Atlantic in Maldonado, famed for Tannat and Albariño.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Uruguay]]
> - **Region:** [[Maldonado]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tannat]], [[Albariño]], [[Cabernet Franc]]

## About
Garzón is a large, design-led estate with a strong focus on sustainability. Its vineyards benefit from cool ocean breezes, giving fresh whites and elegant reds.

## Known for
- Garzón Single Vineyard Albariño
- Garzón Reserva Tannat
- Balasto
