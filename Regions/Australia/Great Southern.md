# Great Southern

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A vast, cool region in the south of Western Australia, with Riesling at its heart.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Australia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Oceania]]
> - **Climate:** Cool continental
> - **Soils:** gravel, loam, clay, granite
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]], [[Syrah]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills and plains stretching from the Stirling Range to the south coast.

## Soil and geology
Gravelly loam, clay and granite soils.

## Climate
Cool and dry, with ocean influence on the coast.

## History
Vines were planted in 1960s experiments, and sub-regions such as Frankland River and Mount Barker emerged.

## Production and wine styles
Riesling, Syrah, Chardonnay and Cabernet.

## Key wines
- Great Southern Riesling
- Frankland River Shiraz

## What to look for
- Dry, long-lived Riesling.
- Peppery, cool-climate Syrah.

- [[Frankland Estate]]
