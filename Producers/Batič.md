# Batič

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A biodynamic Vipava estate, one of the first in the country to farm that way.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Slovenia]]
> - **Region:** [[Vipava Valley]]

## About
A family estate near Šempas in the Vipava Valley working with local varieties. Wines are made with minimal intervention and have a lively savoury character.
