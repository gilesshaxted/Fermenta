# Öküzgözü

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A soft, fruity Turkish red whose name means ox eye.

## Where it's from
Grown around Elazığ and Malatya, in eastern Turkey. It is often blended with the tannic Boğazkere.

## Where it grows
- **Elazığ, Turkey:** the heartland
- **Malatya, Turkey:** also grown

## What it tastes like
- Sour cherry, plum, spice and violet

## Classic wines
- Öküzgözü
- Öküzgözü Boğazkere blends

## Pairs with
- Grilled meat
- Kebabs
- Stews

## Good to know
- Name means ox eye.
- Medium tannin, with bright fruit.
