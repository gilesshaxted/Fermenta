# Pheasant's Tears

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Sighnaghi winery that did much to introduce Georgian qvevri and amber wines to the wider world.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Georgia]]
> - **Region:** [[Kakheti]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Rkatsiteli]], [[Saperavi]], [[Mtsvane]], [[Kisi]]
> - **Styles:** [[Qvevri wine]], [[Orange wine]], [[Natural wine]]

## About
Based in Sighnaghi in Kakheti, it works with local varieties and traditional clay vessels buried in the ground. The wines are made with minimal intervention, and the amber whites are the calling card.

## Known for
- Rkatsiteli
- Saperavi
- Mtsvane
- Kisi
