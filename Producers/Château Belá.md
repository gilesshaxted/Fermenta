# Château Belá

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A modern estate in the south of Slovakia, near the Hungarian border, known for aromatic whites.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Slovakia]]
> - **Region:** [[Južnoslovenská]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]], [[Muscat]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]]

## About
Close to Štúrovo, it has a carefully restored manor and a modern cellar. The whites are fresh and aromatic, with Riesling a key grape.
