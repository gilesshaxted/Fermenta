# Langhe

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The wider hills that contain Barolo and Barbaresco, and their everyday Langhe Nebbiolo, Barbera and Dolcetto.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Piedmont]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** marl, limestone, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Nebbiolo]], [[Barbera]], [[Dolcetto]]

## Landscape and topography
A large area of rolling hills south of the Tanaro, covering the Barolo and Barbaresco zones.

## Soil and geology
Calcareous marl and sandstone, with local differences.

## Climate
Continental, with autumn fog.

## History
Since 2014 the Langhe area has been recognised as a UNESCO World Heritage cultural landscape together with Roero and Monferrato.

## Production and wine styles
Langhe Nebbiolo is a younger, cheaper route into the grape. Barbera d'Alba and Dolcetto d'Alba are everyday reds.

## Key wines
- Langhe Nebbiolo
- Barbera d'Alba
- Dolcetto d'Alba
- Langhe Chardonnay

## What to look for
- Great value for Nebbiolo flavour without Barolo's price.
- Dolcetto is soft and fruity, Barbera juicy.
