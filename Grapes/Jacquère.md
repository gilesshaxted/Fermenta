# Jacquère

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Light, alpine and crisp, the everyday white of Savoie.

## Where it's from
Savoie and the surrounding Alps.

## Where it grows
- **Savoie, France:** Apremont and Abymes are classic zones

## What it tastes like
- Citrus, apple and mountain-fresh minerality

## Classic wines
- Apremont
- Abymes
- Vin de Savoie

## Pairs with
- Fondue
- Raclette
- Freshwater fish

## Good to know
- Low alcohol and zesty.
- Perfect ski-chalet wine.
