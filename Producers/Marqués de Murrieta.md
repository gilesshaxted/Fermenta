# Marqués de Murrieta

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A historic Rioja house at Finca Ygay, known for long aged, traditional reds and a distinctive oak aged white.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Region:** [[Rioja Alta]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tempranillo]], [[Graciano]], [[Macabeo]]

## About
Based around the Ygay estate near Logroño, this is a classic Rioja producer with a strong commitment to long ageing in oak and bottle. The reds are poised and savoury, and the white Capellanía is one of the region's famous traditional whites.

## Known for
- Castillo Ygay Gran Reserva Especial
- Dalmau
- Capellanía
