# Borgo del Tiglio

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small Collio estate admired for rich, age worthy white wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Collio]], [[Friuli-Venezia Giulia]]

> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Friulano]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]]

## About
Nicola Manferrari farms a modest area in Brazzano and makes wines with depth and texture. The whites have a reputation as some of the longest lived in Friuli.

## Known for
- Ronco della Chiesa
- Studio di Bianco
- Collio Friulano
