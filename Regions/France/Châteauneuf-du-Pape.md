# Châteauneuf-du-Pape

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The southern Rhône's flagship, producing warm, spicy Grenache blends, and a little rich white wine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Rhône Valley]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** galets roulés, limestone, clay, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grenache]], [[Syrah]], [[Mourvèdre]], [[Roussanne]]
> - **Styles:** [[GSM]]

## Landscape and topography
A gently undulating plateau close to Avignon, with the Rhône to the west.

## Soil and geology
Famous for large rounded stones (galets roulés) that absorb heat during the day and release it at night, alongside limestone, clay and sand.

## Climate
Mediterranean, with hot dry summers and the Mistral wind.

## History
Founded around the 14th-century papal residence. It was the first French appellation to set legal rules, in 1923, with thirteen (now eighteen counting colour variants) permitted grape varieties.

## Production and wine styles
Red is mostly Grenache with Syrah, Mourvèdre, Cinsault and others. White uses Grenache Blanc, Clairette, Roussanne and Bourboulenc. Bottles carry an embossed papal crest.

## Key wines
- Châteauneuf-du-Pape rouge
- Châteauneuf-du-Pape blanc

## What to look for
- Black cherry, garrigue herbs, leather and spice.
- Alcohol is high, so pair with rich dishes, game and lamb.
