# New Zealand

Part of [[Oceania]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cool, windswept island nation with a clean, bright style, best known for Marlborough Sauvignon Blanc and Central Otago Pinot Noir.

## How New Zealand wine is organised
- **GI** (Geographical Indication) regions are defined, but there are few other rules.
- A label naming a grape or region must have at least 85% of it.
- Screwcaps are nearly universal.
- **Sustainable Winegrowing New Zealand** covers most vineyards.
