# Maremma

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The wild southern Tuscan coast, with a growing mix of Sangiovese, Bordeaux grapes and Vermentino.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Tuscany]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** clay, sand, volcanic, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sangiovese]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Vermentino]], [[Syrah]]
> - **Styles:** [[Super Tuscan]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling coastal hills and plains in southern Tuscany, with Morellino di Scansano as the best-known zone.

## Soil and geology
Clay, sand and volcanic soils, with limestone in places.

## Climate
Warm Mediterranean, with sea breezes.

## History
Once marshland, the Maremma was drained in the 20th century and its wines have risen in reputation since the 1990s.

## Production and wine styles
Morellino di Scansano is Sangiovese-based, plus IGT blends and Vermentino whites.

## Key wines
- Morellino di Scansano
- Maremma Toscana
- Vermentino

## What to look for
- Riper, rounder Sangiovese than Chianti.
- Good value.
