# Château Purcari

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The leading estate of the historic Purcari wine region in southern Moldova, known for Negru de Purcari.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Moldova]]
> - **Region:** [[Purcari]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Saperavi]]
> - **Styles:** [[Bordeaux blend]]

## About
Close to the Black Sea and the Dniester, it has a long reputation for red blends with a Bordeaux accent plus local grapes. The wines are rich and well made.

## Known for
- Negru de Purcari
- Roșu de Purcari
- Alb de Purcari
