# Tsinandali

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A white wine appellation in Kakheti, built on a blend of Rkatsiteli and Mtsvane.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Georgia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Kakheti]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** alluvial, clay, loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Rkatsiteli]], [[Mtsvane]]

## Landscape and topography
Flat to gently sloping vineyards on the right bank of the Alazani river, near the village of Tsinandali.

## Soil and geology
Alluvial and clay-rich soils, with loam.

## Climate
Warm continental, with a long growing season.

## History
The village is linked to the Chavchavadze family estate, one of the best known historic wine estates in Georgia.

## Production and wine styles
Dry white wines in a lighter European style, made from Rkatsiteli with Mtsvane.

## Key wines
- Tsinandali

## What to look for
- Fresh, citrusy dry white.
- A good gateway to Georgian wine.
