# Henry of Pelham

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family run Niagara producer with a strong reputation for value.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Canada]]
> - **Region:** [[Niagara Peninsula]]
> - **Type:** Major producer

## About
Henry of Pelham is an estate in the Short Hills Bench. It makes Baco Noir and Riesling alongside Chardonnay and Pinot Noir.

## Known for
- Speck Family Reserve Baco Noir
- Estate Riesling
- Riesling Icewine
