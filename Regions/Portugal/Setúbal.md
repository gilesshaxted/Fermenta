# Setúbal

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A peninsula south of Lisbon famed for Moscatel de Setúbal, a sweet fortified wine, and Castelão reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Portugal]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Muscat|Moscatel]], [[Castelão]], [[Fernão Pires]]

## Landscape and topography
Gentle hills and sandy plains around Setúbal and the Arrábida mountains.

## Soil and geology
Limestone and clay in the hills, sandy soils on the plains.

## Climate
Warm Mediterranean with Atlantic breezes.

## History
Moscatel de Setúbal is a fortified wine with long skin contact. José Maria da Fonseca has long been a major producer.

## Production and wine styles
Moscatel de Setúbal (fortified), Castelão-based reds and others.

## Key wines
- Moscatel de Setúbal
- Moscatel Roxo
- Palmela Castelão

## What to look for
- Orange peel, honey and nuts.
- A beautiful dessert wine.
