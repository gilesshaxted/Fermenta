# Elbling

> [!abstract] At a glance
> An ancient, high-acid white that is one of the oldest grapes of the Moselle.

## Where it's from
Probably one of the oldest varieties of the Moselle, long used for sparkling base wines because of its sharp acidity.

## Where it grows
- **Moselle, Luxembourg:** the heartland
- **Upper Mosel, Germany:** also grown

## What it tastes like
- Lemon, green apple, herbs and chalk

## Classic wines
- Elbling
- Crémant base wines

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Salads
- Light starters

## Good to know
- Very high acidity.
- Often used for sparkling wine.
