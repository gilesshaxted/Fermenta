# País

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Chile's oldest grape, now reborn in light, fresh reds from old vines.

## Where it's from
Brought by Spanish colonisers in the 16th century, where it is known as Mission in California and Criolla Chica in Argentina.

## Where it grows
- **Itata, Maule and Bío Bío, Chile:** old bush vines
- **California, USA:** as Mission

## What it tastes like
- Red cherry, strawberry and earthy spice

## Classic wines
- Itata País
- Secano Interior País

## Pairs with
- Grilled sausages
- Roast chicken
- Empanadas

## Good to know
- Once only used for cheap wine.
- Serve slightly chilled.
