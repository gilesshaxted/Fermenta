# Doluca

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A large family-owned winery in Thrace, one of the best known names in Turkish wine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Turkey]]
> - **Region:** [[Thrace and Marmara]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Syrah]]

## About
Doluca is centred on Thrace and the Marmara area. It makes a broad range of reds and whites, from easy drinking to its more serious Nirvana.

- Nirvana
