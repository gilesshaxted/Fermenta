# Sciaccarello

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Corsica's peppery, light red grape, often used for rosé.

## Where it's from
Native to Corsica, mainly in Ajaccio and Sartène. The name means crunchy.

## Where it grows
- **Ajaccio, France:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Red cherry, pepper, herbs and spice

## Classic wines
- Ajaccio
- Corsican rosé

## Pairs with
- Grilled fish
- Charcuterie
- Pasta

## Good to know
- Peppery and aromatic.
- Useful for rosé.
