# Penedès

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Catalonia's wine heartland, home of Cava and a growing group of fine still wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, sand, calcareous clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Xarel·lo]], [[Macabeo]], [[Parellada]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Grenache|Garnacha]]
> - **Styles:** [[Cava (style)|Cava style]]

## Landscape and topography
Coastal plain, hills and mountains near Barcelona, with vines from sea level up to about 800 metres.

## Soil and geology
Limestone and clay, with sandy soil on the coast.

## Climate
Mediterranean, cooler at altitude with sea influence.

## History
The Raventós family produced the first traditional-method Cava at Codorníu in 1872. Miguel Torres brought international varieties and modern methods in the 20th century.

## Production and wine styles
Cava from Xarel·lo, Macabeo and Parellada, plus still whites and reds from local and international grapes.

## Key wines
- Cava
- Corpinnat
- Penedès Xarel·lo
- Penedès reds

## What to look for
- Cava is made by the traditional method, like Champagne.
- Look for Reserva or Gran Reserva Cava with longer ageing.
