# Pingus

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small, cult Ribera del Duero estate set up by Peter Sisseck, famous for rich, concentrated Tempranillo.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Region:** [[Ribera del Duero]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tempranillo]]

## About
Farmed biodynamically from old vines, Pingus helped redefine what Ribera del Duero could be. The wines are dense yet precise, and are among Spain's most sought after.
