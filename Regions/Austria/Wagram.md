# Wagram

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A line of loess hills along the Danube, home of Grüner Veltliner and Roter Veltliner.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Austria]]
> - **Part of:** [[Lower Austria]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** loess, clay, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grüner Veltliner]], [[Roter Veltliner]]

## Landscape and topography
A long, high loess terrace overlooking the Danube plain.

## Soil and geology
Deep loess, with some clay and sand.

## Climate
Warm, dry and sunny.

## History
The loess cliffs (Wagram means wave) were shaped by ancient river movement, making ideal vineyard terraces.

## Production and wine styles
Grüner Veltliner, with Roter Veltliner a local speciality.

## Key wines
- Wagram Grüner Veltliner
- Wagram Roter Veltliner

## What to look for
- Rich, creamy Grüner.
- Roter Veltliner is a local curiosity.
