# Salento

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The sun-baked heel of Italy, home of Negroamaro and its rosato.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Puglia]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, terra rossa, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Negroamaro]], [[Zinfandel|Primitivo]], [[Malvasia Nera]]

## Landscape and topography
A flat limestone peninsula between two seas.

## Soil and geology
Red terra rossa over limestone.

## Climate
Hot and dry Mediterranean.

## History
Salice Salentino is the best-known appellation, and Salento IGT is used widely.

## Production and wine styles
Negroamaro-led reds and rosato. Bush vines (alberello) are traditional.

## Key wines
- Salice Salentino
- Salento Negroamaro
- Salento Rosato

## What to look for
- Black cherry, earth and a bitter twist.
- Rosato is excellent with seafood.

## Producers
- [[Cosimo Taurino]]
- [[Leone de Castris]]
