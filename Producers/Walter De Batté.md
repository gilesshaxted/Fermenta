# Walter De Batté

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A tiny Cinque Terre grower on the steep sea cliffs, known for the sweet Sciacchetrà.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Liguria]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Vermentino]]
> - **Styles:** [[Passito]]

## About
De Batté works dramatic terraced vineyards over the Ligurian Sea. The whites are saline and floral, and the dried grape Sciacchetrà is rare and long lived.

## Known for
- Cinque Terre Bianco
- Cinque Terre Sciacchetrà
