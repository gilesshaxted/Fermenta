# Veuve Clicquot

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of the best known Champagne houses in the world, recognised by its yellow label.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Champagne]]

## About
Veuve Clicquot builds its non-vintage blend on a strong Pinot Noir base with a large reserve wine library. The wines are full, fruity and consistent across the range.

## Known for
- Yellow Label Brut
- La Grande Dame
- Rich
- Vintage Rosé
