# Aegean Turkey

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The sunny western coast around Izmir, with a history of winemaking that goes back to ancient Greece.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Turkey]]
> - **Part of:** [[Asia]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, sand, volcanic
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sultaniye]], [[Syrah]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Muscat]], [[Merlot]]

## Landscape and topography
Coastal plains and inland hills in the west, including the Izmir area, Urla, Alaçatı and the Denizli region inland.

## Soil and geology
Clay, limestone and sandy soils, with some volcanic soils in the hills.

## Climate
Warm Mediterranean, with hot dry summers and mild winters, and sea breezes in the coastal areas.

## History
Ancient Greek colonies in Ionia grew vines here, and the region has been a source of wine ever since. Urla and Alaçatı have recently become fashionable wine destinations.

## Production and wine styles
Fresh whites and ripe reds, from Sultaniye, Muscat and international varieties. Sultaniye is also dried as raisins.

## Key wines
- Aegean Syrah
- Urla wines
- Sultaniye white

## What to look for
- Sun-ripe flavours.
- Sea views help.
