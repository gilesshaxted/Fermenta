# Cabernet Gernischt

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A red grape long grown in China, especially Shandong, and generally identified as Carmenere.

## Where it's from
Brought to the Yantai area in the late nineteenth century and long grown under this name. DNA work has shown it to be Carmenere, so the Carmenere name is increasingly used.

## Where it grows
- **Shandong, China:** the heartland
- **Ningxia, China:** also planted

## What it tastes like
- Red berry, green pepper, herbs and spice

## Classic wines
- Yantai Cabernet Gernischt
- Chinese red blends

## Pairs with
- Roast meat
- Peking duck
- Mushrooms

## Good to know
- Can be herbal when under-ripe.
- Often in Bordeaux style blends.
