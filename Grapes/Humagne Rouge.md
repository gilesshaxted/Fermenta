# Humagne Rouge

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A rustic, earthy Valais red with firm tannin and a peppery edge.

## Where it's from
Grown in the Valais for centuries. It is the same variety as the Aosta Valley's Cornalin, and is not related to Humagne Blanche.

## Where it grows
- **Valais, Switzerland:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Blackberry, pepper, earth and spice

## Classic wines
- Humagne Rouge

## Pairs with
- Game
- Roast meat
- Hard cheese

## Good to know
- Often blended.
- A grape to seek out for the local colour.
