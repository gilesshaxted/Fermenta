# Benanti

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of the producers that first brought Etna wines to international attention.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Etna]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Nerello Mascalese]], [[Carricante]]

## About
A family estate on the eastern slopes of Etna, known for expressive whites and reds. The Carricante wines in particular age very well.

## Known for
- Pietramarina Etna Bianco Superiore
- Serra della Contessa
- Rovittello
- Etna Rosso
