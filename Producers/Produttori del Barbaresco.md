# Produttori del Barbaresco

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small growers' cooperative in Barbaresco widely admired for honest, great value Nebbiolo.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Barbaresco]], [[Piedmont]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Nebbiolo]]

## About
Founded by local growers pooling their grapes, the cooperative makes a classic Barbaresco and, in the best years, a series of single vineyard Riservas. The wines are traditional in style and have a loyal following.

## Known for
- Barbaresco
- Barbaresco Riserva Asili
- Barbaresco Riserva Rabajà
- Barbaresco Riserva Montestefano
