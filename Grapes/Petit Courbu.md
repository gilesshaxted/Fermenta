# Petit Courbu

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A rare Basque white used in Irouléguy and Jurançon.

## Where it's from
Native to the Pyrenees foothills, and one of the lesser grapes of Jurançon and Irouléguy.

## Where it grows
- **Irouléguy, France:** in blends with Gros Manseng

## What it tastes like
- Citrus, apricot, herbs and honey

## Classic wines
- Irouléguy blanc
- Jurançon

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Goat's cheese

## Good to know
- Adds finesse to blends.
- Rare.
