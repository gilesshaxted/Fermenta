# Awatere Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cooler, windier valley to the south, with tauter, more herbal Sauvignon Blanc.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[New Zealand]]
> - **Part of:** [[Marlborough]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** gravel, stony loam, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Pinot Noir]]

## Landscape and topography
A narrow, windy valley, close to the coast.

## Soil and geology
Stony soils with clay.

## Climate
Cooler, drier and windier than the Wairau.

## History
Developed in the 1990s and 2000s.

## Production and wine styles
Sauvignon Blanc and Pinot Noir.

## Key wines
- Awatere Sauvignon Blanc

## What to look for
- Crisp, herbal and savoury.
- Often tauter than Wairau wines.
