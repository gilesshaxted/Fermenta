# Kras

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A limestone plateau behind Trieste, famous for its red soils and dark, structured Teran.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Slovenia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Primorska]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** terra rossa, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Refosco]], [[Malvasia]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Merlot]]

## Landscape and topography
A dry limestone plateau and small hills close to the Gulf of Trieste, with a rocky, karst landscape.

## Soil and geology
Iron-rich red terra rossa over limestone.

## Climate
Mediterranean, with bora winds and cool nights, and a dry, windy plateau.

## History
Teran is the signature wine of Kras, where the grape has been grown for a long time. Red soils are said to give the wine its dark colour and sharp acidity.

## Production and wine styles
Teran, a high acid, dark red from a Refosco-type grape, and Malvazija for whites.

## Key wines
- Kras Teran
- Kras Malvazija

## What to look for
- Teran is dark, tart and savoury.
- Often paired with Kras air-dried ham.
