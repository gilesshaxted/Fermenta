# China

Part of [[Asia]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of the world's largest grape growers, with a fast-improving fine wine scene in the north and west.

## How wine is organised here
- China labels wines by region of origin and grape, and wine regions are often named for provinces.
- Ningxia has a Bordeaux-inspired classification of estates.
- In cold regions, vines have to be buried in winter to survive the frost.
