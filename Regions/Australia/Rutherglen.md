# Rutherglen

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A hot north-eastern Victoria region, famous for luscious fortified Muscat and Topaque.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Australia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Oceania]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** red loam, clay, granite
> - **Main grapes:** [[Muscat]], [[Muscadelle]], [[Syrah]], [[Petite Sirah]]
> - **Styles:** [[Rutherglen Muscat]]

## Landscape and topography
Gently undulating land near the Murray River, at about 150 to 200 metres.

## Soil and geology
Red loamy soils over clay.

## Climate
Hot, dry summers, with a long ripening season.

## History
The gold rush brought settlers, and fortified wines became a speciality from the 19th century.

## Production and wine styles
Rutherglen Muscat and Topaque (Muscadelle), plus Durif (Petite Sirah) and Shiraz.

## Key wines
- Rutherglen Muscat
- Rutherglen Topaque
- Rutherglen Durif

## What to look for
- Raisins, toffee and spice.
- Classification levels: Rutherglen, Classic, Grand and Rare.
