# Savatiano

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Greece's most planted white grape, the traditional base of retsina.

## Where it's from
Grown around Athens in the Mesogeia plain, a drought-tolerant grape that survives in heat.

## Where it grows
- **Attica, Greece:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Lemon, apple, herbs and pine

## Classic wines
- Retsina
- Attica Savatiano

## Pairs with
- Fried fish
- Grilled seafood
- Meze

## Good to know
- Retsina is flavoured with pine resin.
- Good versions can age.
