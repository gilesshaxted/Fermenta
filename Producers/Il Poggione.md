# Il Poggione

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A large and reliable Brunello producer from the southern slopes of Montalcino.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Montalcino]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sangiovese]]

## About
A family estate near Sant'Angelo in Colle, working in a traditional style with large oak. The wines are generous, balanced and good value for the appellation.

## Known for
- Brunello di Montalcino
- Brunello di Montalcino Vigna Paganelli Riserva
- Rosso di Montalcino
