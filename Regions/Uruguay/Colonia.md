# Colonia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A historic western region on the Rio de la Plata, home of some of Uruguay's oldest vineyards.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Uruguay]]
> - **Part of:** [[South America]]
> - **Climate:** Maritime
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tannat]], [[Albariño]], [[Viognier]], [[Merlot]]

## Landscape and topography
Flat to gently rolling land along the river, opposite Buenos Aires.

## Soil and geology
Clay and limestone with loam.

## Climate
Maritime, with warm summers moderated by the river.

## History
Colonia is among the oldest settled parts of Uruguay, and wine has been made here since the colonial period.

## Production and wine styles
Tannat, white wines and some blends from family estates.

## Key wines
- Colonia Tannat
- Colonia Viognier

## What to look for
- Close to Buenos Aires by ferry.
- Mix of traditional and new wineries.
