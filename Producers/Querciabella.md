# Querciabella

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A biodynamic Chianti Classico estate that is also notable for being entirely vegan in its approach.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Chianti Classico]]

## About
Farmed biodynamically across vineyards in Greve and Radda. The wines are precise and aromatic, with a modern but restrained feel.
