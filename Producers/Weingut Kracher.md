# Weingut Kracher

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Burgenland sweet wine estate famous for botrytis dessert wines from around Lake Neusiedl.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Austria]]
> - **Region:** [[Neusiedlersee]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Welschriesling]], [[Chardonnay]]
> - **Styles:** [[Noble rot wines]]

## About
A family estate at Illmitz, where lake mist helps noble rot develop. It makes intense sweet wines from Welschriesling, Chardonnay and others.

## Known for
- Zwischen den Seen
- Trockenbeerenauslese
