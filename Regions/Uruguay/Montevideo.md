# Montevideo

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Vineyards around the capital, among the oldest in the country.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Uruguay]]
> - **Part of:** [[South America]]
> - **Climate:** Maritime
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tannat]], [[Merlot]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
Close to the coast and the city, on low hills near the Rio de la Plata.

## Soil and geology
Clay and loam over limestone.

## Climate
Maritime, with cooling breezes and humidity.

## History
Winemaking took hold around the capital in the nineteenth century, and the area still has historic estates.

## Production and wine styles
Tannat, Merlot and fresh whites in a modern style.

## Key wines
- Montevideo Tannat
- Montevideo Sauvignon Blanc

## What to look for
- Often grouped with Canelones.
- Some of the country's oldest wineries.
