# Castello della Sala

> [!abstract] At a glance
> An Antinori owned castle estate in Umbria, best known for its barrel fermented white.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Orvieto]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grechetto]], [[Trebbiano]]

## About
A historic estate near Orvieto that has shaped modern Umbrian white wine. The wines are fresh and clean, with some richer, age worthy bottlings.

## Known for
- Cervaro della Sala
- Orvieto Classico Superiore Bramito del Cervo
- Muffato della Sala
