# Cantina Terlano

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Terlano cooperative famous for long lived white wines held back in the cellar for years.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Alto Adige]], [[Trentino-Alto Adige]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Blanc]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]]

## About
Using grapes from member growers on porphyry soils, the winery ages its top whites in large old oak and on the lees. The wines are mineral and savoury and can last for decades.

## Known for
- Terlaner Nova Domus
- Terlaner Vorberg Riserva
- Winkl Sauvignon
- Terlaner Classico
