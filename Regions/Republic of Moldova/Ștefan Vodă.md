# Ștefan Vodă

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A south-eastern region towards the Dniester and the Black Sea, with the best known red wines in the country.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Republic of Moldova]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Warm continental
> - **Soils:** black earth, clay, limestone, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Rară Neagră]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Pinot Noir]]

## Landscape and topography
A hilly area in the south-east of the Republic of Moldova, with slopes running down towards the Dniester river.

## Soil and geology
Black earth and clay, with limestone and some sandy soils.

## Climate
Warm continental, with some moderation from the Black Sea, which helps long ripening seasons for reds.

## History
Winegrowing here goes back centuries, and the area includes the famous village of Purcari, where wine has been made since the 19th century.

## Production and wine styles
Mostly reds, from Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and the local Rară Neagră, as well as Chardonnay and Sauvignon Blanc.

## Key wines
- Ștefan Vodă Cabernet Sauvignon
- Ștefan Vodă Rară Neagră
- Ștefan Vodă Merlot

## What to look for
- Rich, structured reds.
- Look for Purcari blends.
