# Lambrusco (style)

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Lightly sparkling red from Emilia-Romagna, from dry and savoury to sweet and fruity.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Category:** Sparkling
> - **Colour:** Red
> - **Sweetness:** Varies
> - **Country:** Italy
> - **Main grapes:** [[Lambrusco]]
> - **Regions:** [[Modena and Reggio Emilia]], [[Emilia-Romagna]]

## How it's made
Made as dry (secco), off dry (amabile) or sweet (dolce), usually by the tank method. The best dry examples are frothy, vivid purple and very food friendly.

## Where it's made
Around Modena and Reggio Emilia.

## What it tastes like
- Red berries, violet, cherry
- Frothy mousse
- Dry versions are savoury and tart

## Pairs with
- Prosciutto
- Parmigiano Reggiano
- Tortellini
- Pizza

## Good to know
- Dry Lambrusco is a classic match for rich Emilian food.
- Many different Lambrusco grape varieties exist.
