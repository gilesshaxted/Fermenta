# Natural wine

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Wine made with minimal intervention in vineyard and cellar, with no set legal definition.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Category:** Still
> - **Colour:** Varies
> - **Sweetness:** Varies
> - **Country:** Various
> - **Main grapes:** [[Gamay]], [[Chenin Blanc]], [[Poulsard]], [[Trousseau]], [[Savagnin]]
> - **Regions:** [[Loire Valley]], [[Jura]], [[Beaujolais]], [[Languedoc]], [[Friuli-Venezia Giulia]]

## How it's made
Grapes are usually farmed organically or biodynamically and fermented with wild yeast. Producers add little or no sulphur and avoid fining and filtering. Results vary from brilliant to faulty.

## Where it's made
Strongholds include the Loire, Jura, Beaujolais, Languedoc and Friuli, but producers are found worldwide.

## What it tastes like
- Lively, sometimes cloudy and funky
- Fresh fruit with yeasty, cider like notes

## Pairs with
- Charcuterie
- Shared plates
- Casual food

## Good to know
- There is no legal definition in most countries.
- Pét-nat and orange wine are often natural.
