# Graves

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The wider gravelly area south of Bordeaux, making soft reds and good-value dry whites, plus some sweet wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Bordeaux]]
> - **Climate:** Maritime
> - **Soils:** gravel, sand, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Sémillon]]

## Landscape and topography
Gently rolling land along the left bank of the Garonne, from the edge of Bordeaux city to Langon.

## Soil and geology
Gravelly soils (graves means gravel) over sand and clay, with limestone in places.

## Climate
Maritime, with mild winters and warm summers.

## History
One of the oldest vineyard areas in Bordeaux. Pessac-Léognan was split off in 1987, leaving Graves as the wider, more everyday appellation.

## Production and wine styles
Red and dry white both, plus Graves Supérieures for sweet whites. Grapes are the same as elsewhere in Bordeaux.

## Key wines
- Graves rouge
- Graves blanc
- Graves Supérieures (sweet)

## What to look for
- Good-value dry whites and relaxed, Merlot-friendly reds.
- A step below Pessac-Léognan in price and ambition, and often a bargain.
