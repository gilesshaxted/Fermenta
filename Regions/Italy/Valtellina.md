# Valtellina

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Steep terraced Alpine slopes making a lighter, finer Nebbiolo, locally called Chiavennasca.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Lombardy]]
> - **Climate:** Alpine
> - **Soils:** granite, gneiss, schist
> - **Main grapes:** [[Nebbiolo]]

## Landscape and topography
Very steep terraces held by dry-stone walls on the sunny north side of the Adda valley, between Sondrio and Tirano.

## Soil and geology
Granite, gneiss and schist, with poor, stony soils.

## Climate
Alpine, but the south-facing terraces trap warmth.

## History
The Romans may have planted here, and the terraces are the result of centuries of labour. The terraced landscape is protected.

## Production and wine styles
Nebbiolo-based wines: Valtellina Superiore (with sub-zones such as Sassella, Grumello, Inferno, Valgella) and Sforzato (Sfursat), a dried-grape red.

## Key wines
- Valtellina Superiore
- Sassella
- Grumello
- Inferno
- Sforzato di Valtellina

## What to look for
- Pale, perfumed and savoury.
- Sforzato is the Alpine Amarone.
