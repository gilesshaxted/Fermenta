# Plešivica

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cool hill region west of Zagreb, known for fresh whites and traditional method sparkling wine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Croatia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** clay, marl, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Riesling]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Welschriesling]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills and small valleys in the Plešivica area south west of Zagreb, with vineyards on slopes.

## Soil and geology
Clay, marl and limestone.

## Climate
Cooler continental than the coast, with good acidity.

## History
Part of Croatia's northern, continental wine region, with modern winemakers focusing on sparkling and aromatic wines.

## Production and wine styles
Sauvignon Blanc, Riesling, Chardonnay and Pinot Noir, with traditional method sparkling wines a growing speciality.

## Key wines
- Plešivica Sauvignon Blanc
- Plešivica sparkling
- Plešivica Riesling

## What to look for
- Cool-climate whites and sparkling wine.
- Local grape Škrlet is worth seeking out.
