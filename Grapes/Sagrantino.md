# Sagrantino

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of the most tannic grapes in the world, grown only around Montefalco.

## Where it's from
Umbria, around Montefalco.

## Where it grows
- **Montefalco, Umbria:** almost entirely here

## What it tastes like
- Blackberry, tar and powerful tannin

## Classic wines
- Montefalco Sagrantino
- Sagrantino Passito

## Pairs with
- Roast meat
- Game
- Aged pecorino

## Good to know
- Needs years in the cellar.
- Nearly extinct in the 1960s.
