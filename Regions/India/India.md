# India

Part of [[Asia]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A young but growing wine country, with vineyards on high plateaus in the west and south that avoid the heat of the lowlands.

## How wine is organised here
- India has no formal appellation system for wine, so labels name the producer, grape or blend, and sometimes the state.
- The main areas are Maharashtra, around Nashik, and Karnataka, around Bangalore.
- Vineyards work around the monsoon, with pruning and harvest timed to the dry season.
