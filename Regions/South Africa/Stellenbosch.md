# Stellenbosch

> [!abstract] At a glance
> South Africa's best-known wine district, with mountain-backed vineyards and its great Cabernet-led reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[South Africa]]
> - **Part of:** [[Africa]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** granite, sandstone, shale, clay, decomposed granite
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Syrah]], [[Merlot]], [[Chenin Blanc]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]]
> - **Styles:** [[Bordeaux blend]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling hills and mountain slopes around the university town, with the Simonsberg, Helderberg and Stellenbosch mountains.

## Soil and geology
Ancient decomposed granite, sandstone and shale, with clay-rich soils in places.

## Climate
Mediterranean: dry, warm summers and wet winters, with sea breezes from False Bay cooling the vineyards.

## History
Founded in 1679 by Simon van der Stel, the second oldest European settlement in South Africa. Many estates date from the 17th and 18th centuries.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon and Bordeaux-style blends, Syrah, Pinotage, Chenin Blanc and Sauvignon Blanc.

## Key wines
- Stellenbosch Cabernet Sauvignon
- Stellenbosch red blends
- Stellenbosch Chenin Blanc

## What to look for
- Ripe fruit, firm structure and ageing potential.
- Higher, cooler sites such as Jonkershoek are finer.
