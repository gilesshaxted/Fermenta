# Domaine des Roches Neuves

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A biodynamic Saumur domaine that has helped lift the profile of Saumur-Champigny and Saumur Blanc.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Saumur]], [[Loire Valley]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Chenin Blanc]]

## About
Roches Neuves makes fresh, precise reds from Cabernet Franc and textured whites from Chenin Blanc. The wines are pure, aromatic and carefully made.
