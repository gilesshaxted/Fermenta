# Troodos

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The mountain heartland of Cyprus, with high, cooler vineyards on volcanic and limestone slopes.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Cyprus]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** volcanic, limestone, chalk, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Xynisteri]], [[Mavro]], [[Maratheftiko]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Syrah]]

## Landscape and topography
Steep mountain slopes in the Troodos range, with vineyards often between about 600 and 1,400 metres, among the highest in Europe.

## Soil and geology
Volcanic and limestone-rich soils, with chalk and clay in places.

## Climate
Mediterranean with cool nights from altitude, giving fresher wines than the coast.

## History
Wine has been made on Cyprus for thousands of years. Many of the old bush vines survived on the mountain slopes and are now prized.

## Production and wine styles
Mostly Xynisteri whites and a mix of local and international reds. Mavro and old bush vines are still found in many hillside plots.

## Key wines
- Troodos Xynisteri
- Troodos Maratheftiko
- Troodos Cabernet Sauvignon

## What to look for
- Fresh whites with mountain acidity.
- Old vines on high slopes.
