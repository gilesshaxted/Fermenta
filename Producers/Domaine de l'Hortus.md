# Domaine de l'Hortus

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A leading Pic Saint-Loup estate, known for fresh, peppery reds and good whites.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Pic Saint-Loup]]

## About
A family domaine in a cool part of the Languedoc beneath the Pic Saint-Loup. The reds are lively and savoury with good structure.
