# Giacomo Conterno

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A benchmark traditional Barolo house in Monforte d'Alba, famous for its long-lived Monfortino.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Barolo]], [[Piedmont]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Nebbiolo]], [[Barbera]]

## About
A family estate that makes Barolo in the classic style, with long macerations and large old oak casks. The wines are firm and profound when young and reward decades of patience.

## Known for
- Barolo Monfortino Riserva
- Barolo Cascina Francia
- Barbera d'Alba Cascina Francia
