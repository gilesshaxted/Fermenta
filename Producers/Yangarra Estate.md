# Yangarra Estate

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A biodynamic McLaren Vale estate that has made old vine Grenache its calling card.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Australia]]
> - **Region:** [[McLaren Vale]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grenache]], [[Syrah]], [[Roussanne]]
> - **Styles:** [[GSM]]

## About
A single estate winery farming biodynamically, with concrete and other neutral vessels in the cellar. The wines are lifted, perfumed and silky rather than heavy.

## Known for
- High Sands Grenache
- Ironheart Shiraz
- Roussanne
- Old Vine Grenache
