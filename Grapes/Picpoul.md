# Picpoul

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A fresh, lemony white from the Languedoc coast, known for oysters.

## Where it's from
A Languedoc native whose name means lip stinger. It is grown around the Étang de Thau.

## Where it grows
- **Picpoul de Pinet, France:** the home

## What it tastes like
- Lemon, lime, salt and white flowers

## Classic wines
- Picpoul de Pinet

## Pairs with
- Oysters
- Shellfish
- Grilled fish

## Good to know
- Also spelt Piquepoul.
- Drink young.
