# Greco Bianco

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Calabrian grape used for the sweet wine of Bianco and Greco di Bianco.

## Where it's from
Grown on the Ionian coast of Calabria, where grapes are dried for sweet passito wines.

## Where it grows
- **Bianco, Calabria:** the home of Greco di Bianco

## What it tastes like
- Apricot, honey, orange peel and spice

## Classic wines
- Greco di Bianco

## Pairs with
- Dried fruit
- Pastries
- Blue cheese

## Good to know
- Different from Greco di Tufo.
- A rare and delicious dessert wine.
