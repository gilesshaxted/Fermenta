# Château Haut-Brion

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The only non-Médoc First Growth of 1855, known for smoky, earthy, gravelly reds in Pessac-Léognan.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Pessac-Léognan]], [[Bordeaux]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Franc]]
> - **Styles:** [[Bordeaux blend]]

## About
Haut-Brion lies on gravel just outside the city of Bordeaux. The red is earthy, smoky and refined, and the estate also produces a rare dry white from Sémillon and Sauvignon Blanc.
