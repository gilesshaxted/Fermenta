# Hessische Bergstrasse

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Germany's smallest wine region, a warm, sunny strip along the foot of the Odenwald.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Germany]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Temperate
> - **Soils:** loess, granite, sandstone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]], [[Pinot Gris]]

## Landscape and topography
A narrow strip on the western edge of the Odenwald hills, near Heppenheim.

## Soil and geology
Loess, granite and sandstone.

## Climate
Warm and mild, known for early spring.

## History
Hessische Bergstrasse is known for its mild climate, with almonds in bloom.

## Production and wine styles
Riesling, Grauburgunder and other whites.

## Key wines
- Bergstrasse Riesling

## What to look for
- Light, fresh whites.
- Tiny and mostly drunk locally.
