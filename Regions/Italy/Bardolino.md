# Bardolino

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Light, cherry-red wines from the eastern shore of Lake Garda.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Veneto]]
> - **Climate:** Temperate
> - **Soils:** moraine, gravel, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Corvina]], [[Rondinella]], [[Molinara]]

## Landscape and topography
Gentle hills of glacial moraine on the eastern shore of Lake Garda.

## Soil and geology
Gravel and moraine deposits left by glaciers.

## Climate
Temperate, with a mild lake climate.

## History
Bardolino has a long history as a light, local wine for summer drinking, and rosé Chiaretto is increasingly fashionable.

## Production and wine styles
Corvina-led reds and Chiaretto rosé.

## Key wines
- Bardolino
- Bardolino Chiaretto
- Bardolino Classico

## What to look for
- Cherry, pepper and soft tannin.
- Serve lightly chilled.
