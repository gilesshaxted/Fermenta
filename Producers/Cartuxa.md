# Cartuxa

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The Évora estate behind Pêra-Manca, one of Portugal's most celebrated wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Portugal]]

## About
Cartuxa is run by the Eugénio de Almeida Foundation and is a historic Alentejo name. The wines are traditional, with a focus on long ageing.
