# Chinon

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Cabernet Franc country: raspberry and pencil-lead reds, some made to be enjoyed slightly chilled.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Loire Valley]]
> - **Climate:** Maritime
> - **Soils:** gravel, tuffeau, clay, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Chenin Blanc]]

## Landscape and topography
Gentle slopes and river terraces along the Vienne near its confluence with the Loire, beside the historic town of Chinon.

## Soil and geology
Gravel and sand on the river terraces, and tuffeau limestone with clay on the slopes.

## Climate
Maritime. The gravel terraces tend to give lighter, earlier-drinking wines and the slopes give more structure.

## History
Rabelais was born nearby. Chinon is linked to Joan of Arc, who met Charles VII here in 1429, and to Henry II of England, who died at the castle.

## Production and wine styles
Mainly Cabernet Franc, with a little Cabernet Sauvignon allowed, plus a small amount of Chenin Blanc for white. Neighbouring Bourgueil and Saint-Nicolas-de-Bourgueil are similar.

## Key wines
- Chinon rouge
- Bourgueil
- Saint-Nicolas-de-Bourgueil
- Chinon blanc

## What to look for
- Gravel terrace wines are fresh and light, slope wines firmer and longer-lived.
- A superb food wine, particularly with roast pork, chicken or charcuterie.
