# Mittelhaardt

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The heart of the Pfalz, with famous villages such as Forst, Deidesheim and Ruppertsberg.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Germany]]
> - **Part of:** [[Pfalz]]
> - **Climate:** Temperate
> - **Soils:** limestone, sandstone, basalt, loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]]

## Landscape and topography
A stretch of foothills at the base of the Haardt, with sheltered, sunny sites.

## Soil and geology
Sandstone, limestone and clay, with a famous basalt outcrop in Forst.

## Climate
Warm and dry, with the forest slopes sheltering vines.

## History
A cluster of historic estates such as Dr. Bürklin-Wolf and von Buhl, whose wines were once among Germany's most expensive.

## Production and wine styles
Riesling, both dry and sweet.

## Key wines
- Forst Kirchenstück
- Deidesheim Kieselberg
- Ruppertsberg Gaisböhl

## What to look for
- Rich, spicy Riesling with great depth.
- Often reaches Grosses Gewächs quality.
