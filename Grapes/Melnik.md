# Melnik

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A local red grape from the far south-west of Bulgaria, named after the town of Melnik.

## Where it's from
Grown around Melnik and the Struma Valley, where it ripens in the heat of the south. Its full name is Shiroka Melnishka Loza, meaning broad-leaved vine of Melnik.

## Where it grows
- **Struma Valley, Bulgaria:** the only real heartland

## What it tastes like
- Dark cherry, spice, herbs and tobacco

## Classic wines
- Melnik wines

## Pairs with
- Grilled meats
- Lamb
- Roast vegetables

## Good to know
- Also called Shiroka Melnishka Loza.
- Thin-skinned, so it needs care in the vineyard.
