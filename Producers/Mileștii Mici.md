# Mileștii Mici

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A huge underground wine cellar near Chișinău, famous for its enormous collection of bottles.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Moldova]]
> - **Region:** [[Codru]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Fetească Neagră]]

## About
Housed in old limestone mine tunnels in the Codru region, it works as both a wine producer and a vast cellar of stored wines. The range covers everyday wines and aged reserve reds.

## Known for
- Mileștii Mici Cabernet Sauvignon
- Mileștii Mici Fetească Neagră
