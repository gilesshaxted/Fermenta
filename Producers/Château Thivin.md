# Château Thivin

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A leading Côte de Brouilly estate, working the volcanic slopes of Mont Brouilly.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Beaujolais Crus]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Gamay]]

## About
A family domaine on the hill of Mont Brouilly, with old vines on blue stone soils. The wines are fresh, mineral and can age well.
