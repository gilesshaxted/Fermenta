# Kremstal

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Between the Wachau and Kamptal, with rich Grüner Veltliner on loess and Riesling on primary rock.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Austria]]
> - **Part of:** [[Lower Austria]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** loess, gneiss, conglomerate
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grüner Veltliner]], [[Riesling]]

## Landscape and topography
Terraces and hills around Krems, on both banks of the Danube.

## Soil and geology
Loess and gneiss, with conglomerate soils on Kremstal's slopes.

## Climate
A mix of warm Pannonian and cool northern influences.

## History
The town of Krems and the Göttweig abbey have long been wine centres.

## Production and wine styles
Grüner Veltliner and Riesling, with Kremstal DAC introduced in 2007.

## Key wines
- Kremstal DAC
- Kremstal Reserve

## What to look for
- Full-bodied, spicy whites.
- Often a bargain compared with the Wachau.
