# East Anglia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A dry, flat eastern region of Norfolk and Suffolk, producing aromatic whites and light reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[England]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** clay, chalk, sand, gravel
> - **Main grapes:** [[Bacchus]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Seyval Blanc]], [[Solaris]]

## Landscape and topography
Flat, open landscapes, with low hills and sheltered valleys in Suffolk and Norfolk.

## Soil and geology
Clay, sand, gravel and chalk.

## Climate
Dry and sunny, though cooler and windier at the coast, so site selection is important.

## History
Many small, family-run vineyards have been planted over the past fifty years. The area is England's driest.

## Production and wine styles
Aromatic whites such as Bacchus, plus sparkling wine and light Pinot Noir.

## Key wines
- Norfolk Bacchus
- Suffolk sparkling

## What to look for
- Elderflower and citrus in whites.
- Small producers are worth hunting out.
