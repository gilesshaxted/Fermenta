# Budimir Winery

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A quality-led winery in Župa, closely tied to the revival of Prokupac.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Serbia]]
> - **Region:** [[Župa]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Prokupac]]

## About
Based near Aleksandrovac in the Župa region, this is a family estate that works with local grapes as well as international varieties. The reds are structured and the style leans towards careful oak ageing.
