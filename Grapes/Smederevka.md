# Smederevka

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A high-yielding white grape widely planted in Serbia and North Macedonia, producing simple, fresh wines.

## Where it's from
Named after the town of Smederevo on the Danube, and a widely planted white in the Balkans.

## Where it grows
- **Smederevo, Serbia:** the heartland
- **Povardarie, North Macedonia:** widely planted

## What it tastes like
- Green apple, lemon, herbs and mineral

## Classic wines
- Smederevka

## Pairs with
- Salads
- Light fish
- Fresh cheese

## Good to know
- Mostly simple, everyday wine.
- Very common across the Balkans.
