# Valle d'Aosta

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Italy's smallest region, with some of Europe's highest vineyards and local grapes found nowhere else.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Alpine
> - **Soils:** granite, gneiss, moraine
> - **Main grapes:** [[Petit Rouge]], [[Prié Blanc]], [[Nebbiolo]], [[Pinot Noir]]

## Landscape and topography
Steep Alpine valley slopes under Mont Blanc and the Matterhorn, with vineyards at up to 1,200 metres.

## Soil and geology
Granite, gneiss and glacial moraine.

## Climate
Alpine, with cold winters and sunny summers.

## History
Prié Blanc grows at some of Europe's highest vineyards, and the wines are mostly drunk locally.

## Production and wine styles
Small-scale production of local varieties and international grapes. Cooperatives dominate.

## Key wines
- Valle d'Aosta Petit Rouge
- Blanc de Morgex et de La Salle
- Torrette

## What to look for
- Fresh, mineral whites and light reds.
- Rare outside the region.
