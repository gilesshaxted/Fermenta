# Domaine Camin Larredya

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small Jurançon domaine farmed organically and known for expressive, characterful dry and sweet whites.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Jurançon]], [[South-West France]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Petit Manseng]], [[Gros Manseng]], [[Petit Courbu]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]]

## About
Camin Larredya is a family estate high in the hills of Jurançon, working with native varieties and low intervention methods. The wines are sappy and textured and quite different from the sweeter style most people expect.

## Known for
- Au Capcéu
- La Virada
