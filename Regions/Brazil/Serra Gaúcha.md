# Serra Gaúcha

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Brazil's largest and most important wine region, in the south, known for sparkling wine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Brazil]]
> - **Part of:** [[South America]]
> - **Climate:** Humid subtropical
> - **Soils:** basalt, clay, sandstone, loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Merlot]], [[Tannat]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Muscat]], [[Pinot Noir]]

## Landscape and topography
Hilly highlands in Rio Grande do Sul, settled by Italian immigrants, with small family vineyards.

## Soil and geology
Basalt and sandstone based soils, with clay and loam.

## Climate
Humid and rainy in summer, so growing is challenging. Cool nights and altitude help.

## History
Italian settlers arrived in the late nineteenth century and planted vines. The region grew to produce most of the country's wine.

## Production and wine styles
Sparkling wines (Espumante) and Moscatel, plus still reds and whites.

## Key wines
- Espumante
- Moscatel espumante
- Serra Gaúcha Merlot

## What to look for
- Most of Brazil's wine comes from here.
- Sparkling wine is the standout style.
