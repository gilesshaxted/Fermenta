# Braucol

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A South-West French red, also known as Fer Servadou, with a distinctive peppery edge.

## Where it's from
Known as Braucol in Gaillac and Fer Servadou in Marcillac and Béarn. A family of related grapes.

## Where it grows
- **Gaillac, France:** as Braucol
- **Marcillac, France:** as Mansois

## What it tastes like
- Red berries, pepper, herbs and spice

## Classic wines
- Gaillac Rouge
- Marcillac

## Pairs with
- Duck
- Lamb
- Cassoulet

## Good to know
- Needs warmth to ripen.
- Fresh and rustic.
