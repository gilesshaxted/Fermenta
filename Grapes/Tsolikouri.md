# Tsolikouri

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A fresh, light white of western Georgia, the main grape of Imereti.

## Where it's from
A white grape widely grown in Imereti and neighbouring areas, and sometimes made in a qvevri.

## Where it grows
- **Imereti, Georgia:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Lemon, green apple, white flowers and herbs

## Classic wines
- Imereti Tsolikouri
- Tvishi

## Pairs with
- Fish
- Salads
- Soft cheese

## Good to know
- Often blended with Krakhuna.
- Fresh and crisp.
