# Naoussa

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Greece's answer to Barolo: Xinomavro on cool hillsides, tannic, acidic and long-lived.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Greece]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Xinomavro]]

## Landscape and topography
Terraced slopes on Mount Vermio, at 150 to 350 metres, in northern Greece.

## Soil and geology
Clay and limestone, with sandy soils.

## Climate
Continental, with cold winters, warm summers and good drainage, with the mountain adding cooler nights.

## History
Naoussa was among the first Greek appellations, created in the early 1970s. Boutari and Thymiopoulos helped it to become recognised outside Greece.

## Production and wine styles
100% Xinomavro (sour black). Wines are aged and tannic, with red fruit and tomato, olive and spice.

## Key wines
- Naoussa Xinomavro
- Naoussa Reserve

## What to look for
- Red cherry, dried tomato, olive and spice.
- Often compared with Nebbiolo.
