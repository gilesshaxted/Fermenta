# Pinotage

> [!abstract] At a glance
> South Africa's own grape, a cross of Pinot Noir and Cinsault, making dark, smoky reds.

## Where it's from
Created in 1925 by Abraham Izak Perold in Stellenbosch, as a cross of Pinot Noir and Cinsault (then called Hermitage).

## Where it grows
- **Stellenbosch and Paarl, South Africa:** the traditional heartland
- **Swartland, South Africa:** old bush vines with fresher styles

## What it tastes like
- Blackberry, plum, smoke and spice
- Older styles can show banana or acetone notes

## Classic wines
- Pinotage
- Cape Blend

## Pairs with
- Barbecue
- Braai
- Spicy sausages

## Good to know
- Quality improved sharply with better viticulture and gentler winemaking.
- Cape Blend usually includes Pinotage.
