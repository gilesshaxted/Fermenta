# Testalonga

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cult natural winemaker in the Swartland, known for skin-contact Chenin Blanc.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[South Africa]]
> - **Region:** [[Swartland]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chenin Blanc]], [[Cinsault]], [[Syrah]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]], [[Orange wine]]

## About
A small producer working with old vines and very little added. The wines are textured, cloudy and playful, and pioneered the Cape natural scene.

- El Bandito
- Baby Bandito
- Cortez Chenin Blanc
