# Cave de Pomerols

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A major cooperative in Picpoul de Pinet, producing a large share of the appellation's crisp white wine.

> [!info] Quick facts

## About
A cooperative cellar near the Bassin de Thau making fresh, zesty Picpoul that is designed to go with shellfish. The wines are easy and reliable.
