# Pinela

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A rare, fresh white grape from the Vipava Valley, almost unknown outside the area.

## Where it's from
Native to the Vipava Valley, where it has been grown for a long time and revived by local growers.

## Where it grows
- **Vipava Valley, Slovenia:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Green apple, lemon, pear and almond

## Classic wines
- Vipavska Dolina Pinela

## Pairs with
- Air-dried ham
- Freshwater fish
- Light starters

## Good to know
- Rarely seen outside Slovenia.
- Often fresh with a slightly bitter almond finish.
