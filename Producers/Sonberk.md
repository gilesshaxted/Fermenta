# Sonberk

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A quality estate in the Pálava hills near Mikulov, known for crisp, mineral whites.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Czechia]]
> - **Region:** [[Mikulov]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pálava]], [[Riesling]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]]

## About
The vineyards are on limestone slopes in south Moravia and the winery is modern. The style is fresh and precise, with a focus on Pálava and Riesling.
