# COS

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A pioneering biodynamic estate in Vittoria, famous for its work with clay amphorae.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Vittoria]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Frappato]], [[Nero d'Avola]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]], [[Orange wine]]

## About
Founded by three friends and farmed biodynamically. Many wines are fermented and aged in terracotta amphorae, and are fresh, savoury and very drinkable.

## Known for
- Cerasuolo di Vittoria
- Pithos Rosso
- Pithos Bianco
- Nero di Lupo
