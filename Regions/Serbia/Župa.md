# Župa

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A central Serbian area around Aleksandrovac, known for Prokupac, the national red grape.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Serbia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** clay, sand, gravel
> - **Main grapes:** [[Prokupac]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills around Aleksandrovac below the Jastrebac mountains, with vineyards at about 300 to 400 metres.

## Soil and geology
Clay, sand and gravel.

## Climate
Moderate continental, with warm summers and cold winters.

## History
Župa is one of the oldest Serbian wine areas and is where Prokupac is mainly grown. It has been the centre of the country's reds for generations.

## Production and wine styles
Reds from Prokupac, plus Pinot Noir, Merlot and Cabernet Sauvignon, and some whites.

## Key wines
- Župa Prokupac
- Župa Aleksandrovac

## What to look for
- Prokupac is light to medium, with red cherry and spice.
- Some old vine plots remain.
