# Rutherglen Muscat

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Australia's intensely sweet fortified wine from sun ripened Muscat.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Category:** Fortified
> - **Colour:** Varies
> - **Sweetness:** Sweet
> - **Country:** Australia
> - **Main grapes:** [[Muscat]], [[Muscadelle]]
> - **Regions:** [[Rutherglen]]

## How it's made
Late harvested grapes are fermented briefly, fortified, then aged in old barrels in warm sheds. Classification rises from Rutherglen to Classic, Grand and Rare as ageing and complexity increase.

## Where it's made
Rutherglen in north east Victoria, with a warm continental climate.

## What it tastes like
- Raisin, toffee, orange peel
- Roasted nut, tea
- Thick and luscious

## Pairs with
- Sticky toffee pudding
- Blue cheese
- Vanilla ice cream

## Good to know
- Topaque, formerly Tokay, is made from Muscadelle.
- Rare grade wines are a blend of very old stock.
