# Mittelburgenland

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Blaufränkisch country: Austria's 'Blaufränkischland', with deep, spicy reds on heavy clay.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Austria]]
> - **Part of:** [[Burgenland]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** loam, clay, loess, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Blaufränkisch]]

## Landscape and topography
A rolling basin around Deutschkreutz and Horitschon, with Blaufränkisch dominant.

## Soil and geology
Loam, clay and loess, which suit Blaufränkisch.

## Climate
Warm and dry Pannonian, with cooler nights.

## History
Locals call the area Blaufränkischland. Mittelburgenland DAC requires Blaufränkisch.

## Production and wine styles
Blaufränkisch in styles from fruity Klassik to oak-aged Reserve.

## Key wines
- Mittelburgenland DAC
- Mittelburgenland Reserve

## What to look for
- Black cherry, pepper and spice.
- Fine-grained tannin and fresh acidity.

- [[Weingut Gesellmann]]
