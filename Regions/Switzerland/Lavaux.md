# Lavaux

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A UNESCO World Heritage landscape of steep stone terraces above the northern shore of Lake Geneva, planted almost entirely to Chasselas.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Switzerland]]
> - **Part of:** [[Vaud]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** limestone, molasse, clay, moraine
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chasselas]]

## Landscape and topography
Terraces rise steeply from the lake to a few hundred metres, held up by stone walls built over many centuries.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, molasse sandstone and clay, with moraine deposits in places.

## Climate
The lake, the sun and the heat stored by the stone walls give a warm, bright growing season.

## History
Lavaux was inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage site in 2007. Its terraces were largely built by monks from the Middle Ages onward.

## Production and wine styles
Almost all white, from Chasselas. The Dézaley and Calamin grand crus are the most celebrated.

## Key wines
- Dézaley Grand Cru
- Calamin Grand Cru
- Epesses
- Saint-Saphorin

## What to look for
- Mineral, ageworthy Chasselas.
- Dézaley and Calamin are the benchmarks.
