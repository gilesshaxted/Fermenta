# Irouléguy

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A tiny Basque vineyard on steep Pyrenean slopes making sturdy reds and delicate whites.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[South-West France]]
> - **Climate:** Maritime
> - **Soils:** sandstone, clay, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tannat]], [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Petit Courbu]], [[Gros Manseng]]

## Landscape and topography
Steep, terraced slopes in the foothills of the Basque Pyrenees near Saint-Jean-Pied-de-Port.

## Soil and geology
Red sandstone and clay, with limestone in places.

## Climate
Maritime, with rain from the Atlantic and warm foehn winds.

## History
Pilgrims on the Camino de Santiago passed through here. The Basque cooperative and small domaines keep the tradition alive.

## Production and wine styles
Tannat and Cabernet Franc for reds and rosés, and Petit Courbu and Manseng for whites.

## Key wines
- Irouléguy rouge
- Irouléguy blanc

## What to look for
- Peppery, rustic reds and aromatic whites.
- Excellent with Basque food like piperade and Bayonne ham.
