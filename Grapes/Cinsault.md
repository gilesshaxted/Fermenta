# Cinsault

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Light, perfumed and fruity. A key grape for Provence rosé.

## Where it's from
A Southern French grape, long planted in Provence, Languedoc and the Rhône. It was crossed with Pinot Noir to make Pinotage in South Africa.

## Where it grows
- **Provence, France:** a main grape for pale rosé
- **Languedoc, France:** soft blending partner
- **Swartland, South Africa:** old bush vines make light, vibrant reds

## What it tastes like
- **As rosé:** strawberry, citrus and a floral edge
- **As red:** red cherry, spice and soft tannin

## Classic wines
- Côtes de Provence rosé
- Châteauneuf-du-Pape blends
- Tavel
- Swartland Cinsault

## Pairs with
- Salade niçoise
- Grilled fish
- Charcuterie

## Good to know
- Heat and drought tolerant.
- Yields need to be kept low for quality.
