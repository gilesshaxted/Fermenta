# Murfatlar

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A warm, sunny area near the Black Sea known for rich wines and late-harvest dessert wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Romania]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** limestone, chalk, loess
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Fetească Neagră]], [[Muscat Ottonel]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling hills and plateau close to the Black Sea coast, in Dobrogea.

## Soil and geology
Chalky limestone and loess.

## Climate
Warm, sunny and dry, moderated by the Black Sea.

## History
Murfatlar is one of Romania's historic wine areas, with plantings dating back centuries. Ancient Greek colonies planted vines on the coast.

## Production and wine styles
Chardonnay, Pinot Noir, Cabernet and Fetească Neagră, plus sweet, late-harvest wines.

## Key wines
- Murfatlar Chardonnay
- Murfatlar late harvest

## What to look for
- Ripe fruit and sweetness.
- Great dessert wines.
