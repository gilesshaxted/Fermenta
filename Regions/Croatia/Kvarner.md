# Kvarner

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The northern Adriatic coast and islands, home to the white Žlahtina of Vrbnik on the island of Krk.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Croatia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, karst, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Žlahtina]], [[Malvasia]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
Islands and coast around the Kvarner Gulf, with small vineyards on rocky slopes.

## Soil and geology
Limestone and karst, with some clay.

## Climate
Mediterranean, cooled by sea breezes and the bora wind.

## History
Vrbnik on Krk is the main centre for wine, with Žlahtina the island's native white.

## Production and wine styles
Mostly fresh white wines from Žlahtina, plus Malvasia and some reds.

## Key wines
- Vrbnička Žlahtina
- Krk white

## What to look for
- Light, fresh whites for seafood.
- A tiny region with its own character.
