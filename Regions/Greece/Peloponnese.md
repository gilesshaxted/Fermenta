# Peloponnese

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A large peninsula and Greece's largest wine area, home to Nemea, Mantinia and sweet Mavrodaphne of Patras.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Greece]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, sand, schist
> - **Main grapes:** [[Agiorgitiko]], [[Moschofilero]], [[Mavrodaphne]], [[Roditis]], [[Assyrtiko]]

## Landscape and topography
Mountains, plains and hills, with vineyards rising to 800 metres or more.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, clay and sandy soils.

## Climate
Mediterranean, with cooler mountain areas.

## History
The Peloponnese has been a wine land since antiquity. Patras is known for the sweet, fortified Mavrodaphne.

## Production and wine styles
Reds from Agiorgitiko and Mavrodaphne, whites from Moschofilero and Roditis.

## Key wines
- Nemea
- Mantinia Moschofilero
- Mavrodaphne of Patras
- Patras Roditis

## What to look for
- Plenty of variety.
- A good place to explore.
