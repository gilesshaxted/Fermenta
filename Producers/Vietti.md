# Vietti

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Castiglione Falletto estate known for elegant Barolo and for championing Arneis.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Barolo]], [[Roero]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Nebbiolo]], [[Barbera]], [[Arneis]]

## About
Vietti makes a range of single vineyard Barolos alongside Barbera and the white Arneis from Roero. The house style is refined, with fine tannins and good perfume.

## Known for
- Barolo Castiglione
- Barolo Rocche di Castiglione
- Barolo Ravera
- Roero Arneis
