# Vranac

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The signature dark red of the Balkans, deeply coloured and tannic, called Vranec in North Macedonia.

## Where it's from
A Balkan grape that is grown in North Macedonia and Montenegro. The name means black stallion.

## Where it grows
- **Tikveš, North Macedonia:** the heartland
- **Montenegro:** also the national red

## What it tastes like
- Blackberry, plum, spice and earth

## Classic wines
- Tikveš Vranec

## Pairs with
- Grilled meat
- Lamb
- Hard cheese

## Good to know
- Called Vranec in North Macedonia.
- The name means black stallion.
