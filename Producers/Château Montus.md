# Château Montus

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The flagship estate of Alain Brumont, defining powerful, polished Madiran from Tannat.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Madiran]], [[South-West France]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tannat]], [[Cabernet Franc]]

## About
Château Montus and its neighbour Château Bouscassé helped to modernise Madiran, taming Tannat's tannins with careful vineyard work and ageing. The wines are deep, dark and long lived.
