# Mukuzani

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A red wine appellation in Kakheti, made from Saperavi and aged in oak.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Georgia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Kakheti]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** alluvial, clay, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Saperavi]]

## Landscape and topography
Vineyards on gentle slopes on the right bank of the Alazani river, around the village of Mukuzani.

## Soil and geology
Alluvial soils with clay and limestone.

## Climate
Warm continental with cold winters.

## History
Mukuzani is one of the best known Georgian appellations for dry red wine, and was set up for Saperavi.

## Production and wine styles
Dry reds made from Saperavi, typically aged in oak barrels.

## Key wines
- Mukuzani

## What to look for
- Dark, structured red with good ageing potential.
- Compare with qvevri Saperavi.
