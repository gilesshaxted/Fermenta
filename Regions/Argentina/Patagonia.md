# Patagonia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cooler, windy southern region with some of the world's southernmost vineyards, making fresh Pinot Noir and Malbec.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Argentina]]
> - **Part of:** [[South America]]
> - **Climate:** Cool continental
> - **Soils:** alluvial, sand, gravel
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Malbec]], [[Merlot]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Sémillon]]

## Landscape and topography
Wide river valleys in Río Negro and Neuquén, with vines among desert scrub.

## Soil and geology
Sandy and stony alluvium.

## Climate
Cool, dry and windy, with a short season and strong sun.

## History
Italian immigrants planted vines in the early 20th century, and new investment arrived in the 1990s.

## Production and wine styles
Pinot Noir, Malbec, Merlot, Sauvignon Blanc and Sémillon.

## Key wines
- Patagonia Pinot Noir
- Patagonia Malbec

## What to look for
- Fresh, elegant styles.
- Wine tourism is growing.
