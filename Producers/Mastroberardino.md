# Mastroberardino

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The historic Campania house most associated with Taurasi and the revival of ancient local grapes.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Taurasi]], [[Campania]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Aglianico]], [[Fiano]], [[Greco]]

## About
A long established family estate in Avellino province. The reds are firm and age worthy, and the whites show the texture of Fiano and Greco.

## Known for
- Taurasi Radici
- Taurasi Naturalis Historia
- Fiano di Avellino Radici
- Greco di Tufo Nova Serra
