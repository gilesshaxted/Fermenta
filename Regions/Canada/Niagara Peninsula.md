# Niagara Peninsula

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Canada's largest wine region, moderated by Lake Ontario and famous for icewine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Canada]]
> - **Part of:** [[North America]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, loam, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Vidal]], [[Merlot]], [[Gamay]]
> - **Styles:** [[Icewine and Eiswein]]

## Landscape and topography
A low plain between Lake Ontario and Lake Erie, bordered by the Niagara Escarpment to the south.

## Soil and geology
Clay, limestone, loam and sand, with benches along the escarpment.

## Climate
Cool continental, with lakes that moderate winters and cool summer nights. Reliably cold winters allow icewine.

## History
Modern Ontario winemaking began with the Inniskillin estate in the 1970s. Canadian icewine rose to global fame after a gold medal in Bordeaux in 1991.

## Production and wine styles
Icewine from Vidal, Riesling and Cabernet Franc. Table wines from Chardonnay, Riesling, Pinot Noir and Cabernet Franc.

## Key wines
- Vidal Icewine
- Riesling Icewine
- Cabernet Franc Icewine
- Niagara Chardonnay
- Niagara Riesling

## What to look for
- Icewine is the signature style.
- Dry Riesling and Cabernet Franc are also excellent.
