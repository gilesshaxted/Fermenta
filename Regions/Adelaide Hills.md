# Adelaide Hills

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cool, high region close to Adelaide, making refined Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc, Pinot Noir and sparkling wine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Australia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Oceania]]
> - **Climate:** Cool continental
> - **Soils:** clay, loam, ironstone, quartz
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Syrah]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills and valleys at 400 to 700 metres in the Mount Lofty Ranges.

## Soil and geology
Clay, loam and ironstone soils.

## Climate
Cool, with higher rainfall and altitude.

## History
Modern plantings began in the 1970s, and the region rose rapidly in the 1990s.

## Production and wine styles
Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc, Pinot Noir and sparkling wine, plus cool-climate Syrah.

## Key wines
- Adelaide Hills Chardonnay
- Adelaide Hills Sauvignon Blanc

## What to look for
- Crisp, elegant whites.
- Lenswood and Piccadilly Valley are notable sub-zones.

## Producers
- [[Ochota Barrels]]
- [[Shaw + Smith]]
