# Nero di Troia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A dark, tannic red of northern Puglia, with strong ageing potential.

## Where it's from
Grown around Foggia and in the Castel del Monte area. Also called Uva di Troia.

## Where it grows
- **Northern Puglia, Italy:** Castel del Monte and Cerignola

## What it tastes like
- Black cherry, violet, spice and tobacco

## Classic wines
- Castel del Monte Rosso
- Nero di Troia

## Pairs with
- Lamb
- Roast pork
- Hard cheese

## Good to know
- Often blended with Montepulciano.
- Tannic when young.
