# Turasan

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A long-standing Cappadocian winery in the Ürgüp area, known for Emir.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Turkey]]
> - **Region:** [[Cappadocia]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Emir]], [[Öküzgözü]], [[Kalecik Karası]]

## About
Working at altitude in volcanic Cappadocia, Turasan is closely tied to the local white grape Emir. The wines are crisp and often mineral.

## Known for
- Turasan Emir
- Turasan Öküzgözü
