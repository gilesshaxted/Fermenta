# Maratheftiko

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A rare native Cypriot red that makes deep, spicy, structured wines.

## Where it's from
Once close to being lost, Maratheftiko has been revived by growers since the 1990s. Its flowers are functionally female, so it needs another variety planted nearby for pollination.

## Where it grows
- **Paphos, Cyprus:** a key area
- **Limassol, Cyprus:** also grown

## What it tastes like
- Blackberry, plum, spice and herbs

## Classic wines
- Paphos Maratheftiko

## Pairs with
- Lamb
- Grilled meats
- Hard cheese

## Good to know
- Needs a pollinator planted nearby.
- A quality native red.
