# Franz Haas

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Montagna based family estate known for Pinot Noir and the white blend Manna.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Alto Adige]], [[Trentino-Alto Adige]]

## About
Franz Haas farms high-altitude sites with a lot of attention to detail. The wines are elegant, with freshness from the cool climate.
