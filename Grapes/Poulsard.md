# Poulsard

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A pale, delicate Jura red, with fine tannin and perfumed fruit.

## Where it's from
A Jura native grown for centuries around Arbois. It is also called Ploussard in Pupillin.

## Where it grows
- **Jura, France:** especially Arbois

## What it tastes like
- Strawberry, red cherry, herbs and spice

## Classic wines
- Arbois Poulsard
- Arbois Pupillin

## Pairs with
- Charcuterie
- Poultry
- Comté

## Good to know
- Pale in colour but full of flavour.
- Often compared with light Pinot Noir.
