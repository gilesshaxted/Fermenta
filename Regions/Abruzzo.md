# Abruzzo

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A wild Adriatic region, with generous Montepulciano d'Abruzzo and a rising Trebbiano d'Abruzzo.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Montepulciano]], [[Trebbiano]], [[Pecorino]]

## Landscape and topography
Mountains in the west (the Gran Sasso) and Adriatic coastal hills in the east.

## Soil and geology
Clay and limestone on the hills, sandy soils near the sea.

## Climate
Mediterranean on the coast, cooler in the mountain foothills.

## History
Much Abruzzo wine was sold in bulk. Emidio Pepe and Valentini show the best of the region, with long-lived Trebbiano and Montepulciano.

## Production and wine styles
Montepulciano d'Abruzzo (the grape, not the Tuscan town), Cerasuolo rosé, Trebbiano d'Abruzzo and Pecorino.

## Key wines
- Montepulciano d'Abruzzo
- Cerasuolo d'Abruzzo
- Trebbiano d'Abruzzo
- Pecorino

## What to look for
- Generous, plummy red at modest prices.
- Cerasuolo is a hearty dark rosé.
