# Len de l'El

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A local Gaillac white whose name means far from the eye.

## Where it's from
A very old grape from Gaillac, named after the way its bunches hang far from the shoot. It is often blended with Mauzac.

## Where it grows
- **Gaillac, France:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Citrus, apple, herbs and honey

## Classic wines
- Gaillac Blanc

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Aperitifs

## Good to know
- Often blended with Mauzac.
- Useful for sweet and dry wines.
