# Romania

Part of [[Europe]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of Europe's oldest wine countries, with a recovering industry, native grapes such as Fetească Neagră and good value across the board.

## How Romanian wine is organised
- **DOC** (Denumire de Origine Controlată) is the top tier, with **IG** (Indicație Geografică) below.
- **CMD**, **CT** and **CIB** describe harvest timing: ripe, late and noble-rot grapes.
- The industry was nationalised under communism, and private estates have emerged since 1990.
- Value wines are the strength.
