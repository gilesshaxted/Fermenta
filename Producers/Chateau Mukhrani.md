# Chateau Mukhrani

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A historic estate near Tbilisi in Kartli, restored as a modern winery.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Georgia]]
> - **Region:** [[Kartli]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Saperavi]], [[Tavkveri]], [[Rkatsiteli]]

## About
The estate is built around an old princely cellar and works mainly with Georgian grapes. The wines are polished and aimed at both home and export markets.
