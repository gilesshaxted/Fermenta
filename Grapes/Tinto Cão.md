# Tinto Cão

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A low-yielding, aromatic Douro red that helps blends keep their freshness.

## Where it's from
Known as a valuable Port grape but rare because of its low yields.

## Where it grows
- **Douro, Portugal:** in small amounts

## What it tastes like
- Red cherry, herbs, spice and mineral

## Classic wines
- Port
- Douro red

## Pairs with
- Roast meat
- Cheese

## Good to know
- Good at holding acidity.
- Rare.
