# Yunnan

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A remote south-western highland region, with terraced vineyards on steep valley sides near Shangri-La.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[China]]
> - **Part of:** [[Asia]]
> - **Climate:** Cool highland
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, schist, gravel
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Syrah]]

## Landscape and topography
Steep valleys in the Himalayan foothills, near Deqin, with vineyards at some of the highest altitudes in the world, above 2,000 metres.

## Soil and geology
Clay, limestone and schist soils on steep slopes.

## Climate
Cool and bright from altitude, with strong sun, cool nights and a monsoon influence in summer.

## History
Grapes were brought here by missionaries in the nineteenth century. In the 2000s, high-altitude vineyards were developed by modern producers, including the Ao Yun estate backed by LVMH.

## Production and wine styles
Red Bordeaux style wines from Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Cabernet Franc.

## Key wines
- Ao Yun
- Yunnan Cabernet Sauvignon

## What to look for
- High-altitude reds from tiny village plots.
- Very small production.
