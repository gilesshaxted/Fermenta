## About
A family estate in the Conca d'Oro of Panzano in Chianti, farmed organically. The wines are rich, savoury and firmly structured, with a strong sense of place.

## Known for
- Flaccianello della Pieve
- Chianti Classico Vigna del Sorbo
- Chianti Classico Filetta di Lamole
