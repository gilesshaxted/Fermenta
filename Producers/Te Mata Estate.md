# Te Mata Estate

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of New Zealand's oldest names in fine wine, famous for Coleraine and Bullnose Syrah.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[New Zealand]]
> - **Region:** [[Hawke's Bay]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Syrah]], [[Chardonnay]]
> - **Styles:** [[Bordeaux blend]]

## About
A long established Hawke's Bay estate with a classical European approach. The reds are structured and age well, and the Chardonnay is fresh and precise.

## Known for
- Coleraine
- Bullnose Syrah
- Elston Chardonnay
