# Frank Cornelissen

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Belgian born producer who became a leading, uncompromising figure in Etna natural wine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Etna]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Nerello Mascalese]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]]

## About
Works on the slopes of the volcano with no additions in the cellar and a strongly hands off approach. The wines are distinctive, and can be wild and challenging.

- Munjebel Rosso
