# Uruguay

Part of [[South America]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Uruguay is the home of Tannat in South America, with fresh coastal whites such as Albariño too.

## How wine is organised here
- Wines are labelled by grape and by producer, with a national quality scheme for wines of controlled origin.
- Most vineyards are small and family owned, mainly in the south near Montevideo.
