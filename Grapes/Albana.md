# Albana

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A white grape of Romagna, made dry, sweet and as orange wine.

## Where it's from
Albana di Romagna became the first white wine in Italy to be granted DOCG status, in 1987. The decision remains a talking point.

## Where it grows
- **Romagna, Italy:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Apricot, honey, almond and spice

## Classic wines
- Albana di Romagna
- Albana passito

## Pairs with
- Sweet cheese
- Fruit desserts
- Rich fish

## Good to know
- The sweet passito style is the best known.
- Thick skins suit skin-contact wines.
