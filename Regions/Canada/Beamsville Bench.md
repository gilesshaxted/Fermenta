# Beamsville Bench

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A bench below the Niagara Escarpment, with cooler, mineral styles.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Canada]]
> - **Part of:** [[Niagara Peninsula]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Gamay]]

## Landscape and topography
A terraced step at the foot of the escarpment, rising from the lake.

## Soil and geology
Clay over limestone.

## Climate
Cool, with escarpment breezes moderating the heat.

## History
A leading sub-appellation for cool climate whites and Pinot Noir.

## Production and wine styles
Elegant Riesling, Chardonnay, Pinot Noir and Gamay.

## Key wines
- Beamsville Bench Riesling
- Beamsville Bench Chardonnay
- Pinot Noir

## What to look for
- Mineral, high acid whites.
- Cool climate reds.
