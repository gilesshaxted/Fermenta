# Domaine Dugat-Py

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small Gevrey-Chambertin estate known for dark, concentrated reds from old vines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Côte de Nuits]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]]

## About
Dugat-Py works low yields from old vines and uses little new oak influence on the fruit. The wines are rich, deep and age well.

## Known for
- Gevrey-Chambertin Vieilles Vignes
- Charmes-Chambertin
