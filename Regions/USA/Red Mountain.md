# Red Mountain

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A tiny, hot slope that makes some of Washington's most concentrated Cabernet.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[Columbia Valley]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** sand, gravel, loess
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Syrah]]

## Landscape and topography
A south-facing slope in the Yakima Valley, rising from the river.

## Soil and geology
Sandy, gravelly soils, with limestone in the subsoil.

## Climate
Among the warmest and driest parts of the state.

## History
Planting began in 1975, and the AVA was formed in 2001.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot and Syrah.

## Key wines
- Red Mountain Cabernet Sauvignon

## What to look for
- Dense, tannic and structured.
- Needs ageing.
