> A Bierzo winemaker and consultant admired for his low intervention approach and many projects across north west Spain.

## About
Raúl Pérez works with very old vines and gentle cellar methods, making wines that are fresh, savoury and often quite understated. He has made many wines in Bierzo, Rías Baixas and Ribeira Sacra.
