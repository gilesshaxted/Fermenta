# Château Cos d'Estournel

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Saint-Estèphe Second Growth recognisable by its exotic-looking pagoda-style chai and its rich, plush reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Bordeaux]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]]
> - **Styles:** [[Bordeaux blend]]

## About
Cos d'Estournel stands on a gravel ridge on the edge of Pauillac. The wines are lush and concentrated, with a more opulent character than many Saint-Estèphe neighbours.

- Château Cos d'Estournel
