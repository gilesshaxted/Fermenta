# Allegrini

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family estate in Fumane, one of the leading names in Valpolicella and Amarone.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Valpolicella]], [[Veneto]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Corvina]], [[Rondinella]], [[Molinara]]
> - **Styles:** [[Amarone]]

## About
Allegrini works in the Classico zone and has been influential in raising the quality of Valpolicella. The wines range from lively everyday reds to rich, structured Amarone.

## Known for
- Amarone della Valpolicella Classico
- La Poja
- Palazzo della Torre
- La Grola
