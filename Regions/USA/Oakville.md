# Oakville

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A valley floor and western bench famed for rich, polished Cabernet.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[Napa Valley]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** alluvial, gravel, loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]]

## Landscape and topography
Flat valley floor with gentle benches rising to the Mayacamas foothills.

## Soil and geology
Deep alluvial gravels and loams on the floor, with rockier soils higher up.

## Climate
Warm days, with cooling evening air from the south.

## History
Home to estates such as Harlan, Opus One and Screaming Eagle, and long associated with the best Napa Cabernet.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon dominates, with Cabernet Franc, Merlot and Sauvignon Blanc.

## Key wines
- Oakville Cabernet Sauvignon
- Oakville Bordeaux blends

## What to look for
- Dark fruit, graphite and fine tannins.
- Long-lived wines.
