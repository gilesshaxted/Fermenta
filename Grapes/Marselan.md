# Marselan

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A modern French cross of Cabernet Sauvignon and Grenache, which has found a strong following in China.

## Where it's from
Bred in 1961 by Paul Truel at INRA in Montpellier from Cabernet Sauvignon and Grenache. It is named after Marseillan in the Herault and has become popular in Ningxia and other Chinese regions.

## Where it grows
- **Ningxia, China:** widely planted and well regarded
- **Hebei, China:** also grown
- **Languedoc, France:** where it was bred

## What it tastes like
- Blackcurrant, blackberry, spice and violet

## Classic wines
- Ningxia Marselan
- Marselan blends

## Pairs with
- Grilled lamb
- Duck
- Hard cheese

## Good to know
- Deeply coloured and aromatic.
- A rising star in China.
