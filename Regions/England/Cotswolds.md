# Cotswolds

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Honey-coloured limestone hills with sheltered south-facing slopes and a growing number of small estates.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[England]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** limestone, oolitic limestone, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Bacchus]], [[Seyval Blanc]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling oolitic limestone hills and valleys across Gloucestershire and Oxfordshire.

## Soil and geology
Oolitic limestone and clay.

## Climate
Cool, with some continental influence and good ripening on the sheltered slopes.

## History
Vines were grown in Gloucestershire in the Middle Ages, and a modern revival began in the 20th century.

## Production and wine styles
Still and sparkling wines, with aromatic whites a strength.

## Key wines
- Cotswold sparkling
- Cotswold Bacchus

## What to look for
- Mineral-edged whites.
- Pretty countryside for a vineyard visit.
