# Arneis

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A fragrant Piedmont white with pear and almond, once used to soften Nebbiolo.

## Where it's from
The Roero hills, on the left bank of the Tanaro in Piedmont.

## Where it grows
- **Roero, Italy:** the home

## What it tastes like
- Pear, peach, almond and floral

## Classic wines
- Roero Arneis

## Pairs with
- Antipasti
- Vegetable dishes
- Light fish

## Good to know
- Its nickname means little rascal, for being hard to grow.
- Rare outside Piedmont.
