# Verdicchio dei Castelli di Jesi

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Italy's most underrated white, with high acidity and the ability to age.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Marche]]
> - **Climate:** Temperate
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Verdicchio]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills inland of Ancona, around the town of Jesi.

## Soil and geology
Clay and limestone.

## Climate
Temperate, with sea breezes.

## History
A Classico zone was defined in the heart of the area. Producers such as Bucci and Garofoli led a modern revival.

## Production and wine styles
100% Verdicchio, in stainless steel or oak. Superiore and Riserva carry more depth.

## Key wines
- Verdicchio dei Castelli di Jesi Classico
- Classico Riserva

## What to look for
- Citrus, almond and mineral tension.
- A great match for fish.

## Producers
- [[Umani Ronchi]]
- [[Villa Bucci]]
