# Kadarka

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A pale, peppery red that was once Hungary's main black grape, now enjoying a revival.

## Where it's from
Thought to have arrived with Balkan settlers. It was widely planted, then lost ground to easier grapes, and it is now being replanted with care in Szekszárd and Eger.

## Where it grows
- **Szekszárd, Hungary:** its best home
- **Eger, Hungary:** in Bull's Blood blends
- **Bulgaria:** as Gamza

## What it tastes like
- Red cherry, pepper, spice and herbs

## Classic wines
- Szekszárd Kadarka
- Egri Bikavér

## Pairs with
- Grilled meat
- Paprika dishes
- Goulash

## Good to know
- Light in colour, high in aroma.
- Hard to grow well, so quality varies.
