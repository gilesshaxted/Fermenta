# Moravia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The main wine region of Czechia, in the south-east, with most of the country's vineyards.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Czechia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Warm continental
> - **Soils:** loess, limestone, clay, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grüner Veltliner]], [[Welschriesling]], [[Riesling]], [[Pálava]], [[Blaufränkisch]], [[St. Laurent]]

## Landscape and topography
Gently rolling hills in the south-east, near Austria and Slovakia, with vineyards on south-facing slopes.

## Soil and geology
Loess, limestone, clay and sandy soils.

## Climate
Warm continental, with hot summers, cold winters and a long growing season.

## History
Winegrowing in Moravia goes back centuries and was shaped by Habsburg rule. Modern, quality-focused producers have grown since 1989.

## Production and wine styles
Fresh, dry whites such as Grüner Veltliner, Riesling and Pálava, and reds such as Blaufränkisch and St. Laurent. Sweet wines and sparkling wines are also made.

## Key wines
- Moravian Grüner Veltliner
- Moravian Riesling
- Moravian Pálava
- Moravian St. Laurent

## What to look for
- Crisp, peppery whites.
- Similar in style to neighbouring Austria.
