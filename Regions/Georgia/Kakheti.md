# Kakheti

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Georgia's main wine region in the east, producing most of the country's wine, in both European and qvevri styles.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Georgia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Asia]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** alluvial, clay, limestone, sandy loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Rkatsiteli]], [[Saperavi]], [[Mtsvane]], [[Kisi]], [[Khikhvi]]
> - **Styles:** [[Orange wine]], [[Qvevri wine]]

## Landscape and topography
A broad valley along the Alazani river, with the Greater Caucasus to the north and vineyards on the plain and foothills.

## Soil and geology
Varied alluvial soils, clay, limestone and sandy loam.

## Climate
Continental, with warm summers and cold winters, and mountain influences from the Caucasus.

## History
Kakheti has an ancient winemaking tradition, and Georgia is often described as one of the oldest wine cultures in the world. Wine was traditionally made in buried clay vessels called qvevri.

## Production and wine styles
Amber wines made with skin contact in qvevri, fresh European-style whites, and deep, structured Saperavi reds. Rkatsiteli and Saperavi dominate.

## Key wines
- Kakheti Rkatsiteli
- Kakheti Saperavi
- Tsinandali
- Mukuzani
- Kindzmarauli

## What to look for
- Try an amber qvevri Rkatsiteli.
- Saperavi is dark, tannic and age-worthy.
