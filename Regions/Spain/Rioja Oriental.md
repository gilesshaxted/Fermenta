# Rioja Oriental

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The hot eastern zone, once called Rioja Baja, home of rich Garnacha.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Part of:** [[Rioja]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** alluvium, clay, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grenache|Garnacha]], [[Tempranillo]]

## Landscape and topography
Flatter land along the Ebro towards Alfaro and Calahorra, with warm exposures.

## Soil and geology
Alluvial soils with clay and limestone.

## Climate
Warmer and drier, more Mediterranean in feel.

## History
Renamed Oriental in 2018 to avoid the negative connotation of Baja (lower).

## Production and wine styles
Garnacha is the star here, with ripe, full-bodied wines used in blends.

## Key wines
- Rioja Garnacha
- Rioja Oriental Reserva

## What to look for
- Ripe berry fruit and warmth.
- Often used to add body to blends.
