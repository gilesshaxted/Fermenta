# Armavir

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A warm, flat region west of Yerevan, once one of the main Armenian vineyard areas.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Armenia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Asia]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** alluvial, volcanic, sandy loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Voskehat]], [[Areni]]

## Landscape and topography
A lowland plain in the Ararat basin, at moderate altitude.

## Soil and geology
Alluvial and volcanic soils with sandy loam.

## Climate
Hot and dry in summer and cold in winter, with irrigation.

## History
The area has long been used for growing grapes, much of which went to brandy production during the Soviet era.

## Production and wine styles
Fruity table wines, brandy base wines and some fortified styles.

## Key wines
- Armavir white
- Armavir red

## What to look for
- Ripe, fruit-driven styles.
- Mostly for local drinking.
