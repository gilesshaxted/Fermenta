# Barone Ricasoli

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A historic estate at Castello di Brolio, closely tied to the origins of the Chianti blend.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Chianti Classico]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sangiovese]]

## About
One of Tuscany's oldest wine estates, set around the castle of Brolio near Gaiole. It produces both entry level and top tier Chianti Classico, with a classic, savoury style.

## Known for
- Castello di Brolio Chianti Classico Gran Selezione
- Colledilà
- Casalferro
- Brolio Chianti Classico
