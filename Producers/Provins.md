# Provins

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The big Valais cooperative, one of the largest wine producers in Switzerland.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Switzerland]]
> - **Region:** [[Valais]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chasselas]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Syrah]], [[Cornalin]]

## About
Provins is a cooperative drawing on grapes from growers across the Valais. It makes a wide range, from everyday Fendant to more serious local-grape cuvées.
