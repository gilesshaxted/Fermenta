# Rutherford

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A warm central valley area known for a dusty, earthy style of Cabernet.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[Napa Valley]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** alluvial, gravel, loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Franc]]

## Landscape and topography
Valley floor and western benchland, close to the Mayacamas foothills.

## Soil and geology
Well-drained alluvial gravels and loams.

## Climate
Warm and dry, with less fog than the south.

## History
Beaulieu Vineyard planted here in 1900, and its Private Reserve Cabernet helped establish the reputation of the area.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon led blends, with Merlot and Cabernet Franc.

## Key wines
- Rutherford Cabernet Sauvignon

## What to look for
- Often described as having Rutherford dust, a dry, earthy texture.
- Polished and structured.
