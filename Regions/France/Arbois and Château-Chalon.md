# Arbois and Château-Chalon

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The Jura's best-known appellations: Arbois for its range of styles, Château-Chalon for its oxidative vin jaune.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Jura]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** marl, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Savagnin]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Poulsard]], [[Trousseau]]
> - **Styles:** [[Vin Jaune]]

## Landscape and topography
Hillside vineyards around Arbois, and a tiny hilltop village for Château-Chalon with steep slopes.

## Soil and geology
Marl dominates, and Château-Chalon is on Jurassic blue marl with limestone.

## Climate
Continental, with cool nights.

## History
Louis Pasteur had a house in Arbois. Château-Chalon is a unique appellation for vin jaune, declassified in poor years.

## Production and wine styles
Vin jaune is Savagnin aged six years and three months under a veil of flor-like yeast, without topping up.

## Key wines
- Arbois Poulsard
- Arbois Trousseau
- Château-Chalon (vin jaune)
- Vin de paille

## What to look for
- Walnut, curry spice, apple and a saline finish.
- Serve vin jaune slightly cool with Comté.
