# Pétillant naturel

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Lightly sparkling wine bottled before the first fermentation has finished.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Category:** Sparkling
> - **Colour:** Varies
> - **Sweetness:** Dry
> - **Country:** France
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chenin Blanc]], [[Gamay]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]]
> - **Regions:** [[Loire Valley]], [[Jura]], [[Beaujolais]], [[Languedoc]]

## How it's made
The wine finishes fermenting in the bottle under crown cap, trapping its own carbon dioxide. No second fermentation and often no disgorging, so the wine can be cloudy.

## Where it's made
Common in the Loire, Jura and Beaujolais, and increasingly in the natural wine scene worldwide.

## What it tastes like
- Fresh, lively and slightly yeasty
- Cider like cloudiness
- Light fizz

## Pairs with
- Charcuterie
- Salads
- Casual drinking

## Good to know
- Pét-nat is short for pétillant naturel.
- Wines are often low in alcohol and unfiltered.
