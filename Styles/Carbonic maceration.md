# Carbonic maceration

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Whole bunches ferment inside their skins in a carbon dioxide rich tank.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Category:** Method
> - **Colour:** Red
> - **Sweetness:** Dry
> - **Country:** France
> - **Main grapes:** [[Gamay]], [[Carignan]], [[Tempranillo]], [[Grenache]]
> - **Regions:** [[Beaujolais]], [[Beaujolais Crus]], [[Languedoc]], [[Rioja Alavesa]]

## How it's made
Uncrushed bunches are sealed in a tank filled with CO2. Fermentation begins inside each berry, producing bright fruit and low tannin, with bubblegum and banana notes.

## Where it's made
The signature method of Beaujolais, also used in the Languedoc and Rioja Alavesa.

## What it tastes like
- Bright red fruit, bubblegum, banana
- Soft tannin, low acidity
- Juicy and easy drinking

## Pairs with
- Charcuterie
- Roast chicken
- Light cheeses

## Good to know
- Beaujolais Nouveau is the extreme example.
- Serve lightly chilled.
