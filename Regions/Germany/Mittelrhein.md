# Mittelrhein

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A scenic gorge of the Rhine with castles, steep slate vineyards and a tiny amount of lively Riesling.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Germany]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Temperate
> - **Soils:** slate, quartzite
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]]

## Landscape and topography
Steep terraces along the Rhine gorge, a UNESCO World Heritage area with dozens of castles.

## Soil and geology
Devonian slate.

## Climate
Cool, with sun on south-facing slopes.

## History
Vineyards have shrunk over the decades because of the difficulty of work, and the region is now small.

## Production and wine styles
Riesling, with some Pinot varieties.

## Key wines
- Mittelrhein Riesling

## What to look for
- Crisp, mineral Riesling.
- Rarely exported.
