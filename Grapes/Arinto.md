# Arinto

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Portugal's fresh, high-acid white, found across the country.

## Where it's from
Portugal. Known as Pedernã in Vinho Verde.

## Where it grows
- **Bucelas, Lisbon:** the classic region
- **Vinho Verde and Alentejo, Portugal:** for freshness in blends

## What it tastes like
- Lemon, green apple and a stony, saline edge

## Classic wines
- Bucelas
- Vinho Verde blends

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Salads
- Grilled fish

## Good to know
- Keeps its acidity in hot climates.
- Ages well, developing honey and nuts.
