# Canterbury

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cool, east-coast region centred on Waipara, with Pinot Noir and Riesling.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[New Zealand]]
> - **Part of:** [[Oceania]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** limestone, gravel, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Riesling]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]]

## Landscape and topography
Plains, hills and river valleys north of Christchurch, shielded by the Southern Alps.

## Soil and geology
Limestone-rich clay and gravel.

## Climate
Cool and dry, sheltered by the Alps.

## History
Waipara's first vines date from the 1980s.

## Production and wine styles
Pinot Noir, Riesling, Chardonnay and Sauvignon Blanc.

## Key wines
- Waipara Riesling
- Waipara Pinot Noir

## What to look for
- Poised, mineral Riesling.
- Limestone gives structure.
