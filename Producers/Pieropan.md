# Pieropan

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family estate that helped restore Soave's reputation for serious white wine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Soave]], [[Veneto]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Garganega]], [[Trebbiano]]

## About
Based in the town of Soave, Pieropan farms hillside vineyards on volcanic and limestone soils. The wines are crisp, savoury and mineral, and the single vineyard bottlings age well.

## Known for
- Soave Classico Calvarino
- Soave Classico La Rocca
- Soave Classico
