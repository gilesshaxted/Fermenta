# Passopisciaro

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A leading northern Etna estate known for single contrada bottlings.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Etna]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Nerello Mascalese]], [[Chardonnay]]

## About
Founded by Andrea Franchetti on the north face of the volcano. The wines show clear differences between individual parcels and are precise and ageworthy.

## Known for
- Contrada Rampante
- Contrada Guardiola
- Passobianco
- Contrada Chiappemacine
