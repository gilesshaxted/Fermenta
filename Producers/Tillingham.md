# Tillingham

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A low intervention East Sussex estate making skin contact, qvevri and pet-nat wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[England]]
> - **Region:** [[Sussex]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention

> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]], [[Orange wine]], [[Pétillant naturel]]

## About
Tillingham farms organically near Rye and works with minimal intervention in the cellar. The wines are playful and textural, and have been very influential on the English natural scene.

- Qvevri
