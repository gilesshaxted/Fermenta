# KRSMA Estates

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Karnataka estate best known for Sangiovese and Tempranillo grown in warm, dry hills.

## About
KRSMA is a quality-focused estate in northern Karnataka, working with Mediterranean varieties suited to the climate. The reds are structured and sun-ripened.
