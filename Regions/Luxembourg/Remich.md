# Remich

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The southern part of the Luxembourg Moselle, around the town of Remich, with slightly warmer conditions.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Luxembourg]]
> - **Part of:** [[Moselle Luxembourgeoise]]
> - **Climate:** Cool continental
> - **Soils:** limestone, marl, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Auxerrois]], [[Pinot Blanc]]

## Landscape and topography
Slopes along the river around Remich and nearby villages.

## Soil and geology
Limestone and marl, with clay in places.

## Climate
A little warmer than the north, and good for ripening Pinot Gris and Auxerrois.

## History
Remich is the wine hub of the lower Luxembourg Moselle, and many of the larger cellars and cooperatives are here.

## Production and wine styles
Fruity whites and sparkling Crémant, from Pinot Gris, Auxerrois, Pinot Blanc and Riesling.

## Key wines
- Remich Pinot Gris
- Remich Auxerrois

## What to look for
- Rounder, fruitier whites.
- Good for visits.
