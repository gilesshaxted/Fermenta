# Pauillac

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The home of power: Cabernet Sauvignon at its most structured, with three of the five First Growths.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Bordeaux]]
> - **Climate:** Maritime
> - **Soils:** gravel, clay, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Petit Verdot]]
> - **Styles:** [[Bordeaux blend]]

## Landscape and topography
Gravel ridges rising gently from the estuary and the small port town of Pauillac.

## Soil and geology
Deep, well-drained gravel over clay and limestone, ideal for late-ripening Cabernet Sauvignon.

## Climate
Maritime, with the estuary moderating cold and the gravel storing warmth.

## History
Pauillac holds Lafite Rothschild, Latour and Mouton Rothschild. Mouton was promoted to First Growth in 1973, the only change to the top rank of the 1855 Classification.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon is the main grape, usually blended with Merlot and a little Cabernet Franc and Petit Verdot. Red only.

## Key wines
- Château Lafite Rothschild
- Château Latour
- Château Mouton Rothschild
- Pichon Baron
- Pichon Comtesse

## What to look for
- Blackcurrant, cedar, graphite and pencil lead.
- Built to age, so younger vintages often need time.
