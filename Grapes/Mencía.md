# Mencía

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Perfumed, fresh and mineral red from northwest Spain.

## Where it's from
Northwest Spain, where Bierzo and Galicia are the main homes. It is not related to Cabernet Franc despite old theories, and DNA work links it to Jaen in Portugal.

## Where it grows
- **Bierzo, Spain:** on slate hillsides, the best known source
- **Ribeira Sacra, Spain:** on steep river terraces

## What it tastes like
- Raspberry, violet and red cherry
- Often with a mineral, stony edge

## Classic wines
- Bierzo Mencía
- Ribeira Sacra reds

## Pairs with
- Roast pork
- Grilled chicken
- Mushroom dishes

## Good to know
- Old bush vines give the best wine.
- Often compared with Cabernet Franc or Pinot Noir.
