# Rudolf Fürst

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A respected Franken estate famed for Spätburgunder from red sandstone at Klingenberg.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Germany]]
> - **Region:** [[Franken]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]]

## About
A family estate on the Main with some of Germany's best red wine slopes. The Pinot Noirs are elegant and Burgundian in style, and there are also fine white wines.

- Centgrafenberg
