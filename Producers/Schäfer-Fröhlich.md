# Schäfer-Fröhlich

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small, much admired Nahe estate in Bockenau making intense, mineral Riesling.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Germany]]
> - **Region:** [[Nahe]]

## About
A family estate working slate and volcanic soils around Bockenau. The wines are concentrated and precise, and they are strongly sought by collectors.
