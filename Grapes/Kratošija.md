# Kratošija

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A red grape of Montenegro and North Macedonia, genetically a relative of Zinfandel.

## Where it's from
Grown in the Balkans for centuries, and recognised by DNA testing as a close relative of Zinfandel and Primitivo. Kratošija is often blended.

## Where it grows
- **Tikveš, North Macedonia:** blends
- **Montenegro:** also grown

## What it tastes like
- Red cherry, plum, spice and herbs

## Classic wines
- Tikveš Kratošija

## Pairs with
- Grilled meat
- Stews
- Tomato dishes

## Good to know
- Related to Zinfandel.
- Often blended with Vranac.
