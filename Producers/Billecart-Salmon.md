# Billecart-Salmon

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family-run house in Mareuil-sur-Aÿ famed for finesse and a benchmark non-vintage rosé.

## About
Billecart-Salmon uses long, cool fermentations and extended ageing to build delicate, precise wines. It is especially admired for its rosé and its single-vineyard bottlings.

## Known for
- Brut Rosé
- Cuvée Nicolas François Billecart
