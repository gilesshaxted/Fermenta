# Chasselas

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A soft, delicate white, Switzerland's main white grape, and found in Savoie.

## Where it's from
Old, probably from Lake Geneva. It is the main grape in Swiss Vaud and Valais, where it is known as Fendant.

## Where it grows
- **Savoie, France:** for Crépy
- **Switzerland:** its main home

## What it tastes like
- Apple, herbs, white flowers and almond

## Classic wines
- Fendant
- Crépy

## Pairs with
- Cheese fondue
- Raclette
- Fish

## Good to know
- Soft acid and delicate flavour.
- Reflects soil strongly.
