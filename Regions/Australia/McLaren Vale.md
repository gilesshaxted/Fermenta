# McLaren Vale

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A sunny coastal region south of Adelaide, known for generous Shiraz and Grenache.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Australia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Oceania]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** sandy loam, clay, limestone, ironstone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Grenache]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Mourvèdre]], [[Chardonnay]]
> - **Styles:** [[GSM]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling hills between the Mount Lofty Ranges and Gulf St Vincent.

## Soil and geology
A patchwork, including sandy loam, clay and ancient ironstone.

## Climate
Mediterranean, with sea breezes moderating the heat.

## History
Vines were planted from the 1830s. Quality and fame have grown since the 1980s.

## Production and wine styles
Shiraz, Grenache, Cabernet and Mourvèdre, plus some Italian and Spanish varieties.

## Key wines
- McLaren Vale Shiraz
- McLaren Vale Grenache

## What to look for
- Ripe fruit with chocolate and spice.
- Often organic and biodynamic.
