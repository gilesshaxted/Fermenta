# Rueda

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Verdejo country: fresh, herbal whites from high, stony plateau vineyards.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** limestone, gravel, sand, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Verdejo]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Macabeo|Viura]]

## Landscape and topography
A high plateau in Castilla y León, around 700 to 800 metres, near Valladolid.

## Soil and geology
Stony, gravelly soils over limestone and sand, with ungrafted old vines in some places.

## Climate
Continental, with a big day-night swing that keeps the acidity.

## History
Verdejo nearly disappeared in the 20th century, and was revived in the 1970s by Marqués de Riscal with help from Bordeaux's Émile Peynaud.

## Production and wine styles
Verdejo for the best, usually in stainless steel. Rueda Superior needs at least 85%.

## Key wines
- Rueda Verdejo
- Rueda Superior
- Rueda Sauvignon Blanc

## What to look for
- Lime, fennel, grass and a slightly bitter finish.
- A good, affordable, fresh white.

- [[Belondrade]]
- [[Marqués de Riscal]]
