# Gigondas

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Sturdy Grenache-based reds at the foot of the Dentelles de Montmirail, often a value alternative to Châteauneuf.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Rhône Valley]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, sand, marl
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grenache]], [[Syrah]], [[Mourvèdre]]
> - **Styles:** [[GSM]]

## Landscape and topography
Hillside vineyards beneath the jagged limestone Dentelles de Montmirail, at higher altitude than the plain.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, clay, sand and marl, varying across terraces.

## Climate
Mediterranean with cooler nights because of altitude.

## History
Elevated to Cru status in 1971. The Dentelles are a visible landmark.

## Production and wine styles
Grenache-led, with Syrah and Mourvèdre. Red and rosé are made.

## Key wines
- Gigondas rouge
- Gigondas rosé

## What to look for
- Spiced black cherry, herbs and a firm structure.
- A fine match for roast lamb.
