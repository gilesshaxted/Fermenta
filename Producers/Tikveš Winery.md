# Tikveš Winery

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The best known winery of North Macedonia, based in the Tikveš basin around Kavadarci.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[North Macedonia]]
> - **Region:** [[Tikveš]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Vranac]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## About
A large, long established producer working mostly with estate and contracted vineyards in the warm Vardar valley. It makes everything from everyday bottlings to serious reserve reds built on Vranec.
