# Teroldego

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A deeply coloured, juicy red from the Rotaliano plain in Trentino.

## Where it's from
Trentino, on the Campo Rotaliano plain.

## Where it grows
- **Trentino, Italy:** the Rotaliano plain

## What it tastes like
- Blackberry, wild berry and spice

## Classic wines
- Teroldego Rotaliano

## Pairs with
- Game
- Polenta
- Cured meats

## Good to know
- A close relative of Lagrein.
- Fruity but structured.
