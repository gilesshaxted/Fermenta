# Clos Rougeard

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cult Saumur-Champigny estate making intense, long-lived Cabernet Franc.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Saumur]], [[Loire Valley]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Franc]]

## About
Clos Rougeard was run by the Foucault brothers in a very hands-on, traditional way, with small production. The reds are deep, savoury and mineral and are among the most sought-after in the Loire.

## Known for
- Le Bourg
- Les Poyeux
- Les Clos
