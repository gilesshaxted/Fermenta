# Saint-Chinian

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A hillside appellation with two distinct halves: schist in the north and limestone and clay in the south.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Languedoc]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** schist, limestone, sandstone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Grenache]], [[Mourvèdre]], [[Carignan]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills between the Orb and Vernazobre valleys, climbing towards the Caroux massif.

## Soil and geology
Schist in the north for peppery wines, limestone and clay in the south for richer ones.

## Climate
Mediterranean with warm days and cooler nights at altitude.

## History
Associated with the village of Saint-Chinian and its abbey, the area has long made wine for the region.

## Production and wine styles
Red blends, with white and rosé in smaller quantities.

## Key wines
- Saint-Chinian rouge
- Saint-Chinian-Roquebrun
- Saint-Chinian-Berlou

## What to look for
- Spicy dark fruit, herbs and warmth.
- Good value and good with grilled lamb.
