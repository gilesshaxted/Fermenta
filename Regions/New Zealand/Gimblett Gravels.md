# Gimblett Gravels

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A warm, stony old riverbed that makes concentrated reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[New Zealand]]
> - **Part of:** [[Hawke's Bay]]
> - **Climate:** Maritime
> - **Soils:** gravel, stony alluvial, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## Landscape and topography
A former riverbed on the Ngaruroro, now vineyards on flat, stony land.

## Soil and geology
Deep gravel with very low fertility and good drainage.

## Climate
One of the warmest areas of the North Island.

## History
Declared a Winegrowing District in 2001, after plantings in the 1980s.

## Production and wine styles
Syrah, Merlot and Cabernet blends.

## Key wines
- Gimblett Gravels Syrah
- Gimblett Gravels Bordeaux blends

## What to look for
- Dark fruit and pepper.
- Often finer than the heat suggests.
