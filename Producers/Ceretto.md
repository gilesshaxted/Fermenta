# Ceretto

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A large family business in Alba, known for single vineyard Barolo and Barbaresco and for popularising Arneis.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Barolo]], [[Barbaresco]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Nebbiolo]], [[Arneis]], [[Barbera]]

## About
Ceretto farms across several Langhe and Roero sites and makes a wide range. It is also known for its architecture and hospitality projects around Alba.

## Known for
- Barolo Bricco Rocche
- Barbaresco Bricco Asili
- Blangé Roero Arneis
