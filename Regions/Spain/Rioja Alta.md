# Rioja Alta

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The western upper part of Rioja, with higher altitude and elegant, long-lived wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Part of:** [[Rioja]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, ferrous clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tempranillo]], [[Graciano]], [[Carignan|Mazuelo]]

## Landscape and topography
Hillsides on the right bank of the Ebro near Haro, with higher vineyards and Atlantic influence.

## Soil and geology
Clay and limestone, with some ferrous clay and alluvial terraces.

## Climate
Cooler and wetter than the east, with Atlantic air.

## History
Haro's Barrio de la Estación is where many historic bodegas, such as López de Heredia and La Rioja Alta, were built next to the railway.

## Production and wine styles
Tempranillo-led wines, with strong tradition in long barrel ageing.

## Key wines
- Rioja Alta Reserva
- Rioja Alta Gran Reserva

## What to look for
- Red cherry, tobacco, leather and spice.
- Freshness and finesse over weight.
