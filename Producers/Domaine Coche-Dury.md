# Domaine Coche-Dury

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Meursault domaine with a near-mythical reputation for white Burgundy.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Côte de Beaune]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]]

## About
Coche-Dury makes small amounts of precise, reductive-edged Chardonnay. The wines are tightly wound, flinty and very long lived.

## Known for
- Corton-Charlemagne
- Meursault Perrières
- Meursault Les Rougeots
