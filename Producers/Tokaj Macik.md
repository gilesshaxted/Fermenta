# Tokaj Macik

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family-run Slovak Tokaj house, known for Furmint and sweet Tokaj styles.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Slovakia]]
> - **Region:** [[Slovak Tokaj]]

## About
Based in Malá Tŕňa, it works with local varieties and traditional Tokaj methods. The range includes dry wines and the sweet selections that the area is known for.
