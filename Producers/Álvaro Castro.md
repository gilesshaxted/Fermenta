# Álvaro Castro

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A leading Dão producer making elegant, mineral reds and whites from granite soils.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Portugal]]
> - **Region:** [[Portugal]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Touriga Nacional]], [[Encruzado]], [[Alfrocheiro]]

## About
Álvaro Castro is one of the key figures in the revival of the Dão. His wines are fresh, fine and long lived, and the whites rely on Encruzado.

## Known for
- Quinta da Pellada
- Quinta de Saes
- Pellada Primus
