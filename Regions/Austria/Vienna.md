# Vienna

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of the world's few capital cities with real vineyards, famous for Gemischter Satz field blends.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Austria]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** limestone, loess, clay, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grüner Veltliner]], [[Riesling]], [[Pinot Blanc]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
Vineyards on the Danube-facing hills of Kahlenberg, Nussberg and Bisamberg, within the city boundaries.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, loess and clay, with sandy soils.

## Climate
Continental, with warmth from the city and Danube.

## History
Viennese wine culture centres around the Heurigen, wine taverns that serve the year's young wine. Gemischter Satz (field blend) became a DAC in 2013.

## Production and wine styles
Wiener Gemischter Satz DAC is a blend of at least three white varieties planted together and harvested together.

## Key wines
- Wiener Gemischter Satz DAC
- Heuriger wines

## What to look for
- Fresh, floral and savoury.
- Perfect with a plate of cold cuts at a Heuriger.
