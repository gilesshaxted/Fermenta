# Muscat of Alexandria

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A large, sweet and aromatic Muscat, used for dessert wines and table grapes.

## Where it's from
An ancient variety, a cross of Muscat Blanc à Petits Grains and Axina de Tres Bias. It is known as Zibibbo in Sicily, and Moscatel de Alejandría in Spain.

## Where it grows
- **Samos and Limnos, Greece:** for sweet wine
- **Sicily, Italy:** as Zibibbo
- **South Africa and Australia:** as Hanepoot and Gordo

## What it tastes like
- Orange blossom, grape, honey and spice

## Classic wines
- Pantelleria Passito
- Moscatel de Málaga

## Pairs with
- Fruit desserts
- Blue cheese
- Pastries

## Good to know
- Called Zibibbo in Sicily.
- Also a table grape and raisin.
