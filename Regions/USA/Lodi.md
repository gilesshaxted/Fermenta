# Lodi

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A big Central Valley area with warm days, cool delta breezes and many old Zinfandel vines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[North America]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** sandy loam, alluvial, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Zinfandel]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Petite Sirah]]

## Landscape and topography
Flat, low-lying land within the Sacramento-San Joaquin Delta.

## Soil and geology
Sandy loam and alluvial soils from the Mokelumne River.

## Climate
Hot summers cooled by delta breezes in the evenings.

## History
Home to some of the oldest Zinfandel in California, including pre-Prohibition vines.

## Production and wine styles
Zinfandel, Cabernet Sauvignon, Petite Sirah, Chardonnay and others.

## Key wines
- Lodi Old Vine Zinfandel
- Lodi red blends

## What to look for
- Rich, ripe and fairly priced.
- Old vine Zinfandel is the highlight.
