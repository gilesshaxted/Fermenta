# Dal Forno Romano

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small, perfectionist Valpolicella estate producing intense, concentrated Amarone.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Valpolicella]], [[Veneto]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Corvina]], [[Rondinella]], [[Molinara]]
> - **Styles:** [[Amarone]]

## About
Romano Dal Forno farms in Illasi, east of the Classico zone, with dense plantings and very low yields. The wines are deep and rich, and are made in small amounts.

## Known for
- Amarone della Valpolicella
- Valpolicella Superiore
- Monte Lodoletta
