# Collio

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Italy's most prestigious white wine zone, on hillsides right on the Slovenian border.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Friuli-Venezia Giulia]]
> - **Climate:** Temperate
> - **Soils:** ponca, marl, sandstone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Friulano]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Ribolla Gialla]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]]
> - **Styles:** [[Orange wine]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills around Cormons and Gorizia, with the border with Slovenia running through the vines.

## Soil and geology
Ponca, a layered mix of marl and sandstone.

## Climate
Temperate, with Adriatic warmth and Alpine cool nights.

## History
Collio produced benchmark whites from the 1960s. Many estates straddle the border and still trade across it.

## Production and wine styles
White blends and single varieties. Many producers also make skin-contact 'orange' wines.

## Key wines
- Collio Bianco
- Collio Friulano
- Collio Ribolla Gialla

## What to look for
- Peach, apple, flowers and mineral depth.
- Excellent with fish, prosciutto and white asparagus.
