# Rheinhessen

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Germany's largest wine region, known for rolling hills, plenty of grapes and a dynamic young generation.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Germany]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Temperate
> - **Soils:** loess, limestone, clay, red slate
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]], [[Müller-Thurgau]], [[Sylvaner|Silvaner]], [[Pinot Blanc]], [[Pinot Noir]]

## Landscape and topography
A big plateau of rolling hills in the loop of the Rhine, with the Roter Hang (Red Slope) as a showpiece.

## Soil and geology
Loess, limestone and clay, with red slate on the Roter Hang.

## Climate
Temperate and dry, one of Germany's sunnier regions.

## History
Liebfraumilch made this region famous in the 20th century, but a new wave of ambitious growers, such as the Message in a Bottle group, has turned quality around.

## Production and wine styles
Riesling, Silvaner, Pinot varieties and plenty more. Grosses Gewächs wines come from top sites.

## Key wines
- Rheinhessen Riesling
- Silvaner
- Pinot Blanc
- Spätburgunder

## What to look for
- Lots of choice, with strong value.
- Young producers are experimenting.
