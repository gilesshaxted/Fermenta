# Paarl

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A warm, large region under a granite dome, with rich reds and Rhône-style blends.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[South Africa]]
> - **Part of:** [[Africa]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** granite, shale, sandstone, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Pinotage]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Chenin Blanc]], [[Grenache]]

## Landscape and topography
A broad valley beneath Paarl Mountain, with a granite outcrop above town.

## Soil and geology
Granite and shale, with sandy and clay soils.

## Climate
Warmer and drier than Stellenbosch.

## History
A historic region with a French Huguenot link and home to KWV, which once controlled the industry.

## Production and wine styles
Syrah, Pinotage, Cabernet, Chenin Blanc and Rhône blends.

## Key wines
- Paarl Syrah
- Paarl Chenin Blanc

## What to look for
- Rich, spicy reds.
- Old vine whites.
