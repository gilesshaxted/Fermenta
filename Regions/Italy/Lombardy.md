# Lombardy

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A northern region with Italy's best sparkling wine, Franciacorta, and Alpine Nebbiolo in Valtellina.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Temperate
> - **Soils:** moraine, limestone, gravel, clay, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Nebbiolo]], [[Croatina]]
> - **Styles:** [[Franciacorta (style)|Franciacorta style]]

## Landscape and topography
Lakes, Alpine valleys and hills around Milan, Brescia and the Valtellina.

## Soil and geology
Glacial moraine, limestone and gravel in Franciacorta, steep terraced granite in Valtellina.

## Climate
Temperate, with lake influence and Alpine air.

## History
Franciacorta was recognised as DOCG in 1995 and has become Italy's leading traditional-method sparkling wine.

## Production and wine styles
Franciacorta (Chardonnay, Pinot Nero and Pinot Bianco), Valtellina (Nebbiolo, called Chiavennasca), and Oltrepò Pavese (Pinot Nero).

## Key wines
- Franciacorta
- Valtellina Superiore
- Sforzato di Valtellina
- Oltrepò Pavese

## What to look for
- Franciacorta is made by the traditional method.
- Valtellina Nebbiolo is lighter and more elegant than Barolo.
