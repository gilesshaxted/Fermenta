# Kefraya

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A well known wine village in the western Bekaa, home to one of Lebanon's largest estates.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Lebanon]]
> - **Part of:** [[Bekaa Valley]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, clay, gravel
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Cinsault]], [[Syrah]], [[Carignan]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
A western part of the valley on the lower slopes below Mount Lebanon, with vineyards at about 900 metres.

## Soil and geology
Chalky limestone and clay with stones.

## Climate
Warm days and cool nights, with a dry growing season.

## History
Chateau Kefraya was founded in the 1950s and is among Lebanon's best known estates. It sits in the south-western Bekaa near the Qaraoun lake.

## Production and wine styles
Reds in Bordeaux and Rhone styles, plus Chardonnay and other whites and a range of rose wines.

## Key wines
- Chateau Kefraya
- Kefraya red blends

## What to look for
- Medium to full reds with spice and dark fruit.
- Often blends of several grapes.
