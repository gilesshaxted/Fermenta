# Roditis

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A pink-skinned grape that is one of Greece's most planted whites, with crisp, fresh wines.

## Where it's from
Widely grown in the Peloponnese, Thessaly and Macedonia. It is the main grape in retsina in some areas.

## Where it grows
- **Peloponnese, Greece:** Patras and Mantinia
- **Thessaly, Greece:** also planted

## What it tastes like
- Lemon, green apple, herbs and mineral

## Classic wines
- Patras Roditis
- Retsina

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Salads
- Feta

## Good to know
- Does best at altitude.
- Often blended with Savatiano.
