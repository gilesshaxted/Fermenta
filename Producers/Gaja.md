# Gaja

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family estate in Barbaresco that did more than anyone to bring Piedmont to the world's attention.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Barbaresco]], [[Piedmont]]
> - **Type:** Major producer

## About
Based in the village of Barbaresco, Gaja is a family-run estate with a reputation for exacting, modern-minded winemaking. The wines are polished and age-worthy, and the single vineyard bottlings are among the most sought after in Italy.
