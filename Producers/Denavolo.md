# Denavolo

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Piacenza estate making structured orange wines from long skin maceration.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Emilia-Romagna]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Malvasia]], [[Trebbiano]]
> - **Styles:** [[Orange wine]], [[Natural wine]]

## About
Giulio Armani makes white wines from local grapes that spend weeks on their skins. The results are textured and grippy, with notes of tea and dried fruit.
