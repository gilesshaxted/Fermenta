# Blaufränkisch

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Peppery, fresh, fine-grained red from central Europe, Austria's top red.

## Where it's from
Central Europe. Its name roughly means 'blue Frankish'. It is a parent of Zweigelt.

## Where it grows
- **Burgenland, Austria:** the heartland, especially Mittelburgenland
- **Württemberg, Germany:** known as Lemberger

## What it tastes like
- Blackberry, pepper, violet and a stony edge

## Classic wines
- Leithaberg DAC
- Mittelburgenland DAC
- Württemberg Lemberger

## Pairs with
- Roast duck
- Game
- Grilled sausages

## Good to know
- Called Kékfrankos in Hungary.
- Often compared with Syrah or Cabernet Franc.
