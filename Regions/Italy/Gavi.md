# Gavi

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Crisp, citrusy white from Cortese, one of Italy's best-known whites.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Piedmont]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, marl
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cortese]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills in the south-east of Piedmont near Liguria, close to the town of Gavi.

## Soil and geology
Clay and calcareous marl, with some iron-rich soil.

## Climate
Continental, with some influence from the Ligurian sea breezes.

## History
Gavi was among the first Italian whites to gain international popularity in the 1970s.

## Production and wine styles
100% Cortese. Gavi di Gavi comes from the commune itself.

## Key wines
- Gavi
- Gavi di Gavi

## What to look for
- Lemon, green apple and a mineral, slightly bitter finish.
- Excellent with seafood.

- [[Cascina degli Ulivi]]
