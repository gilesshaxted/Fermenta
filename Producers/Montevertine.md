# Montevertine

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cult Radda estate making delicate, ethereal Sangiovese, famously outside the Chianti Classico rules.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Chianti Classico]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sangiovese]]

## About
A small, traditional estate high in the hills of Radda in Chianti. The wines are pure, graceful and age very well, and are bottled as simple Tuscan reds by choice.

## Known for
- Le Pergole Torte
- Montevertine
- Pian del Ciampolo
