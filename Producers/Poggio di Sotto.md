# Poggio di Sotto

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small, revered Brunello producer known for pure, graceful and long lived wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Montalcino]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sangiovese]]

## About
A small estate on the southern side of Montalcino. The wines are made in a traditional manner with long ageing in large oak, and are prized for their finesse.

## Known for
- Brunello di Montalcino
- Brunello di Montalcino Riserva
- Rosso di Montalcino
