# Retsina

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Greek white wine flavoured with pine resin, a tradition thousands of years old.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Category:** Still
> - **Colour:** White
> - **Sweetness:** Dry
> - **Country:** Greece
> - **Main grapes:** [[Savatiano]], [[Roditis]], [[Assyrtiko]]
> - **Regions:** [[Attica]], [[Macedonia]], [[Greece]]

## How it's made
Pine resin from Aleppo pine was added to seal amphorae in antiquity. Today small amounts are added during fermentation for a piney, refreshing flavour.

## Where it's made
Attica is the heartland, using Savatiano, and Roditis is used in other areas.

## What it tastes like
- Pine, lemon, herbs
- Crisp and refreshing

## Pairs with
- Meze
- Fried fish
- Feta
- Souvlaki

## Good to know
- Serve well chilled.
- Modern producers are making finer, more subtle versions.
