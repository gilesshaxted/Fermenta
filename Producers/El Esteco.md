# El Esteco

> [!abstract] At a glance
> One of the best known producers in Cafayate, making floral Torrontés and tannic mountain reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Argentina]]
> - **Region:** [[Cafayate]], [[Salta]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Torrontés]], [[Malbec]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## About
Based in the high altitude Calchaquí Valleys of Salta, using old vineyards at a range of elevations. The whites are aromatic and fresh, the reds firm and warming.

## Known for
- Old Vines Torrontés
- Old Vines Cabernet Sauvignon
- Don David
