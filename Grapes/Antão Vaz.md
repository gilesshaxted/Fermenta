# Antão Vaz

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Alentejo's key white grape, with a fuller body and good acidity.

## Where it's from
Grown around Vidigueira, and a heat-tolerant white.

## Where it grows
- **Vidigueira, Portugal:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Peach, citrus, tropical fruit and herbs

## Classic wines
- Alentejo branco

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Roast chicken
- Rich fish

## Good to know
- Holds its acidity in heat.
- Good with oak.
