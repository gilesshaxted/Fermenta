# Santa Lucia Highlands

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A bench on the western slopes above the Salinas Valley, making rich Pinot Noir.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[Monterey County]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** granite, gravel, sandy loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Syrah]]

## Landscape and topography
A bench of land that faces east across the Salinas Valley.

## Soil and geology
Well-drained granitic gravels and sandy loam.

## Climate
Cool and windy, with morning sunshine and strong afternoon wind.

## History
Became an AVA in 1992 and attracted ambitious Pinot growers.

## Production and wine styles
Pinot Noir, Chardonnay and Syrah.

## Key wines
- Santa Lucia Highlands Pinot Noir

## What to look for
- Dark, spicy, concentrated Pinot Noir.
- Small berries and high intensity.
