# Bannockburn

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A warm, dry sub-region on the Kawarau River, with richly structured Pinot Noir.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[New Zealand]]
> - **Part of:** [[Central Otago]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** schist, gravel, silt
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]]

## Landscape and topography
Sheltered slopes by the Kawarau, on old gold workings.

## Soil and geology
Schist, gravel and silt.

## Climate
The warmest and driest part of Central Otago.

## History
One of the first plantings of Pinot Noir in the region.

## Production and wine styles
Pinot Noir, mostly.

## Key wines
- Bannockburn Pinot Noir

## What to look for
- Ripe dark fruit and spice.
- Fuller than most of Central Otago.
