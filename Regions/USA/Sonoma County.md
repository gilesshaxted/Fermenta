# Sonoma County

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A varied, less showy neighbour to Napa, with cool coastal Pinot and Chardonnay and warm inland Zinfandel and Cabernet.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[North America]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** clay, loam, gravel, volcanic, sandstone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Zinfandel]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Syrah]]

## Landscape and topography
A large county of coastal ranges, river valleys and hills, running from the Pacific inland.

## Soil and geology
Greatly varied, from coastal sandstone and clay to gravel, loam and volcanic soils.

## Climate
Cool and foggy on the coast, much warmer inland.

## History
A Russian settlement at Fort Ross and Hungarian-born Agoston Haraszthy's plantings at Buena Vista (1857) were early landmarks.

## Production and wine styles
Pinot Noir and Chardonnay (cooler places), Zinfandel and Cabernet (warmer ones), plus sparkling wine and Syrah.

## Key wines
- Sonoma Coast Pinot Noir
- Russian River Chardonnay
- Dry Creek Zinfandel

## What to look for
- Often better value than Napa.
- Sub-appellations make a big difference.
