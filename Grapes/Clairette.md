# Clairette

> [!abstract] At a glance
> An old southern French white that is low in acid and often used in blends.

## Where it's from
Native to the south of France, it appears in Cassis, Châteauneuf-du-Pape and Languedoc. Clairette de Die is a sparkling wine from the Rhône.

## Where it grows
- **Cassis and Provence, France:** in blends
- **Rhône, France:** also found in Die

## What it tastes like
- Lemon, herbs, white flowers and almond

## Classic wines
- Clairette de Die
- Cassis blanc

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Aperitifs

## Good to know
- Prone to oxidation.
- Good in blends.
