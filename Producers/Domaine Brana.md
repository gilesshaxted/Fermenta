# Domaine Brana

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A respected Irouléguy family estate in the Basque Country, known for sturdy reds and fine whites.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Irouléguy]], [[South-West France]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tannat]], [[Cabernet Franc]], [[Petit Manseng]]

## About
Brana works the steep, terraced slopes of Irouléguy at the foot of the Pyrenees. Reds are built on Tannat and Cabernet, and the whites use Manseng grapes.
