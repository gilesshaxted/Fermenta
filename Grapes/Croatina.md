# Croatina

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A dark, soft red grape widely grown in Oltrepò Pavese.

## Where it's from
Grown in northern Italy, mostly in the Oltrepò Pavese region in southern Lombardy. It is also used in blends in Piedmont, where it may be called Bonarda.

## Where it grows
- **Oltrepò Pavese, Italy:** for Bonarda dell'Oltrepò

## What it tastes like
- Blackberry, cherry, violet and spice

## Classic wines
- Bonarda dell'Oltrepò Pavese

## Pairs with
- Salami
- Pasta
- Risotto

## Good to know
- Sometimes called Bonarda locally.
- Often slightly frizzante.
