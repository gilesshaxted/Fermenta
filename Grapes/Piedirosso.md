# Piedirosso

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A light, perfumed red of the Bay of Naples, often on volcanic soil.

## Where it's from
Called Per'e Palummo (pigeon's foot) because the stems are red. It is grown around Vesuvius and the Phlegraean Fields.

## Where it grows
- **Campania, Italy:** on volcanic soils

## What it tastes like
- Red cherry, pepper, herbs and earth

## Classic wines
- Lacryma Christi
- Campi Flegrei Piedirosso

## Pairs with
- Pizza
- Seafood stew
- Pasta

## Good to know
- Often partnered with Aglianico.
- Serve lightly chilled.
