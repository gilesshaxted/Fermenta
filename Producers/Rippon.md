# Rippon

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A scenic, biodynamic family estate on Lake Wanaka in Central Otago.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[New Zealand]]
> - **Region:** [[Central Otago]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer

## About
One of the older estates in the region, farmed biodynamically with a very hands-off approach. The Pinot Noir is delicate and silky.
