# Tavel

> [!abstract] At a glance
> France's most serious dry rosé, deep in colour and structured enough for food.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Rhône Valley]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, sand, galets roulés
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grenache]], [[Cinsault]], [[Syrah]]
> - **Styles:** [[Rosé]]

## Landscape and topography
A flat to gently sloping area on the right bank near Avignon.

## Soil and geology
Limestone, sand and pebbly galets.

## Climate
Mediterranean, hot and dry.

## History
Tavel was allegedly favoured by kings and writers. It is the only Rhône Cru dedicated to rosé.

## Production and wine styles
Rosé only, from Grenache, Cinsault and other permitted grapes, with a notable skin contact to give colour and structure.

## Key wines
- Tavel rosé

## What to look for
- Strawberry, spice and herbal notes, with a firm, dry finish.
- A rosé for the table, not just the terrace.
