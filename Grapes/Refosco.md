# Refosco

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A dark, tannic red of Friuli, often called Refosco dal Peduncolo Rosso.

## Where it's from
A family of related grapes grown in north-east Italy and Slovenia. The best known is Refosco dal Peduncolo Rosso.

## Where it grows
- **Friuli, Italy:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Blackberry, plum, violet and spice

## Classic wines
- Refosco dal Peduncolo Rosso

## Pairs with
- Game
- Grilled meat
- Hard cheese

## Good to know
- Named for its red stalks.
- Tannic when young.
