# Domaine Gauby

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A biodynamic Roussillon estate that did much to put the region on the fine wine map.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Roussillon]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grenache]], [[Carignan]], [[Syrah]], [[Macabeo]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]]

## About
A family domaine in the Agly valley, farming with great care and making wines of freshness and precision from old vines. The whites are particularly admired.

## Known for
- Muntada
- Coume Gineste
- Vieilles Vignes Blanc
