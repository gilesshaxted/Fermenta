# Primorska

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Slovenia's western wine region, sunny and Adriatic-influenced, sitting right on the Italian border with Friuli.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Slovenia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** flysch, marl, sandstone, limestone, terra rossa
> - **Main grapes:** [[Ribolla Gialla]], [[Malvasia]], [[Refosco]], [[Merlot]], [[Pinela]], [[Zelen]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills, river valleys and a limestone plateau between the Julian Alps and the Adriatic, much of it running across the border into Italy.

## Soil and geology
Flysch, a layered marl and sandstone known locally as opoka, is the key soil, with limestone and red terra rossa on the Karst.

## Climate
Warm and sunny, with Mediterranean air from the sea meeting cooler air from the Alps, and the bora wind in some areas.

## History
Wine has been made here since Roman times, and the area shares its history and grapes with Friuli across the border. Skin-contact white wines have become a modern trademark.

## Production and wine styles
Rebula (Ribolla Gialla), Malvazija, Merlot, Teran and local whites such as Pinela and Zelen, with skin-contact orange wines a speciality.

## Key wines
- Goriška Brda
- Vipava Valley
- Kras
- Slovenska Istra

## What to look for
- Orange and skin-contact whites from Rebula.
- Close cousins of Friulian wines, often at lower prices.
