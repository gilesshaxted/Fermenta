# Franken

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Home of earthy, dry Silvaner in distinctive flat, squat bocksbeutel bottles.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Germany]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** muschelkalk, keuper, sandstone, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sylvaner|Silvaner]], [[Müller-Thurgau]], [[Riesling]]

## Landscape and topography
Vineyards along the Main river in northern Bavaria, centred around Würzburg, with hilltop sites such as Würzburger Stein.

## Soil and geology
Muschelkalk (shelly limestone), Keuper clay and sandstone.

## Climate
Continental, with colder winters and shorter summers than other German regions.

## History
The Würzburg Residence and its Hofkeller were important wine estates. The distinctive bocksbeutel bottle dates to the 18th century.

## Production and wine styles
Silvaner is the star, mostly dry. Müller-Thurgau and Riesling are also grown.

## Key wines
- Franken Silvaner
- Würzburger Stein
- Bocksbeutel wines

## What to look for
- Earthy, herbal, round and food-friendly.
- A great partner for white asparagus.
