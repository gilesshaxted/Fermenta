# La Rioja Alta

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Haro based traditionalist famous for Gran Reservas aged for years in American oak.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Region:** [[Rioja Alta]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Tempranillo]], [[Graciano]]

## About
One of the pillars of the Haro station district, making elegant, savoury, slow evolving reds in a deliberately classical style. The house makes its own barrels and releases wines only when they are ready.

## Known for
- Gran Reserva 904
- Gran Reserva 890
- Viña Ardanza
- Viña Alberdi
