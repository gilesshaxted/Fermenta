# Cornas

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Dark, powerful, rustic Syrah from steep granite amphitheatres, once the Rhône's best-kept secret.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Rhône Valley]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** granite
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]]

## Landscape and topography
Steep south and south-east facing granite slopes in an amphitheatre shape that traps heat.

## Soil and geology
Decomposed granite, which gives structure and minerality.

## Climate
Continental and warm, with shelter from the wind.

## History
Cornas was neglected for decades but Auguste Clape and Thierry Allemand have made it a cult name.

## Production and wine styles
100% Syrah. The wines are dense, tannic and need time.

## Key wines
- Cornas

## What to look for
- Black fruit, tar, smoke and graphite.
- Cellar for a decade or more.

## Producers
- [[Auguste Clape]]
- [[Thierry Allemand]]
