# Australia

Part of [[Oceania]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> From warm Shiraz country to cool Tasmanian sparkling wine, Australia is one of the world's most varied and inventive producers.

## How Australian wine is organised
- **GI** (Geographical Indication) areas are zones, regions and sub-regions, with state names (South Australia, Victoria) used for wide blending.
- A label naming a region or grape must have at least 85% of the wine from it.
- Blending of grapes and regions is traditional, as in Penfolds Grange.
- **Shiraz** is Syrah.
