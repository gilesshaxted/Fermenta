# Cava (style)

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Spain's traditional method sparkling wine, mainly from Catalonia.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Category:** Sparkling
> - **Colour:** White
> - **Sweetness:** Dry
> - **Country:** Spain
> - **Main grapes:** [[Macabeo]], [[Xarel·lo]], [[Parellada]]
> - **Regions:** [[Cava]], [[Penedès]]

## How it's made
Made by the traditional method with a second fermentation in the bottle. Cava must age at least nine months on lees, and Reserva and Gran Reserva age for much longer.

## Where it's made
About 95% comes from the Penedès in Catalonia, though the name covers several parts of Spain.

## What it tastes like
- Lemon, green apple, almond
- Earthy, herbal notes from Xarel·lo
- Creamy bubbles with time on lees

## Pairs with
- Tapas
- Fried seafood
- Jamón
- Paella

## Good to know
- Xarel·lo gives the structure, Macabeo the fruit and Parellada the freshness.
- Good Cava is excellent value compared with Champagne.
