# Purcari

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A village and wine estate in the south-east, with a long reputation for red wines, notably Bordeaux-style blends and Rară Neagră.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Republic of Moldova]]
> - **Part of:** [[Ștefan Vodă]]
> - **Climate:** Warm continental
> - **Soils:** black earth, clay, limestone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Rară Neagră]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
Gentle slopes close to the Dniester in the south-east of the Republic of Moldova.

## Soil and geology
Black earth over clay and limestone.

## Climate
Warm, with the Dniester and Black Sea giving some moderation.

## History
Purcari has made wine since the 19th century and has long been known for its red blends.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot blends and Rară Neagră reds, plus some Chardonnay.

## Key wines
- Purcari Negru de Purcari
- Purcari Rară Neagră
- Purcari Cabernet Sauvignon

## What to look for
- Dark, structured red blends.
- Negru de Purcari is the local classic.
