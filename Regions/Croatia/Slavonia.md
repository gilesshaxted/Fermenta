# Slavonia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> An eastern plain of oak forests and wine cellars, best known for Graševina and its barrels of Slavonian oak.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Croatia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** loam, clay, loess, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Welschriesling]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Riesling]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Pinot Gris]]

## Landscape and topography
Gently rolling hills and plains in the north east, between the Sava and Drava rivers.

## Soil and geology
Loam, clay and loess.

## Climate
Continental, with warm summers and cold winters.

## History
Slavonian oak from this region is prized by cooperages in Europe. Kutjevo is a famous wine town with medieval cellars founded by monks.

## Production and wine styles
Mostly white, with Graševina (Welschriesling) the dominant grape, plus Riesling, Chardonnay and Pinot Gris.

## Key wines
- Kutjevo
- Slavonian Graševina
- Erdut

## What to look for
- Graševina is Croatia's most planted grape.
- Easy, fruity, good value whites.
