# Dry Creek Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A warm valley, long a Zinfandel stronghold, with some of California's oldest vines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[Sonoma County]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** gravel, clay, loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Zinfandel]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]]

## Landscape and topography
A narrow valley with benches and hillsides north of Healdsburg.

## Soil and geology
Gravelly alluvial soils on the floor, with red clay on the hills.

## Climate
Warm days with cooling afternoon fog from the south.

## History
Italian immigrant families planted Zinfandel and other varieties here in the late 19th century.

## Production and wine styles
Zinfandel, Cabernet Sauvignon, Sauvignon Blanc and Rhône varieties.

## Key wines
- Dry Creek Zinfandel
- Dry Creek Sauvignon Blanc

## What to look for
- Ripe berry and pepper in Zinfandel.
- Old vine wines have extra depth.
