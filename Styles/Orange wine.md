# Orange wine

> [!abstract] At a glance
> White grapes made like a red, with the juice left on the skins.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Category:** Still
> - **Colour:** Orange
> - **Sweetness:** Dry
> - **Country:** Various
> - **Main grapes:** [[Rkatsiteli]], [[Ribolla Gialla]], [[Friulano]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Malvasia]], [[Malvazija Istarska]]
> - **Regions:** [[Kakheti]], [[Friuli-Venezia Giulia]], [[Collio]], [[Colli Orientali del Friuli]], [[Goriška Brda]], [[Vipava Valley]], [[Istria]], [[Styria]], [[Südsteiermark]]

## How it's made
White grapes ferment and macerate with their skins for days, weeks or months. The skins give colour, tannin and a savoury, nutty character. Some producers use clay vessels (qvevri or amphorae).

## Where it's made
Georgia is the original home, with amber wine made in qvevri for thousands of years. Friuli and the Collio hills lead in Italy, and the style has spread to Slovenia, Croatia, Austria and elsewhere.

## What it tastes like
- Dried apricot, orange peel, tea
- Nutty, savoury, grippy tannin
- Cloudy amber colour

## Pairs with
- Spicy food
- Charcuterie
- Fermented flavours
- Hard cheese

## Good to know
- Orange refers to the colour, not the fruit.
- Wines can be hard to serve too cold. Cellar temperature works best.
