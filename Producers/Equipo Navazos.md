# Equipo Navazos

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A negociant project that selects individual casks of sherry and bottles them without heavy filtering.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Spain]]
> - **Region:** [[Jerez]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Palomino]], [[Pedro Ximénez]]
> - **Styles:** [[Sherry]]

## About
Equipo Navazos helped make unfortified, cask-specific sherry fashionable among wine lovers worldwide. Each La Bota release comes from a particular cask or solera.

## Known for
- La Bota de Manzanilla Pasada
- La Bota de Fino
- La Bota de Palo Cortado
