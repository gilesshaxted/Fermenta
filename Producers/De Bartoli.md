# De Bartoli

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small estate that revived traditional Marsala and made it a serious fine wine.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Marsala]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grillo]], [[Catarratto]]
> - **Styles:** [[Marsala (style)]], [[Passito]]

## About
A family estate near Marsala that is known for unfortified, oxidative and aged Marsala wines. The best are complex, nutty and long lived.

## Known for
- Vecchio Samperi
- Marsala Superiore Riserva 10 Anni
- Bukkuram Passito di Pantelleria
- Integer
