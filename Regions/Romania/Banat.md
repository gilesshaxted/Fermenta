# Banat

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A western region on the Hungarian and Serbian borders, with warm, dry conditions and Austro-Hungarian influence.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Romania]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** clay, sand, limestone, loess
> - **Main grapes:** [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Riesling]], [[Muscat Ottonel]]

## Landscape and topography
Plains and hills in the west, with Recaș the main area.

## Soil and geology
Clay, sand, limestone and loess.

## Climate
Warm and dry with a Pannonian influence, with early ripening.

## History
Austro-Hungarian influence shaped the area's wine. Cramele Recaș has become one of Romania's largest and best-known exporters.

## Production and wine styles
Merlot, Cabernet Sauvignon, Pinot Noir and Riesling, with Muscat Ottonel and others.

## Key wines
- Recaș wines
- Banat Riesling
- Banat Merlot

## What to look for
- Soft, fruity reds and aromatic whites.
- A lot of value.
