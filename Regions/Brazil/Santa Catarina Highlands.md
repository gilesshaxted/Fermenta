# Santa Catarina Highlands

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A high altitude cool climate area in southern Brazil, with some of the country's most elegant wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Brazil]]
> - **Part of:** [[South America]]
> - **Climate:** Cool humid subtropical
> - **Soils:** basalt, clay, loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
Plateau and highland vineyards in Santa Catarina, around São Joaquim, often above 1,000 metres.

## Soil and geology
Basalt based soils with clay and loam.

## Climate
Cool, with cold winters and long ripening seasons. Frost can be a risk.

## History
Altitude vineyards were developed from the late twentieth century to make fresher wines than the lowlands.

## Production and wine styles
Elegant reds and whites with fresh acidity.

## Key wines
- Santa Catarina highland reds
- Altitude Sauvignon Blanc

## What to look for
- One of the highest wine areas in Brazil.
- Cooler, fresher wines.
