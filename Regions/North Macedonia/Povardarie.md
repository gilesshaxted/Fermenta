# Povardarie

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The Vardar river valley, which holds most of North Macedonia's vineyards and its largest wineries.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[North Macedonia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** clay, sand, gravel, granite
> - **Main grapes:** [[Vranac]], [[Žilavka]], [[Kratošija]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Smederevka]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## Landscape and topography
A broad river valley running through the centre and south of the country, between mountain ranges.

## Soil and geology
Clay, sand and gravel, with granite and other soils on the hill slopes.

## Climate
Hot, sunny summers with a Mediterranean influence coming up the valley, and cold winters in the highlands.

## History
Wine has been made in the Vardar valley since antiquity, and the area is rich in sunshine. After independence, a number of private wineries replaced the old state-run ones.

## Production and wine styles
Mostly red, with Vranac and Kratošija, plus white grapes such as Žilavka and Smederevka, and international varieties.

## Key wines
- Tikveš
- Povardarie Vranec
- Povardarie Žilavka

## What to look for
- Rich reds with dark fruit.
- Great value.
