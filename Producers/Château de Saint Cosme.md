# Château de Saint Cosme

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A historic Gigondas estate also making Syrah-based wines from the northern Rhône.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Gigondas]], [[Rhône Valley]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grenache]], [[Syrah]], [[Mourvèdre]], [[Viognier]]

## About
A family estate producing structured Gigondas from old vines, plus a négociant range. The reds are dark and spicy and can age for many years.

## Known for
- Gigondas
- Gigondas Hominis Fides
- Condrieu
