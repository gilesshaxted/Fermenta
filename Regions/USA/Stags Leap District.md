# Stags Leap District

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small eastern district where Cabernet is silkier and more perfumed than most of Napa.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[Napa Valley]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** volcanic, loam, alluvial
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]]

## Landscape and topography
A narrow strip on the eastern side of the valley, beneath rocky palisades.

## Soil and geology
Volcanic soils, with alluvial fans from the hills.

## Climate
Cooler afternoons because of breezes funnelled through the palisades.

## History
A Stag's Leap Wine Cellars Cabernet won the red wine section of the 1976 Judgment of Paris.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon and Merlot.

## Key wines
- Stags Leap Cabernet Sauvignon

## What to look for
- Silky tannins and red and black fruit.
- Refined rather than massive.

- [[Stag's Leap Wine Cellars]]
