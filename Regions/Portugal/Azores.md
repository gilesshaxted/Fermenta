# Azores

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Remote Atlantic islands with vines growing in lava-walled plots, making salty, mineral whites.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Portugal]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Maritime
> - **Soils:** volcanic, basalt, lava
> - **Main grapes:** [[Arinto dos Açores]], [[Verdelho]], [[Terrantez do Pico]]

## Landscape and topography
Volcanic islands in the Atlantic, with vineyards on Pico enclosed by stone walls (currais) in a UNESCO World Heritage landscape.

## Soil and geology
Volcanic basalt and lava.

## Climate
Maritime, humid and windy, with sea spray.

## History
Verdelho from Pico was once exported to the Russian court. The vineyards were nearly abandoned and have been revived.

## Production and wine styles
Arinto dos Açores, Verdelho and Terrantez do Pico for whites, with distinctive salty minerality.

## Key wines
- Pico Arinto dos Açores
- Pico Verdelho

## What to look for
- Saline, citrus and volcanic.
- Rare and exciting.
