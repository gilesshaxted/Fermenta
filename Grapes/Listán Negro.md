# Listán Negro

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A light, volcanic red from the Canary Islands, grown on old vines.

## Where it's from
Probably brought from Spain by settlers in the 16th century. It thrives on volcanic soils in Tenerife and La Palma.

## Where it grows
- **Tenerife, Spain:** on volcanic slopes

## What it tastes like
- Red cherry, spice, earth and herbs

## Classic wines
- Tenerife red
- Ycoden-Daute-Isora

## Pairs with
- Grilled fish
- Pork
- Local cheese

## Good to know
- Often made by carbonic maceration.
- Has a link to Mission and País.
