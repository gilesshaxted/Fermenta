# Domaines Vinsmoselle

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The union of Luxembourg's wine cooperatives, a major force on the Moselle.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Luxembourg]]
> - **Region:** [[Moselle Luxembourgeoise]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]], [[Auxerrois]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Pinot Blanc]]
> - **Styles:** [[Crémant]]

## About
Vinsmoselle brings together growers' cooperatives along the Luxembourg Moselle. It produces a wide range of crisp whites and sparkling Crémant.

## Known for
- Poll-Fabaire Crémant de Luxembourg
- Wormeldange Koeppchen Riesling
