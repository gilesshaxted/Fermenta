# Zuccardi

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family estate in the Uco Valley known for terroir focused wines that explore stony, high altitude sites.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Argentina]]
> - **Region:** [[Uco Valley]], [[Mendoza]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Malbec]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Cabernet Franc]]

## About
Based in Mendoza, with a strong focus on soil, limestone and fresh, restrained wine making. Concrete and larger old vessels are used alongside more modern approaches, and the wines are mostly about freshness and mineral feel.

## Known for
- Concreto
- Aluvional
- Piedra Infinita
- Poligonos
