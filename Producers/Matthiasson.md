# Matthiasson

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Napa family winery known for low alcohol, fresh, restrained wines and organic farming.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Region:** [[Napa Valley]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention

> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]]

## About
Matthiasson farms organically and favours early picking and a light hand in the cellar. The style is fresh and savoury, and quite unlike the big Napa norm.

## Known for
- Linda Vista Chardonnay
- Napa Valley White Wine
- Refosco
