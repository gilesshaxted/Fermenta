# Xarel·lo

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Catalonia's top Cava grape, with body, earthy depth and good ageing potential.

## Where it's from
Penedès in Catalonia, where it is the structural backbone of Cava.

## Where it grows
- **Penedès, Spain:** the home

## What it tastes like
- Apple, fennel and earthy minerality

## Classic wines
- Cava
- Penedès whites
- Corpinnat

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Paella
- Grilled fish

## Good to know
- Pronounced sheh-REH-yo.
- The most characterful of the three Cava grapes.
