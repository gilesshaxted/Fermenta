# Vugava

> [!abstract] At a glance
> An aromatic white from the island of Vis, with apricot notes and good weight.

## Where it's from
Grown on Vis for centuries and the island's signature white, with plantings also in Dalmatia.

## Where it grows
- **Vis, Croatia:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Apricot, peach, herbs and spice

## Classic wines
- Viški Vugava

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Grilled fish
- Aperitif

## Good to know
- A rare grape.
- Often compared to Viognier for its aromatics.
