# Kyperounda Winery

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A high altitude Troodos winery making some of the island's freshest whites.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Cyprus]]
> - **Region:** [[Troodos]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Xynisteri]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## About
Its vineyards sit high in the Troodos Mountains, which gives cooler conditions than the coast. It works mostly with Xynisteri and international red grapes.

- Petritis
- Andessitis
