# Solaris

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A modern, early-ripening hybrid grape suited to cold climates.

## Where it's from
Created in Germany in 1975 and released in the 1990s. It ripens very early and resists disease.

## Where it grows
- **England:** in the cooler counties
- **Northern Europe:** a growing number of vineyards

## What it tastes like
- Lemon, peach, tropical fruit and spice

## Classic wines
- Solaris still white
- Solaris sweet wine

## Pairs with
- Salads
- Fish
- Fruit desserts

## Good to know
- High sugar levels allow good sweet wines.
- Planted in many cold places.
