# Thierry Allemand

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cult Cornas grower making Syrah by hand with minimal intervention and no added sulphur in the best cuvée.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Cornas]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]]

## About
A tiny domaine working old vines on steep granite terraces, with whole bunch fermentation and little new oak. The wines are wild, perfumed and very sought after.
