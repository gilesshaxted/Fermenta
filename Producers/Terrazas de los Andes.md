# Terrazas de los Andes

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A large Mendoza winery working high altitude vineyards in Luján de Cuyo and the Uco Valley.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Argentina]]
> - **Region:** [[Luján de Cuyo]], [[Uco Valley]]
> - **Type:** Major producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Malbec]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Chardonnay]]

## About
Part of a bigger international wine group, but with a reputation for site driven bottlings. The range runs from everyday Malbec to more serious single vineyard wines.
