# Château Kefraya

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A large estate in the Kefraya area of the Bekaa, known for Mediterranean-style red blends.

## About
The estate works with a mix of French varieties in the western Bekaa. It makes a broad range, led by blends in the Rhône and Bordeaux manner.
