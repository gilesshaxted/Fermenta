# Minervois

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Terraced hills north of the Canal du Midi, producing medium-bodied, spicy reds and a few fine whites.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Part of:** [[Languedoc]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, schist, clay, sandstone
> - **Main grapes:** [[Syrah]], [[Grenache]], [[Carignan]], [[Mourvèdre]]

## Landscape and topography
A series of terraces and limestone plateaux rising to the Montagne Noire, with vines at various altitudes.

## Soil and geology
Limestone and clay on the plateau, schist in the north, and sandstone in places.

## Climate
Mediterranean in the south, cooler in the higher north.

## History
Minervois La Livinière is the top cru, a village prized for ageworthy reds.

## Production and wine styles
Mostly red blends, with some white, rosé and sweet Muscat de Saint-Jean-de-Minervois.

## Key wines
- Minervois
- Minervois La Livinière

## What to look for
- Red fruit, herbs and spice.
- La Livinière is more structured and concentrated.
