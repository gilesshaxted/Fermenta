# Bernhard Huber

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Baden estate that helped put German Spätburgunder on the world stage.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Germany]]
> - **Region:** [[Baden]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]]

## About
A family estate in Malterdingen working limestone and shell limestone soils. The Pinot Noirs are fine, structured and Burgundian in feel, with excellent Chardonnay and Pinot Blanc too.

## Known for
- Malterdinger Spätburgunder
- Alte Reben
- Hecklinger Schlossberg
