# Pálava

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Czech cross of Gewürztraminer and Müller-Thurgau, with bold floral aromas.

## Where it's from
Bred in Moravia in the 20th century from a cross of Traminer and Müller-Thurgau, and named after the Pálava hills. It makes aromatic, often off-dry or sweet white wines.

## Where it grows
- **Moravia, Czechia:** the main area
- **Slovakia:** small plantings

## What it tastes like
- Rose, apricot, lychee and spice

## Classic wines
- Mikulov Pálava

## Pairs with
- Spicy food
- Asian dishes
- Blue cheese

## Good to know
- Named after the Pálava hills.
- Aromatic like Gewürztraminer but easier to grow.
