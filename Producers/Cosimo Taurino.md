# Cosimo Taurino

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A pioneering Salento producer that showed the quality of Negroamaro.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Salento]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Negroamaro]], [[Malvasia Nera]]

## About
A family estate in Guagnano, in the heart of Salice Salentino. The reds are deep, earthy and long lived.

## Known for
- Patriglione
- Notarpanaro
- Salice Salentino Riserva
