# Nerello Mascalese

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Pale, perfumed and mineral. Etna's answer to Pinot Noir and Nebbiolo.

## Where it's from
Mount Etna's slopes in Sicily.

## Where it grows
- **Etna, Sicily:** on volcanic soils at altitude

## What it tastes like
- Red cherry, smoke, rose and volcanic mineral edge

## Classic wines
- Etna Rosso

## Pairs with
- Grilled tuna
- Roast pork
- Mushroom dishes

## Good to know
- Often blended with Nerello Cappuccio.
- Great terroir expression.
