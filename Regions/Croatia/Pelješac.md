# Pelješac

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A long, rocky peninsula whose steep south-facing slopes produce Croatia's most celebrated reds, Dingač and Postup.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Croatia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Dalmatia]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** limestone, karst, terra rossa
> - **Main grapes:** [[Plavac Mali]], [[Pošip]], [[Merlot]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## Landscape and topography
A mountainous peninsula above the Adriatic, where Dingač vineyards sit on precipitous slopes above the sea.

## Soil and geology
Stony limestone soils with thin topsoil.

## Climate
Hot, dry and bright, with sunshine reflected from the sea and cooling breezes.

## History
Dingač became Croatia's first protected wine designation. Many vineyards were planted by hand on the steep slopes.

## Production and wine styles
Ripe, powerful Plavac Mali, notably from Dingač and Postup, plus some whites.

## Key wines
- Dingač
- Postup
- Pelješac Plavac Mali

## What to look for
- Powerful reds with dark fruit and spice.
- Often high in alcohol.
