# Japan

Part of [[Asia]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Japan makes wine from native and hybrid grapes as well as European varieties, with Koshu the best known quality grape.

## How wine is organised here
- A national geographical indication system covers Japanese wine, with prefecture level indications such as Yamanashi.
- Since 2018 labelling rules separate wine made from imported grape juice or concentrate from wine made from grapes grown in Japan.
- Koshu is recognised as a variety by the OIV, which helps it appear on export labels.
