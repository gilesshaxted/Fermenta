# Gérard Bertrand

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A big and widely seen Languedoc brand with a range from simple everyday wines to estate icons, run on biodynamic lines at several properties.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]

## About
A large producer with multiple estates across the south of France, covering reds, whites and rosés. It is a good guide to the Languedoc in general, and also known for its rosé Gris Blanc.
