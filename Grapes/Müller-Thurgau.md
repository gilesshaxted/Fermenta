# Müller-Thurgau

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Light, soft, aromatic white, once Germany's most planted grape.

## Where it's from
Created in 1882 by Hermann Müller of Thurgau at Geisenheim, as a cross of Riesling and Madeleine Royale.

## Where it grows
- **Germany:** across many regions
- **Alto Adige, Italy:** high-altitude versions are fresher

## What it tastes like
- Peach, floral and light citrus

## Classic wines
- Franken Müller-Thurgau
- Alto Adige Müller-Thurgau

## Pairs with
- Light salads
- Fried fish
- Pasta

## Good to know
- Early ripening makes it useful in cool climates.
- Quality varies from simple to quite elegant.
