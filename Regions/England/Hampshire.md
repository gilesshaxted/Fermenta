# Hampshire

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Chalk downland with an impressive record in sparkling wine, home of Hambledon and Hattingley Valley.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[England]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** chalk, clay, flint
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Pinot Meunier]]
> - **Styles:** [[English sparkling wine]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling chalk downland and sheltered valleys such as the Test and Meon.

## Soil and geology
Chalk with flint and clay.

## Climate
Cool maritime, a touch warmer than inland areas.

## History
Hambledon is often described as England's oldest commercial vineyard, planted in the 1950s. Hattingley Valley and Exton Park have since won acclaim.

## Production and wine styles
Traditional-method sparkling wine from the Champagne grapes.

## Key wines
- Hambledon Classic Cuvée
- Hattingley Valley Classic Reserve
- Exton Park

## What to look for
- Citrus, orchard fruit and chalky freshness.
- Many wines are on par with good Champagne.
