# San Rafael

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A southern Mendoza province area with traditional vineyards and a French-influenced history.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Argentina]]
> - **Part of:** [[South America]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** alluvial, sand, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Malbec]], [[Chenin Blanc]], [[Bonarda]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## Landscape and topography
Flat plains at about 450 to 800 metres, watered by the Atuel and Diamante rivers.

## Soil and geology
Sandy and gravelly alluvial soils.

## Climate
Warm and dry, with cooler nights.

## History
French settlers and the Chenin tradition gave San Rafael a distinct flavour.

## Production and wine styles
Malbec, Chenin Blanc and Bonarda.

## Key wines
- San Rafael Chenin Blanc
- San Rafael Malbec

## What to look for
- Often good value.
- Chenin can be excellent.
