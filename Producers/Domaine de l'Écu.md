# Domaine de l'Écu

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A biodynamic pioneer of Muscadet, making single-site wines of real depth.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Muscadet]], [[Loire Valley]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Melon de Bourgogne]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]]

## About
Domaine de l'Écu has been farmed biodynamically for many years and champions the different geology beneath Muscadet. The wines are precise and mineral, and bottled by soil type.

## Known for
- Expression de Gneiss
- Expression d'Orthogneiss
- Expression de Granite
