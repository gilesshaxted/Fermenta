# Vino di Anna

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small natural wine estate on the north slope of Etna, run by Anna Martens.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Etna]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Nerello Mascalese]], [[Carricante]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]], [[Orange wine]]

## About
An Australian born winemaker making low intervention wines from old vines, with some amphora and qvevri work. The wines are lively and expressive.

## Known for
- Palmento Rosso
- Qvevri Bianco
