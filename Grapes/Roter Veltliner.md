# Roter Veltliner

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A rare pink-skinned Austrian grape, especially at home in the Wagram.

## Where it's from
Unrelated to Grüner Veltliner despite the name. It is grown mostly in Wagram, with a few hundred hectares.

## Where it grows
- **Wagram, Austria:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Apple, herbs, spice and citrus

## Classic wines
- Wagram Roter Veltliner

## Pairs with
- Pork
- Fish
- Spicy dishes

## Good to know
- Not related to Grüner Veltliner.
- A speciality of Wagram.
