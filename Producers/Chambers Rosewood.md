# Chambers Rosewood

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A family run Rutherglen producer whose fortified Muscats and Topaques are among the finest in the world.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Australia]]
> - **Region:** [[Rutherglen]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Muscat]], [[Muscadelle]]
> - **Styles:** [[Rutherglen Muscat]]

## About
A long established estate in north-east Victoria, working with very old blends in a solera-like system. The wines are sweet, deep and complex.

## Known for
- Rare Rutherglen Muscat
- Special Muscat
- Rare Muscadelle
- Grand Muscat
