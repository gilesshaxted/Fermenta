# Umbria

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Italy's green heart, with powerful Sagrantino from Montefalco and easy Orvieto whites.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Temperate
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, tuff, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Sangiovese]], [[Sagrantino]], [[Grechetto]], [[Trebbiano]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills and valleys in the Apennines, with Montefalco's hill towns and Orvieto's volcanic tuff cliff.

## Soil and geology
Clay and limestone in Montefalco, volcanic tuff at Orvieto.

## Climate
Temperate to continental, with warm summers.

## History
Sagrantino was almost lost, then saved by Arnaldo Caprai from the 1970s. Orvieto has been drunk since Etruscan times.

## Production and wine styles
Montefalco Sagrantino is one of the most tannic reds in the world. Orvieto is a dry white blend led by Grechetto and Procanico.

## Key wines
- Montefalco Sagrantino
- Montefalco Rosso
- Orvieto Classico
- Torgiano Rosso Riserva

## What to look for
- Sagrantino is dark, tannic and powerful.
- Orvieto is crisp and everyday.
