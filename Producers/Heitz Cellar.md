## About
Heitz Cellar helped shape Napa's reputation from the 1960s. Its Cabernet is known for a distinct minty, earthy character and long life.

## Known for
- Martha's Vineyard Cabernet Sauvignon
- Trailside Vineyard Cabernet Sauvignon
