# Dão

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Granite plateau and mountain-ringed vineyards making elegant, fresh reds and fine whites.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Portugal]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** granite, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Touriga Nacional]], [[Encruzado]], [[Alfrocheiro]], [[Mencía|Jaen]]

## Landscape and topography
A high plateau around 400 to 500 metres surrounded by mountain ranges (Caramulo, Buçaco, Estrela).

## Soil and geology
Granite and sandy soils, well drained and low in fertility.

## Climate
Continental with Atlantic influence, cooler than the Douro with cold winters and warm summers.

## History
Dão was one of Portugal's first demarcated regions in 1908, and long dominated by cooperatives. Since the 1990s smaller estates have emerged.

## Production and wine styles
Touriga Nacional for reds, Encruzado for whites. Wines are known for elegance and freshness.

## Key wines
- Dão tinto
- Dão Encruzado
- Dão Reserva

## What to look for
- Red fruit, pine and mineral freshness.
- Often called Portugal's Burgundy.
