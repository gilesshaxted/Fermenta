# Voskehat

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The main native white grape of Armenia, used for dry, fortified and brandy base wines.

## Where it's from
An old Armenian grape whose name means golden berry, widely grown across the Ararat Valley.

## Where it grows
- **Ararat Valley, Armenia:** widely planted
- **Vayots Dzor, Armenia:** also grown

## What it tastes like
- Apple, pear, citrus and honey

## Classic wines
- Voskehat

## Pairs with
- Fish
- Chicken
- Soft cheese

## Good to know
- Also used for brandy.
- Fresh to rich styles.
