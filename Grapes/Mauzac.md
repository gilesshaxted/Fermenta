# Mauzac

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A grape with a distinct apple peel character, used in Limoux and Gaillac sparkling wine.

## Where it's from
Native to south-west France and one of the oldest known grapes there. It is the base of Blanquette de Limoux and Gaillac's méthode ancestrale.

## Where it grows
- **Limoux, France:** for Blanquette
- **Gaillac, France:** still and sparkling wines

## What it tastes like
- Apple, herbs, lemon and spice

## Classic wines
- Blanquette de Limoux
- Gaillac Mousseux

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Aperitifs
- Light cheese

## Good to know
- Smells of fresh apples.
- Used in some of France's oldest sparkling wines.
