# Karnataka

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A southern state with vineyards on the high plateau near Bangalore, and a long-running wine tradition.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[India]]
> - **Part of:** [[Asia]]
> - **Climate:** Tropical
> - **Soils:** red loam, sandy loam, granite, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Syrah]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Chenin Blanc]], [[Viognier]]

## Landscape and topography
A high plateau in the south, with vineyards at about 800 to 900 metres around Bangalore.

## Soil and geology
Red loam, sandy loam and granite-derived soils.

## Climate
A tropical climate cooled by altitude, with mild temperatures and two harvests possible in some places.

## History
Wine here grew from the table grape industry, with commercial plantings from the 1980s. Grover Zampa, founded near Bangalore, was among the early quality producers.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon, Syrah, Sauvignon Blanc and Chenin Blanc, mostly in a fresh, fruity style.

## Key wines
- Grover Zampa wines
- Karnataka Cabernet Sauvignon

## What to look for
- Mild climate at altitude.
- A smaller industry than Maharashtra.
