# Istria

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A heart-shaped peninsula in the northern Adriatic, with fresh Malvazija Istarska whites and Teran reds.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Croatia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** terra rossa, flysch, limestone, marl
> - **Main grapes:** [[Malvazija Istarska]], [[Refosco]], [[Merlot]], [[Muscat]], [[Chardonnay]]
> - **Styles:** [[Orange wine]]

## Landscape and topography
A rolling peninsula of red, white and grey soils, with hills inland and the sea never far away.

## Soil and geology
Terra rossa (red), flysch (grey) and white limestone soils, a famous trio of Istrian colours.

## Climate
Mediterranean, with sea breezes and cool nights, and a long, mild growing season.

## History
Istria has been growing wine since ancient times and shares its Adriatic wine culture with Italy and Slovenia. It was part of the Venetian Republic and Austria, and its modern wine scene has been developed since the 1990s.

## Production and wine styles
White and orange Malvazija Istarska, sometimes aged in oak, Teran (Refosco-type) reds and Merlot, with some Muscat and Chardonnay.

## Key wines
- Istrian Malvazija
- Istrian Teran
- Istrian Muscat

## What to look for
- Malvazija Istarska is the star white.
- Skin-contact styles and oak-aged styles both exist.
