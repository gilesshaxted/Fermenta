# Trincadeira

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A spicy, aromatic red of Alentejo and Douro, tricky to grow but characterful.

## Where it's from
Known as Tinta Amarela in the Douro. It is sensitive to rot, so needs dry conditions, which Alentejo supplies.

## Where it grows
- **Alentejo, Portugal:** the heartland
- **Douro, Portugal:** as Tinta Amarela

## What it tastes like
- Raspberry, herbs, pepper and spice

## Classic wines
- Alentejo red

## Pairs with
- Grilled meat
- Stews

## Good to know
- Aromatic and peppery.
- Often blended with Aragonez.
