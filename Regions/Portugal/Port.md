# Port

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Fortified wine from the Douro, aged in Vila Nova de Gaia lodges, in styles from ruby to tawny to vintage.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Portugal]]
> - **Part of:** [[Douro]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** schist
> - **Main grapes:** [[Touriga Nacional]], [[Touriga Franca]], [[Tempranillo|Tinta Roriz]], [[Tinta Barroca]]
> - **Styles:** [[Port (style)|Port style]]

## Landscape and topography
Steep terraces in Cima Corgo, with the wine shipped to the lodges opposite Porto.

## Soil and geology
Schist.

## Climate
Hot, dry, with Atlantic influence in the west.

## History
The wine trade with England was boosted by wars with France. Brandy was added to stop fermentation, leaving sweetness, and by the 18th century this was standard practice.

## Production and wine styles
Fermentation is stopped with grape spirit, leaving sweetness. Ruby and Reserve are fruity, Tawny is aged in barrels for nutty flavours, Vintage is the best years from bottle.

## Key wines
- Ruby Port
- Tawny Port (10, 20, 30, 40 years)
- Late Bottled Vintage
- Vintage Port
- White Port

## What to look for
- Tawny Port is wonderful with nuts and cheese.
- Vintage Port needs decanting and years of ageing.
