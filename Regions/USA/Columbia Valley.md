# Columbia Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Washington's vast semi-desert wine area, with irrigated vineyards, long sunny days and cold winters.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[North America]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** loess, basalt, sand, gravel
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Merlot]], [[Syrah]], [[Riesling]], [[Chardonnay]]

## Landscape and topography
A huge basin carved by the Missoula floods, with rolling plains and basalt hills.

## Soil and geology
Windblown loess over ancient basalt, with sandy and gravelly flood deposits.

## Climate
Dry and sunny, with very little rain, hot days and cool nights. Winters can be very cold.

## History
Vines were planted in the 1960s. Chateau Ste. Michelle helped establish the industry, and quality took off in the 1990s.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Syrah, Riesling and Chardonnay.

## Key wines
- Columbia Valley Cabernet Sauvignon
- Washington Riesling
- Washington Syrah

## What to look for
- Ripe, balanced fruit with good acidity.
- Washington is the second largest wine state.
