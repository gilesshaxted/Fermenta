# Miani

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A tiny cult estate in Buttrio making rare, deeply concentrated Friulian wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Colli Orientali del Friuli]], [[Friuli-Venezia Giulia]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Friulano]], [[Merlot]], [[Refosco]]

## About
Enzo Pontoni works very low yields and bottles small quantities. The wines have a following among collectors and show unusual power for the area.
