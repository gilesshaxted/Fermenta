# Château Ksara

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Lebanon's oldest and largest winery, in the Bekaa Valley, known for its historic cellars.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Lebanon]]

## About
Ksara has underground Roman-era caves used for ageing. It makes a wide range from everyday wines to serious reds.
