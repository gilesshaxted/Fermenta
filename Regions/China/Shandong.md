# Shandong

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A coastal province on the Yellow Sea and China's oldest big wine centre, with the Penglai and Yantai areas.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[China]]
> - **Part of:** [[Asia]]
> - **Climate:** Warm continental
> - **Soils:** sandy loam, brown earth, clay, gravel
> - **Main grapes:** [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Cabernet Gernischt]], [[Merlot]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Riesling]]

## Landscape and topography
Coastal plains and low hills on the Shandong peninsula, near the sea.

## Soil and geology
Sandy loam, brown earth and gravelly clay.

## Climate
Warm and humid, with monsoon rain in summer and a maritime influence from the sea. Rain at harvest is a risk.

## History
Changyu was founded in Yantai in 1892, and is among the oldest large wine companies in China. Foreign grape varieties were planted here in the late nineteenth century.

## Production and wine styles
Cabernet Sauvignon, Cabernet Gernischt and Merlot reds, with Chardonnay and Riesling whites and sparkling wine.

## Key wines
- Yantai Cabernet Gernischt
- Penglai Cabernet Sauvignon
- Changyu wines

## What to look for
- Cabernet Gernischt is a regional speciality.
- Wineries often combine wine with tourism.
