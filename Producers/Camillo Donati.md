# Camillo Donati

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small Parma grower making rustic, cloudy bottle fermented sparkling wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Emilia-Romagna]]
> - **Type:** Low intervention
> - **Main grapes:** [[Malvasia]], [[Lambrusco]]
> - **Styles:** [[Natural wine]]

## About
Donati works organically and bottles wines with little intervention, often finishing fermentation in bottle. The wines are lively, earthy and food friendly.

## Known for
- Malvasia Frizzante
- Lambrusco Frizzante
