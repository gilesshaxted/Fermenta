# Friuli-Venezia Giulia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Italy's finest white wine region, on the Slovenian border, with crisp whites and orange wines.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Temperate
> - **Soils:** ponca, marl, sandstone, gravel
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Gris]], [[Friulano]], [[Ribolla Gialla]], [[Sauvignon Blanc]], [[Merlot]]
> - **Styles:** [[Orange wine]], [[Natural wine]]

## Landscape and topography
Hills near the Slovenian border and plains stretching to the Adriatic, with the Julian Alps to the north.

## Soil and geology
Ponca (a flaky marl and sandstone) in Collio and the Colli Orientali, and gravel on the plains.

## Climate
Temperate, with cool Alpine air and warm Adriatic breezes.

## History
Controlled for centuries by Venice, Austria and later Italy, the area has a mixed cultural background. Producers such as Gravner and Radikon sparked the orange wine movement.

## Production and wine styles
Friulano, Ribolla Gialla, Pinot Grigio and Sauvignon for whites, with Merlot, Refosco and Schioppettino for reds.

## Key wines
- Collio
- Colli Orientali del Friuli
- Friulano
- Ribolla Gialla
- Orange wines

## What to look for
- Textured whites with real depth.
- Friulano is a local star.
