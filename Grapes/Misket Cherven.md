# Misket Cherven

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A pink-skinned native Bulgarian grape making floral, lightly aromatic white wines.

## Where it's from
Its name means red muscat, after its pinkish-red skin, though it is not a true Muscat. It has been grown in Bulgaria for a long time and is used for dry whites.

## Where it grows
- **Thracian Valley, Bulgaria:** the main area
- **Black Sea coast, Bulgaria:** also grown

## What it tastes like
- Rose, grape, peach and citrus

## Classic wines
- Misket Cherven

## Pairs with
- Fish
- Salads
- Soft cheese

## Good to know
- The name means red Misket.
- Aromatic but low in alcohol.
