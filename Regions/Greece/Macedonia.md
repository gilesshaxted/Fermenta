# Macedonia

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Northern Greece, with cool, high vineyards and bold modern wines, as well as Naoussa and Amyndeon.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Greece]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, sand, schist
> - **Main grapes:** [[Xinomavro]], [[Malagousia]], [[Assyrtiko]], [[Limnio]]
> - **Styles:** [[Retsina]]

## Landscape and topography
A mix of mountains, plains and hills across northern Greece, from Thessaloniki to the borders.

## Soil and geology
Clay, limestone, sand and schist.

## Climate
Continental in the north, with cold winters and warm summers, and Mediterranean influence near the coast.

## History
Modern Greek winemaking has strong roots in Macedonia, with Porto Carras on Halkidiki an important pioneer.

## Production and wine styles
Xinomavro dominates the reds, and whites include Malagousia, Assyrtiko and others. Amyndeon makes sparkling Xinomavro.

## Key wines
- Naoussa
- Amyndeon
- Goumenissa
- Rapsani
- Halkidiki wines

## What to look for
- Savoury reds and crisp whites.
- A varied area with great value.
