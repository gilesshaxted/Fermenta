# Ticino

> [!abstract] At a glance
> The Italian-speaking south of Switzerland, where Merlot is the dominant grape and the style leans Italian.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Switzerland]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Temperate
> - **Soils:** granite, gneiss, clay, gravel
> - **Main grapes:** [[Merlot]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Cabernet Franc]]

## Landscape and topography
Vineyards sit on terraces and valley slopes around Lake Maggiore and Lake Lugano, south of the Alps.

## Soil and geology
Mostly granite, gneiss, and glacial gravel and clay.

## Climate
Milder and wetter than the rest of Switzerland, with plenty of sun and a Mediterranean feel in summer.

## History
Merlot was brought in after phylloxera in the early twentieth century and became the standard. The area has since developed a serious reputation for the grape.

## Production and wine styles
Merlot dominates. Merlot del Ticino is the main label, with Bianco di Merlot, a white made from Merlot, as a local speciality.

## Key wines
- Merlot del Ticino
- Bianco di Merlot
- Ticino Rosso

## What to look for
- Smooth, medium bodied Merlot.
- Try Bianco di Merlot.
