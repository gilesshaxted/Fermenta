# Port (style)

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Sweet fortified wine from the Douro, from fresh ruby to long aged tawny.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Category:** Fortified
> - **Colour:** Red
> - **Sweetness:** Sweet
> - **Country:** Portugal
> - **Main grapes:** [[Touriga Nacional]], [[Touriga Franca]], [[Tinta Barroca]], [[Tinto Cão]], [[Tempranillo]]
> - **Regions:** [[Port]], [[Douro]]

## How it's made
Fermentation is stopped part way by adding grape spirit, which leaves sweetness and raises alcohol to around 20%. Ruby styles age briefly in large vessels. Tawny styles age in small barrels and slowly oxidise. Vintage Port is bottled young and ages in bottle.

## Where it's made
Grapes grow in the steep terraced Douro Valley, and the wine was historically shipped from Vila Nova de Gaia.

## What it tastes like
- Ruby: blackberry, plum, chocolate
- Tawny: caramel, walnut, dried fruit
- Vintage: dense, spicy, structured

## Pairs with
- Stilton
- Dark chocolate
- Nuts
- Christmas cake

## Good to know
- Tinta Roriz is the local name of Tempranillo.
- White and rosé Port are also made.
- Vintage Port needs decanting.
