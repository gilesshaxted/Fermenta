# Saperavi

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Georgia's best known red grape, dark, tannic and high in acidity.

## Where it's from
A very old Georgian variety. It is one of the few grapes with red flesh as well as dark skins.

## Where it grows
- **Kakheti, Georgia:** the heartland
- **Finger Lakes, USA:** a small amount

## What it tastes like
- Blackberry, plum, spice and earth

## Classic wines
- Mukuzani
- Kakheti Saperavi

## Pairs with
- Grilled meat
- Stews
- Hard cheese

## Good to know
- Deep colour, even in the flesh.
- Ages well.
