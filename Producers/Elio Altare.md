# Elio Altare

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A La Morra grower who became a leading figure of the modernist movement in Barolo.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Barolo]], [[Piedmont]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Nebbiolo]], [[Barbera]], [[Dolcetto]]

## About
Altare cut yields and used small French barriques to produce fruit forward, supple Barolo. His work helped change how many young growers in the Langhe approached their vineyards.

## Known for
- Barolo Arborina
- Barolo Brunate
- Barolo Vigna Larigi
- Barolo La Villa
