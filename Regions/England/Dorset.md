# Dorset

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Chalk downs and limestone coast with dramatic scenery and rising sparkling wine reputation.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[England]]
> - **Part of:** [[Europe]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** chalk, limestone, clay
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Pinot Meunier]]
> - **Styles:** [[English sparkling wine]]

## Landscape and topography
Rolling chalk downs and limestone hills inland of the Jurassic Coast.

## Soil and geology
Chalk, limestone and clay.

## Climate
Cool maritime, with sheltered valleys.

## History
Dorset's chalk geology is part of the same belt as the South Downs, and several producers have taken advantage.

## Production and wine styles
Mostly sparkling wine.

## Key wines
- Dorset sparkling

## What to look for
- Crisp, mineral sparkling wines.
- Often sold direct from the vineyard.
