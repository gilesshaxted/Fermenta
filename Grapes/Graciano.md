# Graciano

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small but valuable Rioja grape that adds acidity, colour and ageing potential.

## Where it's from
Rioja, in northern Spain. It is related to the Tintilla grape of Jerez.

## Where it grows
- **Rioja, Spain:** in small amounts in blends

## What it tastes like
- Blackberry, violet and spice

## Classic wines
- Rioja Reserva blends
- Rioja Gran Reserva

## Pairs with
- Lamb
- Roast meat

## Good to know
- Hard to grow but adds elegance.
- Sometimes bottled on its own.
