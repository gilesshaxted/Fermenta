# Clare Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A cool, high valley with bone-dry Riesling that ages brilliantly.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Australia]]
> - **Part of:** [[Oceania]]
> - **Climate:** Continental
> - **Soils:** terra rossa, limestone, shale, loam
> - **Main grapes:** [[Riesling]], [[Syrah]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]], [[Grenache]]

## Landscape and topography
A string of valleys at 300 to 500 metres, north of the Barossa.

## Soil and geology
Terra rossa over limestone, plus shale and loam.

## Climate
Warm days, cold nights and altitude moderating the heat.

## History
Jesuit missionaries planted vines in 1851, and Riesling has been a hallmark ever since.

## Production and wine styles
Riesling above all, with Shiraz and Cabernet.

## Key wines
- Clare Valley Riesling
- Clare Valley Shiraz

## What to look for
- Lime, steel and kerosene with age.
- Screwcap pioneers in 2000.

- [[Grosset]]
