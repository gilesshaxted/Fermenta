# Slovenia

Part of [[Europe]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small country with a big wine culture, from skin-contact Rebula in the west to crisp aromatic whites in the north east.

## How wine is organised here
- Wine is classified into three regions: Primorska, Podravje and Posavje, each divided into districts.
- Quality wines carry the district or region, and labels show the grape variety and vintage.
- Many wines use local names for grapes, such as Rebula for Ribolla Gialla and Šipon for Furmint.
