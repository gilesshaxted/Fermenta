# Domaine Charles Joguet

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A respected Chinon estate making elegant, age-worthy Cabernet Franc from different terroirs.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[France]]
> - **Region:** [[Chinon]], [[Loire Valley]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer

## About
Charles Joguet is one of the names that built the modern reputation of Chinon. The wines range from light and fresh to serious and structured, with a clear sense of place.

## Known for
- Les Varennes du Grand Clos
- Clos de la Dioterie
- Cuvée de la Cure
