# Žilavka

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A full, mineral white grape from Herzegovina, also grown in North Macedonia.

## Where it's from
The main white of Herzegovina, grown around Mostar. It is also planted in North Macedonia.

## Where it grows
- **Herzegovina, Bosnia and Herzegovina:** the heartland
- **Tikveš, North Macedonia:** also grown

## What it tastes like
- Peach, apple, herbs and almond

## Classic wines
- Tikveš Žilavka
- Mostar Žilavka

## Pairs with
- Grilled fish
- Poultry
- Soft cheese

## Good to know
- Can age well.
- Rich yet fresh.
