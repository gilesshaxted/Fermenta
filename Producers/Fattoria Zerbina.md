# Fattoria Zerbina

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Faenza estate that showed the quality that Romagna Albana and Sangiovese can reach.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Italy]]
> - **Region:** [[Romagna]], [[Emilia-Romagna]]
> - **Type:** Notable producer
> - **Main grapes:** [[Albana]], [[Sangiovese]]
> - **Styles:** [[Passito]]

## About
Zerbina works in the hills around Faenza and makes both dry and sweet wines. The sweet Albana is a regional highlight, and the Sangiovese is rich and savoury.

## Known for
- Scacco Matto
- Pietramora Sangiovese
- Marzieno
