# Petite Sirah

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Inky, tannic and powerful, a California speciality.

## Where it's from
The grape is Durif, a cross of Syrah and Peloursin found in France in the 1860s. Californian vineyards often have it in field blends.

## Where it grows
- **California, USA:** Napa, Lodi and Paso Robles
- **Australia:** known as Durif, in Rutherglen

## What it tastes like
- Blueberry, blackberry, pepper and dark chocolate
- Big tannin and deep colour

## Classic wines
- Napa Petite Sirah
- Rutherglen Durif

## Pairs with
- Barbecued ribs
- Strong cheese
- Game

## Good to know
- Not the same as Syrah, though it is a parent.
- Ages well thanks to tannin.
