# Grüner Veltliner

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Austria's signature white, peppery and savoury with great food flexibility.

## Where it's from
Austria, where DNA analysis links it to Traminer and a rare variety found in St. Georgen.

## Where it grows
- **Weinviertel, Austria:** the largest zone, light and peppery
- **Wachau, Kamptal and Kremstal, Austria:** richer, ageworthy versions

## What it tastes like
- **Young and light:** white pepper, lime and green apple
- **Reserve:** richer, spicy, honeyed and complex

## Classic wines
- Weinviertel DAC
- Wachau Smaragd
- Kamptal Reserve

## Pairs with
- Schnitzel
- Asparagus
- Asian dishes
- Vegetable dishes

## Good to know
- Known as Gruvee or GV by sommeliers.
- The pepper aroma is a signature.
