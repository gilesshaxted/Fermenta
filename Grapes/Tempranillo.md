# Tempranillo

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Savoury cherry and leather with oak spice. The great red grape of Spain.

## Where it's from
Northern Spain. The name comes from temprano, meaning early, because it ripens ahead of many Spanish grapes.

## Where it grows
- **Rioja, Spain:** classic blends, graded Crianza to Gran Reserva
- **Ribera del Duero, Spain:** darker, more powerful, called Tinto Fino
- **Toro, Spain:** big, deep and concentrated
- **La Mancha, Spain:** everyday reds, called Cencibel
- **Douro and Alentejo, Portugal:** known as Tinta Roriz and Aragonez, used in Port
- **Mendoza, Argentina:** ripe and polished

## What it tastes like
- **Fruit:** red cherry, plum, dried fig
- **Savoury:** leather, tobacco, dried herbs
- **With oak:** vanilla, coconut, dill (American oak)

## Classic wines
- Rioja Crianza, Reserva and Gran Reserva
- Ribera del Duero
- Toro
- Douro reds

## Pairs with
- Roast lamb
- Chorizo and jamón
- Roast pork
- Manchego

## Good to know
- Rioja labels tell you how long the wine was aged: Crianza, Reserva, Gran Reserva.
- Traditionally aged in American oak, which gives the vanilla and coconut notes.
