# English sparkling wine

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Traditional method sparkling wine from the chalk and clay of southern England.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Category:** Sparkling
> - **Colour:** White
> - **Sweetness:** Dry
> - **Country:** England
> - **Main grapes:** [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Noir]], [[Pinot Meunier]]
> - **Regions:** [[England]], [[Sussex]], [[Kent]], [[Hampshire]], [[Surrey]], [[Essex]], [[Cornwall and Devon]], [[Dorset]], [[Wiltshire]]

## How it's made
Mostly made from the Champagne grapes using the traditional method. The chalk of the south downs is the same band that runs under Champagne, and the cool climate gives high acidity.

## Where it's made
Sussex, Kent, Hampshire, Surrey, Dorset, Essex, Cornwall and Devon, and also Wiltshire.

## What it tastes like
- Green apple, lemon, white flowers
- Brioche and toast with ageing
- Bright, mouth watering acidity

## Pairs with
- Fish and chips
- Oysters
- Smoked salmon
- Afternoon tea

## Good to know
- Sparkling wine is by far the biggest category in English wine.
- Protected names include English Quality Sparkling Wine and PDO Sussex.
