# Kayra

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A major Turkish producer known for wines from local Anatolian grapes.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Turkey]]
> - **Region:** [[Eastern Anatolia]], [[Thrace and Marmara]]
> - **Type:** Major producer

## About
Kayra works with grapes from the eastern highlands as well as other regions. It is well known for blends of the native red grapes Öküzgözü and Boğazkere.
