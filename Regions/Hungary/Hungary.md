# Hungary

Part of [[Europe]].

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A Central European wine country with a long tradition, famous for Tokaji sweet wine, the Furmint grape and Bull's Blood red blends.

## How Hungarian wine is organised
- **PDO** (OEM) covers defined wine regions, with **PGI** (OFJ) for broader areas.
- Hungary has 22 wine regions, from Tokaj in the north east to Villány in the south.
- Tokaj has its own rules for Aszú and Szamorodni.
- Native grapes such as Furmint, Hárslevelű and Kadarka are central to the story.
