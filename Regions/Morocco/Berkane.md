# Berkane

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A small area in the northeast of Morocco near the Algerian border and the Mediterranean, known for reds and rosés.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[Morocco]]
> - **Part of:** [[Africa]]
> - **Climate:** Mediterranean
> - **Soils:** clay, limestone, sand
> - **Main grapes:** [[Grenache]], [[Cinsault]], [[Syrah]], [[Carignan]], [[Cabernet Sauvignon]]

## Landscape and topography
Plains and low hills near the Beni Snassen mountains, in the northeast.

## Soil and geology
Clay, limestone and sand.

## Climate
Warm and dry Mediterranean, with a maritime influence nearer the coast.

## History
Berkane is a designated wine area in the northeast, with a long history of Grenache and Cinsault.

## Production and wine styles
Mostly Mediterranean reds and rosés, from Grenache, Cinsault, Carignan and Syrah.

## Key wines
- Berkane red
- Berkane rosé

## What to look for
- Sunny, rustic reds.
- Hard to find abroad.
