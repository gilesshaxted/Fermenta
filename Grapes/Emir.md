# Emir

> [!abstract] At a glance
> A crisp, mineral white from the volcanic soils of Cappadocia.

## Where it's from
Grown in Cappadocia for centuries, in volcanic tuff soils, and now used for still and sparkling wines.

## Where it grows
- **Cappadocia, Turkey:** the heartland

## What it tastes like
- Lemon, green apple, mineral and white flowers

## Classic wines
- Cappadocia Emir

## Pairs with
- Seafood
- Mezze
- Light dishes

## Good to know
- High acid.
- Good for sparkling wine.
