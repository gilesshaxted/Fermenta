# Willamette Valley

> [!abstract] At a glance
> Oregon's flagship region, making Burgundy-like Pinot Noir in a cool, green valley.

> [!info] Quick facts
> - **Country:** [[USA]]
> - **Part of:** [[North America]]
> - **Climate:** Cool maritime
> - **Soils:** volcanic, basalt, loess, marine sediment
> - **Main grapes:** [[Pinot Noir]], [[Chardonnay]], [[Pinot Gris]], [[Riesling]]

## Landscape and topography
A broad valley between the Coast Range and the Cascades, with hills on the western side.

## Soil and geology
Volcanic basalt (Jory soils), windblown loess (Laurelwood) and marine sediments.

## Climate
Cool, rainy winters and dry, mild summers, with a long ripening period.

## History
David Lett planted Pinot Noir in 1965 at Eyrie Vineyards, and the region was recognised internationally in the 1980s. Burgundy's Drouhin planted in Dundee Hills in 1987.

## Production and wine styles
Pinot Noir leads, with Chardonnay, Pinot Gris and sparkling wine.

## Key wines
- Willamette Valley Pinot Noir
- Willamette Chardonnay
- Oregon Pinot Gris

## What to look for
- Red cherry, earth and acidity.
- The valley has seven nested AVAs, including Dundee Hills and Eola-Amity Hills.
